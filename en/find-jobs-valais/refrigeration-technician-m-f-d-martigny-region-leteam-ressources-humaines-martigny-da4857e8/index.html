Description of post

For one of our customers, a company active in the field of cold and building technology, we are looking for a Frigorist to strengthen his team.

You will be required to intervene on different construction sites and with different customers for the installation, commissioning, maintenance and troubleshooting of refrigeration installations.

Profile sought

  • Experience in the installation and maintenance of refrigeration systems
  • Troubleshooting and troubleshooting skills
  • Good technical and electrical knowledge
  • Ability to work independently
  • Service and customer contact
  • Good physical condition
  • Driving licence, an asset
  • Motivation, seriousness and reliability
