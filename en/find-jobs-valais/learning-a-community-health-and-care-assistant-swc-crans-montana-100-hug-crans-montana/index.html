Full description

At the Crans-Montana Clinic, community health and care assistants work closely with nurses to support patients and patients in day-to-day care that includes body hygiene, food, mobilization and comfort care. You develop and promote human relations. As an apprentice or apprentice in community health and care, you provide medical procedures such as the administration of medicines, injections and the setting up of dressings on delegation and in collaboration with nurses. You are also required to assess the level of their pain. In collaboration with the medical-care team, you manage emergency situations by detecting signs, giving alerts, and managing first actions with the nursing or medical body. You accompany people in crisis situations with an appropriate attitude. You support people with somatic disorders by identifying their needs Stewardship of care materials, cleaning and maintaining the patient's environment, receiving patients upon admission, etc., are also part of the ASSC competency profile. The variety of services provided and the requirement imposed by the hospital environment allows for a varied course of training. To complete your journey, we offer in-house and external internships of the institution that allow you to discover other types of patient.
