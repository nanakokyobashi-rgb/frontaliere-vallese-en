Full description

The Quartier LôDzé is looking for a 100% (M/F) game leader to strengthen its teams. We are looking for a person who is passionate about cooking, creative and rigorous, with an excellent sense of organization and concerned with the quality of the products, the presentation of the dishes and the satisfaction of our customers.

Your missions

  • •Preparing and making the dishes of your party in collaboration with the Executive Chef.
  • •Ensure the finishing, dressing and quality control of the dishes before they start in the room.
  • •Ensure compliance with the hotel's standards, procedures and culinary identity.
  • •Organize implementation and manage the technical and material needs of your party.
  • •To work effectively with the entire kitchen team.
  • •Participate in the development of culinary offerings and in the implementation of innovative projects.
  • •Ensure quality, freshness and good product conservation.
  • •Strictly apply HACCP hygiene and food safety rules.

Profile sought

  • •Training in cooking (CFC or equivalent training).
  • •Experience confirmed in a similar position, ideally in a restaurant or hotel establishment.
  • Mastery of culinary techniques and preparation methods.
  • •Cooking pass, creativity and attention to detail.
  • Organizational awareness, autonomy and ability to effectively manage your part.
  • Team spirit and excellent communication skills.
  • Ability to work under pressure and manage priorities during services.
  • •Strict compliance with HACCP hygiene and food safety rules.
  • •Flexibility and availability according to the needs of the establishment, including weekends and holidays.

We offer you

  • •Reduction for employees at different points of sale
  • 5 holiday weeks, 6 holidays
  • •Salary according to the applicable CTC and 13th month.
  • •A dynamic and varied working framework.

We will only examine the complete files (CV, cover letter, work certificates, etc.) of the candidates corresponding to the profile sought.
