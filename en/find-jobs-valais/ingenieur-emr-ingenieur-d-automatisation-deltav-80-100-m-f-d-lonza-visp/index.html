Lonza is now a world leader in life sciences, operating on three continents. We work in science, but there is no magic formula for how we approach work. To strengthen our engineering & maintenance team at the Visp site, we are looking for an electrical, measurement and control (EMR) engineer. You are responsible for maintenance as well as for projects in mono- or multi-product plants.

This is what we offer you

  • An agile career and dynamic work culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize performance
  • In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous benefits for lifestyle, family and leisure. All global services can be found here: Benefits - Visp

Your tasks

  • Technical project management or participation in small projects and investment projects
  • Professional planning, preparation and coordination of EMR maintenance, including internal and external partners
  • Order allocation, capacity planning, maintenance of reporting in SAP and ensuring plant availability
  • Collaboration in the interdisciplinary engineering team, support of operations management
  • Compliance with safety, quality, hygiene and environmental standards
  • Continuous optimization of plants, processes and maintenance processes, participation in risk analyses
  • Maintenance of plant documentation in SAP/COMOS, flowchart maintenance
  • Budget monitoring for projects, investments and maintenance

Your profile

  • Completed Technical School (HF), Bachelor (FH) or Master (ETH) in Electrical, Automation or Systems Engineering
  • Experience in EMR maintenance/projects in industry is beneficial
  • Knowledge of SAP/COMOS/DeltaV/Modicon/Cimplicity, good office knowledge
  • German fluent, English beneficial
  • Independent, flexible, capable of teamwork, structured and organized
  • Reliable, resilient, solution-oriented, open to new ideas and highly motivated
