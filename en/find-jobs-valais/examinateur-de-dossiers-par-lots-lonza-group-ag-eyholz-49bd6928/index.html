Batch Record Reviewer

Are you looking to make a direct impact on the delivery of life\-saving medicines? Join our dynamic Quality Assurance team in Visp as a Batch Record Reviewer, where you will ensure flawless compliance, data integrity, and operational excellence across critical production projects.

This is a fully site\-based role. Working together in person supports close, real\-time collaboration and the technical precision needed to manufacture medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What you will get:

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace.

What you will do:

  • Ensure the timely release and compliance of life\-saving medicines by performing rigorous batch record reviews.
  • Partner with cross\-functional teams, including Manufacturing and Quality, to investigate and resolve batch discrepancies.
  • Analyze electronic and paper\-based batch records within state\-of\-the\-art MES (Syncade), DeltaV, SAP, TrackWise, and LIMS environments.
  • Evaluate process parameter failures, material consumption, and operator comments in strict alignment with ALCOA\+ and GDP principles.
  • Draft clear, compliant quality impact statements and author post\-production lifecycle documentation.
  • Drive continuous improvement and data integrity standards across site production lines.

What we are looking for:

  • Degree in Life Sciences, Biotechnology, or a related scientific discipline (or equivalent practical experience).
  • Proven track record working within a GMP\-regulated manufacturing or quality assurance environment.
  • Hands\-on experience with electronic batch record systems (MES Syncade, DeltaV) and quality management systems (TrackWise, SAP, LIMS).
  • Solid understanding of Good Documentation Practices (GDP) and ALCOA\+ data integrity principles.
  • Strong problem\-solving mindset with the ability to work independently and meet strict production deadlines.
  • Fluency in English (written and spoken) is required
  • German reading proficiency is a strong advantage.

About Lonza At Lonza, their people are their greatest strength. With 30\+ sites across five continents, their globally connected teams work together every day to manufacture the medicines of tomorrow.

Their core values of Collaboration, Accountability, Excellence, Passion and Integrity reflect who they are and how they work together.
