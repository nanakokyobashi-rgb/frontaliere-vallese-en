Responsibilities & Qualifications

Lead. Transform. Deliver.

We are looking for an experienced and inspiring Site Manager to lead our industrial operations in Sierre. This is a key leadership role with full responsibility for site performance, people development, and operational excellence in a demanding and highly regulated environment.

Your role

As Site Manager, you will lead the overall performance of the plant, ensuring safety, quality, delivery, and cost targets are achieved while driving continuous improvement and long-term competitiveness, in line with the Novelis Operating System (NOS) to achieve World Class Manufacturing standards. You act as a true business leader, combining operational rigor, financial accountability, and strong people leadership.

Your responsibilities

Overall site performance

  • Deliver on KPIs (safety, quality, cost, productivity, delivery)

Health, Safety & Compliance

  • Ensure strict compliance with Swiss labor law, EHS standards, and company policies
  • Foster a strong safety culture across all levels of the organization

Operational Excellence

  • Drive continuous improvement (Lean mindset)
  • Optimize processes, productivity, and cost efficiency

Strategy & Transformation

  • Define and implement site strategy aligned with business objectives
  • Lead investment projects and technological upgrades

Financial Leadership

  • Manage site budget and performance targets
  • Ensure sustainable profitability and cost discipline

Leadership & People Development

  • Lead and develop your direct reports and broader organization (~200+ employees)
  • Build a high-performance culture based on accountability and engagement

Stakeholder Management

  • Collaborate with matrix functions (Quality, Supply Chain, EHS, HR, Finance)
  • Represent the site internally and externally at local and global level.

  • Excellent leadership, communication, and stakeholder management skills

Location Profile

Novelis’ Sierre is located in the Valais Switzerland employing around 550 people. It mainly focuses on production of aluminium sheet for the automotive market covering the entire process from casting to finishing. The plant also hosts a world-leading Research & Innovation Center specialized in the development of new automotive sheet alloys surface treatment and joining technologies. In Feburary 2022 together with research institutions and a local energy provider Novelis founded the Net Zero Lab Valais to develop carbon-neutral solutions for aluminium manufacturing. The lab focuses on identifying and implementing innovative solutions to neutralize the carbon footprint of Novelis’ manufacturing operations and neighboring communities starting in Sierre. The Sierre plant supports its community through direct sponsorship of many annual events promoting education sports and culture.
