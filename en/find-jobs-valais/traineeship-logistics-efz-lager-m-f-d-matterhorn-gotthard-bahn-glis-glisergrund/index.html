Full description

Your drive The heart of BVZ Holding is the railway company Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn and the international top brands Gornergrat Bahn and Glacier Express. Passionate and qualified employees form the basis of our success. Every day, around 700 employees are committed to offering our guests a first-class experience. We live diversity and have one thing in common: Emotions are our drive. What is your drive? At the beginning of our apprenticeship in August 2027, we offer an apprenticeship as a logistics provider EFZ in our workshop in Glisergrund. What to expect

  • You are responsible for the management (storage, entry and departure, inventory) of the allocated replacement material
  • You take care of the goods entry and exit control, the booking of the goods, cover letter of deliveries, the distribution and the storage of the goods
  • You will be trained in driving loading vehicles such as forklift trucks and will be responsible for loading and unloading freight vehicles safely
  • You will learn how to safely handle hazardous substances and goods and help with waste disposal in Glisergrund

Your profile

  • You are enthusiastic about the world of public transport and have a great interest in logistics processes
  • You think logically, work organized and are characterized by a good sense of order
  • You work carefully and structured and are sociable and communicative
  • You are happy to take responsibility, especially when handling hazardous substances safely

Your advantages

  • A varied and exciting entry into the professional world
  • Attractive learning and working environment with responsible and interesting work
  • A GA and other attractive benefits along our railway line
  • Annual learning trip and team events
  • Financial participation in the private laptop for the vocational school
  • 6 weeks vacation during your apprenticeship
