Full description

Where necessary, eligible candidates and their families will be assisted in moving.

Become part of our logistics team in Visp! As a Logistics Process Engineer / Logistics Expert 80-100% (m/f/d), your professional expertise, your processual thinking and your organizational skills ensure that logistics processes within Logistics Visp are executed efficiently, GMP-compliant and optimized. You monitor and take responsibility for end-to-end processes and accompany and control the further development of processes in the area of logistics as well as cross-divisional processes such as cold chain material management. In addition, optimize You provide the interfaces between production, operation logistics, supply chain, QA/QC and contribute significantly to process stability and transparency in this complex GMP environment.

Your tasks

  • Independent recording, analysis, planning, optimization and presentation of logistical workflows
  • Evaluating the technical and operational feasibility of new layouts, process and system changes while ensuring scalability, security and performance.
  • Implementation of operational requirements into detailed technical specifications and technical documentation (floor plans, process descriptions, material flow concepts).
  • Presentation of analysis, project results and operational insights to stakeholders at all levels of the organisation.
  • Support of the operational logistics teams in the area of process monitoring and operational KPIs
  • Responsibility for developing concepts to improve processes in close coordination with operational teams
  • Management of optimization projects in the field of logistics
  • Coordination of interfaces between Logistics and the stakeholders listed above
  • Creation of a database for controlling and controlling process optimizations
  • Responsibility for creating KPIs Dashboards, SOPs or Work-Instructions

Your requirement profile

  • Degree in logistics engineering, industrial engineering, mechanical engineering or a related technical field
  • In-depth professional experience in logistics, economics or systems engineering, ideally with projects in the field of logistics or intralogistics
  • Vocational education / further education as a logistics specialist
  • Min. 3 years in the field of logistics in a GMP environment
  • Skills in the creation and evaluation of technical documentation, drawings and specificationsExcellent understanding and knowledge in the field of logistics processes. Very good understanding of GMP. IT skills (Office, SAP, Trackwise, DMS, and Power Bi)
  • A practical approach, coupled with a strong sense of responsibility
  • Appropriate step-by-step communication with internal interfaces (supply chain, operation logistics, QA, QC), solution-oriented working methods, outstanding planning and organizational talent within complex projects, excellent team and social skills
  • Structured, focused and organized way of working, assertiveness and motivated, working independently, flexible and resilient - strength in networked thinking
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing as well as good knowledge of English are an advantage.
