Full description

Lonza is today a leading global life sciences company that operates on three continents. We work in science, but there is no magic formula for how we approach our work. Our most important scientific solution is talented employees who work together and develop ideas that help other companies help people. In return, our employees manage their careers independently. Because their ideas, both big and small, improve the world. And that is the kind of work we want to be part of.

At the Visp site, an apprentice plant and apparatus builder (EFZ) is being sought. As a plant and apparatus builder at Lonza, you manufacture various products based on technical drawings.

  • You mainly process various types of sheet metal, profiles and pipes.
  • You assemble these into components, containers, piping systems or apparatuses, which are subsequently integrated into systems and plants.
  • In addition, you carry out the associated assembly, maintenance and repair work.

In the training workshop, you can practise the various techniques on different materials so that you can later correctly prepare the product directly for the respective client from the production department.

Your training

Plant and apparatus builders are trained for 4 years in the training company and specialist workshops, with up to two days of vocational school per week.

During the basic training in the first half, knowledge and skills are developed with a focus on sheet-metal, metal-profile and pipe processing using traditional tools, as well as through training on computer-controlled bending machines, welding robots and laser-cutting systems.

In the second half, specialised training takes place in operational fields and specialist workshops, with specialist knowledge deepened through practical learning projects for Lonza.

From the 3rd year of training, you will specialise in one of the various specialisations:

  • Construction technology
  • Pipe construction technology
  • Welding technology

Your profile

  • Interest in technical contexts and manual dexterity
  • Good spatial visualization skills
  • Good technical understanding
  • Reliable, independent work
  • No allergies to metals, greases and oils
  • Ability to work in a team
  • Very good German language skills (at least B2 level)
