Your missions

The powers of the Registrars are listed in Art. 9a of the law implementing the Swiss Code of Criminal Procedure of 11 February 2009. They consist essentially of:

  • To assist the judges of the Public Prosecutor's Office, in particular by examining cases,
  • the drafting of legal opinions, the holding of minutes and the drafting of
  • draft decisions
  • On delegation, investigate and judge cases by criminal order where
  • the penalty does not appear to exceed a fine or penalty
  • pecuniary amount not exceeding 90 days fine; make decisions on suspension of
  • procedure, non-entry and classification
  • On warrant, investigate cases where the penalty appears not to be met
  • or a six-month deprivation of liberty.
