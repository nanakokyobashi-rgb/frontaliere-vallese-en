Full description

Job Profiles Hotel Auditor The Hotel Auditor is a professional specialized in evaluating the performance, compliance, and profitability of a hotel or tourist establishment. His/her role is to analyze management practices, service quality, regulatory compliance, and profitability in order to propose strategic recommendations . As a true expert in control and consulting, he/she assists hotels, resorts, and hotel chains in optimizing their operations and competitiveness.

Responsibilities

  • Evaluate financial and operational management: Control budgets, costs, billing, and profit margins.
  • Analyze compliance and service quality: Verify adherence to hygiene, safety, and quality standards (ISO, hotel labels, brand standards).
  • Observe and audit processes: Analyze the work organization across various departments (accommodation, catering, front office, housekeeping, F&B).
  • Measure client satisfaction: Through surveys, mystery audits, and monitoring online reviews.
  • Formulate strategic recommendations: Suggest areas for improvement to optimize performance, reduce costs, or increase client satisfaction.
  • Support management: Provide an external and objective perspective to guide strategic decisions.

Required Skills

  • Solid knowledge of hotel management and financial analysis.
  • Mastery of quality, safety, and hotel regulatory standards.
  • Capacity for observation, analysis, and synthesis.
  • Communication skills to present results and convince management.
  • A sense of teaching (pedagogy) and guidance in change management.
  • Good command of IT tools (reporting, ERP, hotel management software).
  • Fluency in foreign languages (English in particular) is an asset for auditing international establishments.

Education to Become a Hotel Auditor A degree level of Bac+3 to Bac+5 (Bachelor's to Master's equivalent) is generally required:

  • Bachelor's in Hotel Management , Management, Audit, or Finance.
  • MBA specialized in hospitality management or in audit and management control.
  • Prior experience in the hotel industry (management, revenue management, management control, quality) is highly valued.

Specific certifications in quality auditing or internal control may complement the professional background. Salary of a Hotel Auditor

  • Entry-level: Between €2,800 and €3,500 gross per month.
  • Confirmed Profile (Experienced): Between €3,500 and €5,000 gross per month.
  • Senior or Independent: Up to €6,000 or more, depending on the size and reputation of the audited establishments.

Career Progression After a few years, the Hotel Auditor can progress to roles such as:

  • Quality Director in a hotel chain.
  • Management Controller specializing in the hotel sector.
  • Independent Consultant in the hotel and tourism industry.
  • Hotel Director or Department Head (Accommodation, Catering, F&B) with reinforced expertise in operational steering.
