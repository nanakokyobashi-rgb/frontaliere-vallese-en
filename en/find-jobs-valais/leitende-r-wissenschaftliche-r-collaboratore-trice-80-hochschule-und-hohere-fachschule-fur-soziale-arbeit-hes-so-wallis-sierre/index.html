Full description

The HES-SO Valais-Wallis trains more than 2-800 students and makes an important contribution to the economic and social development of Valais through research and innovation.

In order to strengthen teaching and research, HESTS offers the following job with its FH degree programme, two RF courses and an institute:

Senior Scientific Officer (80%) ##

Their tasks

  • Contribute to shaping teaching (50-60%): They teach in the German-language bachelor program Social Work, in particular in the module Interprofessionality (InterPro), and accompany students in their bachelor theses.
  • Actively shape research and think about research processes (20-30%): They participate in research projects of the Institute for Social Work as well as in projects with external clients. They also deal with current issues relating to research data management, open access and research ethics and contribute to the further development of corresponding practices. You can develop your own research ideas, contribute specialist focuses and support the conception, further development and implementation of further education offers.

Your profile

  • have a master’s degree in social work or a related discipline
  • An ongoing doctorate is an advantage.
  • They have at least five years of professional experience (research, teaching, social work practice).
  • In-depth knowledge of quantitative and mixed method approaches to research is beneficial.
  • They enjoy building and maintaining partnerships.
  • You can understandably prepare and convey complex content.
  • They work in a structured way, have organizational skills and a strong team spirit.
  • They have excellent knowledge of German and good oral and written knowledge of French.
