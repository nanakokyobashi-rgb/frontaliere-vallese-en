Full description

If necessary, relocation assistance is available to eligible candidates and their families.

Become part of our EHS team in Visp and actively contribute to ensuring a safe, healthy and sustainable working environment in a state-of-the-art production environment. In this role, you will work closely with various departments, advise employees and managers on EHS topics and support compliance with the highest safety and quality standards. Together, we promote a strong safety culture and drive continuous improvement.

This position requires a full on-site presence. Personal collaboration allows for close coordination in real-time and ensures the technical precision required to produce medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What we offer you

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous benefits in the areas of lifestyle, family and leisure. You can also find the full list of our global benefits at: Benefits - Visp

Your tasks

  • Ensuring compliance with occupational safety, health and environmental regulations in the field of logistics
  • Carrying out risk assessments and developing and implementing appropriate protective measures.
  • Support and advice for managers and employees on all EHS-related issues.
  • Conduct safety walks, audits, and inspections, and follow up on corrective actions.
  • Investigation of incidents and near-incidents together with the company, including root cause analysis and determination of measures.
  • Planning and implementation of training and awareness-raising measures in the field of occupational health and safety.
  • Contribute to the continuous improvement of the safety culture as well as the EHS processes and management systems.

Your requirement profile

  • Completed training at Bachelor's or Master's level in chemistry, biotechnology, pharmaceutical technology, engineering sciences or a comparable discipline; alternatively, further training as a technician, master craftsman or equivalent qualification with many years of professional experience
  • Recognised further training as a safety specialist EKAS / ASGS or the willingness to acquire this qualification in a timely manner
  • Certification as a dangerous goods safety advisor according to GGBV is an advantage
  • Several years of professional experience (approx. 5-10 years) in chemical, pharmaceutical or biotechnological production as well as in a regulated environment.
  • Sound knowledge of occupational safety, health and environmental protection (EHS) in scientific or technical work environments.
  • Very good written and spoken German; good English skills are an advantage for working in an international environment.
  • Strong communication and consulting skills as well as the ability to implement safety standards in a practical way and promote a sustainable safety culture
