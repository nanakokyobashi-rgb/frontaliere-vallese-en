HR Assistant Recruitment (M/F)

We have been living the "Human Dimension" for over 20 years, with passion, entrepreneurial fiber and team spirit.

Benevolence, smile energy and a strong commitment are the key words to accompany you at every stage of your professional transitions.

For one of our clients, active in the field of big work in Valais, we are looking for a committed, versatile and enthusiastic personality to strengthen its administrative team, we are looking for a HR Assistant (M/F)

Your future responsibilities:

  • You will ensure the physical and telephone reception of employees with professionalism and friendliness.
  • You will be responsible for the entry of hours and administrative management of contracts.
  • You will support the recruitment process, from the first analysis of applications to the screening of profiles.
  • You will manage and maintain the administrative records of staff.
  • You will follow up on accident reports, claims for family allowances and questions related to salary counts.
  • You will facilitate exchanges and coordination between the teams on the construction sites and the various interlocutors.
  • You will manage unexpected events with reactivity and find suitable solutions.
  • You will actively contribute to the proper organization and functioning of the administrative department.

Your profile:

  • You benefit from a confirmed experience in a similar HR administrative function, ideally in the construction sector
  • You have perfect command of French, both oral and written, and excellent editorial skills.
  • You speak fluently Portuguese and/or Spanish (indispensable).
  • You master the usual computer tools and easily adapt to new software.
  • You are an organized, rigorous and autonomous person.
  • You stand out for your smile, your positive energy and your excellent sense of relationship.
  • You show discretion, reliability and a true team spirit.
  • You know how to manage priorities, take initiatives and keep your effectiveness even in urgent situations

Your conditions:

  • You integrate a dynamic and stimulating professional environment.
  • You occupy a versatile function, at the heart of the company's activities.
  • You are in an area where human contact and team spirit are essential.
  • You have the opportunity to value your skills and play an important role in the daily operation of the company.
