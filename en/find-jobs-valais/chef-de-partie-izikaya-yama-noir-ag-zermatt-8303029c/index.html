Full description

Here we are

Yama Noir combines Japanese cuisine, authentic aromas and a unique atmosphere. With great attention to detail, we create a special enjoyment experience that combines tradition and modernity.

Are you interested and motivated to become part of the Yama Noir team? Then use this unique opportunity and become part of our culinary journey!

These are yours

  • Tasks:

Kitchen Tour: Guide the Izakaya kitchen independently and ensure the quality of all dishes. Izakaya & Sushi: Professionally prepare Japanese dishes such as Yakitori, Robata, Tempura and simple sushi variations. Purchasing & Storage: Responsible for orders, suppliers, receipt of goods, storage and inventory. Quality & Food Cost: Ensure freshness, product quality, cost control and food waste prevention. Team & Organization: Plan Mise en Place, guide the kitchen help and coordinate workflows. Hygiene & Safety: comply with HACCP standards, perform temperature controls and ensure the cleanliness of the kitchen. Events & Creativity: Prepare food for events according to function sheets and contribute ideas for seasonal dishes and specials.

These are your characteristics

Education & experience: Completed culinary training (EFZ) or comparable qualification as well as several years of experience as Demi Chef or Chef de Partie, ideally in Japanese or Asian cuisine. Expertise: Basic knowledge of sushi preparation, shari production and safe handling of raw fish. Organization & self-employment: Experience in ordering, receiving goods and warehouse management as well as the ability to manage a kitchen independently and solution-oriented. Quality & Hygiene: Very good knowledge of HACCP standards as well as a strong quality and cost awareness. Resilience & Flexibility: Physical resilience, stress resistance and readiness for shift work. Appearance & Team Spirit: Friendly, well-groomed appearance and joy of working in an open kitchen. Languages: very good knowledge of German and English

  • Other languages are beneficial.
