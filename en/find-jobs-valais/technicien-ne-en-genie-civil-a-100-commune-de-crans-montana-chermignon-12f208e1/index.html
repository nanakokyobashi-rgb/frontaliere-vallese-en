A TECHNICIAN IN CIVIL GENIA 100%

The Public Domain Service brings together various activities related to the development, equipment and maintenance of the territory, road infrastructure and network. He leads, develops and directs projects and profiles his expertise while having optimal and sustainable management of public resources, in order to offer the population a quality service.

You have a sense of public service, look for an attractive work environment within a dynamic team, you show a good understanding, a strong analytical capacity and an initiative spirit. Do you like to learn, explore and meet new challenges? This post is for you, join us and team up!

To replace the retirement of one of our employees, we are looking for:

  • You have an EWC Designist in Civil Engineering, a diploma in Civil Engineering Technician or an equivalent training.
  • You benefit from several years of experience confirmed in a study office or in a construction or civil engineering company.
  • You have knowledge of VSS and SIA standards.
  • You are able to adapt to any kind of situation, know how to cope with the unexpected, demonstrate a strong interest in a multidisciplinary activity, coordination and planning tasks.
  • You master the main computer software as well as computer assisted drawing programs.
  • You are able to maintain a high level of performance in a demanding work environment.

You recognize yourself in the profile you are looking for, want to experience a rewarding professional experience, so submit your complete file until November 22, 2026\.

The appointment is indefinite with immediate or to be agreed upon entry into office. Our Human Resources Department is at your disposal for further information by email or telephone at \ \.

The Commune of Crans\-Montana places human beings at the heart of its organisation and encourages the reconciliation of professional and private life by offering an attractive working environment and working conditions to its employees.
