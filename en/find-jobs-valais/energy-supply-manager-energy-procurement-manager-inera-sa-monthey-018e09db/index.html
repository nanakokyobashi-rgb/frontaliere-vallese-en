Energy Supply Manager

INERA SA has been operating since its creation in 2018 and now comprises eight distributors from Western Switzerland: ALTIS, Genedis, Gruyère Energie, IB\-Murten, Monthey Energie, SACEN SA, SEFA and SI\-Fully. Objective: to meet together the challenges of the 2050 Strategy\. Energy transition is everyone's business. Renewable energies, energy efficiency of buildings, etc., we accompany you and offer tailored, economical and reliable solutions.

Description of post

Occupancy rate: 8

Acting: to be agreed

Workplace: Monthey or Bulle

Your mission

  • Define, with management, the INERA partners' electricity supply strategy, coverage policy and risk limits.
  • Carry out futures purchases, day\-ahead and intraday, deposit appointments and manage flexible assets in order to reduce imbalances at the lowest cost.
  • Negotiate and monitor supply contracts (framework, bilateral, PPP) and acquire original guarantees.
  • Establish the purchase budget and cost of energy, monitor the results of the portfolio and report to management and partners.
  • Operate the EDM Energy system, manage the\-balance sheet group with Swissgrid and manage consumption forecasts with the data team.
  • Be the preferred contact point for partners, shareholders\-clients and suppliers, and ensure the day before markets and regulation (LApEl, OApEl, ElCom, REMIT).

Profile sought

Your profile and skills

  • Higher education in economics, finance, energy, electrical engineering, data science or related fields, with a solid knowledge of the functioning of energy markets
  • 3 to 5 years of experience with a GRD, supplier, aggregator or electrical trading desk, in energy purchase, portfolio management or spot markets.
  • Strong knowledge of Swiss and European electricity markets, balancing mechanisms, hedging instruments and energy contracts (EFET, PPP).
  • Very good mastery of Excel and reporting tools (Power BI, Grafana or equivalent), concepts of ; the practice of an ETRM system is an asset.
  • A sense of negotiation, decision-making capacity in a volatile market, rigour and excellent communication.
  • Common English and good knowledge of German (level B2\).

We offer

We offer you exciting and varied work, in a field of activity that shapes the energy transition and within a small team with a high level of skills. We offer flexible and modern working conditions, with the possibility of teleworking.
