Full description

Eversys SA recruits a Process Improvement Engineer!

Want to contribute to the success of the world leader in professional automatic coffee machines? We are looking for a Process Improvement Engineer passionate and field oriented to join our teams at Sierre (Valais, Switzerland).

Your main missions

  • Factory Optimization: Definition of assembly flows, update of plant layout, design and installation of new production lines.
  • Continuing Improvement Projects: Workstation Ergonomics, room conditioning review and cell storage monitoring.
  • Operational effectiveness: QTF analysis, implementation of corrective action plans and enhancement of overall efficiency.
  • Assembly process: Definition of mounting times and writing instructions.
  • Collaboration: Close work with Production, Quality and Process Improvement teams.

Your profile

  • Training: Degree in engineering or equivalent training.
  • Experience: Experience confirmed in a similar position (process, methods, industrialization).

Skills \&Qualities

  • Pragmaticism, sharp analysis and strength of proposal.
  • Relationship and field orientation.
  • Mastery of French (English is an asset).

Who are we?

We have become a leader in the world of professional automatic coffee machine by developing innovative solutions in the methods of extraction and quality control of coffee products.

Our vision: to bridge the gap between traditional Italian coffee machine by the speed and electronic intelligence of the super\-automatic machine.

We provide products and services that bring real added value to our customers; a unique sensory experience in cups, consistency, real-time connectivity, high productivity and ease of maintenance.

We export 97% of our production and are present in more than 50 countries.

Do you want to have a concrete impact in a team engaged in a growing society? Apply! We'll be happy to talk to you.
