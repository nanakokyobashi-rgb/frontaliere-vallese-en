Full description

Your mission

  • Ensure meticulous cleaning at the end of the construction site, ensuring a clean space that meets quality standards.
  • Complete office cleaning, including dust removal, suction and surface maintenance for a healthy working environment.
  • Make cleaning of vacation and end-of-lease apartments, caring for every detail to meet the expectations of tenants and owners.
  • Ensure regular and punctual cleaning of holiday cottages for preparation for tourist seasons.
  • An opportunity to eventually access a fixed position as a 100% manager.

Now if we talked about you?

  • You justify a professional experience of at least 3 years in Switzerland in cleaning, with a minimum of 1 year in the mentioned fields.
  • You are flexible in terms of working hours, able to adapt to the varying needs of worksite or apartment interventions.
  • You have a valid driver's license, making it easier for you to travel within 30 kilometres of your home.
  • You are rigorous and attached to the work well done, with constant attention to detail and quality of cleaning.
  • Good skills in French, ideally native language, facilitating communication on site and with customers.
  • School level or title in the field of cleaning or hygiene is desired but not compulsory.
