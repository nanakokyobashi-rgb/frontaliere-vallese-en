SPA Therapist M/F

Company description

Mandated by one of our clients, a prestigious hotel establishment in the village of Evolène, as part of a 60% year-round post, we are actively looking for a:

Description of post

Within the SPA, you welcome and take care of customers with professionalism, kindness and discretion. You perform different body and facial treatments as well as massages, adapting your services to the needs and expectations of each client. You also ensure that cabins are prepared and re-ordered between treatments, while ensuring that the hotel's hygiene and quality standards are met.

You advise customers on the treatments and products offered and help them provide a personalized and harmonious experience. You also participate in the overall functioning of the SPA and ensure that you maintain a calm, clean and relaxing environment.

Profile sought

You have a recognized training in aesthetics, massage or therapeutic care and have a first experience in a SPA, institute or hotel establishment. You are comfortable with a demanding and international clientele and know how to listen, discretion and professionalism.

Smileing, well-kept and with an excellent sense of service, you appreciate contact with the customer and are committed to offering a quality experience. You are flexible, self-reliant and appreciate teamwork. Proficiency in English and French is an asset, and any other language is appreciated.

  • We offer you

An elegant and professional work environment within a recognized establishment, as well as a varied activity with an international clientele. You will have the opportunity to put your know-how at the service of a high-end well-being experience.
