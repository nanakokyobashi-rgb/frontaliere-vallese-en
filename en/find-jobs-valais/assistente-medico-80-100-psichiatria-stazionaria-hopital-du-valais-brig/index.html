The Hospital Wallis is looking for the Hospital Center Oberwallis in the Psychiatry Center Oberwallis, Department of Stationary Psychiatry:

Assistant doctor 80-100 %

The PZO is responsible for the basic psychiatric care of the Upper Valais population (80,000 inhabitants). At the PZO, there are outpatients, day clinics, acute care units, consultancy and liaison services for every age group. The PZO is recognized as a training institution of category A (3 years) stationary and category A (3 years) ambulant.

With 6,260 employees, Spital Wallis is one of the most important employers in the canton. Every year, around 41,800 patients are treated inpatiently and more than 670,300 outpatient consultations are carried out.
