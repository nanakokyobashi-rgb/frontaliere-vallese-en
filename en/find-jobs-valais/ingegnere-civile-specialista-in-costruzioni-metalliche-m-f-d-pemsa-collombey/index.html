We at Pemsa offer the perfect balance between the flexibility of temporary work and the safety of indefinite employment. Our standards are high: we value motivation and enthusiasm, because they are the basis of excellence. If you want to join a dynamic and motivated team, the position of Civil Engineer – (M/F/D) Metal Construction Specialist is waiting for you to face exciting challenges.

Your assignment

  • Degree in civil engineering at a professional university (HES), a federal Polytechnic (EPF) or equivalent education recognized.
  • Comprehensive experience in the metal construction industry – mandatory requirement.
  • Excellent knowledge in the calculation and sizing of steel structures.
  • Provision of SIA and Eurocodes applicable to metal structures.
  • Good mastery of structural computing software (RFEM, SCIA Engineer, Robot Structural Analysis or equivalents).
  • Good knowledge of technical drawing software (AutoCAD, Tekla Structures or equivalents), is an advantage.
  • At least 2 years of professional experience in Switzerland.
  • Excellent French mastery, minimum level B2.
  • Category B driving licence and personal vehicle.
  • Resident in the Canton of Valais.
  • Autonomous, rigorous, organized and equipped with a good team spirit.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Study, design and size of metal structures.
  • Execution of static calculations and resistance of materials.
  • Processing and verification of calculation notes according to SIA and Eurocodes.
  • Analysis of executive drawings and technical files.
  • Measuring of metal joints (welded and bolted).
  • Technical coordination with architects, engineers, designers and metal construction companies.
  • Technical monitoring of projects, from design to construction.
  • Participation in meetings at work and resolution of technical issues.
  • Control of the conformity of works to safety standards and requirements.
