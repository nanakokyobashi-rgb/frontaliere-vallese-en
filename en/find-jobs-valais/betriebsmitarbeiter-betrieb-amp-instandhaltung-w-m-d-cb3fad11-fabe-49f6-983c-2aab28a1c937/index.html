Full description

Pensum: 80% or 100% | Stalden

Work where others go on holiday – in the middle of the Saastal mountains. Make a contribution to the energy future. Are you ready to become an indispensable part of our team? Do you love technology, nature and like to get involved yourself? Then a varied task awaits you at Kraftwerken Mattmark AG. With us, you make sure that our water tanks, power plants and technical plants work reliably.

Tasks & Responsibilities

  • Control, flushing and cleaning of our water traps
  • Maintenance of our facilities such as dam, water traps, power plants and pumping station
  • Collaboration in the Pikettdienst der Wassersatzen (about 5 weeks per year)Help with revision and renewal work
  • Care of the surroundings and green areas around our facilities
  • Compliance with safety, health and environmental protection requirements
  • Replacement of shift workers in the command room (about 10 days per year)

Profile & Requirements

  • Completed technical apprenticeship (e.g. polymechanic, sanitary, carpenter, mason, etc.)
  • Experience in the operation and maintenance of technical plants and machines is advantageous
  • Willingness to act as an all-rounder
  • Self-employed and responsible working practices
  • Good physical condition (mountainous)
  • Joy and interest in technology and nature
  • Place of residence mandatory in the Saastal
  • Category B driving licence
