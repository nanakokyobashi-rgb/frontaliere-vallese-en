Within the framework of our department Sigma Temporary Building, we are pleased to collaborate with several companies recognized in Valais, active in the fields of electrical installation of the building, renovations and new constructions. To meet their needs, we are actively looking for a

Electricist CFC (M/F)

Your responsibilities

  • Reading and interpreting electrical drawings and diagrams
  • Construction of electrical installations in residential, commercial and administrative buildings
  • Installation of ducts, tubes, pipes and cable paths
  • Drawing and connection of electrical cables
  • Installation and connection of electrical panels
  • Installation of electrical appliances, sockets, switches, lighting and equipment
  • Processing, renovation and extension of existing facilities
  • Controls and checks before commissioning
  • Troubleshooting and troubleshooting
  • strict compliance with NIBT standards, safety rules and quality requirements

Your profile:

  • Installation CFC\-electrician or equivalent training
  • Experience confirmed in building electrical installations
  • Very good mastery of reading electrical drawings and diagrams
  • Good knowledge of existing standards and requirements
  • Autonomous, rigorous and conscientious
  • Good organizational capacity and sense of responsibility
  • Team spirit and good relationship
  • Essential driving licence

What we offer

Active collaboration with a dynamic team, with strong technical know-how and recognized skills.

Absolute discretion guaranteed, with immediate or suitable start-up.

Attractive remuneration in line with your skills and experience.

Opportunities for further training, in order to develop your knowledge and develop professionally.

Personalized support throughout your mission with a dedicated contact.

Access to a network of recognized companies offering varied opportunities in industry, energy and technology.

Perspectives of evolution, with real possibilities of fixed engagement in our partner clients.

Simplified recruitment process, allowing rapid and effective integration.
