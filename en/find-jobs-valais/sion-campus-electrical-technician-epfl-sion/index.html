Full description

Mission

The EPFL Valais Wallis Campus in Sion currently has 19 laboratories active in the key fields of energy, environment and health. The campus is in the middle of an expension. In order to ensure the proper functioning of the buildings of the site and, by extension, the success of our scientific research, we are looking for a versatile technician capable of operating and monitoring our technical installations. This involves a range of tasks ranging from day-to-day maintenance of facilities to the coordination of many external stakeholders, while ensuring rigorous administrative management. If the main mission focuses on the proper functioning of electrical installations, a versatility in tasks is expected. The technician will collaborate proactively within the operating team.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Monitor and coordinate external enterprises including administrative management of interventions;

• Master electrical installations and maintain technical and normative documentation (ITTO) in GMAO;

  • Conduct preventive and curative maintenance of electrical installations in buildings;
  • Track tickets and perform various interventions;

• Support the CVCS technician and provide technical expertise to other team members; • Contribute to the implementation of the Masterplan and business continuity plans;

  • Follow various technical projects.

Profile

  • Electrician installer CFC or equivalent recognized by ESTI;
  • To bear Article 13 ITTO;
  • Knowledge of ITBL 2020 or 2025;
  • Minimum 5 years of experience;
  • Increased knowledge of the general operation of a building;
  • Oriented solutions and good ability to work as a team in a demanding environment;
  • Good computer skills, in particular basic office tools;
  • Strength of proposals and good capacity to address priorities;
  • Stress resistance and ability to manage multiple requests in parallel;
  • Knowledge of CVCS technical facilities, an asset;
  • Good oral and written communication in French, English an asset;
  • Mastering regulatory/automation tools, an asset;
  • Knowledge of a GMAO, an asset;
  • Experience in a scientific/academic/laboratory environment, an asset.

We offer

  • A dynamic and multicultural environment in a globally recognized institution;
  • Expecting missions in terms of multidisciplinarity, but always exciting;
  • Attractive working conditions conducive to balance, development and professional commitment;
  • An opportunity to contribute to the development of a key institution for Valais and tomorrow's science.

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
