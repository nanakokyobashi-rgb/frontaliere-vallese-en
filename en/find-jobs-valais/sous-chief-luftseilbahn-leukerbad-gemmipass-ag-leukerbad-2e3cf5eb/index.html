Full description

Supporting the Head Chef in leading and working in the kitchen. Deputizing for the Head Chef and sharing responsibility for high-quality, flawless and innovative menu planning for our panoramic restaurant.

Responsibilities

  • Deputizing in leading the kitchen team - recruitment, structure, onboarding
  • Ensuring quality, hygiene and stable processes
  • Sharing responsibility for food costs, orders and suppliers
  • Collaboration with local producers
  • Deputizing in coordinating with the Chef de Service and the Hotel Manager
  • Working in daily operations
  • Supporting the training and development of the kitchen team
  • Ensuring compliance with HACCP and food law requirements

Requirements

  • Training as a chef (EFZ) or equivalent
  • Several years of experience as a chef or sous-chef
  • Experience in establishments with high guest volumes
  • Passion for Alpine and healthy cuisine, regional products and contemporary dishes
  • Experience in team leadership or deputizing

Languages

  • German: basic conversational skills
  • Other languages: an advantage

Specific knowledge

  • Knowledge of HACCP and food safety
  • Experience organizing kitchen workflows

What we offer

  • Around two months of vacation per year
  • Support in finding accommodation in Leukerbad
  • Modern structures and development opportunities
  • Working in a place with character and history
  • Responsible position in a company focused on growth
  • Attractive compensation
