The Valais Hospital is looking for the Valais Romand Hospital Centre

Psychologist-Psychotherapist with responsibility for clinical supervision between 70-100% for the Department of Psychiatry and Adult Psychotherapy of Central Valais

Mission

Clinical supervision and training

  • Supervise assistant psychologists and psychologist interns, in collaboration with responsible physicians
  • Assist trained psychologists in developing their clinical and psychotherapeutic skills
  • Contribute to the recruitment and integration of psychologists/assistants into the service
  • Intervening in multidisciplinary clinical colloquiums at the request of teams or responsible physicians
  • Support teams in the analysis of complex clinical situations
  • Establish and animate, as required, intervision or group supervision spaces
  • Involve in continuing training activities and the development of clinical skills within the service.

Clinical activity

  • Conduct clinical and diagnostic assessments
  • Conduct individual psychotherapy in the context of acute psychiatric care or outpatient follow-up
  • Design and animate therapeutic groups
  • Participate in the development of therapeutic projects in collaboration with the multidisciplinary team
  • Maintain patient records and prepare clinical, medical, and sancurological reports related to management
  • Perform the administrative tasks inherent in clinical activity

Contribution to the functioning of the service

  • Contribute to the evolution of therapeutic practices and service development projects
  • Work closely with multidisciplinary teams and partners of the regional social health network

Profile

University degree in psychology or equivalent recognized title, supplemented by a federal post-graduate degree in psychotherapy

Conditions met for compulsory care insurance (AOS), according to the requirements applicable to the function

Confirmed clinical experience in adult psychiatry

Strong psychopathological assessment and clinical conceptualization skills

High interest in the training and transmission of clinical and psychotherapeutic skills, as well as institutional and interdisciplinary work

Experience in clinical supervision, supervision or training

Ability to support professionals in the analysis and management of complex clinical situations

Organizational sense and ability to work in a demanding clinical environment

Editorial and communication skills

Only offers responding to the profile will be processed.
