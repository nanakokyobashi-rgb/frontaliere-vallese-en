Full description

Your Mission

Within a dynamic and multidisciplinary team, you alternate between several activities that make your work rich and stimulating:

  • Welcome, prepare and accompany patients throughout their care.
  • Directly assist physicians during endoscopic examinations.
  • Participate in the reprocessing and control of endoscopes according to quality standards.
  • Managing the administrative activities of the service in relation to the patient file: telephone reception, planning, summons, and and medical documentation, using innovative digital tools, including artificial intelligence dictation assistance.
  • Work closely with the clinic's nurses, doctors and teams to provide a quality patient experience.
  • Actively participate in continuous improvement of service operation

The profile we're looking for

  • Diploma or SWC of medical assistant or training deemed equivalent.
  • An experience in endoscopy is an asset, but we also welcome motivated candidates wishing to train in this specialty.
  • Excellent relational qualities and sense of welcome.
  • Experience with IT tools and administrative tasks.
  • Team spirit, autonomy, organizational sense and ability to manage priorities.
  • Rigor, versatility and desire to invest in a constantly evolving service

Why join us? You will join a united team, where mutual help, collaboration and the pleasure of working together are values lived daily.

The Clinic offers

  • A versatile position that combines technical, relational and administrative activities.
  • A modern working environment within a human-sized clinic, fostering proximity, responsiveness and quality of exchange.
  • A dynamic, caring and supportive team, in which mutual aid is part of everyday life.
  • Personalised support during your integration as well as continuing training opportunities to develop your skills.
  • Community management that promotes dialogue, collaboration and professional development.

We pay great attention to the diversity of life paths of our collaborators. Our positions are open to both women and men.

Located in the heart of Sion, the Valère Clinic is one of the most renowned acute care clinics in Valais. In collaboration with more than 60 doctors, its 180 employees provide several thousand patients with the best care each year. In a relaxing environment and a warm atmosphere, the Clinic offers personalized medical follow-up for all Valaisans and Valaisans, regardless of their insurance coverage (community, semi-private and private). Since 2013, the Valère Clinic has been part of the Swiss Medical Network, a network of clinics and medical centres across Switzerland. It is on the hospital list of the Canton of Valais.
