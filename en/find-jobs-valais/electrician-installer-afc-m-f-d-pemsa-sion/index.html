Your assignment

  • Read and interpret electrical designs and schematics accurately to ensure the technical compliance of plants.
  • Participate actively in the renovation and construction work in the electrical sector.
  • Install low and high voltage electrical appliances, in strict compliance with current standards.
  • Place canals, install lighting fixtures and electrical devices in accordance with projects and contract specifications.
  • To contribute to the continuous improvement of the on-site systems, in particular in terms of automation, modernization and optimization of reliability.
  • Make different types of electrical connections, ensuring the safety and quality of the networks.
  • Work in a multidisciplinary team and collaborate effectively with other professional figures.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Essential requirements: Owner of a Federal Qualification Certificate (CFC) as an electrical installer, validated and compliant with OIBT and INB standards.
  • Comprovata mastery in reading electrical projects and schemes for weak and strong current interventions.
  • Proven experience in renovation and electrical installations in Switzerland, ideally in Valais.
  • Valid driving licence, indispensable for work travel.
  • Ability to operate independently in compliance with current safety procedures and standards.
  • They are appreciated knowledge of automation, instrumentation or electrotechnical, as well as a strong will for continuous improvement.
  • Spiccato analytical sense, methodological rigour and strong team spirit.
  • Resident in the Canton of Valais, with a working distance of less than 30 km.
  • Useful Requirements: At least 3 years of professional experience in Switzerland and mastery of French at the mother tongue level.
