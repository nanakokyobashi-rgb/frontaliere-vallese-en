Full description

Crans-Montana Food and Beverage SA (CMFB) is looking for a (M/F) Cook for one of its restaurants in Crans-Montana.

Your missions: ensure the setting up, preparation, cooking and dressing of dishes according to the recipes and standards of the restaurant. Ensure the quality of preparations, the proper preservation of foodstuffs and the maintenance of the workplace, in accordance with hygiene and safety rules.

Your profile: cooking training or equivalent experience. Good command of culinary techniques, autonomy, rigor and team spirit. Ability to work effectively during periods of high influx and good understanding of French.
