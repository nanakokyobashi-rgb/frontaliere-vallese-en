We are looking for an experienced and passionate coach driver for mountain driving

Car driver PERMIS D

Your main missions

  • Ensure the daily transport of passengers (tourists, skiers, pendulums) safely on snowy or icy mountain roads.
  • Mount and disassemble snow chains according to weather conditions.
  • Ensure daily technical inspection of the vehicle (levels, lighting, winter equipment) and cleanliness.
  • Receive, inform and assist passengers with courtesy, including when loading luggage and ski equipment.
  • Respect strictly the timetables, the road code and the Ordinance on working and rest time.

Your profile

  • Holder of the driving licence category D and ACP card up-to-date (indispensable).
  • Experience in driving heavy vehicles on mountain roads and in winter conditions.
  • Excellent control of snow chain laying.
  • Acute sense of responsibility, cold blood and great vigilance.
  • Mastery of French
  • High availability and flexibility (work in service cut off, weekends and public holidays).
