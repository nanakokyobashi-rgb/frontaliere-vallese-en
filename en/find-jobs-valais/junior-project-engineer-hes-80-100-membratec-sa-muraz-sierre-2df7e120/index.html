Junior project engineer HES 8

Membratec produces drinking water production facilities for public authorities by offering high-performance technical solutions that are distinguished by their reliability and low operating costs, with a deep respect for the environment. Today, the company is part of the AquaNest Group, which seeks to sustain its expertise in water treatment (drinking water) by providing innovative solutions to societal challenges.

Look for a

Want to move into a meaningful sector, within a continuously growing business that focuses on people and innovation? Join our team in Sierre, and take part in ambitious projects throughout Switzerland.

What awaits you

  • A specific and demanding area, with structured support to master our drinking water treatment facilities
  • Direct collaboration with highly qualified engineers and technical specialists
  • An entrepreneurial environment focused on precision, quality and innovation
  • Projects throughout Switzerland, offering a diverse daily life

Your missions

Post-sales service

  • Provide assistance and technical support (on site or remotely) to operators of existing facilities
  • Support the process manager to organize the activity
  • To contribute to the implementation of VAS offers and to the timely completion of interventions, using the necessary resources (internal or external)
  • Develop and maintain documentation for VAS activities
  • Maintain commercial contact with existing customers

Project Management, Implementation

  • Full monitoring of implementation projects: planning, construction, commissioning
  • Stakeholder coordination: project management, suppliers, subcontractors
  • Financial management, timeliness and budgets
  • Organization of construction sites in collaboration with the project manager
  • Field monitoring, site meetings, technical documentation
  • Ensuring performance, quality and safety

Purpose

  • HES engineer in industrial systems, mechanical engineering or life sciences or equivalent training ( or .)
  • Knowledge of the field of water treatment (drinking or spent water), an asset
  • Good control of IT tools (MS Office) and digital agility
  • Working as a team and able to work in the fall
  • Sense of analysis, organizational capacity and great flexibility
  • Entrepreneurial spirit, solutions oriented, sensitive to rigour and reliability

We offer

  • A business culture based on excellence and innovation
  • A vision for continuous improvement and sustainability
  • The opportunity to contribute to high impact projects
