Role overview

Dining with the Stars" combines a dinner on the Gornergrat with a journey into the starry sky.

As a guide, you will lead through the evening during the season from January to March 2027: You will give an approximately 20-minute presentation and then go outside with the guests in three small groups to show and explain concrete constellations.

You provide a round process and are available to the guests for questions throughout the evening. Your field of activity
