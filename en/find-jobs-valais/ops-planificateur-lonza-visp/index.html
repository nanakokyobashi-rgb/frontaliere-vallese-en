As member of the Manufacturing Support Team, the Production Scheduler performs the holistic operations planning for mid-scale operations including upstream and downstream production with regard to safety, quality standards and on-time-in full delivery.

General functional responsibilities

  • Development of an integrated plan for the coordination of all operational activities with consideration to process, facility, and resource requirements. Other scheduling activities include equipment/facility maintenance planning, customer and audit visitations, as well as the coordination of large and small plant changes including qualification activities
  • Collaboration with Supply Planning, Bioprocess engineers, Production shift leads, Manufacturing Science and Technology, Engineering, EMR, Lonza Services departments to coordinate operational and maintenance activities
  • Contributes to the Master Production Schedule (MPS) based on supply planning
  • Responsible for capacity modelling including facility throughput improvements
  • Proactively develop strategies to anticipate or counteract possible future supply bottlenecks
  • Active development of measures to improve planning accuracy (process and system optimization)
  • Authoring and reviewing GMP-relevant documents and SOPs
  • Preparing and reviewing quality records such as change controls

Project-specific responsibilities

  • Responsible for driving performance through dashboards, Hoshin boards and other reporting tools

Job requirements

  • Several years (>3 years in total) of experience in either supply planning or project planning for pharmaceuticals, as well as experience in production environment
  • Fluent in English
  • Experience using planning tools such as Microsoft Project, Schedule Pro, Orchestrate, Excel.
  • Strong ability to coordinate and collaborate with multiple departments

Additional desired skills and experience

  • Proficiency in German is highly desirable
  • Experience with Trackwise, DMS, SAP, MES Syncade

Required education and qualifications

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in supply chain or biotechnology.
  • Alternatively, relevant work experience in biotechnology production environment
