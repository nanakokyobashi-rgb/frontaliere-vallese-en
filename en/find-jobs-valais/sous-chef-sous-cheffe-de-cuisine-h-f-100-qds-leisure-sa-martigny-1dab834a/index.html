Full description

The Quartier LôDzé is looking for a Sub-Chief / Sub-Kitchen (M/F) 100% to strengthen its teams. We are looking for a person who is passionate about cooking, organized and with excellent leadership, able to accompany the Chef in the daily management of operations and guarantee the quality of the culinary services.

Your missions

  • Assist the Chef in the planning, supervision and realization of daily culinary production.
  • Participate in the development of menus, suggestions and new preparations, in accordance with the concept of establishment and seasonality.
  • Ensure the quality, presentation and conformity of dishes with the specifications and standards of the establishment.
  • Coach, accompany and train kitchen workers, promoting a professional and collaborative working environment.
  • Participate in the management of orders, stocks, inventories and control of material costs.
  • Ensure compliance with hygiene, HACCP food safety, traceability and internal procedures.
  • Ensure effective communication between the kitchen, the Chef and the room staff.
  • To contribute to the continuous improvement of organization, working methods and culinary supply.
  • Ensure the proper functioning of the kitchen and take the necessary operational responsibilities in the absence of the Chef.

Profile sought

  • Vocational training in cooking (CFC or equivalent training).
  • Confirmed experience in cooking, ideally in a function of Deputy Head or Party Chef.
  • Excellent mastery of culinary techniques and quality standards.
  • Ability to mentor, motivate and form a team.
  • Organizational sense, autonomy and ability to manage priorities.
  • Natural leadership, team spirit and excellent communication skills.
  • Creativity, rigor and sense of detail.
  • Good control of inventory management, orders and material costs.
  • In-depth knowledge of HACCP standards and food safety rules.
  • Ability to maintain business continuity in the absence of the Chef.
  • Flexibility and availability according to the needs of the establishment, including weekends and holidays.

We offer you

  • Reduction for employees at different points of sale
  • 5 weeks of holidays, 6 holidays
  • Salary according to applicable CLC and 13th month.
  • A dynamic and varied working environment.

We will only examine the complete files (CV, cover letter, work certificates, etc.) of the candidates corresponding to the profile sought.
