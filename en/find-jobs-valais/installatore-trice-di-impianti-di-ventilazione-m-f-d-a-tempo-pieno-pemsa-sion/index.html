Your task

  • Place and install ventilation ducts, both square and circular, with precision and efficiency.
  • Cut the frames necessary for the adaptation on site in compliance with technical drawings.
  • Place the various ventilation ducts ensuring a sealed connection and reliable assembly of the components.
  • Assemble monoblocks in compliance with current safety and quality standards.
  • Install grids, ducts, filters, engines and extractors, ensuring proper operation and integration in existing plants.
  • Strictly collaborate with technical teams to ensure the quality and compliance of the plants within the agreed deadlines.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Driving licence (required): you must be in possession of a valid and valid driving license.
  • Technical training: ideally in possession of a federal capacity certificate (CFC) as an installer of ventilation systems or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland.
  • Professional experience: at least 3 years of experience in Switzerland in the installation and installation of ventilation systems, with a proven experience of at least 2 years in this position.
  • Advanced technical skills: mastery of laying techniques and adjustment of square and circular ventilation ducts, precise cutting of frames, assembly of single-blocks, and laying of grids, ducts, filters, engines and extractors.
  • Language: French spoken and written at the native level, indispensable for understanding projects and communication with teams.
  • Mobility: residence less than 30 kilometers from the workplace, in order to ensure optimum availability.
  • Personal quality: you are dynamic, rigorous and have an excellent team spirit, which allows you to integrate quickly and effectively into our teams on the field.
