Full description

"Expect to grow", personally and professionally: At Siegfried, we offer fascinating challenges and numerous opportunities to showcase your talents. As a trusted and respected partner in the pharmaceutical industry, we uphold the highest standards in terms of safety, quality, and sustainability, while cultivating a culture that allows you to flourish. Join a global company in full expansion, where diverse perspectives and experiences come together in a collaborative environment. Here, you will find a workplace that values both collective success and your individual contributions—offering long-term opportunities to evolve and make an impact. ##

  • Responsibilities
  • In the context of implementing new manufacturing processes for pharmaceutical active ingredients, you will perform, as part of the laboratory team, the necessary chemical analyses of laboratory experiments to set up an optimized manufacturing process for the factory.
  • In accordance with GMP requirements, you will actively participate in analytical validations/transfers.
  • You will also be responsible for laboratory experiments conducted in the context of investigations following non-conformity issues at the factory. During these troubleshooting, you will be able to provide hypotheses and solutions in collaboration with production chemists, R&D, and DA. You will establish and manage documents related to experiments and analyses (lab journals, analysis/experiment files, reports).
  • Finally, you will participate in the maintenance of analytical equipment as well as laboratory logistics. All these activities must be carried out in strict compliance with HSE and Quality guidelines. ##
  • Requirements
  • You hold a laboratory technician training in chemistry (CFC) or a higher diploma with several years of experience in analytical chemistry
  • You master classic computer tools such as the Microsoft Office package (Excel, Word, Outlook).
  • You are able to provide rigorous work in an autonomous manner.
  • You effectively monitor the respect of quality, safety, and environmental requirements.
  • Good team spirit ##

  • Reka vouchers: Siegfried AG grants its employees a 20% discount
  • Attractive company pension fund

  • Access to the Swibeco employee benefits platform
  • 5 weeks of vacation and possibility of redemption up to 2 weeks ## Contact Founded in 1873 in Zofingen, Switzerland, Siegfried has become a global network of 13 sites spread across three continents. With a team of over 3,800 highly qualified professionals, we bring our pharmaceutical clients' innovations to an industrial level and produce safe medicines for patients worldwide. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few organizations today able to guarantee the development and production of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs) and finished galenic forms under one roof. This unique combination of expertise and experience makes us the most reliable partner in the pharmaceutical industry. Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operates manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
