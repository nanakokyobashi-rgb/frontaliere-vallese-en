Founded in 1920 by Dr Alfred Germanier, the Valère Clinic is the oldest acute care facility in Valais and is now known for its multidisciplinary approach. The Clinic of Valère deals with the health of its patients in an interdisciplinary manner. Thanks to personalized medical services and a state-of-the-art technical platform, it guarantees multidisciplinary medical care for all Valaisans, regardless of their insurance coverage (general, semi-private and private).
