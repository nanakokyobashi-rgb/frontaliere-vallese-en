Your assignment

  • Read and interpret electrical designs and schematics accurately to ensure a consistent and secure installation.
  • Participate actively in the renovation and construction work, ensuring the quality and performance of electrical installations.
  • Install low and high voltage electrical appliances, including laying canals, lighting fixtures and various devices.
  • Perform different types of electrical connections in compliance with Swiss safety and quality standards.
  • To contribute to the continuous improvement of the systems through automation, modernization and strengthening of the reliability of electrical systems.
  • Effectively collaborate within a multidisciplinary team, in a dynamic and demanding environment.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Training and qualifications: Owner of a Federal Qualification Certificate (CFC) as an electrical installer, with a solid knowledge of OIBT – INB standards, as well as expertise in automation, instrumentation or electrotechnical (big advantage).
  • Driving licence: It is mandatory to have a valid driving license, indispensable for the trips to the Valais yards.
  • Experience: Demonstrate a significant experience in Switzerland for at least 3 years in the electrical sector, with autonomy and a rigorous approach in performing tasks.
  • Technical skills: Ability to read and interpret electrical designs and schemes, install and connect plants and contribute to the continuous improvement of electrical systems.
  • Location: Residenza nel Vallese and availability to intervene within a maximum of 30 km to guarantee proximity and reactivity in the yards.
  • Transversal skills: Team spirit, professional rigour, ability to analyse and autonomy in the management of technical interventions.
  • Language: Excellent French mastery, ideally as a mother tongue, for a smooth communication with teams and customers.
