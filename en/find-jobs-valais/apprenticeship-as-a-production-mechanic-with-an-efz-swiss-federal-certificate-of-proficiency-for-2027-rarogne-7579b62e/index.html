# Shape your future #

# An apprenticeship at JABIL - an apprenticeship with a future! #

Jabil produces high-precision medical technology products that help improve people’s health worldwide.

Are you currently attending the Orientation School or are you close to completing your compulsory schooling? Are you interested in technology, like working exactly and want to start your professional life with an exciting apprenticeship?

At Jabil, you can expect more than just an education. You will become part of a strong team of learners, work with modern machines and be accompanied by experienced trainers on your way. Step by step you learn everything you need for a successful future as a polymechanic.

Start your professional future with us and discover the fascinating world of medical technology.

# Apprenticeship as production mechanic/in EFZ for 2027 #

at the Raron location

Are you interested in technology and modern machines? As a production mechanic you work with workpieces made of stainless steel, titanium and plastic and get to know different production processes such as milling, turning and grinding.

You work on modern production facilities, produce high-quality components for medical instruments and implants and provide precise measurement controls for the high quality of our products. In this way, you make an important contribution to the production of products that are used in the healthcare sector worldwide.

You bring that with you.

  • Interest in technology, mechanics and technical contexts
  • Logical thinking skills
  • Craftsmanship
  • Good spatial imagination
  • Enjoyment of precise work
  • Concentration ability and endurance
  • Reliability and responsibility
  • Motivation to learn new
  • Self-employed and careful working methods
  • Good performance in mathematics and physics

Requirements for your application ##

  • Attendance or completion of compulsory schooling
  • Good knowledge of German in words and writing
  • Residency in Switzerland or in the border area is advantageous

What awaits you at Jabil? ##

In addition to an exciting and varied education, you can look forward to many other advantages:

  • Personal support - Your vocational trainers and practical trainers accompany you throughout your training and support you with professional and personal questions.
  • Strong learning team - You become part of a community of over 40 learners. Joint projects, events and activities ensure strong cohesion.
  • Various training - You get to know different departments and work areas and gain versatile practical experience.
  • Attractive Benefits - You will benefit from a SBB general subscription, contributions to school supplies and laptops, catering allowances and discounts at Reka. With a performance wage of up to 50%, your commitment will be rewarded additionally.
  • Optimally prepared - With our introductory week, internal training and a targeted preparation for the qualification process, we support you optimally on your way to the teaching degree.
  • Early Responsibility - We trust you and gradually give you responsibility so that you can develop professionally and personally.
  • Future prospects - More than 80% of our learners stay with Jabil after their education and benefit from attractive development and further education opportunities.
  • Meaningful work - With your work in medical technology, you contribute to creating products that improve the lives of people worldwide.
  • Family culture - Although Jabil is an international company, we maintain a personal, appreciative and uncomplicated relationship with each other.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
