Bonvin Cleaning SA recruits

100 % versatile operating agent

Place of work: Ovronnaz Region (VS)

To strengthen our team in the field, Bonvin Cleanings SA is looking for a multi-purpose, self-sustaining operating agent with an excellent practical sense for maintaining, maintaining and operating various infrastructure.

Your main missions

  • Ensure general maintenance, cleanliness and good condition of buildings and facilities.
  • Perform various technical interventions, minor repairs and routine maintenance.
  • Monitor the proper functioning of technical installations and report any malfunctions.
  • Provide maintenance of outdoor areas, including winter service.
  • Ensure the proper management of equipment, equipment and waste.
  • To monitor and monitor the quality of the services performed (controlling).
  • Conduct regular reporting of activities, interventions and possible anomalies to the hierarchy.
  • Collaborate with various external stakeholders and providers.
  • Demonstrate initiative to effectively solve everyday problems.

Your profile

  • Training in technical or building maintenance.
  • Work experience from 1 to 3 years in a similar position.
  • Good practical knowledge in technical maintenance, maintenance and minor repairs.
  • Knowledge of professional cleaning techniques appreciated.
  • Good mastery of common IT tools for monitoring activities and reporting.
  • Person versatile, resourceful, autonomous and oriented solutions.
  • Good relationship, team spirit and sense of service.
  • Ability to organize work, manage priorities and report on activities.
  • Driver's licence category B essential.

We offer you

  • A varied and responsible position within a recognised and well established company in Valais.
  • A diversified activity.
  • Autonomy in the organisation of daily tasks.
  • A dynamic working framework and collaboration with a professional team.

Participation rate: 100%

Entry into office: 1 January 2027

Are you a field person, versatile, resourceful and like to find concrete solutions? We look forward to receiving your application.
