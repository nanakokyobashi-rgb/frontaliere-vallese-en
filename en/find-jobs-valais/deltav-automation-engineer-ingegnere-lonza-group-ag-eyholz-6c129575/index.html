DeltaV Automation Engineer (EI\&C)

Are you ready to develop and implement world-class DeltaV automation solutions that transform life-saving medicines from concept to reality? Become part of our team at the Visp site and take over the management of central tasks in the areas of automation technology, system integration and qualification for important CAPEX\ projects.

Purely location-based This is a pure presence function on site. Direct cooperation on the ground promotes close exchange in real time and ensures the technical precision required for the production of medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What we offer you

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical working environment.
  • An attractive hourly rate.

Your duties

  • Planning and configuration of DeltaV automation solutions, control strategies, interfaces, visualizations and alarm structures for location-based CAPEX\ projects.
  • Control of the entire project life cycle in close, interdisciplinary cooperation with the teams from engineering, operation, validation, quality assurance and project management.
  • Head of system integration activities to connect DeltaV to process plants, PLCs, supply systems and MES\ platforms.
  • Preparation, implementation and maintenance of GxP\-compliant documentation, including protocols for commissioning, qualification and validation (FAT, SAT, IQ/OQ).
  • Practical troubleshooting and root cause analysis to solve complex automation and communication problems.
  • Consistent compliance with GMP\ standards, data integrity guidelines and formal change\management procedures.
  • Professional guidance and mentoring of younger team members as well as the development of continuous improvement processes in the area of process automation.

This is what you bring with you

  • Completed study of automation technology, electrical engineering or a related technical discipline.
  • Demonstrate practical experience in the development and configuration of DeltaV systems in a regulated life\sciences\ environment.
  • In-depth knowledge of GxP\ compliance, data integrity guidelines and formal validation and qualification methods (FAT/SAT/IQ/OQ).
  • Proven experience in troubleshooting complex control systems and communication interfaces in the context of handling CAPEX\ projects.
  • Fluent knowledge of German (required), reliable English (preferred).
  • Strong technical problem-solving skills and excellent communication skills in interdisciplinary teams.

About Lonza

At Lonza, the employees are the greatest strength. With more than 30 locations on five continents, the globally networked teams work together every day to produce the medicine of tomorrow. The company values – collaboration, responsibility, excellence, passion and integrity – reflect who they are and how they work together. Every idea, big or small, has the potential to improve the lives of millions of people. It is precisely in this task that you can participate. Innovation arises when people from different backgrounds contribute their individual perspectives. Lonza values diversity and is committed to an inclusive working environment.
