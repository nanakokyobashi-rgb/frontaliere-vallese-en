Full description

The canton’s leading employer, the Valais Hospital has 6,260 employees. Each year, 41,800 patients are treated and nearly 670,300 outpatient visits are provided. For the Center Hospitalier du Valais Romand, the Valais Hospital is looking for ASSCs (care and community health assistants) of 80-100% on fixed-term and permanent contracts for the Psychiatry and Psychotherapy Center, for its psychiatry and psychotherapy units for the elderly. Mission:

  • Provide delegated care while respecting the Professional Practice Model (MPP) in force within the Valais Hospital.
  • Delegated medicotechnical care according to the list of acts authorized by the HVS Care Department.
  • Support and encouragement of the patient in his life project. Profile:
  • CFC Assistant in Community Care and Health
  • A few years of professional experience an asset
  • Ability to collaborate in a multidisciplinary team
  • Interest in the elderly psychiatry clinic
  • Dynamism, open-mindedness, empathy
  • Self-control, resistance to stress
  • Computer skills Only offers meeting the profile will be processed. Offer: A stimulating and diverse environment with the opportunity to put your knowledge and experience to good use; attractive employment conditions; continuing education and development opportunities.
