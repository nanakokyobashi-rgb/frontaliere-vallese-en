Senior Project Leader

CIGES SA is an IT services company with an operational headquarters in Sion. Since 1968, we have developed know-how in integrating innovative IT solutions for municipal administrations and energy companies.

Our experience in the field, combined with our technical skills, enables us to successfully support our customers in achieving their digital ambitions. CIGES is a team of \+60 employees who share human values, the taste of excellence driven by a common desire: the success of our customers.

In order to strengthen our team, we are looking for

TA FUTURE MISSION

* Manage cross-cutting IT projects in all their dimensions (budget, resources, risks, delays, quality).

  • Manage and coordinate a portfolio of application and/or IT projects in line with the company's strategy.
  • Develop and implement the company's project management methodology.
  • Train, assist and supervise individual project leaders.
  • Identify and implement project portfolio monitoring indicators and communicate them regularly to management.

TON PROFIL

* Higher education (university or HES) in computer science or equivalent.

  • Mastering the main project management methodologies (Agile, Scrum, Waterfall, ...), certification is a plus.
  • A strong experience in managing IT projects and programs, experience in a PMO role is an asset.
  • Experience in managing teams, suppliers and external providers.
  • Excellent relational skills and customer service.
  • Rigorous organization, autonomy and sense of responsibility.
