Full description

Brand of the Planzer Group: Alpin Cargo.

Whether train, road or carriage - we keep goods moving uphill and downland. We move these goods safely and reliably and rely on the latest technology and sustainable processes. Become part of our team and shape the future of logistics!

Tasks

  • You ensure that goods are correctly accepted, checked and processed on time
  • In the camp you keep track and correct irregularities immediately
  • With regular deliveries to customers, you are an important part of the logistics chain
  • The professional loading and unloading as well as the adequate securing of the goods are your responsibility
  • You maintain and control your vehicle reliably and with care
  • Order and cleanliness in the camp are a matter of course for you and part of your professional working method

Profile

  • Local knowledge is advantageous and makes it easier for you to get started
  • You already have a valid category B driver’s license in your pocket
  • You are physically resilient, reliable and like to participate
  • Your way of working is independent, structured and efficient
  • In German you can communicate well orally

Benefits

  • With us you start with 5 weeks vacation and benefit from the 5th year of service even from 6 weeks!
  • With us you can develop - we promote and challenge talents
  • You will spend your first working days in the central introduction, where you will be taught the most important basics
  • Good afternoon! Bonjour! Buongiorno! Language courses: we support your progress

For us as a company, the focus is on a supportive cooperation and a positive working atmosphere. That is why we particularly appreciate qualities such as team spirit, helpfulness and motivation. We also attach great importance to punctuality, loyalty and accuracy, as these values form the basis for a reliable and customer-oriented way of working.
