Full description

Are you interested in safety, do you like to travel and do you appreciate direct contact with passengers on public transport? Then it will become part of PostBus and assume a central role in ensuring security and checking tickets in the canton of Valais. You ensure that all passengers are safe and travel with a valid ticket.

You can do that.

  • With your experience in the security sector and your peace of mind, you prove order and actively contribute to public transport safety in Valais.
  • Together with your team, you carry out ticket checks professionally and thus make a decisive contribution to securing revenue.
  • You inform, advise and support our customers competently and friendly with questions about tariffs, schedules and services and thus ensure positive experiences.
  • You are self-employed on our varied regional routes, from Monday to Sunday (05:00 – 23:00), and masterfully demanding situations with calm, diplomacy and sovereignty.
  • You are ready to carry out special orders in the area of security.
  • Despite your independence, you work closely with your team and pursue a solution-oriented approach even in challenging situations.

You bring that with you

  • You have completed basic training in the security area and have the category B driving licence.
  • You are bilingual and speak German and French verbally as in writing. Other foreign languages are an advantage.
  • Thanks to your solution-oriented way of thinking, you argue skillfully and stay calm and safe even in stressful situations.
  • You value daily contact with customers and cope well with irregular working hours (including Sundays and public holidays).
  • You are well versed in using digital tools and bring the necessary physical resilience for demanding situations.

Please attach a current criminal record to your application. Incomplete applications cannot be considered!
