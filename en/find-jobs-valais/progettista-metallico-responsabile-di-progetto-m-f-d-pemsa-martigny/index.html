Your assignment

  • Build precise technical plans and designs in the metal construction industry using AutoCAD, Tekla or similar software.
  • Comprehensive documentation for production and assembly, ensuring project compliance.
  • Prepare the technical details and executive drawings necessary for the correct realization of the works.
  • Ensure rigorous monitoring of projects from initial design to final delivery on site.
  • Effectively coordinate communications between customers, production teams and other technical subjects involved.
  • Participate actively in the measurements directly in the yard to ensure the perfect correspondence of the projects under the conditions of the ground.
  • Ensure compliance with the deadlines and costs for each project, ensuring regular reporting at the top.

And now we're talking about you?

  • You have a solid training in the field of metal construction, technical design or training considered equivalent, which you expect your mastery of fundamental notions.
  • Prove an excellent level of French, at least B2, both written and oral, indispensable to communicate effectively with teams and customers.
  • Masterpieces with ease of software AutoCAD, Tekla or their equivalents, fundamental tools for the processing of detailed technical drawings.
  • You must absolutely have a valid driving license, necessary to go to the different yards in Switzerland.
  • Ideally, you have gained at least 2 years of experience in Switzerland in a similar position, demonstrating your ability to fit into the local context.
  • Stay within a maximum radius of 30 km from your workplace, thus ensuring your availability and punctuality.
