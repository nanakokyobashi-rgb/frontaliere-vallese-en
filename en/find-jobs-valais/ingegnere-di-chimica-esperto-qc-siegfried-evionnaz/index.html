Your Role

Your Responsibilities

Conduct and coordinate scientific activities related to the analysis of raw materials, finished products and stability studies. Develop, optimize and implement analytical methods, including liquid and gas chromatography. Evaluate, interpret and document analytical results in accordance with GMP requirements. Participate in analytical investigations, including in the framework of OSOs, deviations and CAPA, providing scientific expertise. Contribute to the transfer, validation and implementation of analytical methods. Participate in the qualification, monitoring and optimization of analytical equipment. Identify and propose improvements to laboratory analytical methods, processes and practices. Collaborate with the various internal partners to solve analytical problems and support production activities. Participate in drafting, reviewing and updating scientific procedures, protocols, reports and other documents. Contribute to internal and external audits by providing expertise in the analytical field. Provide scientific and technical oversight in its field of expertise and contribute to the introduction of new analytical technologies. Ensure the application of GMP requirements, safety rules and quality standards throughout the activities carried out.

Your Profile

  • Diploma of Engineer in Chemistry
  • Professional experience in an analytical environment, ideally in the pharmaceutical or chemical industry.
  • A strong experience in managing analytical investigations (OOS, deviations, CAPA) is an asset.
  • Solid knowledge and practical experience in liquid and gas chromatography.
  • Good knowledge of GMP environments and associated documentation.
  • Good analytical, problem-solving and scientific interpretation capabilities.
  • English.
  • Rigorous, autonomous and structured personality, with a strong interest in scientific and technical issues.
  • Collaborative spirit, solutions orientation and ability to work effectively in a dynamic industrial environment.

Founded in 1873 in Zofingue, Switzerland, Siegfried has become a global network of 16 sites spread over three continents. With a team of more than 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to the industrial scale and manufacture safe medicines for patients around the world. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few organizations able today to ensure the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished galenic forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most reliable partner in the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operators manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
