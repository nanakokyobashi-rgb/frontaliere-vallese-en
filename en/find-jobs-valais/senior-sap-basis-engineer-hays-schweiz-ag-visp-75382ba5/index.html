  • Lead troubleshooting and root cause resolution for complex SAP Basis and performance issues
  • Design and maintain SAP system architectures aligned with enterprise and business requirements
  • Lead SAP installations, upgrades, migrations, system refreshes and technical conversions
  • Support S/4HANA transformations, SAP Private Cloud environments and strategic SAP infrastructure initiatives
  • Manage SAP security activities including Security Notes, kernel patches, vulnerability remediation and system hardening
  • Support GxP, SOX and cybersecurity-related audits, validation activities and compliance documentation
  • Coordinate high availability, disaster recovery, capacity planning, monitoring and system resilience activities
  • Provide technical governance for SAP and external managed service providers and drive continuous service improvements
  • Act as SAP Basis technical lead across projects, cutovers, migrations and post-go-live support

  • Bachelor's degree in Information Technology, Computer Science, Engineering or equivalent professional experience

  • Hands-on experience with SAP S/4HANA and HANA database administration
  • Experience working with SAP Private Cloud Edition environments
  • Strong knowledge of SAP Basis technologies including ECC, Solution Manager, Cloud ALM, BTP, Web Dispatcher and SAP Router
  • Experience with SAP upgrades, migrations, technical conversions, transport management and system lifecycle activities
  • Knowledge of Linux, Windows Server, database concepts, infrastructure, networking, monitoring and automation
  • Experience managing outsourced SAP operational services and external technology providers
  • Experience leading SAP technical projects, migrations and cross-functional technical activities
