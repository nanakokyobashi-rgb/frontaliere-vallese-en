Full description

Who you are

As an IKEA Food collaborator, you will strive to make people smile while offering irresistible food offers, in a welcoming atmosphere that gives our customers a good reason to come back.

  • You have experience in a high volume environment and in direct contact with customers, ideally in service or sales.
  • You are accessible, you like to work directly with customers and you always strive to provide them with an excellent service.
  • You are physically resilient and able to stand for an extended period of time, even when the frequency of clients is lower.
  • You have a strong team spirit, but you also like to take responsibility for your own work.
  • You flourish in a very dynamic and stimulating environment and work well under pressure.
  • You master computer science, especially MS Office.
  • You are flexible and understand that your working hours depend greatly on the opening hours of the store.
  • You speak fluently French and have a good level of English. Knowledge of any other national language is an advantage.

What you'll do every day

  • Serve and meet clients with an empathic and caring attitude based on IKEA values.
  • Ensure that foods, beverages and products in your area of responsibility are of the highest quality and freshness and are presented in the most attractive way possible.
  • Ensure your area of responsibility is always clean, organized and attractive.
  • Accept payments and process cash transactions at the caisse.
  • See the food sector through our customers' eyes to constantly improve our products and meet their needs.
  • Find more efficient working methods to reduce costs and be more cost-conscious, including controlling waste, correcting portion sizes, verifying production levels and updating cooking methods.
  • Respect and monitor internal rules, policies and daily operational routines regarding quality, food security and stock numbers.

You think this could work for you?

As we have already indicated, we value the well-being of our collaborators.

So we want to say 'thank you'!

We offer as such

  • 28 days of vacation (based on 100 % shift)
  • 8 weeks paid parental leave for fathers; 20 weeks paid parental leave for mothers
  • Very attractive social benefits
  • 15% discount on your purchases - in each IKEA store in Switzerland.
  • Would you rather come to work on public transport? We cover between 85% and 100% of the price of an annual subscription.
  • Unlimited access to the LinkedIn learning platform
  • Favourable and balanced meals in our canteen (4.50 CHF for meat dishes and 3.50 CHF for vegetarian dishes).
  • Premiums paid to all employees

Border-zone rules and processing times vary by neighbouring country and canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
