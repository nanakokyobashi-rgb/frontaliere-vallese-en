Full description

A head of the accounting department (100%) to the Cantonal Finance Administration, General State Accounting, Sion

Your tasks

  • You are the head of the accounting section of the General Accounts of the State. You manage the activities of the section and ensure the development of the skills of employees. You ensure the planning, monitoring and execution of the closing tasks and budget of the state of Valais You ensure the regular maintenance of the accounts in accordance with the accounting standard (MCH2) and the legal bases in force
  • You advise the state services on management and accounting
  • You contribute to the development and monitoring of the internal control system and to the improvement of processes You follow up on the recommendations made by the supervisory bodies including the Cantonal Finance Inspectorate
  • You perform other tasks and participate in projects at the request of your superiors

Your profile

  • You have a federal degree in finance and control or accounting, a certificate in finance and accounting or equivalent university training You have a solid experience in public sector financial management and administrative management (organization, planning, standards development) You have extensive accounting knowledge and proven experience in accounting standards
  • You appreciate interdisciplinary team work and have experience in the conduct of staff You master the computer tools and especially the SAP system You have a developed sense of communication French or German mother tongue with very good knowledge of the second official language

Entry into office

Immediately or to be agreed
