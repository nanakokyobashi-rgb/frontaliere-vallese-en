Full description

We are a trust company operating throughout Switzerland with six locations. More than 60 employees serve SMEs from all sectors as well as private individuals - from accounting to taxes and real estate to auditing and consulting. Experienced professionals and young professionals work closely together with us, knowledge is shared, responsibility is handed over. Our claim: With mind from the heart.

For our team at the location Susten we are looking for a personality as:

Author/in Treuhand (80 \- 100%) with development perspective

Your tasks

  • You manage financial accounting independently and prepare the corresponding financial statements.
  • You create tax returns for individuals and companies.
  • You are responsible for the payroll of your customers.
  • You participate in consulting mandates and take on your own mandates with growing experience.

Your profile

  • You have a basic commercial education and ideally a further education in trust or accounting – or would like to take this step next.
  • You bring experience in the escrow sector, especially in accounting and preparation of financial statements.
  • You enjoy working with numbers, are careful and reliable and appreciate direct contact with customers.
  • You work independently, enjoy working in a team and actively participate.
  • Our offer *
  • Development with perspective: With growing experience, you gradually assume your own mandate responsibility.
  • Further education: We support professional education financially.
  • Know-how in-house: You benefit from the exchange with experts from trust, audit, taxes and real estate as well as internal training workshops.
  • Employee participation: Our company belongs entirely to the employees. Executive staff may acquire shares
  • We also honor our long-standing commitment with participation certificates.
  • Workplace in Susten: You can expect a motivated team at a modern and centrally located workplace.

Questions about the job?

Jean\-Christoph Lehner, site manager, is happy to give you information by phone. For a first contact, your CV is enough for us – we discuss everything else personally.
