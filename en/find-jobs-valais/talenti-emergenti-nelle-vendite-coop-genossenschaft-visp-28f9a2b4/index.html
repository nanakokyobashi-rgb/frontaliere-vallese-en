Tasks

You live for the retail trade and strive with your friendly and courteous way to inspire our customers every day anew? Then you are the right person for our team. They serve and advise our demanding customers in the various rayons. In compliance with our quality and POS guidelines (fresh/quality, hygiene, temperature regulations, expiration dates) you manage the goods and implement the sales promotion measures. In addition to your activities in the rayon, you also make cash assignments. As a talented young person, you will get to know the tasks at a sales point in full, so that you can optimally prepare for a function with management and professional responsibility.

Requirements

  • Completed basic training in the retail trade or corresponding professional experience in the sales sector
  • Innovative, committed and communicative personality with the potential to perform a leadership function in the future
  • Self-employed, clean and careful work
  • High commitment and willingness to provide services
  • Flexibility in terms of operating times
  • Readiness and interest in continuing education and training
