Physiotherapy Sottile is a modern and personally run physiotherapy practice in the heart of Täsch, close to Zermatt. Our goal is to offer our patients individual, holistic and high-quality care – with time, personal attention and modern treatment methods. We combine classical physiotherapy with innovative therapeutic approaches and state-of-the-art technology. We attach great importance to tailoring each treatment individually to the needs and goals of our patients. Our international and committed team works in an open, appreciative and collegial atmosphere. We see different professional backgrounds and experiences as enrichment and promote mutual exchange. In addition to individual patient care, professional development, further training and a modern way of working are particularly important to us. We want to give our employees the opportunity to further develop their own priorities and at the same time be part of a motivated team. Physiotherapy Sottile stands for personal care, modern physiotherapy, quality and a warm working environment. We look forward to expanding our team with dedicated physiotherapists who enjoy their profession and want to further develop physiotherapy together with us.
