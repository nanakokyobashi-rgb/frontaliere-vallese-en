Full description

Our passion. For a better life. ##STR1##

We advise, support and care for hundreds of people in Oberwallis every day. Children, adolescents, parents, grandfathers, great grandmothers. Older and younger people who are experiencing difficulties. We are committed so that they can all live independently. Whether they live in Gondo, Guttet, Grächen, Gampel or Goms.

Help, make the lives of people from your area more enjoyable. Support our 300 employees now or by arrangement and become part of our team as a social worker/intake.

Your new job ##

  • low-threshold and effective situation detection of people in challenging situations
  • Examination of subsidiary claims over social insurance and other support options
  • calculate and apply for entitlement to economic assistance
  • Apply the cantonal legal requirements
  • Advise and accompany the client
  • Involvement of professionals, authorities and institutions in case management

Your qualification ##

  • solution- and resource-oriented acting personality
  • Training in social work or related fields with knowledge of social security
  • Authentic and convincing attitude towards clients, authorities and partners
  • secure written language in the official language
  • Knowledge of foreign languages is advantageous
  • Careful and reliable working methods

Our Promise ##

  • versatile, demanding and largely self-employed work
  • Dedicated and dynamic team
  • Advanced employment conditions and the opportunity for home office
  • well-founded familiarization with the task area Intake
  • generous training opportunities
