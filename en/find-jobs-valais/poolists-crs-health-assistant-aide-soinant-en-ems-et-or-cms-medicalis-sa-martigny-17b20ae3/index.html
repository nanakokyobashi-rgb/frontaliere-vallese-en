Poolists \- CRS Health Auxiliaries / Aide\-Careers in EMS and / or CMS

Description of post

On these missions ofCRS Health Auxiliaries / Aide\-Career in EMS and / or CMS

  • You join our proactive team and are delegated to different medical/social institutions (EMS) or CMS.
  • You provide basic care (toilet, meal aid, nursing\-).
  • Under the responsibility of a nurse you accompany the beneficiaries in the actions of daily life, taking into account the accompanying project and their level of autonomy.
  • One-off (or long-term) missions.
  • Available between 50 and 100 %

Profile sought

You must be trained as a CRS Health Assistant 120 hours or a carer.

With a sense of responsibility, your versatility and flexibility (day/night schedules, weekends, breaks) will allow you to adapt to changes without difficulty. You are able to put your skills at the service of beneficiaries, families and relatives, to have very good stress management and to show empathy. A good command of French and written is required. Your team spirit is one of your major assets.

We offer you

You will be able to carry out your activities as a CRS/Aide\-Health Carer in EMS and/or CMS within a stimulating framework at the heart of the population, rich in contacts, employing human and technical qualities. A dynamic and motivated multidisciplinary team, with personalized coaching, will accompany you.

If you are interested in this new challenge, we encourage you to send us your complete file (motivation letter, CV, diplomas and certificates). We assure you a quick and discreet response.
