Administrative Assistant

COMBUSTIA SA is a Valaisan and family-owned company founded in 1948, which quickly became a supplier of heating oil and fuel to many customers, both in the plain and in the mountains. In Sion, the company has more than 30 service stations in Valais.

The company launches a recruitment for a position of

Administrative Assistant \- (M/F) 80/100%

We are looking for a versatile and motivated person to join our team. You will be in charge of daily administrative management. You will play a key role in internal organization and fluidity and monitoring of administrative processes.

Missions

  • Telephone and physical reception of clients and partners.
  • Managing internal and external email, mail and communications.
  • Writing of administrative documents (contracts, reports, estimates, letters, etc.).
  • Order tracking and creation of daily and monthly invoices.
  • Coordination of delivery schedules.
  • Management Combucard Customers.
  • Updating client databases and managing administrative records.
  • Responsibility for various administrative tasks related to the proper functioning of the company.

Search profile:

  • SWC training trade assistant management, business management, or equivalent.
  • Experience of at least 2 years in an administrative position.
  • Mastery of Office Tools Suite Office.
  • Experience and good knowledge LES.
  • Good time and priority management, organizational sense.
  • Excellent communication skills, both written and oral.
  • Rigor, autonomy.

If you are dynamic, organized and wish to join COMBUSTIA, please send us your application (CV and cover letter)
