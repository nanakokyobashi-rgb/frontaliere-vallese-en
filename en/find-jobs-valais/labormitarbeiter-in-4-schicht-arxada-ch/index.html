Arxada is a global leader in providing innovative solutions to protect our planet. Our cutting-edge technologies, extensive regulatory expertise, and our experience in the production and development of processes support our customers in efficiently protecting nutrition, health, and infrastructure through chemistry and biotechnology that promote sustainability.

  • We offer a wide range of ingredients and services for various end markets, including health and nutrition, home and body care, professional hygiene, paints and coatings, and wood protection. With customers in over 100 countries, the company recorded a revenue of 2 billion CHF in 2024. The company's headquarters is located in Basel, Switzerland. The company employs 3,200 people worldwide, distributed across 24 production sites and 14 research and development centers, all working towards the success of our customers. For our site in Visp, Switzerland, we are currently looking for a quality control shift operator. As a member of our shift quality control team, you will be responsible, within the 4-shift system, for the execution and release of time-critical samples (GC, HPLC, UV titration, pH, etc.) in accordance with ISO standards and internal guidelines. The maintenance of existing infrastructure is also part of your responsibilities. ##
  • Responsibilities
  • Autonomous execution, particularly, of time-critical IPC analyses and tests on products and laboratory samples (e.g., GC, HPLC, titration, UV, pH).
  • Complete and compliant documentation of all results, in line with ISO standards and internal guidelines.
  • Control and resolution of simple malfunctions of analysis tools.
  • Support the proper functioning of the QC laboratory on shifts and actively contribute to continuous improvement. ##
  • Requirements
  • Completed professional training (e.g., in the field of laboratory, chemistry, analysis, or similar).
  • Excellent knowledge of spoken and written German.
  • Previous experience in the analytical field is an advantage.
  • Ability to work in a team, sense of responsibility, stress resistance, and clear and constructive communication.
  • Strong attention to quality and ability to work efficiently, reliably, and autonomously.
  • Good understanding and safe handling of potentially hazardous substances.
  • Good knowledge of PC and common standard software. ## What We Offer Arxada offers a world-class offering in two different areas:
