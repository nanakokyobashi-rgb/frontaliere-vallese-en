Pemsa Valais accompanies you in the search for employment, whether it be a fixed-time job or a permanent job.

Your assignment

  • Participate in design studies and pre-design phases, in collaboration with customers, in order to accurately identify their needs and expectations.
  • Take part in the development and realization of complete and innovative projects in the field of industrial automation.
  • Draw detailed functional analysis and draft FS and FDS documents.
  • Design, program, test and validate automation and supervision systems.
  • Do the commissioning of plants, even with transfers abroad.
  • Create, complete and ensure the updating of technical documentation.
  • Manage your projects independently or with the support of a project manager, from functional analysis to commissioning.

And now we're talking about you?

  • ET/HES/EPF training in electricity, automation or training considered equivalent.
  • At least 3 years of experience in industrial automation, with practical field experience in an indispensable industrial environment.
  • Ability to develop a complete automation project, from the URL to the PLC, through the FS and FDS phases.
  • Excellent mastery of Rockwell Studio 5000 Logix. The knowledge of Siemens, Schneider or Beckhoff is an advantage.
  • Experience with SCADA systems, especially AVEVA System Platform. The knowledge of WinCC or FT View is a plus.
  • Knowledge of processes in the agri-food sector; an experience in the pharmaceutical sector is an advantage.
  • French and English mastery is indispensable. German knowledge is an advantage.
