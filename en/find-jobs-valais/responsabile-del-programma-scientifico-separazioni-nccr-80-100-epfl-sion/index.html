Full description

Mission

The Scientific Program Officer supports the scientific coordination, quality, and implementation of NCCR Separations. The position has responsibility for scientific reporting and communication, Open Science and research data management, Knowledge and Technology Transfer (KTT), organisation of scientific meetings, management of the NCCR associates and digital tools for program coordination and monitoring .

The Scientific Program Officer works closely with the Program Manager, NCCR Directors, Principal Investigators, Work Package Leaders, researchers, and relevant institutional services to support the effective coordination and impact of the NCCR’s scientific activities and outputs.

Main duties and responsibilities

Scientific Reporting and Communication

  • Coordinate the collection and consolidation of scientific information for SNSF reports, annual reviews, and strategic documents.
  • Develop common reporting templates, guidelines, timelines, and processes across Work Packages and scientific pillars.
  • Monitor scientific progress, milestones, deliverables, publications, collaborations, and key program indicators.
  • Contribute to scientific presentations, research highlights, newsletters, websites, and stakeholder communications.

Open Science

  • Coordinate implementation of the NCCR Open Science strategy in accordance with SNSF and institutional requirements.
  • Monitor Open Access and Open Research Data requirements and track compliance across NCCR research outputs.
  • Promote FAIR, reproducible, transparent, and appropriately documented research practices.
  • Develop guidance and training, and connect researchers with relevant repositories, licensing approaches, and institutional expertise.

Research Data Management

  • Coordinate the development, implementation, and updating of the NCCR Data Management Plan (DMP).
  • Establish common approaches for data organisation, metadata, documentation, storage, sharing, archiving, and preservation.
  • Support research teams in implementing effective data-management practices throughout the research lifecycle.
  • Coordinate with EPFL and partner-institution services on research data infrastructure, repositories, policies, and support.

Knowledge and Technology Transfer (KTT)

  • Support the identification of research results, methodologies, technologies, software, datasets, and know-how with potential for scientific, industrial, or societal impact.
  • Facilitate interactions between researchers, institutional KTT services, industry, and other external stakeholders.
  • Contribute to developing the NCCR KTT strategy and to monitoring and reporting KTT activities, collaborations, outputs, and impact.

Scientific and Management Digital Tools

  • Develop and maintain digital tools, databases, dashboards, and indicators for scientific coordination, program management, monitoring, reporting, and strategic decision-making.
  • Ensure the quality, consistency, completeness, and regular updating of scientific and program-management information.
  • Document digital processes and provide guidance and training to NCCR users.

Profile

  • PhD in chemistry, chemical engineering, material science, process engineering, physics, mechanical enginering, environmental or electrical engineering. Alternatively, a Master's degree with at least 5 years of relevant experience
  • Experience in the area of separation science or other related field
  • Experience in scientific writing, reporting, research coordination, or research program management; recent PhD or Master’s graduates with relevant coordination or writing experience (e.g., from lab management, conference organisation, or research administration) are encouraged to apply.
  • Knowledge of Open Science, research data management, FAIR principles, and reproducible research practices.
  • Experience with databases and collaborative digital tools; experience with Airtable is highly desirable.
  • Experience or familiarity with KTT, research valorisation, intellectual property, or industry–academia collaboration is an asset.
  • Excellent scientific writing, synthesis, organisational, and communication skills.
  • Ability to work independently and coordinate effectively with researchers, management, institutional services, and external stakeholders; onboarding and mentoring will be provided.
  • Excellent command of English
  • French and/or German is an asset.
