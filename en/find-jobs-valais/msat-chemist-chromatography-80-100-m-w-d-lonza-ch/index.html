Full description

Relocation support: Available for eligible candidates and their families.

For our site in Visp, we are looking for an MSAT Chemist for the area of highly active substances (HPAPI) and processes that are introduced or operated at the HAPI Kilo Suites and Apollo production facilities.

What awaits you

  • An agile career and a dynamic work culture
  • An inclusive and ethical work environment
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance.

In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous benefits for lifestyle, family and leisure time. You can find our complete list of local benefits below:

  • The Commission has also been involved in the implementation of the new programme for the development of the European Union's research and development programme for the period 1994-99.

Your responsibilities

  • The MSAT Chemist supports process transfers of commercial GMP processes from the HAPI Labs to HKSM (HAPI Kilo Suite Manufacturing) and/or the Apollo operation in close cooperation with production personnel, the project team and customers
  • He supports the commercial production of the highly potent products in handling problems during the ramp-up phase and during regular production (troubleshooting, use test, fault finding, hypothesis testing,..) and in qualification tasks (new suppliers, starting materials,…)
  • In his role as an MSAT Chemist, he develops an excellent understanding of the process and ensures robust process operation and production
  • He leads and develops the assigned laboratory technicians, and contributes to the department as a process optimization specialist

Your qualifications

  • University degree in chemistry (Ph.D. or equivalent professional experience) in synthetic organic chemistry.
  • Experience in process scale-up and process development, as well as experience with preparative chromatography, is desirable
  • Knowledge of problem-solving techniques, statistical process data analysis, and cGMP is an advantage
  • You are flexible, independent, and possess good communication and teamwork skills, as well as a passion for interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Good German and English skills are required
