Assistant restaurant (Manora) 100% (m/f/d)

Monthey Full time 100% Permanent 01.12.2026

Each visit is unique, and it is precisely on this singularity that our service rests. Thanks to our team spirit, agility and close customer proximity, Manor is the No.1 department store for everyday life, both online and in our outlets. Do you like dynamic environments and want to fully value your assets? Then join us to shape tomorrow's shopping experience!

To strengthen our team in the sector Restauration, we are looking from 01.12.2026, for an indeterminate duration, on the site of Monthey, a person motivated and engaged as Assistant / restaurant, 100%.

Your responsibilities

  • You ensure that operations are carried out properly and you apply the quality and hygiene guidelines to Manora restaurant.
  • You make sure the market atmosphere is elegant, according to the concept.
  • You identify, evaluate and exploit the potential of local supply.
  • You're in charge of personnel management.
  • You are responsible for a structure organised and oriented towards the employees of the restaurant (planning, etc.).
  • You train and develop employees and young talent.
  • You are present in the field, you direct and support employees in their daily work.

Your profile

  • Ideally, you have completed a basic course in catering or graduated from a hotel school.
  • You have extensive experience in the field of restoration.
  • You live and like the host/hostess role.
  • Ideally, you've gained a first experience in leadership.
  • You convince with your stress resistance, perseverance and reliability.
  • You have a positive attitude to work, you know how to pass it on to your employees and motivate them sustainably.
  • You have a sense of improvisation and you like to face challenges.
  • You are able to adapt to a strong clientele and you keep your calm and an overview in the turbulent situations.

Our offer

  • Personal discounts ranging from 10 to 25% on all product groups, including Manora restaurants and supermarkets
  • Training and development programmes for all levels and functions
  • Between 5 and 7 weeks of vacation per year
  • 3 weeks paid paternity leave up to 4 months additional maternity leave without pay possible
  • Possibility of a sabbatical of 1 to 6 months from the 3rd year of service
  • Internal pension fund with very strong coverage rate and above average savings pay

Your contact

Manor SA, headquartered in Basel, is the Swiss market leader in the department store sector with a market share of about 60%. In total, the company employs about 6,800 people and trains about 200 apprentices per year. Manor SA operates 56 department stores, 21 Manor Food markets, 23 Manora restaurants and 3 distribution centres.
