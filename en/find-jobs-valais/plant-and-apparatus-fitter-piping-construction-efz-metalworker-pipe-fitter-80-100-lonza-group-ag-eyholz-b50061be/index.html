Your tasks

  • Technical and economical production of pipelines
  • Preparation of isometrics according to plan
  • Operation of bending and other machines
  • Coordination of work processes in coordination with the team leader and the clients
  • Advice and technical assistance to contracting entities

Your profile

  • Completed apprenticeship EFZ as plant and apparatus builder (department C), pipe locksmith, heating installer or sanitary installer
  • Experience in handling large machines

  • Sound knowledge of the BN107 standard
  • High flexibility and willingness to participate in other departments
  • Independent, resilient working method with strong safety and quality awareness
  • Team orientation, networked thinking and economic and entrepreneurial action
  • Very good knowledge of German in word and writing
