Geomatician CFC 100% (M/F/D)

Do you want to put your geomatics skills at the service of various projects and contribute to quality measurement work? Do you enjoy technical environments, the use of modern technologies and teamwork? This opportunity is for you!

Experienced CFC Geomatician 100% (M/F/D)

Responsibilities

  • Participate in formal measurement and field data collection and processing
  • Conduct topographic surveys using specialized measuring equipment and instruments
  • Process, control and integrate measurement data into GIS systems
  • Making technical plans and documents using specialized CAD software
  • Ensure the quality, accuracy and consistency of the geographical data produced
  • Participate in the maintenance and management of geospatial databases
  • Collaborate with various stakeholders on projects to ensure their successful implementation
  • Ensure monitoring of new technologies and contribute to improving working methods and tools

Profile

  • Geomatician CFC, Federal Geomatics Technician Certificate or equivalent training
  • Experience in official measurements in the canton of VS
  • Knowledge of the Valais context in particular the DOCDO
  • Good control of survey tools, GIS and CAD software
  • Rigor, precision, sense of organization and concern for quality
  • Team spirit, autonomy and ability to manage the tasks entrusted to you
  • High interest in new technologies and developments in geomatics
  • Mastery of French, both oral and written

Benefits

  • A stimulating, modern and collaborative working environment
  • Various, technical and innovative projects
  • Competitive remuneration and attractive social benefits
  • The possibility to evolve within a specialized team and at the cutting edge of geomatics technologies
  • Entry into office: to be agreed
