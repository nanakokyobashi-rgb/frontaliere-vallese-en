Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire with great commitment construction customers and private customers on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

To strengthen our regional organization, we are looking for an experienced, autonomous and flexible logisticsian, ready to intervene in our various GBM branches in the Valais – Riviera – Chablais region from 1 May 2027.

Missions

A real regional operational reinforcement, you ensure the continuity of logistics activities in our Shops, especially when there are no employees or depending on the specific needs. Thanks to your experience and versatility, you quickly integrate with different teams and help ensure the availability of goods and quality customer service.

  • Ensure receipt, control and computer processing of goods.
  • Manage inventory, locations and movement of goods.
  • Prepare and ship customer orders and internal transportation.
  • Track E-Shop orders and inform customers of their availability.
  • Manage returns, claims and possible anomalies.
  • Ensure order, storage and security of the halls.
  • Replace logistics staff who are absent from the various branches.
  • Provide timely support to Shop counters and sales teams.
  • Participate in continuous improvement of logistics processes.

Your Profile

  • Logistician CFC.
  • Minimum 5 years experience confirmed in a similar logistics environment.
  • Valid forklift/carist permit.
  • Good control of IT tools and logistics processes.
  • Experience in the construction materials sector is an asset.

We offer

  • A stimulating, varied and interesting activity in a good working environment, where the emphasis is on active collaboration and at the same time on autonomy
  • Intensive training is on the agenda, as our new colleagues can benefit from training on products and systems within the company
  • Support from our employees in their professional and personal development
  • Remuneration related to skills development
  • 5 weeks of vacation (6 weeks from 50 years) and vacation buy-back up to 10 days maximum
  • Additional benefits such as a fully funded independent pension fund and reductions in our full range of products as well as external premium benefits

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
