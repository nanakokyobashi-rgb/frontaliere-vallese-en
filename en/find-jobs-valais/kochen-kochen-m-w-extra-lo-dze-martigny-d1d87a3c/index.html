Full description

The Quartier LôDzé is looking for an extra cook / cook (M/F) to strengthen its kitchen team. We are looking for a motivated, organized and self-sustaining person, concerned with the quality of the preparations and committed to the observance of the establishment's standards.

Your missions

  • Prepare and produce dishes in collaboration with the Executive Chief, the Deputy Chief and the party leaders.
  • Ensure the finishing, dressing and quality of the dishes before they are sent to the room.
  • Monitor the conformity of preparations and ensure compliance with establishment restoration standards.
  • Participate in the establishment and organization of the work station.
  • Manage the means and technical requirements necessary for the proper functioning of the service.
  • Communicate effectively with team members and contribute to the proper coordination of the kitchen.
  • Participate in the development of innovative projects and the evolution of the catering offer.
  • Strictly apply food hygiene and safety rules, including HACCP procedures.
  • Ensure cleaning and maintenance of kitchen equipment and equipment.

Profile sought

  • Training in cooking or equivalent work experience.
  • Experience in restoration.
  • Good command of culinary techniques and preparation methods.
  • Organizational sense, rigour and attention to detail.
  • Ability to work effectively under pressure and meet service times.
  • Team spirit, autonomy and good communication.
  • Strict compliance with HACCP hygiene and food safety standards.
  • Flexibility and availability for various schedules, including weekends, holidays and cuts.

We offer you

  • Reduction for employees in the various outlets
  • 5 weeks of holidays, 6 holidays
  • Salary according to applicable CLC and 13th months.
  • A dynamic and varied working environment.

We will only examine the complete files (CV, cover letter, work certificates, etc.) of the candidates corresponding to the profile sought.
