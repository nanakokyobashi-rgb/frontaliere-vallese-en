Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the safety of permanent employment? For 30 years, we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, offering them the best talents. We will not lie to you, but we are demanding. So, if you are a motivated and extremely enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of category A autogru operator.

Your assignment

  • Drive a car in the building yards safely, strictly complying with the SUVA norms and internal directives.
  • Driving autogroups requiring the licence for heavy vehicles of category C or EC, as well as the OACP certificate in progress.
  • Plan and prepare lifting operations, including the installation of stabilizers and load control, to ensure precise and risk-free work.
  • To cooperate closely with the shipyard teams and logistics managers to ensure optimum coordination of operations.
  • Meet the specific requirements of exceptional transport when required, respecting the limits of 50 km from home for efficient and consistent mobility.
  • Ensure the first level maintenance of the cargroup, checking the levels, tyre pressure and the general state of the vehicle before any use.
  • Fill out the daily activity reports and report any anomalies found during the service, thus ensuring the traceability of the interventions.

And now we're talking about you?

  • You followed an SUVA training course.
  • You must have a licence for heavy trucks of category C or CE, as well as the SUVA license for category A.
  • You know perfectly the SUVA safety standards and demonstrate a rigorous competence in handling heavy machinery on site and in other contexts.
  • You are able to work within a maximum radius of 50 km from your home, which guarantees a logistics adapted to Swiss needs.
  • The knowledge of the French language is indispensable; ideally the masters at native language level for a smooth communication with the teams on site.
  • Demonstrate autonomy, sense of responsibility and a great sense of organization to ensure proper operation, even in complex technical situations.
  • You have a good physical shape and the ability to maintain concentration in the long term to ensure the safety and precision of lifting and transport operations.
