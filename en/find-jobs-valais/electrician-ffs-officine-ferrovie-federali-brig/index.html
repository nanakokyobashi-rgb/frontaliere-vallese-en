Electrical installer

Brig, 01.09.2026 or by arrangement, 60-100 % Apply now

You can look forward to that.

You are an electrical installer with body and soul: in and ready for an interesting job in a motivated and collaborative team in the area of security systems? An innovative working environment awaits you, in which you can drive major changes and thus contribute to the digitalization of SBB. Together with your teammates, you develop solutions for current challenges and implement them in a team. Further education is also not neglected for individual professional development. Attractive working conditions are very important to us. Our benefits such as child allowances, free 2nd class GA and discounted travel abroad - also for the family - can be found on our website under « Benefits ». You can move that.

  • You take care of the construction, maintenance and maintenance of infrastructure facilities and thus make an important contribution to smooth rail traffic.
  • A varied and varied working day awaits you, mainly day shifts at various SBB stations in the Upper Valais region.
  • You ensure the installation and commissioning of various security systems – from electronic signal box systems to relay signal boxes.
  • Through your systematic approach, you recognize and report the irregularities and defects and initiate their correction and further development.
  • You will undergo a two-year internal training as a safety equipment technician. Here you will be trained to locate and repair disturbances on the interior systems independently.
  • With your newly learned expertise and experience, you can independently locate and repair disturbances at the security systems in the Pikettdienst.

You bring that with you.

  • Safety comes first with you. In order to ensure this, we require unrestricted hearing and vision in order to be able to detect important acoustic and visual signals during track operations.
  • You are involved in our organization as well as in the team and take responsibility.
  • Working indoors, as well as in the track area, you consider exciting.
  • You have completed a four-year basic training as an electrical installer: in EFZ.
  • Occasionally irregular work or pikettdienst at night and on weekends do not matter to you.
  • You have a Cat driver's license. B and good oral and written knowledge of German (minimum B2).
