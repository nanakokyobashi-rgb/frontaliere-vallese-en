[Consultation ]( )

The Consultation SA is an organisation of psychologists\-psychotherapists located at several sites in French-speaking Switzerland (Aigle, Bulle, Chavornay, Martigny, Montreux, Moudon, Palézieux, Payerne, Peseux, Rolle, Saint\-Maurice, Sion) and in Ticino (Bellinzona). It offers individual psychotherapeutic care (children, adolescents, adults), couple and family care and seeks psychotherapeutic psychologists or psychotherapy training for several of its sites.
