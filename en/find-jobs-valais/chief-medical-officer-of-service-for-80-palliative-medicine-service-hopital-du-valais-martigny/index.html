Full description

The canton's first employer, the Hôpital du Valais has 6,260 employees. Each year, 418,800 patients are cared for, and nearly 670 300 outpatient visits are provided.

The Valais Hospital is looking for the Valais Romand Hospital Centre

Chief Medical Officer for its 80% palliative medicine service

Mission

  • To assume medical responsibility for the care of patients entrusted.
  • Provide medical and medico-economic management of the service, including human resources, planning and development, in accordance with the guidelines established by the Medical Directorate.
  • Organize or delegate responsibility for the custody of the service.
  • Ensure quality and proper delivery of care, clinical interventions and treatment.
  • Ensure effective collaboration with all hospital services.
  • Ensure efficient use of resources and budget compliance by participating in cost control and appropriate use of available resources.
  • Contribute to the maintenance of the quality of service certification and the renewal of its FMH recognition as a post-graduate training centre in palliative medicine.
  • Provide training and supervision to assistant doctors, clinical directors, students and paramedical staff, together with senior doctors.
  • Participate in and supervise clinical research.
  • Develop and update employee career plans and service succession plans.
  • Maintain his/her professional skills and meet the continuing training requirements necessary to maintain his/her FMH/and Schwerpunkt Palliative care.

Profile

  • Federal specialist or equivalent training.
  • Swiss certificate of advanced training in palliative medicine, or equivalent. Several years of experience in palliative care structures: in Palliative Care Unit and Mobile Team.
  • Academic title (e.g. Privat-docent, Professor) or involvement in university research and teaching would be an asset.
  • Experience confirmed in team management and supervision of doctors in training.
  • A developed sense of interdisciplinary collaboration, commitment and great availability.
  • Knowledge of German would be desired but not indispensable.

Only offers responding to the profile will be processed.
