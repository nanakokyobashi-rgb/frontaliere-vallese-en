Collaborator in Francophone internal communication (40%)

Place of work · Brig

Entry into office · 1 January 2027

Accredited according to the LEHE, UniDistance Switzerland offers university courses entirely online. Studies follow part-time, distance and flexiblely. On its campus in Brig, the institution conducts active and interdisciplinary research. For more than 30 years, she has been developing her education, teaching and research in five faculties: law, psychology, economics, history, and mathematics and computer science. It has more than 2,700 students and about 550 collaborators.

Within the Communication and Marketing department, you ensure the writing and coordination of internal communication content in French, under the responsibility of the head of the service. In collaboration with your German-speaking counterpart and the various services, you facilitate the flow of information and help the teams to better understand the projects and priorities of the institution.

Your missions

  • Write, revise and publish content in French for intranet, annual report and other internal communication media.
  • Collect service needs, select relevant information and coordinate its dissemination according to audiences and priorities.
  • Participate in editorial planning of the service and monitor internal publications.
  • Translate and adapt content from German to French, ensuring consistency of terminology and editorial quality.
  • Contribute to the management and improvement of internal communication channels, including intranet.
  • Participate in the organization of information sessions and internal meetings on the Brig campus.
  • Collect feedback from teams and propose improvements in content and formats.

Your profile

  • Advanced training in communication, journalism, media science or equivalent.
  • Experience in internal communication and writing, ideally in the academic sector, training or within a public institution.
  • Excellent mastery of French and ability to write with clarity and precision for different audiences.
  • Good knowledge of German to collaborate in a bilingual environment and to translate content into French. English is an asset.
  • Experience with digital tools, including MS Office, Teams and content management systems.
  • Ability to prioritize information, coordinate multiple contributions and meet deadlines.
  • Autonomy, rigor and reliability in the work.
  • Listening, team spirit and ease of communication with interlocutors and interlocutors from different fields.

We offer

  • A varied activity that contributes to the life of a bilingual university institution and encourages exchanges between disciplines and services.
  • Modern working conditions, good social benefits and opportunities for continuing training.
  • A campus in Brig, close to the SBB station and the old town.
