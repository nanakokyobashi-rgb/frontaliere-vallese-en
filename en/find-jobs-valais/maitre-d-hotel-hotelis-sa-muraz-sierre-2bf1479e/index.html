HOTELIS is the only company specializing in the recruitment of catering and hotel occupations for stable and temporary posts. HOTELIS is the preferred partner of many top-class hotels, restaurants, catering services (events, stadiums and exhibition centres) and community restaurant operators.

Hotel Master M/F

Company description

For our client, a renowned establishment offering a high-end care and living environment in the heart of the Valais Alps, we are looking for a:

Description of post

A true ambassador of the art of receiving, you are responsible for the organization and the proper functioning of the catering service. You make sure to offer a quality experience, combining professionalism, friendliness and personalized attention, in an environment where the well-being and satisfaction of the hosts occupy a central place.

Present in the field, you coordinate and supervise daily activities, mentor and accompany your team while ensuring that service standards and procedures are met. You ensure smooth operations, anticipate needs, manage unforeseen events and ensure that you maintain an excellent quality of service.

In close cooperation with the kitchen teams and the various departments of the establishment, you are actively involved in the coordination of services, the management of stocks and orders and the monitoring of daily operations. You also contribute to the training and development of employees, while instilling positive dynamics and a true team spirit.

Profile sought

For the benefit of hotel training\-restoration, you justify an experience confirmed in a similar function, ideally acquired in high-end hotel establishments or structures offering personalized services.

You have strong organizational, operational and team management skills. With an excellent sense of hospitality and service, you are recognized for your careful presentation, relationship ease and ability to anticipate the expectations of a demanding clientele.

Autonomous, dynamic and versatile, you can demonstrate leadership while remaining close to your employees. Your sense of responsibility, flexibility and ability to manage priorities allow you to evolve calmly in a demanding environment. Mastering French and German is an important asset for this position.

We offer you the opportunity to join a recognized institution, within a privileged professional environment and a committed team. You will evolve in a stimulating work environment, where the quality of the welcome, the sense of detail and the well-being of the hosts are at the heart of the concerns.

This position offers real operational and human responsibilities, with the ability to leverage your experience, organizational sense and managerial qualities.

Entry into office: immediately or to be agreed
