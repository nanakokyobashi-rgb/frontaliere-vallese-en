Building maintenance technician (a)

Place of work : Canton Valais Participation rate: 100% Start date: To be agreed - duration 6 months

At ISS, we all call ourselves "Placemakers". Our goal is to provide our clients with the best possible environment and make them feel good in their workplace. Our 14'200 placemakers have a high level of service knowledge to meet the different needs of our customers. We achieve them according to the highest quality requirements.

As part of a new mandate in Sion, we are looking for a Technical Concierge / Building Maintenance Technician:

Your tasks:

  • Perform preventive and corrective maintenance services on technical facilities
  • Ensure maintenance, troubleshooting and adjustment of technical installations
  • Manage maintenance and maintenance activities
  • Take care of various requests and small troubleshooting.
  • Process daily work reports, delivery orders and purchase orders
  • Overseeing and monitoring the execution of the work by mandated enterprises
  • Propose and implement appropriate changes and improvements for different sectors
  • Administrative tasks (GMAO, dispatching, etc...)
  • Manage small repair projects
  • Provide picket service (1 week / 5)

Your profile:

  • Diploma of technician ES, CFC or patent in building technology or maintenance, electricity or energy, or diploma deemed equivalent
  • Several years of experience confirmed in a similar position in the services, facility management or technical field
  • Driving licence
  • French language (B2/C1)
  • Very good mastery of common IT tools (MS Office)
  • Strong customer orientation, teamwork and flexibility
