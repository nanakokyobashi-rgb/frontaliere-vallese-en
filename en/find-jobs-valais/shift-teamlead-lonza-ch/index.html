Lonza is now a world leader in the Life Sciences business, which operates on three continents. We are active in science, but there is no magic formula for how we work. Our most important scientific solution is talented employees who work together and develop ideas that help other companies to help people. In return, our employees manage their careers independently. Become part of our QC 24/7 Analytical Services team – and take on a key role in Bio-Cens production at the Visp site!

Our team is the central hub for all analytical samples from production – around the clock. As a Shift Team Lead, you take responsibility for an interdisciplinary team and ensure that time-critical analyses are carried out reliably, efficiently and in compliance with GMP. They combine operational excellence with leadership and are thus a key success factor for our production.

What makes this role so exciting? As a Shift Team Lead, you not only manage the day-to-day business, but also actively develop your team and drive quality, performance and collaboration forward. You keep track of all analyses, set priorities and ensure that results are delivered on time and in the highest quality – in a dynamic 24/7 environment with direct impact on production.

What we offer you

  • A responsible leadership role in a state-of-the-art, technology-driven environment
  • Support through a strong network of experts and executives
  • Attractive development and career opportunities in the field of the brand
  • Working in the 4-shift model with appropriate flexibility in everyday life

Your tasks

  • Professional and personnel management of a shift team in 24/7 operation
  • Ensuring safety, quality and timely execution of all analyses
  • Coordination and prioritization of all relevant tasks in the team (shift control)
  • Promotion and further development of employees (coaching, training, feedback)
  • Ensuring GMP compliance, data integrity and proper documentation
  • Close coordination and communication with production and other QC units
  • Management of shift H-h-knives and ensuring structured shift transfers -

Identification and implementation of improvement measures in everyday laboratory life

Your requirement profile

  • multi-year experience in the GMP environment, ideally in analytics (QC)
  • First leadership experience or keen interest in leadership role
  • High willingness to take responsibility and structured and solution-oriented working methods
  • High communication and team skills
  • Very good understanding of quality, data integrity and regulatory requirements
  • Negotiation-proof German skills, English skills are a plus
  • readiness to shift work (4 shift-company)
