Your task

  • Precisely apply products with the squeegee or roller to ensure a quality finish.
  • Prepare and mix the different materials according to the specific formulations for each construction site.
  • Carry out smoothing and sandblasting of screeds or other coverings, ensuring the safety and conformity of the surfaces.
  • Lay technical resins on terraces, industrial floors and car parks in compliance with quality standards and established deadlines.
  • Work autonomously, effectively organizing your interventions and anticipating the specific needs of the construction site.
  • Manage the logistics of traveling to construction sites, using a personal vehicle to ensure punctuality and responsiveness.
  • Actively contribute to the care and maintenance of the tools and equipment used.

And now let's talk about you?

  • Mastery of French at a minimum B1 level, essential for understanding instructions and communicating on site.
  • Proven practical experience in applying with a roller or spatula, including mixing and preparing specific products.
  • Solid skills in sanding and sandblasting screeds or various coverings, with attention to detail and quality.
  • Ability to lay resins on terraces, industrial floors and car parks, in compliance with Swiss technical standards.
  • Several years of professional experience in this sector, ideally gained in Switzerland.
  • Driving license required, to facilitate movement between different construction sites.
  • Motivated, conscientious person with a spirit of initiative, ready to commit fully to their tasks.
  • Resident in Valais, to guarantee the essential proximity to the intervention sites (a maximum distance of 30 km is preferred).
  • Successful professional experience of at least 3 years on the Swiss market is a serious advantage.
