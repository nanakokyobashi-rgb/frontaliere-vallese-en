Full description

At Sunrise, we work closely with a team and are in daily contact with our clients in the shops. Our culture combines respect, openness and new ideas with a strong team spirit. We support each other, assume our responsibilities on a daily basis and know that every voice counts and that every point of view helps us improve.

Our passion drives us to innovate and perfect ourselves constantly, always with the aim of offering a positive customer experience.

Do you like working as a team, have a sense of responsibility and enjoy direct contact with clients? Then join our team.

WHAT IS HEARING YOU

Our teams in direct contact with customers shape Sunrise's customer experience. Thanks to personalized support, tailor-made solutions and an impeccable service, each contact becomes a lasting customer relationship.

  • You actively meet our clients, you understand their needs and you offer them tailored solutions.
  • On sale, you can seduce with clear and honest advice and a convincing presence.
  • You show great personal motivation, you achieve your monthly goals and you actively contribute to the success of the shop.
  • You process service and support requests (e.g. after-sales service, product questions or subscriptions) using a solution-based approach, with competence and reliability.
  • With the team, you help create a welcoming shopping experience and a positive atmosphere.
  • You manage all administrative and operational tasks (e.g. cash, stock, good handling of the shop).
  • You take advantage of hollow times to conduct interesting prospecting campaigns and generate additional sales opportunities.
  • You embody the values of Sunrise Bold, Passionate and One on a daily basis, and actively encourage collaboration, closeness to customers and initiative.

YOUR SKILLS

  • Ideally, you already justify an experience in a similar position in the retail sector, or you know how to be convincing as a candidate from another sector, because of your motivation and keen sense of sales and service.
  • You are full of enthusiasm for digital technology and products, and you can present them in a clear and understandable way.
  • You work by focusing on the goals and needs of the clientele, you are reliable and you keep an overview even in stressful situations.
  • You are flexible and ready to work regularly on Saturdays and evenings.
  • You have very good knowledge of German, and any other language is an asset.

At Us, you evolve in a dynamic and multinational environment where every voice is heard, perspectives are shared and values are lived daily. For us, diversity is not just a program, it is part of our DNA. No matter who you are or where you come from, you will find your place at Sunrise. We create an inclusive environment in which every collaborator feels fully integrated and can develop its full potential. Our commitment to equality and diversity is also recognized externally: Sunrise is a partner of Advance Gender Equality in Business, holds the Swiss LGBTI Label and is Fair-ON-Pay Advanced certified for equal pay for work of equal value.
