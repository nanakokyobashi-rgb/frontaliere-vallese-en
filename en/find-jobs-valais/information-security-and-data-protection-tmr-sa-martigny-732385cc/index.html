INFORMATION SECURITY AND DATA PROTECTION

Are you convinced that cybersecurity is not limited to technology, but is a real challenge for the whole company? Do you want to combine technical expertise, data protection and team support, with the long-term ambition of moving towards a RSSI function?

Join TMR Transports de Martigny and Régions SA, active in public and private transport by rail and road, with 250 employees.

We put the following position in the competition

Your mission

Attached to the SI manager, you protect the company's information (IT) and operational systems (OT, embedded systems, IoT) as well as the personal data they process. You contribute to the security strategy and WSIS, administer cybersecurity tools, manage incidents, and pilot intrusion tests and remediation plans. As a Data Protection Advisor (DPO), you monitor LPD/GDPR compliance and accompany the services. You conduct internal audits, sensitize employees and collaborate with the system and network team, including automation (scripts, RPA, IA). Evolving position to the RSSI function.

What you will do every day

  • Protect information systems, IT/OT infrastructures and TMR data by identifying risks and vulnerabilities.
  • Monitor security events, analyze alerts and actively contribute to incident management.
  • Deploy and evolve cybersecurity measures and tools: access management, EMIS/BDU, vulnerabilities, network security and systems.
  • Ensure the protection of personal data and support the company in the application of the LPD and associated good practices.
  • Advise and sensitize teams and managers to make safety a shared reflex in everyday life.
  • Participate in ISD projects by integrating security from the design stage and supporting the evolution of digital and operational environments.
  • Help structure and advance the governance of information security, with the prospect of moving progressively towards an IRS function.

Your profile

To benefit from a HES degree in computer science, a federal ICT certificate or diploma or a training deemed equivalent, you have 1 to 3 years experience in a similar function, ideally in the industrial or transport environment. You master network and application security, security solutions (antivirus, IDS/IPS, DLP), IAM, virtualization (VMware, Hyper\-V) and scripting (, PowerShell, Bash). You are familiar with ISO 27001/NIST, LPD/GDPR, OT environments and IT/OT segmentation. Certification (ISO 27001, Security, IEC 62443, CIPP/E) is an asset. You speak French (C1\) and technical English (B2\); German is a plus. Rigourous\-se and pedagogue, you are available for ad hoc interventions outside of office hours.

Why join us?

At TMR, we transport much more than passengers: we connect people and territories with reliability, security and proximity. We value commitment, collaboration and innovation, in a dynamic environment where every employee contributes to our daily mission.

You will evolve in a flexible and user-friendly working environment, at the heart of the Martigny region, with passionate teams and strong values: proximity, team spirit and social responsibility. Being part of TMR is joining an organization that places people at the centre of their choices and gives you the means to grow and innovate in your role.

TMR will also offer you benefits such as:

  • A 2nd class AG at the end of your trial period, fully supported by the company (tax value declared in the salary certificate)
  • The possibility of teleworking regularly (one to two days a week depending on the activity rate)
  • A more generous LPP than the legal minimum, with a reduced amount of coordination and an employed share maintained at 7.5% regardless of age
  • Reduced fare travel facilities for your loved ones and international train trips at a reduced rate after a year of service
  • Small mores like the free Mont\- Express to Chamonix on request, free entrances to the Gianadda Foundation or tickets to FC Sion matches
  • Modern computer and digital tools and an ergonomic desk in a user-friendly and pleasant HR space
  • And above all an exceptional professional playground, between plain and mountain
