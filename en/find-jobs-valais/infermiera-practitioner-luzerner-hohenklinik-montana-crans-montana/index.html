Full description

100%, immediately or to be agreed

The Bernoise Montana Clinic, elected the best rehabilitation clinic in Switzerland in 2022 and 2023, with 110 beds, deals with patients in the fields of neurology, psychosomatics, orthopaedics, internal medicine and oncology. Our rehabilitation center located in Crans-Montana, in the idyllic setting of the Valais Alps, search for the site of its Clinic Lucernoise, one/one Nurse Practitioner/Trainer 100% Your activities

  • Identifying, planning, implementing and evaluating nursing services in preventive, curative and educational areas specific to rehabilitation
  • Welcoming, integrating and accompanying nursing students during their practical training period
  • Define individualised learning objectives adapted to the level of training and the skills to be developed
  • Promote the development of autonomy, clinical reasoning, professional posture and student relational skills
  • Support the integration of theoretical knowledge into daily clinical practice
  • Conduct intermediate and final evaluations in accordance with the training institution benchmarks
  • Promote the involvement of patients in their care project and the recovery of their self-help
  • Performing delegated medical acts, delegating acts dependent on your own role and ensuring their accountability
  • Contributing to collaborative solutions within the interdisciplinary team
  • Participate in the supervision and evaluation of students and trainees, as well as the integration of new collaborators

Your profile

  • Diploma in nursing ES or HES (with recognition Swiss Red Cross if foreign)
  • CAS or microcertification of practitioner/trainer or willingness to do so
  • Interest in working as an interdisciplinary team
  • French or German mother tongue (level B2), with good knowledge of the other language
  • Very good communication skills
  • Responsibility, autonomy, rigour, vigilance and technicality
  • Mastery of common computer tools
  • Flexibility, stress resistance
  • Adaptability to change and involvement in service projects
  • Skills development, professional development

We offer

  • A unique experience of interdisciplinary work, within a motivated, committed and dynamic rehabilitation team, combining several disciplines (psychotherapy, physiotherapy, sports therapies, occupational therapy, logopedia, swimming pool and balneotherapy, art therapy, music therapy, hippotherapy, etc.)
  • Community medical supervision
  • Paradisiac workplace, with leading rehabilitation infrastructure
  • Opportunities for internal and external training
  • On-site accommodation and catering facilities
