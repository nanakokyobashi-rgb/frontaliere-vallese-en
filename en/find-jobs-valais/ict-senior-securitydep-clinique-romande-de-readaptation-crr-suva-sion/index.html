ICT Senior Security Engineer 80–100% In this high level of responsibility function, you assume the operational management of central and recurring security tasks while contributing to the development of internal skills and know-how within Suva Clinics. Your activity is at the interface between daily operations, strategic initiatives and collaboration with our specialized external partners. You have real flexibility to change our security practices, strengthen our cyber-resilience and further develop our information security management system ( SMSI/ISMS ). Your responsibilities

  • Provide security monitoring and troubleshooting in the areas of XDR, SIEM, NAC and NDR, including solution maintenance and technical incident resolution.
  • Conduct core activities in Digital Forensics & Incident Response (DFIR) and coordinate interventions with SOC and specialized partners.
  • Develop Vulnerability Management and monitor and implement security measures in collaboration with local teams.
  • Maintain and continuously develop the information security management system ( SMSI/ISMS ).
  • Ensure compliance with ISO 27001 and minimum ICT standards, including through maturity analyses, controls and Gap Assessments.
  • Participate in the design and development of security strategy and architecture in network, cloud ( Microsoft 365/Azure) and endpoints.
  • Conduct security configuration controls and contribute to the continuous improvement of our security solutions, including Microsoft Defender, SentinelOne, CrowdStrike, Microsoft Azure and Microsoft 365.
  • Carry out risk analysis, ensure continuous risk tracking and produce reporting tailored to the various levels of internal and external stakeholders. Your profile
  • Advanced training completed in the field of computer science (ES/HES ), ideally with a specialization in IT/cybersecurity security, or equivalent continuing training and/or recognized certifications in the field, such as CISSP, AZ-500 or SC-100.
  • Several years of professional experience in an operational function in cybersecurity.
  • Strong practical knowledge in SIEM/XDR/NDR/NAC, Vulnerability Management, IAM/MAP, AD/DNS/DHCP/CS and security architecture.
  • Good knowledge of security standards and frameworks, including ISO 27001 and minimum ICT standards.
  • Experience in working with external security service providers, including SOC or MSSP, is an asset.
  • Analytic method of work, structured and autonomous, combined with a high level of initiative and a strong sense of responsibility.
  • Excellent relational and communication skills, allowing to interact with technical specialists as well as with various internal and external stakeholders.
  • Very good knowledge of German and English; knowledge of French is an asset.
