Full description

HES-SO Valais-Wallis trains more than 2,800 students and contributes, through research, to innovation, economic and social development and job creation. Located in the heart of Valais, innovative and progressing with its time, the Haute Ecole de Gestion offers professionalising tertiary training focused on the student's experience. Thanks to its fields of study, its research institutes and its proximity to the economic environment, it offers a true ecosystem of innovation combining training and research. We are contributing to the Valais of tomorrow. Join us!

HES teachers or HES teachers

In order to strengthen training and applied research within the HEG, we are looking for HES or HES teachers in the following areas:

Creative economy

Culture

  • Event

Tourism economy

Quantitative analysis

Your Mission

  • Acquire, conduct and carry out applied research and development (Ra&D) projects related to the area(s) of expertise specified in this announcement.
  • Teaching within the Bachelor and Master courses of the HEG in the corresponding topics.
  • Collaborate with regional, national and international actors (businesses, public institutions, colleges, universities) to develop interdisciplinary projects and partnerships.
  • Publish scientific work in the form of articles and presentations to symposia.

Your profile

  • Doctorate or title deemed equivalent.
  • Experience in the filing and management of Ra&D projects (e.g. Innosuisse, Innotour, FNS, NPR, Interreg, Horizon Europe, etc.).
  • Proven professional experience in applied research and service delivery in an economic or academic context.
  • Experience and strong interest in higher education and support for students in various educational contexts.
  • Strong interest in innovation, leadership skills and ability to collaborate on interdisciplinary teams.
  • French or German mother tongue with good knowledge of the second national language and English.
