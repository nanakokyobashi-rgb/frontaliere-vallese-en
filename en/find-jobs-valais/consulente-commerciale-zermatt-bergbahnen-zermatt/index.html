Become part of the Zermatt Bergbahnen

Zermatt Bergbahnen AG operates the year-round, international excursion and snow sports area in Zermatt and is a modern employer, firmly anchored in Zermatt and the Mattertal. With a dedicated team of 280 employees, we offer visitors unforgettable mountain adventures through important ticket and information services in our sales outlets in the impressive mountains of Zermatt.
