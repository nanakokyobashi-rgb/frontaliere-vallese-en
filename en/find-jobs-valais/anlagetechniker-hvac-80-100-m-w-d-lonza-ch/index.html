Full description

Where necessary, eligible candidates and their families will be assisted in moving.

As an investment technician HVAC & production in Visp You are responsible for the operation and maintenance of the heating and ventilation systems (HLK) as well as the production area of a modern biopharma company.

You ensure the economical and reliable operation of the assigned operating facilities and coordinate the professional implementation of the maintenance work and qualification activities in compliance with applicable GMP guidelines.

This position requires a full presence on the ground. Personal collaboration enables close coordination in real time and ensures the technical precision required for the production of medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What we offer you

  • An Agile Career and Dynamic Work Culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous benefits in the areas of lifestyle, family and leisure. The full list of our global services can also be found at: Benefits - Visp

Your tasks

  • Planning and coordination of the professional troubleshooting and maintenance work of the HVAC and production plants of a biopharmaceutical plant taking into account the GMP guidelines.
  • Creation, planning, implementation of the necessary maintenance and periodic activities according to the maintenance strategy with the involvement of internal and external partners.
  • Responsible for the maintenance and creation of the plant flow diagrams, layout plans and technical plant documentation assigned to it.
  • Ensuring maximum plant availability.
  • Continuous optimization of plants, processes and internal processes
  • Contact person for audits and inspections in the area of HVAC
  • Responsible for cleanroom requalification
  • Cooperation in monitoring and complying with the available budget (maintenance, conversions and investments)
  • Ensuring safety, hygiene, environmental and quality standards in the technical plant sector

Your profile

  • Completed technical apprenticeship (ideally with further training as a technician).
  • Several years of professional experience in the operation and maintenance of HVAC and production plants in the GMP-regulated sector of the pharmaceutical or biotech industry are desirable.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method
  • Ideally, knowledge of SAP, DMS, TrackWise and COMOS
  • You have good knowledge of German
  • English skills are beneficial
