Scholarship ATC & Swiss Sherpa Foundation

Air Zermatt Training Center & Swiss Sherpa Foundation - Together for global rescue competence

The scholarship is aimed at dedicated rescue professionals worldwide. It allows participation in specialized courses of the Air Zermatt Training Center – especially for people with limited training access and financial resources.

Possible courses

  • AHEMS Crew Member Course
  • Mountain Emergency Medicine (MEM) Winter Course
  • Mountain Emergency Medicine (MEM) Summer Course

What will be covered?

  • Travel expenses and accommodation
  • Course fees

Who can apply?

The scholarship is aimed at rescue professionals worldwide, especially from regions with limited training and financial resources.

Applications may be

  • Specialists with a medical background (sanitarians, emergency doctors, nurses with emergency experience) → MEM courses
  • Technical rescue specialists (mountain, water, altitude or desert rescue) → HEMS course
  • Members of local or international rescue organisations actively providing or improving emergency care

Conditions

  • Proven professional experience (completed or ongoing) in the rescue sector
  • Clear financial need
  • Objective: Improvement of rescue structures in the country of origin
  • International willingness to cooperate
  • Good English skills

Application

  • Application documents:
  • Personal data
  • Letter of motivation (max. 1000 words)
  • Evidence of training and experience
  • Justification and proof of financial need
  • Reference letters

Time limit: 31. October each year

Email: training@air-zermatt.ch

Language: English

All information about the Swiss Sherpa Foundation can be found here: https://swiss-sherpa.org/
