Precautionary expert/in Oberland

Hello Team spirit.

You have a good sense of people and their needs. And you bring valuable experience in preventive counseling and have a healthy ambition. Take advantage of this unique opportunity and participate in our new “Life” division right from the start. You support our agencies on site and strengthen the customer advisors through targeted coaching in lead generation. You focus on proactively advising our customers for our pension products. The customer advisors of the general agency support you with leads from our broad customer base. From us comes a first-class employment package, a top internal education and training and the flexibility so that you can put your job and private life on the line. Let’s talk about a common future.

This is what your everyday life looks like. *

  • You handle the leads of your general agency and as a coach you support the consultants in their professional and sales development.
  • You maintain existing relationships and actively win new customers for private provision.
  • You conduct preventive consultations personally or remotely and develop individual solutions that you sell successfully.
  • You strengthen the bond with existing customers, recognize sales opportunities and work closely with colleagues to exploit these cross and upselling potentials.
  • For valuable customer interactions, you use modern tools and represent us with suitable marketing and sales measures.
  • You also answer the concerns and questions of our customers in partnership and with a lot of empathy.

You bring that with you

  • First things first: You have completed vocational training and have successfully received the VBV\ certification. If you also have a further education such as dipl. Financial advisor/in IAF? All the better!
  • You bring at least three years of experience in preventive counseling and pursue your sales goals with great passion.
  • You realize that you like to be among people – you can put yourself well into your counterpart, recognize his needs and respond to them individually in your advice.
  • You express yourself very well in German (verbal and written). Maybe you even know other languages?
  • You have a large relationship network, maintain it sustainably and preferably live in the region.
  • For consultations outside the agency and even outside the official contact times, you are flexible and mobile.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
