[Transports Publics Neuchâtelois SA]( )

The Neuchâtelois Public Transports SA (transN) are the result of successive mergers by companies in the canton of Neuchâtel. Specializing in the operation of a public rail and road transport network, both urban and regional, we transport millions of passengers per year on 38 lines and 293 km of network.

We are nearly 600 professional\-le\-s who work daily as a team to provide quality service to satisfy customers. We place our innovation capacity at the service of regional development and are motivated by the ambition to be the main player in mobility in the canton of Neuchâtel by proposing a reliable and efficient mobility chain between the different modes of public and individual transport.

To complete our workforce, we are looking for a

Rail maintenance technician

Your main missions

Within our maintenance centre, you ensure the maintenance, troubleshooting and repair of rail vehicles to ensure their safety, reliability and availability.

Your responsibilities

  • Conduct preventive and curative maintenance of vehicles
  • Diagnosing faults and proposing durable solutions
  • Administrative monitoring of interventions and guarantees
  • Ensure compliance with safety, quality and environmental standards
  • Participate in continuous process improvement (Lean, 5S)
  • Contribute to the Department's technical projects
  • Intervening on different sites as needed
  • Working closely with technical and operational teams
  • Participate in audits and report non-compliance

Your profile

  • CFCs in a technical field (mechanical, electronic, automatic, mechatronic or equivalent)
  • Good knowledge of mechanics and hydraulics
  • Comfortable with computer and diagnostic tools
  • Accountability and strong security orientation
  • Spirit of analysis and ability to solve problems
  • Flexible, autonomous and equipped with a good team spirit
  • Driving licence category B80 VE and an asset

We offer

  • A stimulating and constantly changing technical environment
  • Attractive working conditions
  • The possibility of actively contributing to the safety and performance of rail transport
  • Development and continuing training opportunities

Are you ready to meet this challenge? The Department of Human Resources is looking forward to your complete file.
