Full description

Economic employee (40-50%) at the Legal Affairs Section of the Department for Industry, Trade and Labour, in customs

Their tasks

  • They process the dossiers of the unemployment insurance in German, for which the section is responsible, namely the transfers for the decision of the clarification of the ability to placement, the pre-registrations of short-time work and the notifications of weather-related work loss as well as the request for remission You advise the employees of the regional employment centres as well as employers and job seekers in all matters of unemployment insurance You work together with the various partners (unemployment funds, etc.) They support the French-speaking economic employee of the Unterwallis and the other employees of the section if necessary

Your profile

  • You are in possession of a completed education at tertiary level (Federal Diploma, HF, FH, University, etc.) with some years of professional experience
  • You have an overview of the economy of our canton and are very interested in legal issues as well as those of social security
  • They have good analytical skills and are able to make decisions Your communication skills are an important advantage in answering the concerns of our clients They have good stress resistance
  • You have good editorial skills You are responsible and able to work in a team or independently You can cope with the common IT tools native German with good knowledge of the second official language
