Business Customer Team Manager – Bas-Valais

  • Martigny
  • 80-100%

Do you have a solid experience as a business customer advisor with a managerial fiber? If you are looking for an opportunity to value your skills and support local entrepreneurs, this position is your chance.

Specifically?

  • The aim will be to drive, coach and support a team of business clients' advisors and collaborators.
  • You will develop and maintain relationships of trust and proximity with customers.
  • You will accompany customers and analyze their requests for financing for commercial, industrial, artisanal or performance buildings.
  • You will be constantly training on changes to the guidelines and will develop your knowledge of the services and products available to this type of customer.
  • You will participate in the Bank's demonstrations.

The challenge is tempting you? This post is for you if...

  • You have superior business training with excellent relational skills.
  • You benefit from several years of activity in a similar position with the corporate customer sector, especially in the area of corporate financing.
  • You have experience in team management.
  • Dynamic and pragmatic, you benefit from a strong sense of initiative and appreciate teamwork.
  • Mastering computer tools, you have strong analytical capabilities.
  • You are able to manage your priorities and work independently to ensure proactive customer monitoring.
  • Your language skills would be an asset.

Let's meet! Would you like to join us? We look forward to discovering your application. To do this, send your complete file to Marisa Berclaz, our HR Business Partner, at: recruitment@bcvs.ch no later than September 11, 2026.
