Every year, thousands of travellers rely on us to take them to their destinations, daily as well as at major events, events or construction sites. To meet this challenge, we are looking for someone who can anticipate, coordinate and optimize our resources to ensure the smooth and efficient operation of our road network.

Responsible for Event Management and Highway Fleet (M/F)

Your mission

At the heart of the operation, you ensure the management of our fleet of vehicles and coordinate planned events impacting the transportation offer. A true conductor, you link operating teams, planning, garage, authorities and organizers to ensure reliable, safe and efficient solutions.

What awaits you

*Road Fleet Management*

  • Optimize vehicle availability and support daily production.
  • Plan and coordinate the integration of new vehicles.
  • Collaborate with sector managers, operating office, CGT and garage.
  • Participate in annual vehicle resource planning and fleet renewal.
  • Monitor fleet management data and tools.

*Management of Operations Events and Projects*

  • Organize transport devices related to events, substitutions and special events.
  • Defining the vehicle and personnel requirements necessary to achieve them.
  • Participate in sessions with organizers, municipalities and authorities.
  • Ensure operational feasibility, regulatory compliance and quality of information disseminated to travellers.
  • Develop and coordinate the operational directions necessary for the success of events.

Your profile

  • Vocational training completed.
  • Experience in transport, ideally as a professional driver, regulator or operator.
  • Strong organizational skills and ability to manage multiple files simultaneously.
  • Aisance with computer tools.
  • A sense of responsibility, analysis and ability to work with many stakeholders.
  • Category D licence and fleet management training, an asset.

What we offer you

  • A key position in a changing public transport company.
  • A varied activity combining planning, coordination and operational management.
  • Visible and concrete projects with a direct impact on the mobility of the region.
  • A committed team and passionate interlocutors.
  • The opportunity to actively contribute to the quality of service offered to our travellers.

Why join us?

At TMR, we transport much more than passengers: we connect people and territories with reliability, security and proximity. We value engagement, collaboration and innovation, in a dynamic environment where each employee contributes to our daily mission.

You will evolve in a flexible and user-friendly working environment with passionate teams and strong values: proximity, team spirit and social responsibility. Being part of TMR is part of an organization that places the human at the centre of its choices and gives you the means to grow and innovate in your role.
