Your assignment

  • Working in new construction and renovation sites
  • Draw the signs on the wood and cut the various elements (supporting structure, beams, fixtures, etc.)
  • Working wood pieces: sawing, mortar, planing, cutting, drilling, etc.
  • Treat, impregnate and paint wood to protect it from parasites, moisture and atmospheric agents
  • Mounting, assembling and adjusting the elements of the building’s bearing structure and bone according to the established scheme
  • Place wall and ceiling coverings, stairs, balconies, window fittings, insulating panels, beams etc.
  • Apply protective layers, lay thermal and acoustic insulations
  • To restore and maintain the supporting structure and wood elements during renovation work
