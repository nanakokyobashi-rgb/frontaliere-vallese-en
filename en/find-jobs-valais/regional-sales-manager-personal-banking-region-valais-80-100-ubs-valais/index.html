Full description

Key responsibilities Are you passionate about sales? Do you understand the needs of our Affluent and Retail client base and want to help our team develop and implement optimal sales opportunities? Do you want to contribute to ensuring our clients experience an exceptional sales journey?

Then you are the person we are looking for for the position of Regional Sales Manager PB for the Valais region. Your tasks:

  • launching sales campaigns: you implement our centralized PB campaigns in your region and ensure they are a success
  • seizing opportunities: you identify regional sales opportunities, develop intelligent measures to realize them, and accompany their success
  • supporting and coaching: you act as a sparring partner for our client advisors and the regional management team, provide them with valuable feedback, and help them give their best
  • working with figures: you analyze successes in your region and implement targeted improvements

Your Career Comeback We are open to applications from people returning to the workforce. Here is more information about our program: ubs.com/careercomeback.

The team You work in close collaboration with the Regional Head PB and have access to all branches, front-office employees, and regional reporting. Your workplace is located within the region. You report directly to the Head Sales Development PB (central) and thus have a direct link to the Personal Banking management. By regularly exchanging best practices with other regional sales managers, you drive success forward in your region and beyond – teamwork is important to us.

Your skills and experience

  • University degree, UAS (HES), or equivalent education, supplemented by several years of experience in the banking sector, ideally in client advisory or front-office roles.
  • Strong results orientation, a developed sense of initiative, and a genuine passion for sales.
  • Excellent organizational, analytical, and problem-solving skills, with both a creative and pragmatic approach.
  • Natural leadership with a zest for change, motivation, and team coaching.
  • Marked interest in local markets and availability to travel regularly within the region.
  • Excellent communication skills in German and English.
