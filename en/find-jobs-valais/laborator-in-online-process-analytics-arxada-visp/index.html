Full description

Arxada is a global specialty chemicals company dedicated to solving the world’s most challenging conservation challenges through better science. With a proud history of innovation dating back more than a century, we aim to help our customers develop more sustainable solutions that protect and preserve people’s health and well-being and extend the life of vital infrastructures as we work to reduce our environmental footprint and that of our customers.

Sustainability is one of our top priorities. We make significant strategic investments that not only reduce our own environmental footprint, but also help our customers adapt to long-term environmental and social change by developing cleaner and greener solutions.

Arxada in Visp is looking for a laboratory on-line process analysis. In this role, you will take care of the assembly, maintenance and adjustment of modern process analysis equipment. You ensure that the systems function properly at all times, thus supporting the safety of the plants, the control of emissions and a smooth, automated operation.

Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Ensuring optimal availability of process analysis equipment through safe and proper installation, commissioning and maintenance
  • Maintenance of process analysis equipment as an essential part of process safety, emission monitoring and controlled automated plant operation
  • Timely and cost-efficient planning and implementation of projects
  • Responsibility for precise safety and quality relevant PA equipment

Qualifications and experience

  • Completed training as laboratory technician, electronics engineer, mechatronics engineer or chemical engineer
  • Independent work and analytical thinking are essential for the maintenance activities in the field
  • Teamwork, responsibility and decision-making
  • Flowing German and good English skills
  • Experience in process analysis desirable
  • Experience in the chemical industry is an advantage
  • Experience in the maintenance of technical equipment is a plus
