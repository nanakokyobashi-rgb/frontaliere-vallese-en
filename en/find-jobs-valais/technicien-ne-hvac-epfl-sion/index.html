Full description

Mission The EPFL Valais Wallis campus, in Sion, currently has 17 active laboratories in the key areas of energy, environment and health. The campus is booming. In order to ensure the proper functioning of the site's buildings and, by extension, the success of our scientific research, we are looking for a versatile technician capable of ensuring the operation and monitoring of our technical installations. This involves a range of tasks ranging from daily maintenance of the installations to the coordination of numerous external stakeholders, while ensuring rigorous administrative management. If the main mission focuses on the proper functioning of HVAC installations, versatility in tasks is expected. The technician will be required to collaborate proactively within the operations team. Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Ensure the operation and monitoring of the HVAC technical installations of the various buildings on the EPFL Valais Wallis campus (via an MCR and in person);
  • Ensure interface with internal users, process their stewardship requests and guarantee a good level of service;
  • Ensure preventive and curative maintenance of HVAC installations on campus;
  • Diagnose breakdowns, propose solutions and guarantee rapid return to service of installations (be flexible for possible emergency interventions);
  • Plan, coordinate and supervise external stakeholders, including quote requests, monitoring and reception of work;
  • Carry out operation and maintenance work of routine complexity and support internal teams during major work;
  • Ensure administrative management of interventions, write clear and usable intervention reports and guarantee rigorous monitoring via a CMMS;
  • Update and guarantee the conformity of technical files (plans, diagrams, nomenclatures, documentation);
  • Be a force for proposals on energy optimization options and/or HVAC improvements;
  • Apply and enforce safety rules, particularly those related to laboratory activities;
  • Contribute to internal processes and support management in technical projects, including the development of the infrastructure Masterplan. Profile
  • CFC Constructor of ventilation installations / Heating installer / Sanitary installer / title deemed equivalent;
  • Minimum 3 years of experience in HVAC operation;
  • Increased knowledge of the general operation of a building;
  • Solution-oriented and good ability to work in a team in a demanding environment;
  • Good computer skills, in particular mastery of basic office tools;
  • Strength of proposals and good ability to handle priorities;
  • Resistance to stress and ability to manage several demands in parallel;
  • Good oral and written communication in French, English an asset;
  • Mastery of regulation/automation tools, an asset;
  • Knowledge of a CMMS, an asset;
  • Experience in a scientific/academic/laboratory environment, an asset;
  • Being a holder of ITTO article 13, an asset (training possible). We offer
  • A dynamic and multicultural environment in a globally recognized institution;
  • Demanding missions in terms of multidisciplinarity, but always exciting;
  • Attractive working conditions promoting balance, development and professional commitment;
  • An opportunity to contribute to the development of a key institution for Valais and the science of tomorrow. Information Start date: to be agreed Occupancy rate: 80% - 100% Contract type: CDI Reference: 2223
