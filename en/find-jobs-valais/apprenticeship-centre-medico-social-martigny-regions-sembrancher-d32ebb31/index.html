You wish to give meaning to your future professional practice by accompanying people in their daily lives, with respect and benevolence. By developing your listening and your skills, you will learn to offer appropriate care, both preventive and curative, and support, while helping to preserve the autonomy of the accompanied.

If these values exist and you wish to train in a useful and humane profession, this opportunity is for you.

For our Entremont and Martigny sites, we are looking for

Apprenticeship

Your objective:

Learn how to become an actor of well-being by actively contributing to improving the quality of life of our valued customers.

To be trained in the implementation of all the care provided for in the ASSC training order, through support at the home of our clients by competent, experienced and motivated professionals.

Discover multidisciplinary team work and play an essential role in keeping our clients at home.

What we are looking for:

  • Completion of compulsory education;
  • One or more years of secondary education is an asset;
  • Interest in the care and accompaniment of others;
  • Personal maturity and sense of responsibility;
  • Ability to listen, empathy and understanding of others;
  • Motivation to learn a profession involving both independent and multidisciplinary work;
  • Adaptability and thinking;
  • A sense of discretion and respect for confidentiality;
  • Aisance with computer tools.

What we offer

  • A varied and meaningful activity in a changing sector;
  • A dynamic and collaborative working environment;
  • Mobile and modern work tools;
  • A working environment conducive to reconciliation between private and professional life;
  • Personalized coaching by competent and motivated professionals;
  • A progressively autonomous activity, linked to validated capabilities and competencies;
  • Attractive employee benefits;
  • Conditions in accordance with CMS Staff Rules.
