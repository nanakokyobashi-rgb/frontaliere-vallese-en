Job description – cook / cook EFZ (m/f/d)

Employer: Restaurant Moosalp, 3923 Törbel (VS) Location: Moosalp, 2’048 m above sea level. Type of employment: temporary seasonal post, 100 % Period: Winter season 2026/27, to 22. March 2027 Job: By appointment

About us

The restaurant Moosalp is a well-known mountain restaurant in Upper Valais, appreciated for its high-quality, bourgeois and seasonal cuisine. Our company was awarded as the best mountain restaurant at Blick-Check. We are a family-run company in the 3rd generation.

For the coming winter season we are looking for a qualified, motivated and self-employed cook to strengthen our kitchen team.

Their tasks

  • Independent preparation and attractive preparation of à la carte dishes
  • Organisation and responsibility for the assigned kitchen post
  • Preparation and implementation of the daily Mise en Place
  • Ensuring a smooth operation during lunch and evening service
  • Compliance with hygiene and quality standards according to HACCP
  • Control of the quality of goods and proper storage of foodstuffs
  • Support of the kitchen team in all work

Your profile

  • Completed vocational training as chef/chef EFZ or equivalent training
  • Several years of professional experience are beneficial
  • Independent, structured and efficient working method
  • Resilience and overview even in the case of high numbers of guests
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Teamwork, reliability and responsibility
  • Enjoying a fresh, high-quality kitchen

We offer

  • An attractive workplace in a unique mountain landscape
  • A motivated and experienced kitchen team
  • Varied tasks and independent work
  • Current employment conditions according to L-GAV
  • Staff accommodation subject to availability
  • Fair remuneration according to qualification and experience

Application

We look forward to applications from qualified and dedicated chefs who would like to support our team during the winter season.
