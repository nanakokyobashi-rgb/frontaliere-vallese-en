Full description

Drinking and bathing water inspector (80%) at the Office for Consumer Protection and Veterinary Affairs, in Sitten We are the department dedicated to food safety, animal health, safe handling of chemicals and bathing water quality. We work on these complex areas with a dynamic, networked and open-minded team. We offer flexible working conditions, far-reaching responsibility and versatile and thorough training opportunities.

Their tasks

  • Planning and implementation of food controls/inspections of public drinking water supplies and public bath and shower facilities
  • Verification and assessment of facilities, workflows and quality assurance (including HACCP systems) Planning and conducting on-site sampling and measurements Assessment of chemical-physical and microbiological water analysis Writing inspection and investigation reports They create orders and establish measures to correct deficiencies
  • Assessment of construction dossiers and drinking water regulations Information provision to water suppliers, authorities and consumers in terms of drinking, bathing and shower water project management in the area of drinking and bathing water

Your profile

  • They have completed university studies in food technology (life technologies) chemistry, biochemistry or biology or have completed vocational apprenticeship with further education or training. Experience in the field of drinking or bathing water You have the federal diploma for official food control or are ready to acquire it You already have several years of experience in the drinking water and/or bathing water sector and have a strong communication ability You have assertiveness and organizational skills You are skilled in oral and written expression and have a safe appearance You are physically fit and have a valid category B driving licence You have very good IT skills
  • Mother tongue German with very good knowledge of the second official language
