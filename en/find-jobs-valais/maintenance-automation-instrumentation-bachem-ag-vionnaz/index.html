Main responsibilities

  • Your Responsibilities
  • Ensure assembly, commissioning, preventive and corrective maintenance of electrical equipment, instrumentation and automation.
  • Diagnosis and resolve breakdowns to ensure continuity of our operations.
  • Participate in improving the reliability of installations and propose innovative solutions to optimize their performance.
  • Identify the problems of obsolescence and contribute to the definition of the technical standards of tomorrow.
  • Ensure compliance with GMP requirements, CAPA and Change Control processes.
  • Perform periodic inspections and calibration activities of instrumentation.

  • Good knowledge of Siemens S7 controllers and industrial communication protocols.
