Full description

Mission The EPFL Valais Wallis Campus in Sion currently has 17 laboratories active in the key fields of energy, environment and health. The campus is in the middle of an expension. In order to ensure the proper functioning of the buildings of the site and, by extension, the success of our scientific research, we are looking for a versatile technician capable of operating and monitoring our technical installations. This involves a range of tasks ranging from day-to-day maintenance of facilities to the coordination of many external stakeholders, while ensuring rigorous administrative management. If the main mission focuses on the proper functioning of electrical installations, a versatility in tasks is expected. The technician will collaborate proactively within the operating team. Main tasks and responsibilities

  • Conduct preventive and curative maintenance of electrical installations in buildings;
  • Ensure legal compliance of electrical works according to ITTO standards;
  • Establish ITTO measurement reports following electrical works;
  • Provide troubleshooting of electrical installations;
  • Carry out small electrical installations;
  • Provide information in the GMAO;
  • Conduct work related to electrical machinery installations, peripheral equipment;
  • Monitoring of external undertakings for electrical works, including administrative monitoring of interventions;
  • Follow-up of various technical projects. Profile
  • Electrician installer CFC or equivalent recognized by ESTI;
  • Article 13 ITTO;
  • Knowledge of ITBL 2020 or 2025;
  • Minimum 5 years of experience;
  • Increased knowledge of the general operation of a building;
  • Oriented solutions and good ability to work as a team in a demanding environment;
  • Good computer skills, in particular basic office tools;
  • Strength of proposals and good capacity to address priorities;
  • Stress resistance and ability to manage multiple requests in parallel;
  • Knowledge of CVCS technical facilities, an asset;
  • Good oral and written communication in French, English an asset;
  • Mastering regulatory/automation tools, an asset;
  • Knowledge of a GMAO, an asset;
  • Experience in a scientific/academic/laboratory environment, an asset. We offer
  • A dynamic and multicultural environment in a globally recognized institution;
  • Expecting missions in terms of multidisciplinarity, but always exciting;
  • Attractive working conditions conducive to balance, development and professional commitment;
  • An opportunity to contribute to the development of a key institution for Valais and tomorrow's science. Information Start date: To be agreed Min occupancy rate: 80% Max occupancy rate: 100% Type of contract: CDI Reference: 2316 ​
