R\&D Engineer \- Technical Investigation \& Failure Analysis

Are you an expert in technical investigation and a passionate problem solver? As R\&D Technical Investigation \&Failure Analysis Engineer, you will be the reference expert in R&D to transform technical uncertainty into lasting solutions.

Your mission will be to identify the root cause (Root Cause) of complex, recurring or novel problems in production or field, for which no trivial solution exists. You will bridge directly between the real context of use in our customers and R&D engineering.

Your Principal Responsibilities

Technical Investigation, Diagnostic / Resolution

  • Failure Analysis: Conduct in-depth analysis of complex failures (system, hardware, software or processes).
  • Data analysis \& exchanges: Work closely with technicians and suppliers to analyze fault history and exploit uplift data (logs, telemetry, returned parts).
  • Reproduction \& Simulation: Formulate technical assumptions and design test benches, test protocols or simulations to replicate R&D environmental problems.
  • Prototyping \& Validation: Isolate critical variables, technically validate diagnosis and propose proof of concept (PoC) or emergency bypass solutions.
  • Specifications \& Follow-up: Define the technical specifications of the final solution to transmit them to R&D development teams or the Product Improvement team, while following field tests and corrective actions.

Capitalization \& Knowledge Transfer

  • Documentation: Carefully record each investigation in R&D technical reports.
  • Industrial Transfer: Transfer knowledge to the Product Improvement team as soon as a problem is documented for its industrial resolution.
  • Upgrading skills: Enrich the company's diagnostic methodologies (advanced troubleshooting guides, check\-lists) to equip field technicians.

Search profile

  • Training \& Experience: Degree in R&D Engineer, Mechatronics, Complex Systems, Mechanical/Electronic Engineering or equivalent.
  • Technical Competencies: Demonstrated ability to conduct systematic investigations, design test benches, analyse logs/telemetry and define R&D specifications.
  • Analytical Spirit \& Rigor: Ability to solve novel problems, rigor in technical writing and root cause research (Root Cause Analysis).
  • Communication \& Collaboration: Relationships to exchange with diverse profiles (field technicians, suppliers, R&D development teams)

Who we are:

We have become a leader in the world of professional automatic coffee machine by developing innovative solutions in the methods of extraction and quality control of coffee products.

Our vision: to bridge the gap between traditional Italian coffee machine by the speed and electronic intelligence of the super\-automatic machine.

We provide products and services that bring real added value to our customers; a unique sensory experience in cups, consistency, real-time connectivity, high productivity and ease of maintenance.

We export 97% of our production and are present in more than 50 countries.

Do you want to have a concrete impact in a committed team? Apply, we will be happy to exchange with you.
