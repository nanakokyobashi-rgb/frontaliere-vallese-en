IT Project Manager & Business Analyst

  • Zion
  • 80-100%

A true actor-actor of change, if you excel in understanding customer needs and implementing effective solutions, this challenge is for you! Join our dynamic IT team, the bearer of a wind of innovation and the architect of our bank BCVS En Mouvement.

In concrete terms?

  • In active collaboration with your colleagues, you will understand, analyse and define the needs of tomorrow.
  • In compliance with standards, you will develop and carry out major cross-functional projects that meet needs and challenges.
  • Solution-oriented, you will identify potential for improvement and propose new systems (applications, processes, interfaces, automations, etc.).
  • You will be an agent of change and will support colleagues in the digital transition.

Tempted by the challenge? This position is for you if...

  • You have a higher education (EPFL, HEC, HES, or equivalent).
  • You have relevant experience in the complete lifecycle management of complex projects – an affinity with the financial world (ideally Finnova) would be an asset.
  • An open person, with interpersonal skills, you know how to bring a wind of improvement that is beneficial to all.
  • With a sharp mind, you are dynamic and provide innovative and effective solutions.
  • Finally, your strong analytical skills and your result orientation allow you to have a positive impact on your environment.

Let's meet! Would you like to join us? We look forward to hearing from you. To do so, send your complete application to Marisa Berclaz, our HR Business Partner, at the address: recrutement@WKB.ch to 23 October 2026 at the latest.
