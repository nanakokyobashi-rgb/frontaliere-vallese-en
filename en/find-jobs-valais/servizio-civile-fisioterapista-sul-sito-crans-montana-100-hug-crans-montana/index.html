Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 trades, the University Hospitals of Geneva (HUG) is a national and international reference institution. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . Crans-Montana Clinic, nestled in the heart of the Valais Alps, is a hospital site of the HUG. Its hospital duties consist of caring for patients for rehabilitations in general and psychosomatic internal medicine, multi-purpose post-operative or post-hospital rehabilitations in acute settings.

Description of post

Crans Montana Clinic offers a physiotherapist assignment to perform your civilian service. During this assignment, you perform patient support missions on several units of the clinic. You guide patients in the clinic and co-host gymnastics or FTE groups. You collaborate with physiotherapists for the administration of passive therapies and also perform administrative tasks. You help store rooms following physiotherapy sessions. This assignment takes place in a hospital setting within multidisciplinary teams.

Qualifications

Your admission to civil service is recognized and validated by the Federal Civil Service Office (CIVI). You are a reliable, responsible and discreet person, with a real sense of service. Interested or interested in the social, educational, administrative or medico-social field, you are comfortable working in a multidisciplinary team and exercise with autonomy. You are recognized or recognized for your communication skills. Of a curious and motivated nature, you want to evolve in a stimulating professional setting. You are involved in missions that are useful to the community and the proper functioning of the HUGs. In this context, you benefit from careful supervision and conditions that comply with civil service. We offer you a formative and rewarding experience in a large public institution.
