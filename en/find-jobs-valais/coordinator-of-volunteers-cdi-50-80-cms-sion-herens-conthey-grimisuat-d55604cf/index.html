Volunteer Coordinator (M/F) CDI 50\-80%

The CMS Sion\-Hérens\-Conthey offers medico-social benefits, assistance and social and vocational integration which meet the expectations and needs of the population.

Its mission is to foster home maintenance, autonomy and social integration by providing individualised support in each situation and by offering a full range of services.

With its 5 sites and close to 560 collaborators, the CMS Sion\-Hérens\-Conthey accompanies more than 7'000 beneficiaries on all 16 communes in the region.

As part of the organization and development of its meals and transportation services that promote home care, CMS\-SHC recruits an\-e:

In a dynamic team spread over several sites in the districts of Sion, Herens and Conthey, you contribute to the smooth functioning of community services, in collaboration with beneficiaries, their relatives, volunteers, and other partners.

Your Mission

  • Plan and supervise home meal delivery and health transportation, in coordination with the care service, ensuring their quality and functioning.
  • Organize and animate the network of volunteers: recruitment, reception, support, loyalty and valorization of their commitment.
  • Ensure liaison with beneficiaries, their loved ones and partners, assessing needs and ensuring the satisfaction of accompanied persons.
  • Ensure administrative monitoring of services, in collaboration with the site's administrative staff.
  • Participate in the coordination and evolution of service activities, proposing improvements and contributing to the development of home care projects.

Your profile

  • HES training in the social sociocultural field, or training deemed equivalent,
  • Experience in volunteer facilitation and coordination or staff supervision,
  • Excellent relational skills, listening skills and ability to build trust,
  • Organizational sense, autonomy and ability to manage multiple activities simultaneously,
  • Team spirit, flexibility and ease in collaboration with different interlocutors,
  • Good control of the usual computer tools,
  • Sensitivity to home retention issues and strong interest in volunteer engagement.

We offer you

  • A varied, meaningful activity, and in contact with many local actors,
  • The possibility of making a concrete contribution to the well-being and autonomy of people at home,
  • A collaborative working environment,
  • A function offering autonomy and responsibility,
  • Continuing training supported by the institution,
  • Attractive LPP plan
  • 6 weeks of holiday from 40 years,
  • Deferred kms,
  • Development prospects,
  • Collaborating advantages with local businesses,
  • Health welfare offers.

Wage

Depending on the salary scale of the CLC SLD 2026, your remuneration will be 17\. Annuity will be determined based on your work experience and relevance to the proposed position.

Working conditions

Type of contract: CDI

Activity rate: 50 to 80%, flexible choice according to your interests, profile and organization of the service

Entry into office: February 2027 or to be agreed

Place of work: Site du Coteau, Grimisuat (principal)
