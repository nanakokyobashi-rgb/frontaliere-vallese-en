Primary Teaching Assistant — Verbier International School SA

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Verbier International School is seeking an enthusiastic and dedicated Primary Teaching Assistant to join our Primary team for the Winter Term 2027, from January through to the end of March.

VIS is a growing international day and boarding school located in the heart of the Swiss Alps. Our approach, Education with Altitude, combines ambitious learning with strong pastoral care, outdoor education and the unique opportunities that come from living and learning in Verbier.

The successful candidate will work closely with class teachers to support the learning, development and wellbeing of pupils across the Primary School. We are looking for someone who is positive, creative and flexible, with the initiative to contribute fully to classroom life and the wider school community.

The role will include supporting individual pupils and small groups, assisting with classroom activities and learning, supervising pupils during the school day and helping to create a warm, purposeful and inclusive learning environment.

Experience working with primary aged children is required. Knowledge of French or another European language would be an advantage.

Applicants should

  • Have experience working with primary aged children
  • Be enthusiastic, caring and highly dependable
  • Be confident supporting pupils with a range of individual learning needs
  • Work effectively as part of a collaborative international team
  • Bring energy, flexibility and initiative to a busy school environment
  • Be willing to contribute to the wider life of the school
  • Hold an EU passport or already have the right to work in Switzerland
  • This is an excellent opportunity to spend the winter working in an ambitious and supportive international school community in one of Europe’s most distinctive mountain environments.

Verbier International School is committed to safeguarding and promoting the welfare of children and young people. Any offer of employment will be subject to recruitment checks including right to work in Switzerland, child protection screening, reference checks and an enhanced police check.

Start date: January 2027 Contract: Temporary, full-time, January to March 2027

Applications should be submitted through the TES platform.
