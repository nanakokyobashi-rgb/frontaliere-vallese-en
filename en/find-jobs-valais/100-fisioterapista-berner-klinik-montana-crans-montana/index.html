Full description

Our rehabilitation clinic located in Crans-Montana, in the idyllic setting of the Valais Alps, is looking for a 100% physiotherapist

Your missions

  • Treatment of patients in various fields (Musculoskeletal, Neurology, Internal Medicine, Psychosomatic)
  • Individual and group therapies
  • Participation in working groups

Your profile

  • You are a graduate physiotherapist with CR recognition or positive precheck
  • You are native French or German with very good knowledge of the other language (level B2 desired)
  • Your practice is part of an EBP approach
  • You want to participate in the development of patient care processes
  • You are a motivated, autonomous and flexible person with a good team spirit
  • You have extensive knowledge and professional experience in the treatment of patients in the fields of orthopaedics, internal medicine, neurology or psychosomatics
  • Specialisation or specific knowledge in an area of our activity would be a plus
  • You appreciate the interdisciplinary work
  • You are also comfortable with IT tools

Our benefits

  • A professional activity within a motivated, committed and dynamic team
  • Beneficial contractual and social conditions
  • Opportunities for internal and external training
  • Accommodation and on-site dining opportunities
