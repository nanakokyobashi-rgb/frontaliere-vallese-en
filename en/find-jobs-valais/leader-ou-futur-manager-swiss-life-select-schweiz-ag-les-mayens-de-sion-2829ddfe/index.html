Full description

Want to build and lead your own team? Some people have a particular talent: they motivate, transmit and grow others. If this is your case, Swiss Life Select offers you a real development path. Your journey

  • You learn the occupation of financial adviser, with full training up to the IAF degree.
  • You prove yourself in the field by accompanying your own customers.
  • You are moving towards management: you recruit, train and accompany new advisors.

What we offer

  • Accompaniment by experienced managers, from the first day until you take responsibility
  • Compensation related to your results and, ultimately, those of your team
  • A great autonomy in the organisation and development of your structure
  • A demanding and supportive community of entrepreneurs

Your profile

  • Experience in coaching, coaching, sport, sales or association: no matter the field, as long as you know how to unite
  • Ambition, sense of responsibility and long-term taste
  • 23 years minimum, domicile in Switzerland, completed training (CFC, Maturity, Bachelor or Master)
