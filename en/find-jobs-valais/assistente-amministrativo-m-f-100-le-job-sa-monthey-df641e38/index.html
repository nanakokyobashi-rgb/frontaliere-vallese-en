Administrative Assistant (M/F) – 100%

Entry into office: January 2027 or to be agreed

Your profile

  • Holder of an SWC of a business employee or a training deemed equivalent.
  • Experience in staff planning is an asset.
  • Excellent mastery of computer tools including Windows, Microsoft 365, Word and Excel.
  • Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written. Knowledge of English or German is an advantage.
  • People who are organized, methodical, autonomous and able to manage priorities.
  • Good stress resistance and ability to manage unforeseen events.
  • Team spirit and excellent communication skills.
  • Ability to coordinate and frame assembly teams.

  • Ensure coordination and collaboration with the various departments, including after-sales service (SAV), repair and construction.

We offer you

  • A varied position within a company in the Chablais region.
  • A fixed commitment of 100%.
