Full description

Who you are

Do you like to help others and find simple and pragmatic solutions to everyday problems?

Do you feel as comfortable with a screwdriver as with our customers?

Then you could be the perfect person to join our team.

At IKEA, we have a mission: to improve the daily lives of the largest number.

To achieve this, we need close colleagues, comfortable with practical work and close to our clients – like you.

What you bring

-You have a strong sense of service, an open and professional attitude, and a good understanding of the real needs of our clients.

-You can listen actively, answer questions with pleasure and find solutions adapted to each situation, even when the rhythm accelerates.

Unexpected things don't destabilize you. You're looking for solutions rather than excuses.

  • - You work independently and are able to make decisions on your own.
  • -You communicate fluently in German and ideally also in English.
  • -You enjoy human contact and invest every day to offer our customers an excellent shopping experience.
  • - You're as comfortable with IT as with tools. Putting up and disposing of furniture doesn't scare you.
  • - You've been a Class B driver's license holder for at least a year. .and a blank criminal record.

-The mastery of French is essential for this position, both oral and written. Good knowledge of German is a real asset and will allow you to evolve with ease in our multilingual working environment and to offer quality service to all our customers.

Your responsibilities

You advise and accompany our clientele, while always welcoming with a smile.

At the entrance of the store: you welcome customers so that they feel welcome and well informed upon arrival.

In Småland, you care for the well-being and safety of children aged 3 to 10. At the return counter: you find quick, simple and customer-oriented solutions, in accordance with our internal guidelines.

In the box: every day you show that an excellent service can be both quick and warm. You take leave of our clients so that they want to come back, always with a smile.

You advise customers and monitor their specific requests in our computer system.

Maintenance interventions and after-sales service: you perform urgent interventions directly at the home of our customers (delivery, assembly, expertise) and guarantee a service of the highest quality.

Does that look like you?

If you recognize yourself in this description and want to help improve the daily lives of our clients, then join our team.

We look forward to receiving your application!
