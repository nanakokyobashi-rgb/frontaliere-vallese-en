Full description

A coordinator or coordinator for the protection of the population (replacing or replacing Chief EM OCC, 80-100%) with the Civil and Military Security Service, Cantonal Office for the Protection of the Population, in Sion At the heart of crisis management, you contribute to the preparation and coordination of the protection of the population in Valais.

Your tasks

  • You are the replacement of the Chief of Staff of the Cantonal Driving Body (CCO) You drive the administrative processes of the OCC You guarantee the coordination and updating of the driving documentation of the OCC
  • You coordinate the public protection partners in the preparation of the measures for the benefit of the CCO You ensure the availability and operation of the infrastructure and systems necessary for the conduct of the CCO You train the members of the CCO and its partners, design and organize exercises to strengthen their operational readiness You conduct projects or working groups in the field of public protection You participate in the picket service for the benefit of the CCO, according to the tournus established You are ready to work on irregular schedules (evening, weekend)

Your profile

  • Tertiary level training or training deemed equivalent Experience confirmed in one of the areas of protection of the population (police, army, firefighters, civil protection, health) Additional training in project management Solid experience in the fields of staff work, crisis management and adult training High flexibility, stress resistant, with a strong spirit of analysis and synthesis Initiative spirit and structured working method Writing skills Intergent and negotiation skills French or German mother tongue, with very good knowledge of the second official language

Entry into office

Immediately or to be agreed
