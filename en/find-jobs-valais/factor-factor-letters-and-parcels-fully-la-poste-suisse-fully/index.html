This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

Are you looking for a part-time job that keeps you fit? Thanks to your local knowledge, you distribute parcels and letters quickly and make our customers happy with the punctuality of the distribution. With you, dreams are coming!

Make a difference

  • Early in the morning (around 6 a.m.), you load the distribution vehicles with your team and you prepare your tour.
  • Through your work, you mark the urban landscape of Fully and embody the values of reliability and quality.
  • You are responsible for the timely and irreproachable distribution of parcels and letters in your area.
  • During your tour, you answer questions from our clients, and you contribute to their satisfaction.
  • You work on a schedule of five days a week and regularly perform services on Saturdays, distributed among the team.

What you bring

  • Thanks to your fitness, you are able to load and lift 30 kg packages regularly without any problems.
  • You like to spend time on the road, you have a category B driver's license and ideally A1, or you agree to pass it before you start working.
  • You appreciate teamwork, but you also like to move autonomously and you're comfortable with the big vehicles on the road.
  • You're reliable and working efficiently. Besides, you live within a radius of about 30 km and know our area well.
  • Good oral knowledge in French as well as good mastery of digital devices complete your profile.
