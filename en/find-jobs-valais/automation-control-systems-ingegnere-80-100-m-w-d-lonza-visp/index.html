Full description

If necessary, eligible candidates and their families will be assisted in moving.

Lonza is now a world leader in the Life Science business, which operates on three continents. We are active in science, but there is no magic formula for how we work. Our most important scientific solution is to create talented employees who work together and develop ideas that help other companies to help people. In return, our employees manage their careers independently. Their ideas, whether large or small, improve the world. And that's the kind of work we want to have part in.

To strengthen our engineering & maintenance team at the Visp site, we are looking for an electrical, measurement and control engineering (EMR) engineer. You are responsible for maintenance and for projects in mono or multi-product plants.

We'll give you this

  • A career and dynamic working culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Remuneration programmes that recognise performance
  • Besides a competitive salary, numerous salaried ones are waiting for you for lifestyle, family and leisure. All global services are available here: Bitly, bit.ly/3wjkoFi

Your tasks

  • Technical project management or cooperation with small projects and investment projects
  • Professional planning, preparation and coordination of EMR maintenance, including internal and external partners
  • Orders, capacity planning, maintenance of reporting in SAP and ensuring plant availability
  • Cooperation in the interdisciplinary engineering team, support of the management
  • Compliance with safety, quality, hygiene and environmental standards
  • Continuous optimization of plants, processes and maintenance processes, cooperation in risk analyses
  • Maintenance of the system documentation in SAP/COMOS, flow picture maintenance
  • Budget monitoring for projects, investments and maintenance

Your profile

  • Completed training Technician School (HF), Bachelor (FH) or Master (ETH) in electrical, automation or system technology
  • Experience in EMR maintenance/projects in industry advantage
  • Knowledge of SAP/COMOS/DeltaV/Modicon/Cim-Rhefy advantage, good office knowledge
  • German fluent, English advantage
  • Self-employed, flexible, able to team, structured and organized
  • Reliable, resilient, solution-oriented, open to new ideas and highly motivated

Lonza's products and services have a daily positive impact on millions of people. This is not only a great honour for us, but also a great responsibility. How we achieve our business results is just as important for us as our successes themselves. Lonza places great emphasis on respect and we protect our employees as well as our environment. Success for us means morally acceptable progress.

Lonza is where people come to meet challenges and develop new ideas for complex problems with their creativity in life science. Together, we contribute to improving the lives of many people around the world, offering them the certainty and satisfaction of making a real difference with their own work.
