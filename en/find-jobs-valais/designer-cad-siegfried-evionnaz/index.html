Locations Within the Maintenance & Engineering department, you are the guarantor of the quality, reliability and governance of the technical documentation of the industrial site.

You manage technical plans and P&IDs throughout their life cycle, pilot their update after installation changes and contribute to the digitization of documentary heritage. True CAD reference, you set site standards and ensure their application to ensure reliable documentation for the Engineering, Maintenance, Production and HSE teams.

Depending on the workload, you also bring your expertise on small investment projects related to infrastructure and utilities.

  • Ensure quality, consistency and updating of the technical documentation of the site (plans, P&ID, DWG and plans "as-'t').
  • Define and enforce CAD standards and document management rules (versioning, archiving, traceability).
  • Ensure consistency between plans, P&IDs, equipment and technical codification.
  • Manage migration of historical plans to DWG format and coordinate external providers if necessary.
  • Be the reference of technical numbering and maintain associated reference.
  • To participate in the deployment of CAD/BIM tools and contribute to the digitization of documentary heritage. - To provide documentary support to engineering projects and, as appropriate, to support small infrastructure projects (CAPEX/OPEX).

Profile sought

  • Technical training (CFC of draftsman, ES Technician or Engineer) with a specialization in CAD.
  • Experience confirmed (minimum 5 years) in a similar function in industrial environment, ideally in fine/pharmaceutical chemistry (GMP).
  • Master's degree in AutoCAD or equivalent CAD software (DWG).
  • Good understanding of P&IDs, industrial utilities and documentary management principles.
  • Knowledge of technical codification and small project management, an asset.
