We at Pemsa offer the perfect balance between the flexibility of temporary work and the safety of indefinite employment. Forty of 30 years of experience, we strongly support hundreds of Swiss companies specialized in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry, providing them with high-level talents. Our standards are high: we value motivation and enthusiasm, because they are the basis of excellence. If you want to join a dynamic and motivated team, the position of Carpentiere M/F/D – workshop and construction site awaits you to face exciting challenges.

Your assignment

  • Prepare the structure elements according to drawings and reliefs.
  • Draw the pieces and perform the different wood processing operations.
  • Prepare and assemble structures before their installation.
  • Perform assembly and adjustment of the elements directly in the yard.
  • Participate in the realization of bones, bearing structures, beams and other wooden structures.
  • Place insulation, coatings and complementary elements.
  • Intervention in transformation and renovation of existing buildings.
  • Ensure the quality of the finishes and compliance with safety standards.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Federal diploma of carpenters (CFC) or equivalent qualification recognised in Switzerland.
  • At least 2 years of professional experience.
  • Mastery of traditional instruments and techniques of carpentry.
  • Good ability to read projects and work with precision.
  • French level B2 minimum.
  • Driving licence B mandatory.
  • Residence at maximum 30 km from the site.
  • Versatile, autonomous and motivated person to teamwork.
