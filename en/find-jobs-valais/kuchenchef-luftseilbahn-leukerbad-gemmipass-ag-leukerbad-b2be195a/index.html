Full description

You manage the kitchen professionally and organizationally and shape the structure. You develop an offer that fits our daily rhythm, lead the kitchen team and define processes, standards and working methods that make the operation stable and reliable. Your handwriting is evident in processes, in quality and in a kitchen that is regionally anchored and modernly managed.

  • Experience in building, developing and leading a kitchen team
