Full description

Want to contribute to photovoltaic installations? This function covers the preparation of the site, the assembly and the AC and DC connections.

For one of our customers, we are looking for a photovoltaic assembler

Responsibilities

  • Prepare the construction site (flat or sloping roofs)
  • Receiving and preparing equipment
  • Mount structures and panels
  • Ensure the drawing of cables and connections of photovoltaic installations on the AC and DC sides according to plans and diagrams
  • Know how to make connections
  • Working in collaboration with different professionals
  • Ensure safety and regulatory compliance

Your profile

  • Experience in installing photovoltaic panels
  • Manual ability, practical sense developed and not subject to dizziness
  • Motivated and dynamic person
  • A sense of responsibility
  • Particularly sensitive to safety rules
  • Driving an asset
  • Organized person
  • Good presentation and comfortable in contacts

We offer

  • A long-term temporary position that can lead to fixed positions.

We support you in your professional development through ongoing training in relation to your profession, fully supported by the Temptraining Training Fund.
