Full description

Your missions

  • Develop and retain a portfolio of professional clients (transporters, construction companies, artisans, SMEs, municipalities)
  • Actively prospect new customers
  • Analyze your customers' needs and offer them tailored solutions: new and used vehicles, body and specific equipment, financing, leasing and service contracts
  • Set offers, negotiate and conclude sales related to carrossers and funding partners
  • Track orders until delivery and delivery of vehicles
  • Manage repeats and participate in the marketing of used vehicles
  • Work closely with the after-sales (workshop, spare parts) to ensure a global service to your customers
  • Representing the company at shows, events and vehicle presentations
  • Maintain CRM and ensure regular reporting of your activity

Your profile

  • Successful experience of a few years in the sale of commercial vehicles or heavy goods vehicles, or in the sale of technical equipment goods B2B
  • Good technical knowledge of vehicles and strong interest in the world of transport
  • Meaning of negotiation, results orientation and entrepreneurial spirit
  • Relationship building, ability to build lasting relationships with a demanding professional clientele
  • Autonomy, rigor and sense of organization
  • Mastery of common computer tools; experience of CRM tools is appreciated
  • French mother tongue; good knowledge of German is an asset
  • Driver's licence B

We offer you

  • A varied and autonomous position within a firm that is firmly established
  • Recognized brands and a full range of products
  • Attractive remuneration
  • Regular ongoing training of manufacturers

Interested? We look forward to receiving your complete dossier (motivation letter, CV, work certificates and diplomas) Only files corresponding to the profile sought will be processed.
