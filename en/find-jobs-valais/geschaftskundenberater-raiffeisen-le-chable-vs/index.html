13.10.2026 The Chable VS

Be part of our team!

Strongly rooted in the region and close to its customers, the Banque Raiffeisen Entremont is looking for a committed personality, with a strong sense of service and customer relationship, to join its team as customer advisor (m/f).

What are you waiting for?

  • Manage and actively develop a portfolio of corporate clients, in line with the bank's strategy
  • Analyze clients' needs and offer them adapted financial solutions
  • Evaluate and review real estate estimates related to financing
  • Develop relationship with existing customers and conduct targeted actions to acquire new customers
  • Acting as trusted interlocutor and playing a key role in local economic development

What are you bringing?

  • Full or equivalent banking training (business management)
  • Experience in consulting with corporate clients, as well as in-depth knowledge of banking products and services
  • Strong skills in financial analysis, good understanding of funding mechanisms and risk management
  • Relationship, sense of responsibility and proactivity
  • Good knowledge of the local economic fabric
  • Mastery of English, a plus

We offer you a motivating work environment where team spirit and collaboration are fully valued. We look forward to receiving your application. Nicolas Gabioud Head of customer consulting companies Why Raiffeisen?
