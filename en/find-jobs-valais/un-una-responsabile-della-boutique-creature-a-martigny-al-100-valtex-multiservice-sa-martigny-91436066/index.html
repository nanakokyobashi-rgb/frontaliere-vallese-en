Full description

Valtex Multiservice SA is a social integration company based in Monthey, Valais. It is active in textile valuation and restoration. Valtex Multiservice SA is a company of the CRTO cooperative.

We are looking for

A\-e Manager\-e of the Boutique Créature in Martigny 100%

CDI · Entry into office to be agreed

The sale of second-hand clothing provides training space for those who sort, value and radiate them. At the head of the Boutique Créature, you combine commercial performance and accompaniment of people in professional reintegration.

Your role:

You run the shop and its team: 8 to 10 participants in vocational rehabilitation (unemployment insurance, social action, disability insurance) and several permanent employees. You are responsible for both the results of the shop and the progress of the people you supervise.

Your main responsibilities

  • Take responsibility for the shop and ensure its attractiveness: presentation, presentation of the goods, quality of reception.
  • Managing business: customer advice and sales monitoring.
  • Organize daily operations: planning activities, allocating tasks according to each person's skills and abilities, ensuring quality of service.
  • Coaching the team in the field: setting a clear framework, training and motivating participants.
  • Support each participant: help develop their skills, monitor their progress and report to the teaching team.
  • Work hand in hand with the teaching team to promote the success of each course.
  • Provide administrative monitoring and reporting of activity with MS Office tools.

Your profile:

  • You have confirmed experience in managing a shop and training in sales. Experience in accompaniment of adults or in the occupational integration sector is an asset.
  • You are a field manager: you know how to set a clear framework, mobilize a team and unite it around common goals, including when people's journeys and needs are very different.
  • You effectively organize and coordinate activities and priorities on a daily basis.
  • You are comfortable with MS Office tools, which you use for activity monitoring and administrative management.
  • You speak and write with ease in French, oral and written.
  • You know how to create a climate of trust and commitment and you appreciate teamwork.

We offer you:

  • A mission that makes sense: your work contributes directly to the return to employment of people in reintegration.
  • A complete role, between commercial management and human support.
  • The support of a teaching team for the follow-up of participants.
  • Collaborative management, in an atmosphere of caring work.
  • A stable job (CDI) and conditions related to your qualifications and responsibilities.

Ally operational performance and social utility mission. Join us!
