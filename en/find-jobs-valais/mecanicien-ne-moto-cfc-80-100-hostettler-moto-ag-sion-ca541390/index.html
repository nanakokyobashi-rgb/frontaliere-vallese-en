Mechanic CFC 80 \- 100%

Hostettler motorcycle ag is part of the hostettler group – a family-owned company with a history of over 100 years and a passion for mobility. With eight branches in German-speaking Switzerland and French-speaking Switzerland, prestigious brands such as Ducati, Kawasaki, KTM, Vespa and Yamaha, as well as state-of-the-art workshops and its own fashion and accessories shops, we offer everything that makes motorcycle enthusiasts beat their hearts. More than 150 collaborators live this passion on a daily basis, with a clear objective: not only to satisfy our clients, but to inspire them.

To strengthen our team in Sion, we are looking for a motivated colleague who does not just work on machines, but lives his passion for his profession. At home, you will work on high-performance machines, in a modern environment, with a team that shares your enthusiasm and passion.

Your tasks

  • You perform maintenance and repair work on the Yamaha and Ducati motorcycles
  • You perform warranty work and recall campaigns independently and reliably
  • You prepare our used motorcycles professionally
  • You set clear and transparent quotes for repairs
  • You ensure that our large fleet of test motorcycles is always in perfect condition and you perform maintenance and maintenance work
  • You prepare new motorcycles with the utmost care so that our customers can take delivery of their bike with pleasure
  • You customize motorcycles by installing suitable accessories according to the wishes of our customers

Your profile

  • You have a motorcycle mechanic CFC, an automobile mechatronician or equivalent training
  • You have a good command of French. Knowledge in German is an asset
  • Teamwork is obvious to you and you actively contribute to an excellent working atmosphere
  • You stand out for your sense of service and professionalism in your customer relations
  • You work independently, reliably and efficiently

What We Offer

  • An innovative company evolving in a forward-looking market, with a strong team spirit
  • Attractive workstation with free parking
  • Active support for personal development, including support for internal and external training
  • Days of unpaid absence above average, 16 weeks of maternity leave and 3 weeks of paternity leave
  • Flexible hours model, 5 weeks of vacation (from 50 years and/or for managerial duties: 6 weeks) and full paid holidays
  • Possibility of purchasing additional vacations and flexible part-time positions
  • Attractive employee conditions on all hostettler Group products

Many advantages with partners (insurance, fleet discounts, courses, entry tickets, etc.)

  • Various events and events, such as our legendary hoschi days

... and many other benefits
