Private/Individual Customer Advisor

  • Savièse
  • 80-100%

Do you have commercial training and have experience in the banking world? If you are looking for an opportunity to showcase your skills, this position is for you.

Concretely?

  • You will support clients in their requests for financing, investments and wealth planning.
  • You will acquire new customers in an entrepreneurial spirit.
  • You will establish and maintain a relationship of trust with customers.
  • You will ensure proactive follow-up with customers following a request for an offer/information or following personal contact.
  • You will receive ongoing training on changes to internal and external directives.
  • You will ensure risk management and compliance with legal provisions.

Are you tempted by the challenge? This position is for you if…

  • You have commercial and/or financial training.
  • You have good sales and advisory skills on all investment, financing and wealth advisory products and services.
  • Dynamic and pragmatic, you benefit from a strong sense of initiative and contribute to the success of your team.
  • You have good analytical skills and are able to act quickly in unforeseen situations.
  • Your knowledge of English and/or German is a real asset.

Let's meet! Would you like to join us? We look forward to discovering your application. To do this, send your complete file to Marisa Berclaz, our HR Business Partner, at the address: recruitment@bcvs.ch.
