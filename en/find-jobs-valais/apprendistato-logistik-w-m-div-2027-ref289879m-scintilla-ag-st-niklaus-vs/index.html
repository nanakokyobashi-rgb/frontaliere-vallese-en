Business description

The big step into the exciting world of Bosch As an international company of the Bosch Group and one of the world’s largest manufacturers of power tools and accessories, we focus on single-minded and inquisitive learners. With modern learning methods and intensive support from experienced specialists, we support you until the successful completion of your training. Even after the apprenticeship, we offer you a wide range of attractive career opportunities in various areas. In the Upper Valais region, we are also one of the largest industrial and training companies and can draw on years of experience in the training of learners.

Job description

  • You are responsible for the goods receipt and exit and get to know different storage areas
  • You receive goods, check and store them in the right place
  • In the computer you book deliveries and shipments of goods, so you always have the overview of the stocks
  • You prepare goods for shipping: everything from labelling, packaging to delivery documents is included
  • You move goods with industrial trucks and make sure that they are in the right place at the right time.

Qualifications

  • You attend the orientation school or already have a school degree
  • You can plan well ahead, always work carefully and with safety in mind
  • The German language you master in word and writing
  • You are a team-capable, patient and committed personality with a quick apprehension and a logical and abstract mindset.
