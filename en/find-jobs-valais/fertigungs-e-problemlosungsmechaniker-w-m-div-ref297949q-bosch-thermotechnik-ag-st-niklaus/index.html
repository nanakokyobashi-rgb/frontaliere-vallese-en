Full description

Your tasks

As a manufacturing and problem-solving mechanic (f/m/div.) in the field of carbide in MFO22, you take on the responsibility for supporting and ensuring the functionality of various plants and processes. With the work preparers you maintain a close cooperation. You are the link and contact person for technical aspects in production (MSE 2). Your tasks essentially include:

  • Problem recognition, problem analysis and problem solving fall within your responsibilities
  • Introducing optimization suggestions, process and process development and carrying out production tests are part of your work area
  • Training of the machine operators and the Teamleader Production you lead
  • You are responsible for the management and replacement procurement of wear parts
  • The execution of maintenance and resulting repairs are part of your responsibilities
  • You support in improving the 5S work design and work ergonomics
  • When converting the equipment and ensuring availability you help
  • Documentation of the work and processes round off your area of responsibility

Your profile

  • Training: Basic technical training (e.g. polymechanic / automatic technician / auto mechanic (f/m/div.))
  • Personality and working method: Communicative, solution-oriented and team-capable personality with an independent, systematic and precise working method
  • Experience and know-how: Experience in production technology of advantage
  • Languages: Good communication and expressiveness in German
