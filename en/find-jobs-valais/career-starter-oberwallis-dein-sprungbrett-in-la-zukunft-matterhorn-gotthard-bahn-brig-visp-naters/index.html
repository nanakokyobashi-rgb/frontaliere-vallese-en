Full description

We are looking for a trainee (80 – 100%) 18 months. 3 companies. 1 region that you help shape. Start: Fall 2026 | Duration: 18 months | Assignments: 6 months at Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn | 6 months at Lonza AG | 6 months at another company e.g. RWO (Regional and Economic Center Upper Valais) You don't want just any career entry - but one that takes you further, connects you and moves you? Then you've come to the right place! Your field of activity

  • Over 18 months you will get to know three exciting organizations - and get involved in key future issues
  • You take on tasks and work on real projects in the areas of corporate development, HR, education & training, strategy & digitalization
  • You participate in projects, design workshops and can take on responsibility
  • You develop creative approaches and new ideas in topics such as innovation, sustainability and corporate culture. Your possible final project will have a concrete impact on the Upper Valais

This is what you bring with you

  • A completed bachelor's or master's degree in business administration, business informatics or a related field
  • You are enthusiastic about project work, new ideas and interdisciplinary collaboration
  • You work independently, in a structured manner and can win others over to your ideas
  • You have organizational and communication talent and ideally have already gained initial experience in project management
  • You are flexible and open to new things

You can look forward to that

  • Varied insights and responsible tasks in three top companies and industries
  • Building your regional network
  • Personal mentoring & professional development
  • Strong prospects for your career in Upper Valais - possible chance of a follow-up solution in one of the companies
  • And by the way: an exciting time in the most beautiful region of Switzerland or in your home country

Are you ready? Send us your CV and a short video application (max. 2 minutes) with your answers to these three questions:

  1. Why do you want to be part of the Career Starter program?
  2. What special experiences do you bring with you?
  3. Who are you - in three keywords?

Deadline for your application: September 22, 2026 Questions? Simply contact Jose Piening, Head of Human Resources at MGBahn Let’s shape the future – together in Upper Valais!
