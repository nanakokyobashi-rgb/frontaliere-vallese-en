Secretary (100%) at the Office for Industry, Trade and Labour for the Oberwallis Regional Employment Centre in Brig

Their tasks

  • You are responsible for the reception (telephone and counter) and provide the persons concerned with the first information You organize appointments, invitations and information events You carry out the registration and administration of job seekers’ dossiers in the IT system You answer questions from job seekers, employers and unemployment insurance partners
