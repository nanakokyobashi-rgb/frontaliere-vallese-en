This is what Bosch is all about: about you, our business, our environment. * At Bosch, we shape the future with high-quality technologies and services that inspire enthusiasm and improve people’s lives. Our promise to you is solid: We grow together, enjoy our work and inspire each other.

With its accessories business unit, Bosch Power Tools is the world’s leading supplier of accessories for home and craft users as well as industrial customers. At the subsidiary Scintilla AG in St. Niklaus, a workforce of around 800 employees worldwide develops, manufactures and distributes more than 8,000 accessories for power tools.

Scintilla AG has a worldwide unique machine park, which is further developed by qualified specialists. Maintaining these machines is challenging and varied at the same time. If you have a basic electrical or mechanical education and are happy to take on responsibility, you are in the right place with us!

Job description ##

As a process technician (m/f/div.) in the field of accessories for the oscillating multi-tool with a focus on production processes, you take on the responsibility for supporting and ensuring the functionality of a wide variety of plants and processes. As a deputy of the work preparer, you support in various topics and projects. You are the link and contact person for technical aspects of the plants and processes in production assigned to you (MSE 3).

As a process engineer (f/m/div.), you are responsible for the technical support of the production plants and carry out production tests. The development of improvement potentials is part of your area of responsibility.

Your tasks essentially include

  • The standardization and further development of processes are subject to your responsibility
  • In the case of new starts, you support
  • As a problem solver, you will continue to develop processes and procedures
  • The daily support of the production team is one of your responsibilities
  • You are responsible for compliance with 5S standards and training team leaders, operators and other mechanics
  • Assistance in projects for cost reduction (remuneration, process times, auxiliary materials) rounds off your area of responsibility

Qualifications

  • Training: Basic technical training (e.g. polymechanic / automatic technician / auto mechanic (m/f/div.)) ideally with further training as a technician HF / FH / university
  • Personality and working methods: Solution-oriented, communicative and team-capable personality with an independent and structured working method and a flair for improvement potential
  • Experience and know-how: Experience in production technology is an advantage
  • Languages: Good communication and expressiveness in German, English skills are a plus
