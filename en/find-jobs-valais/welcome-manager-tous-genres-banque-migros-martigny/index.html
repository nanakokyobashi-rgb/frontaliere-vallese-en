Full description

Make a difference as our customers' first point of contact! You are much more than just a welcome: you are the face and soul of our company. For this function, we seek a radiant personality, driven by the passion of welcoming and human contact. If you want to turn every visit into a memorable and unique experience, this mission is for you!

Mansioni

  • First contact point in our branch, you offer every visitor an irreproachable welcome and an exceptional experience
  • Your curiosity and interest in the banking sector will allow you to quickly understand the mechanisms and operation of our institution, to guide your interlocutors with ease and assurance
  • You have completed training in a branch that will give you the means to support the team for a number of administrative tasks
  • If you have banking training, it's an asset! You can perform some basic tasks with our clients

Requirements

  • 3 to 5 years: Customer reception
  • Completed Professional Learning (CFC) French (Very good mastery): Ideally, you have good knowledge of German and/or English
  • You have several years of experience in customer contact and you know how to ensure an exciting customer experience
  • Empathy, a common sense of listening and an excellent ability to communicate give you proven and recognized social skills
  • You take a positive attitude at all times and you have confidence in yourself that allows you to approach all types of situations calmly

Cosa offeriamo

  • Migros Bank: Accounts, mortgages and preferential financial benefits
  • Offers for employees: Annual generous "well-being" contribution
  • Child care: Up to CHF 300 per month per child for external child care
  • Centralised workplace: Central workplace, very well accessible
  • Equitable compensation: A solid comprehensive package involving wages, social security and welfare
  • Social benefits: Optimal coverage in case of sickness and private accident
  • Individual continuing training: Attractive contributions to external training
