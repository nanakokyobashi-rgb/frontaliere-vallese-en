Full description

Lonza

Lonza is now a world leader in the Life Sciences business, which operates on three continents. We are active in science, but there is no magic formula for how we work. Our most important scientific solution is to create talented employees who work together and develop ideas that help other companies to help people. In return, our employees manage their careers independently. Because their ideas, whether large or small, improve the world. And this is the kind of work we want to have part in.

Tasks

  • Cooperation in the development, transfer and validation as well as routine analysis of biological and biochemical methods in the ELISA team (ELISA, Dot Blots and biochemical content regulations)
  • Implementation of methods under ISO or GMP conditions
  • Active cooperation in investigations of failed Assays under guidance with mercies of mercantiles
  • Control and release of auxiliary protocols of other team members
  • Maintenance and calibration of laboratory equipment
  • Cooperation in the preparation of GMP relevant documents

Your work focuses on the safe and quality-compliant processing of the assigned assignments. You are independent in the context of studies and projects and assume responsibility for their success. Also, apprentices are very happy to be taken into account for this position.

Qualifications

  • You have completed a suitable professional apprenticeship (biology laboratory or comparable)
  • Ideally they have already gained basic experience in the GMP environment, preferably in an analytical laboratory
  • It is fun to get involved in the changing challenges of the projects and to tackle your implementation in the laboratory flexibly, independently and carefully
  • Besides German you also have basic English knowledge (at least reading comprehension)
  • A flexible personality, team spirit, initiative, willingness to learn and use complete your profile
