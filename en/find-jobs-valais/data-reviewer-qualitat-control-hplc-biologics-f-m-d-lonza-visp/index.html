Full description

We are currently looking for a Data Reviewer (m/f/d) to join our Quality Control team in Visp, Switzerland. In this role, you will review analytical data generated during routine QC testing, ensuring compliance with GMP requirements and internal procedures to support the timely release of monoclonal antibody batches. Join our international team and contribute to the quality of innovative biologic medicines.

What you’ll get

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • A variety of benefits dependent on role and location. The full list of our global benefits can also be found at lonza.com/careers/benefits.
  • Relocation assistance for eligible candidates and their families, if needed

What you’ll do

  • Review analytical data from HPLC and capillary electrophoresis (CE) analyses
  • Evaluate quality parameters supporting stability studies and batch release
  • Investigate unexpected or ambiguous results in collaboration with the QC laboratory team
  • Ensure analytical data and associated documentation comply with GMP requirements, SOPs and internal procedures
  • Support timely batch release through accurate and thorough data review

What we’re looking for

  • A BSc or MSc in chemistry, biochemistry or a related field, or equivalent experience
  • Experience in a GMP-regulated Quality Control environment is highly preferred
  • Hands-on experience with HPLC and/or CE techniques, including icIEF, is highly preferred
  • Strong attention to detail and a precise, structured approach to reviewing analytical data
  • An inquisitive mindset and the ability to critically assess and investigate unexpected results
  • Flexibility to adapt to changing priorities in a fast-paced environment
  • Fluency in English
  • German language skills are advantageous
