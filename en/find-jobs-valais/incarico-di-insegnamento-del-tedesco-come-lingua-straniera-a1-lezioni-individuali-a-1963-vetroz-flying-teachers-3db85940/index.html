Full description

Flying Teachers offers qualified and experienced German teachers the opportunity to take on an individual German course at level A1 as part of an operational language promotion in 1963 Vétroz.

The teaching assignment includes face-to-face individual lessons with an adult person. The focus is on the systematic development of basic German language skills, the development of communicative skills and the practical application of the language in everyday and professional situations.

The lessons are based on the individual learning level, the personal learning goals and the learning pace of the participant.

The exchange rate is planned as soon as possible. There is a certain degree of flexibility when it comes to determining periods of study.

Course information

  • Language: German as a foreign language
  • Course type: Company course – Individual lessons
  • Location: 1963 Vétroz
  • Form of teaching: classroom lessons
  • Language level: A1
  • Number of participants: 1 person
  • Course size: 24 lessons
  • Frequency of lessons: 1 × 60 minutes per week
  • Preferred day: Wednesday
  • Preferred lessons: 10.00-11.00
  • Alternative teaching hours: Possible by arrangement
  • Start of the course: As soon as possible

The teaching hours can be agreed individually in consultation. Even if you are not available on Wednesdays at 10 a.m., we are happy about alternative appointment suggestions.

Please indicate your possible teaching hours when applying.

Teacher requirements This teaching assignment requires sound linguistic, didactic and methodological skills as well as relevant teaching experience in the field of German as a foreign language.

The following qualifications and competences are important to us:

  • Completed training in German as a foreign language (DaF), language didactics, German studies, teaching or a comparable qualification
  • Sound and verifiable teaching experience in the field of German as a foreign language
  • Very good knowledge of German at at least C1 level, ideally C2 or native language level
  • Experience in individual teaching and in the individual promotion of adult learners
  • Good knowledge of the Common European Framework of Reference for Languages, in particular the A1 level learning objectives
  • Ability to convey linguistic basics in an understandable, practical and target group-oriented manner
  • Communicative, varied and learner-oriented teaching methods
  • Independent, structured and reliable working method
  • Enjoyment of working with adult learners and strong communication skills
  • Possibility to conduct regular face-to-face lessons in 1963 Vétroz
  • Time availability for a weekly lesson of 60 minutes

Advantages are

  • SVEB certificate, fide qualification or comparable adult education training
  • Experience in the field of company language support and in teaching corporate clients
  • Experience with German courses at beginner level
  • Knowledge of French to support communication with French-speaking learners

Their tasks

  • As part of this teaching assignment, you take on the independent planning and implementation of an individual, goal-oriented German lesson at level A1.
  • Their main tasks include:
  • Planning, preparation and implementation of structured and practice-oriented German lessons
  • Imparting basic grammatical structures, an everyday and professional vocabulary as well as elementary communication skills
  • Promoting language skills listening, speaking, reading and writing at level A1
  • Adaptation of teaching content and methods to the individual learning status and personal learning goals
  • Selection and use of appropriate teaching materials and supplementary teaching materials
  • Continuous observation and promotion of individual learning progress
  • Creating a motivating and supportive learning atmosphere
  • Reliable organizational coordination with the administration team of Flying Teachers

What we offer you Flying Teachers offers you a professional, international and appreciative environment in which you can contribute your professional and educational skills.

We offer you

  • A clearly defined teaching task with transparent course information
  • Flexibility in coordinating school hours
  • Self-responsible teaching within the framework of the agreed learning objectives
  • The opportunity to accompany an adult individually and sustainably in language acquisition
  • Organizational and administrative support from our experienced team
  • Professional, cooperative and appreciative cooperation
  • The opportunity to take on further teaching assignments within our course offer if appropriate and available

Who are we? Flying Teachers is a private language and educational school with over 25 years of experience in the organization and implementation of language courses and individual educational solutions.

We offer practice-oriented language teaching for private individuals, companies and institutions and attach great importance to teaching quality, individual support and professional cooperation with our teachers.
