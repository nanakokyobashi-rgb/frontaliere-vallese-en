Full description

BMS Building Materials Switzerland is a wholesaler of construction materials for the large and second work. Our group of companies includes renowned specialists such as Baubedarf, Richner, Gétaz Miauton and RegusciReco. Our experienced specialists advise, support and inspire construction customers and private customers with a great deal of commitment on 110 sites throughout Switzerland. Close to the daily construction, we are the local and reliable partner for every construction project. Fast, simple and competent.

For our Sierre Shop, we are looking for our future Team Leader Shop to ensure the proper functioning and development of the point of sale by guaranteeing a quality customer experience

Missions

As a Team Leader Shop in Sierre, you are at the heart of the point-of-sale performance: you inspire the team, guarantee an irreproachable customer experience and ensure the excellence of business operations, while ensuring a safe, neat and welcoming environment.

Management and team development

  • Organize, coordinate and distribute the work of the team to ensure the smooth functioning of the Shop on a daily basis.
  • Support employees in their development through coaching, regular monitoring and interviews.
  • Promote a positive team spirit, versatility and collaboration with other sectors.
  • Coach, train and follow apprentices.
  • To help maintain a motivating, structured and efficient working environment.
  1. Business development and customer service
  • Ensure the commercial development and performance of the Shop.
  • Ensure high quality hospitality, advice and customer service.
  • Supervise the processing of customer requests, orders and tracking with rigour and responsiveness.
  • Ensure effective coordination with Back-Offices for more complex applications.
  • Contribute, with the team, to offer an impeccable customer experience, service oriented and satisfaction.
  1. Operational management of the Shop
  • Ensure proper management of the Shop's daily operations.
  • Supervise cash, inventory, receipt of goods, shipments and returns.
  • Ensure the availability of products and the proper organization of the point of sale.
  • Ensure the reliability of business and administrative processes related to the activity.
  • Organize and monitor inventory progress.
  1. Security and exemplaryity
  • Know, respect and enforce existing safety rules.
  • Identify and promptly report any defects, hazards or non-compliant material.
  • A daily example of safety, behaviour, order and cleanliness.
  • Ensure compliance with good practices on the site, including with regard to the movement and wearing of protective equipment.
  1. Maintenance, storage and presentation
  • Ensure regular maintenance and cleanliness of sales spaces, furniture and storage areas.
  • Ensure daily storage of offices and workspaces.
  • Ensure clean, clean and well-equipped premises.
  • Regularly monitor the general presentation of the Shop to ensure an orderly, attractive and welcoming environment.
  • Pilot Shop related projects such as transformations, redevelopments, furniture developments, commercial actions and promotions.
  • Actively contribute to the good running of the store and the quality of customer service.

Your Profile

  • Personality who knows how to lead a team through strong and constructive leadership, with excellent quality of communication
  • Your qualities are the strength of proposal, improvement and continuous optimization of processes and system
  • Know how to work independently while easily integrating into a team
  • Holder of a trade/sales CFC
  • Ideally, experience in construction
  • Have predispositions to sale and administrative tasks
  • Strong customer orientation and able to manage multiple tasks at the same time thanks to excellent priority management
  • Mastering common IT tools

We offer

  • A challenge within a constantly changing company
  • A comprehensive integration programme; our new colleagues also benefit from in-house training on products and systems
  • Support for the professional and personal development of our employees
  • Collaborating discounts, various discounts such as fleet discounts, discounts in selected fitness centres and numerous offers to promote their mental and physical health
  • An attractive wage package combined with a strong pension fund, with flexible savings contributions and above average employer share
  • 5 weeks of holidays (6 weeks from 50 years) and the possibility to buy up to 10 additional days of holidays, as well as additional benefits such as a highly funded independent pension fund and discounts on our entire product range as well as external premium benefits
