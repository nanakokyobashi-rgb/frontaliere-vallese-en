Your assignment

  • Read and interpret electrical designs and schematics accurately to ensure plant compliance.
  • Participate actively in the renovation and construction work in the electrical field, in compliance with Swiss standards in force (OIBT – INB).
  • Install strong current and weak current electrical appliances, ensuring perfect functionality and safety.
  • Place canals, install lighting fixtures and electrical devices in accordance with technical specifications.
  • Perform the necessary electrical connections while ensuring the maintenance and continuous improvement of the plants, in particular with regard to automation and modernization.
  • Work closely with the team on site to meet deadlines and ensure the quality of the interventions.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Essential requirements: Owner of a Federal Qualification Certificate (CFC) as an electrical installer, with a solid experience in Switzerland in the electrical sector.
  • Essential requirements: Mastery in reading projects and schematics as well as OIBT – INB mandatory standards.
  • Essential requirement: Ability to install and perform electrical work on strong current and weak current, including laying cans and equipment.
  • Essential requirement: valid driving licence, necessary for trips to the yard; compulsory residence in Valais with a maximum working distance of 30 km.
  • Essential: Ability to work independently, rigorously and methodically, with an excellent team spirit.
  • Useful: Complementary knowledge in automation, instrumentation or electrotechnical, which guarantee a technical added value.
  • Useful: Perfect French mastery.
  • Useful: Professional experience of at least 3 years in Switzerland.
