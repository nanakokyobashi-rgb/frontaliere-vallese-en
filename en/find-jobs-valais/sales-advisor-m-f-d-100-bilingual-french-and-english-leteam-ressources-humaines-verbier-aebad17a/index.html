Company description

The team is one of the leading investment agencies at national level with a total of 27 branches in Switzerland and the Principality of Liechtenstein. For more than 39 years, we have specialized in the placement and rental of personnel services in various sectors, mainly in the areas of work and second work, crafts, technology and industry.

On behalf of one of our clients, a gallery specializing in photography and collectibles located in Verbier, we are looking for a bilingual French/English sales advisor/advisor for the winter season.

You will be responsible for the reception of visitors, the proper running of the gallery and the sale of exhibited works.

Description of post

  • Welcoming and advising local and international clients
  • Present and sell art photographs and models
  • Provide reception and orientation for visitors
  • Manage sales, receipts and order tracking
  • Ensure the proper presentation of the works and the irreproachable handling of the gallery
  • Participate in the daily operation of the sales space

Profile sought

  • Bilingual French/English essential, with excellent oral ease
  • Experience in the desired sales, reception or customer relationship
  • Excellent sense of service and careful presentation
  • smiling, dynamic, autonomous and organized personality
  • Aisance with an international and demanding clientele
  • Interest in photography, art or the world of luxury appreciated
  • Availability for the entire period from 15 December to 15 April

We offer

  • A 100% position in the heart of Verbier
  • An elegant and artistic working environment
  • A privileged contact with an international clientele
  • A rewarding experience in a world-renowned station
  • A seasonal mission in a specialized gallery
