Full description

Your drive The heart of BVZ Holding is the railway company Matterhorn Gotthard Bahn and the international top brands Gornergrat Bahn and Glacier Express. Passionate and qualified employees form the basis of our success. Every day, around 700 employees are committed to offering our guests a first-class experience. We live diversity and have one thing in common: Emotions are our drive. What is your drive? At the beginning of the apprenticeship in August 2027, we offer an apprenticeship as a merchant / merchant EFZ in our administrative building in Brig. During the 3-year apprenticeship you will get to know a different area every year: personnel, purchasing and finance. So you get an exciting insight into our company and can find out what suits you best. What to expect

  • In the staff you support our HR Support Team in various administrative tasks. For example, you help with the management of employee data and get an insight into the management of service clothing
  • In purchasing, you are involved in order processing and are in contact with suppliers
  • In finance you immerse yourself in the world of numbers and support in the processing of invoices as well as in dunning

Your profile

  • You enjoy varied tasks and are constantly motivated to learn new things
  • You are passionate about the world of public transport
  • You are reliable, like to work in a team and are ready to take on responsibility
  • You enjoy working with the computer and you enjoy interacting with different people

Your advantages

  • Exciting entry into the professional world with responsible and interesting work
  • Attractive learning and working environment
  • A GA and other attractive benefits along our railway line
  • Annual learning trip and team events
  • Financial participation in the private laptop for the vocational school
  • 6 weeks vacation during your apprenticeship
