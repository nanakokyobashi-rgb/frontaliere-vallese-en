Verbier Policlinic is looking for a medical assistant (or equivalent) to complete its team for the winter season 2026-2027.

•• Duration and activity rate, open to discussion - Potentially evolving position ••

Your missions

Your profile

  • Autonomy, reliability, punctuality and flexibility
  • Team spirit
  • TARDOC knowledge desired
  • Knowledge of Achilles medical software is an asset
  • Good command of French
  • English knowledge desired due to an important international patient, other languages an asset
