Temporary Mission 1 year: HR Specialist

Do you have experience as an HR specialist and are looking for a position where your expertise really makes a difference? This job is for you!

For our client, a company active in the industrial field in Sion, we are looking for an HR specialist for a one-year temporary mission and a start-up in January 2027\.

Your duties with your new employer

  • You lead the entire recruitment process, from the publication of announcements to the engagement of talent
  • You manage staff administration independently and are the preferred contact person for the teams
  • You write employment contracts, certificates and certificates guaranteeing the confidentiality of the data
  • You track entries and departures to offer a fluid and professional collaborative experience
  • You participate in various HR projects and propose concrete ideas to optimize and digitize HR processes

You offer to your new employer

  • You hold a federal HR specialist certificate or equivalent human resources training
  • You have confirmed experience in recruitment and personnel management, ideally with training in labour law
  • You are native French and your knowledge of German is a real asset in everyday life.
  • You master the Microsoft Office suite, and knowledge of SAP, Abacus or SuccessFactors is a real plus
  • You are organized, autonomous, discreet and communicative, with a common sense of analysis and team spirit
