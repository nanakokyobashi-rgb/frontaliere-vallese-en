Full description

We are looking for a qualified and self-contained tile, available immediately, for new construction and renovation.

Your mission

  • Preparation of supports before laying
  • Reading plans and taking action
  • Floor and wall tile
  • Laying of earthenware, ceramic stoneware, mosaic and slabs
  • Cut, fit and align tiles
  • Preparation and application of mortar glue
  • Making cement and silicone joints
  • Clean and neat finishing work
  • Repairs and replacement of existing tiles
  • Compliance with site safety instructions
  • Cleaning and storage of the work area

Your profile

  • SWC or equivalent training
  • Confirmed experience in tile
  • Autonomous, accurate and careful
  • Ability to work alone or in a team
  • Good control of laying and cutting techniques
  • Reliable, timely and motivated person
  • Driving licence desired
  • Vehicle and tooling
  • Available immediately
  • Ready to work quickly

We offer

  • Temporary mission as required
  • Possibility of long-term mission according to profile
  • Work environment
  • Immediate or to be agreed upon
