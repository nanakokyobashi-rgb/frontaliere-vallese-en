Full description

We are looking for a Senior Engineer who is experienced in fire protection systems on Pharma companies.

Key responsibilities

  • Support the definition and implementation of fire protection concepts and requirements for capital projects
  • Coordinate with architects, engineering partners, fire protection specialists, project teams, and the site fire brigad
  • Lead the technical oversight of fire protection systems, including sprinkler systems, deluge systems, dry extinguishing systems, firewater networks, and firewater retention solutions
  • Manage designers, contractors and vendors responsible for technical fire protection installations
  • Ensure project delivery with respect to cost, schedule, quality, and compliance requirements
  • Participate in 3D design reviews and multidisciplinary coordination to resolve clashes and technical issues
  • Oversee commissioning, functional testing, and acceptance activities for fire protection systems
  • Participate in integrated system tests together with engineering teams, contractors, and stakeholders
  • Review, collect, and hand over as-built documentation and ensure smooth transfer of systems to operations and end users.
  • Required Qualifications
  • Bachelor's or Master's degree in Safety Engineering, Mechanical Engineering, Process Engineering, Chemical Engineering, or a related engineering discipline
  • Certifications: VKF certification such as "Fachperson für Wasserlöschanlagen VKF" is considered a strong advantage
  • Extensive experience working with and designing fire protection systems and related regulations, standards, and guidelines (e.g., VKF, SES)
  • Experience within pharmaceutical, biotechnology, chemical, or industrial manufacturing environments
  • Strong understanding of GMP and cleanroom environments is advantageous
  • Technical expertise in fire protection systems and infrastructur
  • Knowledge of fire safety codes, regulations, and industry standards
  • Experience with commissioning and integrated testing activities
  • German: Business Fluent (required), English: Basic to intermediate, French: Nice to have
