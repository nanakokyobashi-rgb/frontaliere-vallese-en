Full description

Crans-Montana Food and Beverage SA (CMFB) is looking for a (M/F) kitchen clerk for the staff canteen in Crans-Montana.

Your missions: participate in the preparation and service of meals for employees. Assist the kitchen team in setting up and simple preparations. Contribute to the storage of goods, diving and cleaning of equipment and workspaces in accordance with hygiene and safety rules.

Your profile: first experience in cooking or collective restoration appreciated. Motivation, rigor, organizational sense and team spirit. Good understanding of French and ability to follow preparation instructions.
