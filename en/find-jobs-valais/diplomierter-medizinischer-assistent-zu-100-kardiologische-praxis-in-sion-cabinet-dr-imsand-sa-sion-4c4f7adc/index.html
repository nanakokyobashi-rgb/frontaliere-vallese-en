100% medical assistant \- Cardiology office in Sion

To complement our team, we are looking for a motivated and dynamic graduate medical assistant who wants to invest in a cardiology firm in Sion.

Your missions

  • Patient reception and care.
  • Managing calendar, appointments, telephone calls, emails and routine administrative tasks.
  • Preparation of patients for various cardiac examinations such as ECG, stress test, echocardiography, pacemakers and defibrillators.
  • Assistance to the doctor during the cardiac examinations.
  • Setting up Holter, R\-Test, Remler
  • Contribution to the smooth functioning of the firm.

Your profile

  • SWC medical assistant or equivalent
  • Cardiology experience would be an asset but not indispensable.
  • Interest in Cardiology
  • Feeling welcome, listening and ease in human contacts.
  • Organized, rigorous, reliable and autonomous personality.
  • Team spirit and desire to learn.

We offer you

  • A varied position in a specialised firm
  • Progressive training in cardiac examinations
  • The ability to develop your technical skills and autonomy
  • A professional working environment and close collaboration with the doctor
  • An activity combining relational, organization and medical technique.

We look forward to receiving your application package including your cover letter and CV.
