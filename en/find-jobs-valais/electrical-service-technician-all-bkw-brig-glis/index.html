Full description

We make habitats worth living.

Are you an electrical professional with a passion for technology and customer service? Then we are looking for you! Become part of a well-established and motivated team that ensures every day that electricity not only flows – but inspires.

Your tasks

  • You carry out service, maintenance and repair work independently in private, commercial and industrial buildings.
  • With your expertise, you competently support and advise our customers and maintain long-term relationships.
  • You recognize potential, advise on new solutions and actively support the acquisition of orders.
  • You plan and dispose of your material and tools efficiently so that you are optimally prepared.
  • You can repair electrical disturbances quickly, accurately and safely – quality and customer satisfaction come first.
  • In addition, you support the service manager in the offer sector and take on administrative tasks that ensure a smooth process in the service area.

You bring that with you

  • Completed training as an electrical installer: in EFZ and several years of professional experience – ideally in the service sector.
  • An independent, responsible and solution-oriented way of working, even if it gets hectic.
  • Enjoy direct customer contact and interest in new technologies.
  • A strong entrepreneurial thinking and action – you want to shape, not just execute.
  • Category B (PW) driving licence.
