Projectors Building 80\-100% CDI (M/F/D)

Introduction

Do you want to manage challenging construction and renovation projects independently and fully contribute your technical, organizational and communication skills? For our customer, we are looking for a committed personality who accompanies projects from planning to successful implementation. Enjoy a modern working environment, attractive employment conditions and versatile development opportunities within an established company in Valais.

Tasks

  • Independent planning, coordination and monitoring of building construction, conversion and renovation projects
  • Ensuring compliance with deadlines, budgets and quality and safety requirements
  • Leadership, control and coordination of construction companies, subcontractors, craftsmen and other project partners
  • Monitoring of construction according to plans, technical specifications, legal provisions and safety standards
  • Creation, monitoring and optimization of schedules, cost estimates as well as budget and cost controls
  • Active cooperation with builders, architects, engineers and authorities during all project phases
  • Preparation of project documentation, reports and site reports as well as ensuring an efficient flow of information

Profile

  • Higher training in construction management with a focus on building construction, construction project management or a similar specialisation
  • At least 5 years of professional experience in managing and coordinating construction projects
  • Very good knowledge of Swiss construction and interest in economic and financial issues
  • Sound knowledge of building technology (HLKSE)
  • German mother tongue and very good knowledge of French (at least level B2\)
  • Safe handling of Microsoft 365
  • Knowledge of AutoCAD and Messerli is an advantage
  • Entrepreneurial thinking, strong leadership skills and negotiating skills
  • Independent, reliable and structured working method with a high level of initiative
  • Category B driving licence

Advantages

  • Responsible activity with varied construction projects
  • Great scope for design and independent work
  • Modern working conditions and flexible working models
  • Attractive employment conditions and progressive social benefits
  • An attractive compensation in line with your experience and responsibility
  • Entry: By arrangement
