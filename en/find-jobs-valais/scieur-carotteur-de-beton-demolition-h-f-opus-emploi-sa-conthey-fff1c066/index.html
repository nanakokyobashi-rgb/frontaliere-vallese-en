Description of position

For one of our customers active in the field of sawing, carroting and demolition of concrete, Opus Emploi SA is looking for an experienced concrete sawer/carroter for missions in Valais.

Your tasks

Casting work in concrete

  • Sawing of concrete walls, slabs and elements
  • Controlled demolition work
  • Cutting and drilling of concrete according to construction instructions
  • Use of machines and tools specific to sawing and cutting
  • Preparation and security of the work area
  • Evacuation of materials and maintenance of clean site
  • Strict compliance with safety rules

Your profile

  • Confirmed experience in concrete sawing and core
  • Experience in demolition work
  • Good control of the machinery and tools of the trade
  • Independent, serious, careful and reliable person
  • Good physical condition
  • Ability to work as a team
  • Compliance with safety instructions
  • Driver's licence category B, an asset
