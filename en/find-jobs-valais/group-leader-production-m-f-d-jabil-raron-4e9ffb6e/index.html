# Group leader production (m/f/d) #

In order to strengthen the team in Raron, a motivated, quality-conscious and leadership-strong personality as group leader production (m/f/d) is sought.

In this permanent full-time position with employment, the vacancy holder takes over the technical and personnel management of a production team immediately or by agreement.

The Group Manager Production (m/f/d) ensures a smooth production process, sets daily priorities in coordination with production management and ensures compliance with quality, safety and deadlines.

In addition, the position is responsible for the optimal operational planning of employees and resources, monitors relevant production indicators and actively drives improvements in the areas of efficiency, quality and productivity.

With a practice-oriented way of working, the selected person supports the further development of processes and employees and thus contributes sustainably to the success of the production area.

TASKS

Key responsibilities include

  • Management and coordination of a production team of about 15 employees
  • Support in planning and prioritizing production orders
  • Ensuring production, quality and safety standards
  • Coordination with purchasing, quality assurance and maintenance
  • Monitoring of production indicators and initiating improvement measures
  • Analysis and correction of production and process deviations
  • Reduction of scrap and optimization of material use and resources
  • Ensuring order, cleanliness and safety at work in the production area
  • Training, support and further development of employees
  • Participation in continuous improvement processes (CIP)

Requirements

To be considered for this position, the ideal candidate brings:

  • Basic technical or mechanical training (e.g. polymechanic, production mechanic or equivalent) with initial experience in managing staff.
  • In-depth knowledge of global and regional logistics processes as well as the logistics industry
  • Experience in the analysis of logistical requirements and the development of corresponding strategies
  • Experience in change management as well as in productivity and cost reduction programs
  • Secure handling of MS Office (especially Excel, Word and PowerPoint) and email applications
  • Confident knowledge of German, good knowledge of English are a plus
  • Knowledge of international tax and customs regulations
  • Convincing communication and presentation skills
  • Strong financial and analytical skills

ANNEX II

  • Progressive employment conditions with a long-term perspective
  • A responsible and varied activity in a global company with a strong local presence
  • A high degree of personal responsibility and independent work
  • Freedom for creative thinking as well as personal and professional development
  • Attractive bonus program, additional holidays and other attractive benefits
