Job Description

At the Crans-Montana Clinic, the hotel service team contributes every day to the comfort, welcome and well-being of patients. She ensures the maintenance of rooms and premises according to current hospital hygiene standards, participates in serving meals in the dining room and in the cafeteria, and ensures that a pleasant and quality environment is provided. As an apprentice manager in hotel management, you are required to discover and carry out numerous activities within different sectors of the institution. You participate in cleaning and maintenance work, serving meals and drinks, managing linen, welcoming patients at reception as well as certain preparation activities in the kitchen. You also carry out various administrative tasks, particularly related to inventory management and orders. The diversity of the missions offered and the requirements specific to the hospital environment allow you to benefit from comprehensive and varied training, promoting the development of both technical and relational skills. In order to enrich your career, external internships are organized to allow you to discover other areas of activity, in particular the professional management of a lingerie. Throughout your apprenticeship, you develop your sense of service, your autonomy and your versatility, while acquiring the skills necessary to practice the profession, in accordance with the objectives of your training.

You have successfully completed your compulsory education. You have a sense of welcome and enjoy contact with people. You enjoy teamwork and communicate with ease and respect. You demonstrate organization, rigor and a sense of responsibility. You are versatile and motivated to discover different areas of hotel management. You are committed to contributing to the comfort and well-being of patients and colleagues. You have an interest in the hospital environment and are ready to invest in professional training alternating practical work in a company and theoretical teaching.
