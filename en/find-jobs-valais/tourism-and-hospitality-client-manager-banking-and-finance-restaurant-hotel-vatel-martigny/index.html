Job Profiles Tourism and Hospitality Client Manager – Banking and Finance Client Manager specializing in the tourism and hospitality industry is a relationship management professional who works within a bank. Client Manager specializing in the tourism and hospitality industry is a relationship management professional who works within a bank. He/she supports hotels, travel agencies, tour operators, and other tourism businesses with account management, project financing, and cash flow optimization. He/she acts as a dedicated point of contact, combining financial expertise with an in-depth understanding of the tourism industry. Key

  • Responsibilities

The role of a Client Manager specializing in tourism and hospitality industry is to develop and retain a portfolio of professional clients within the tourism industry. Their main responsibilities include: Client account management and monitoring: Following up on financial transactions, analyzing client needs and profitability, and providing personalized advice to optimize cash flow and investments. Business development: Prospecting for new clients and creating financial solutions tailored to the industry's specific needs, such as loans, leasing, insurance, and financing for hotel or tourism projects. Project support: Advising on financing solutions for the construction, renovation, or expansion of hotels and tourism facilities. Client relations and retention : Maintaining regular contact with clients, monitoring their performance, anticipating their needs, and proposing custom solutions. Required Skills

To succeed in this position, several skills are essential

Financial and accounting skills: Expertise in financial analysis, banking products, and financing solutions. Knowledge of the tourism/hospitality sector: Understanding the economic challenges, operational specifics, and business cycles of the industry. Communication and client relations: The ability to negotiate, advise, and retain clients. Organization and rigor: The capacity to manage multiple files simultaneously and adhere to banking procedures. Proficiency in English and other foreign languages: Essential for dealing with international clients.

Education

A bachelor's or master's degree is generally required, with a specialization in banking, finance, management, or business. A background in hospitality and tourism ( MBA or Bachelor at Vatel) supplemented by financial training can also lead to this role. Experience in the tourism or hotel sector is highly valued. Salary The average salary for a Client Manager specializing in tourism and hospitality ranges from €30,000 to €45,000 gross per year at the beginning of their career. With experience and depending on the size of the bank or the complexity of their portfolio, it can reach €60,000 or more.
