Full description

In order to strengthen our Quality Control team, we are looking for a Quality Control Laboratory Technician (all), 100% for our Vionnaz Production site (Valais/Wallis, Switzerland).

Your Missions

  • Analysis of raw materials, products in progress and finished products
  • Control and signature of laboratory analyzes
  • Controls and maintains laboratory equipment
  • Documentation management
  • Sampling of materials
  • Participation in the validation of analytical methods
  • Monitoring of quality (GMP) and safety (EHS) procedures
  • Identify areas for improving procedures and contribute to their implementation.
  • Actively participate in optimization groups (quality, safety, standards)

Your Profile

  • Holder of a CFC laboratory assistant in chemistry, you have technical analytical physico-chemical and chromatographic skills (HPLC and GC).
  • You benefit from experience in a chemical and/or pharmaceutical industrial environment.
  • You are familiar with GMP standards.
  • Knowledge of LIMS and/or Chromeleon is an asset.
  • You are organized and methodical.
  • You are flexible and willing to work staggered hours ~ 1x / week.
  • You support Quality Control in all its tasks. And enjoy working in a team.
  • You speak French.
  • You are familiar with common IT tools.

Our Offer

We offer interesting and challenging work in an innovative and growth-oriented company, which guarantees versatility and diversity. You will be part of a collegial environment and will have the opportunity to grow professionally within the framework of a permanent job. Modern employment conditions await you, including attractive fringe and social benefits as well as development opportunities.

We look forward to receiving your complete application file via our application portal.
