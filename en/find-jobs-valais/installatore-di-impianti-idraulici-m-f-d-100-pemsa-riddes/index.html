Your assignment

  • Perform the installation and connection of new sanitary fixtures or during renovation, on site or with private individuals, ensuring compliance with the technical and safety standards in force.
  • Perform electrosalding, crimping, threading and gluing operations with rigor and precision, in compliance with the supplier directives, in particular Geberit.
  • Ensure repair and maintenance of existing sanitary systems to ensure optimum and lasting operation.
  • Read and interpret with meticulousness the technical drawings in order to effectively organize the interventions and coordinate the different stages of work.
  • Working independently in compliance with deadlines, quality requirements and safety standards on site.
  • To collaborate closely with other professional figures to optimize the performance and coordination of the interventions in the yard.
  • Check the compliance of the plants to the current standards and adapt the interventions according to the specific requirements of the projects.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Valid driving license and personal vehicle: indispensable to move quickly to the various yards located in Valais.
  • Residence in Valais: geographical requirement necessary to ensure rapid intervention within a maximum radius of 30 kilometers from the yards.
  • Federal Capacity Certificate (AFC) as a health installer or equivalent experience of at least 3 years in Switzerland: demonstrating your proven mastery of installation, repair and maintenance techniques.
  • Excellent knowledge of Geberit products: specific technical skills are required, guaranteeing the quality and reliability of the plants.
  • Employers' working methods: including electrical welding, clinging, threading, and excellent rigor in compliance with safety standards.
  • Autonomy and sense of responsibility: ability to independently manage interventions in compliance with deadlines and quality standards.
  • Language skills: French spoken and written, essential to communicate effectively with customers and teams on site.
  • Complementary training or experience in the field of safety at work: an additional value added strength for your application.
  • Title or level of education: Federal Capacity Certificate (CFC) recommended to strengthen your professional credibility.
  • Professional experience: at least 3 years proven in Switzerland for a perfect knowledge of the local market.
