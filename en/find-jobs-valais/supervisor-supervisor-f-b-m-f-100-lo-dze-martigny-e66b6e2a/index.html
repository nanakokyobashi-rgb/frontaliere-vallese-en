Full description

The Quartier LôDzé is looking for a 100% F&B Supervisor (M/F) to strengthen its teams. We are looking for a dynamic, organized person with an excellent sense of service, able to supervise the daily operations of the restaurant and the Saucithèque, to accompany the teams and to guarantee a quality customer experience.

Your missions

  • •Coordinate and supervise the smooth running of the service in the rooms, ensuring that quality and service standards are met.
  • Provide an operational presence in the room and ensure client satisfaction.
  • •Facilitate communication between the room and kitchen teams to ensure smooth service.
  • Supervise the setting up, cleanliness and atmosphere of the room, as well as the opening and closing procedures.
  • •Manage reservations, table allocation and use of point-of-sale (Mews) systems.
  • •Monitor operational stocks and report requirements to management.
  • •Ensure compliance with internal procedures, HACCP hygiene and food safety standards.
  • •Ensuring, accompanying and training employees, especially when they are integrated.
  • •Participate in recruitment and contribute to team skills development.
  • identify and report operational problems and participate in continuous service improvement.

Profile sought

  • •Professional training in catering, hotels or equivalent.
  • •Experience confirmed in a similar position or in the supervision of a restoration team.
  • •Excellent mastery of service standards and high customer satisfaction.
  • •Natural leadership and ability to mentor, motivate and accompany a team.
  • Organizational awareness, autonomy and ability to manage priorities.
  • •Excellent communication skills and teamwork.
  • •Mastered computer tools and reservation management systems and point of sale.
  • •Knowledge of HACCP hygiene and food safety rules.
  • •Presentation with care, dynamism and professional attitude.
  • •Flexibility and availability according to the needs of the establishment, including weekends and holidays.

We offer you

  • •Reduction for employees at different points of sale
  • 5 holiday weeks, 6 holidays
  • •Salary according to the applicable CTC and 13th month.
  • •A dynamic and varied working framework.

We will only examine the complete files (CV, cover letter, work certificates, etc.) of the candidates corresponding to the profile sought.
