MANAGER IMMOBILIER(E) POLYVALENT(E)

Human-sized real estate agency, specialized in property management, PPE administration and sales, we are looking for an experienced real estate manager in Martigny.

In this versatile function, you are primarily responsible for the day-to-day management of our real estate portfolio and participate, in collaboration with the team, in the various activities of the agency.

Your main missions

Real estate and administrative management

Provide administrative monitoring and day-to-day management of buildings.

Conduct real estate accounting, billing and tracking payments and receipts.

Prepare expense counts.

Organize visits to vacant objects and publish rental announcements.

Prepare leases for rent and make records of places of entry and exit.

Ensure contact with co-owners and participate in EPP meetings.

Coordinate technical monitoring and maintenance of buildings.

Trade development and sales

Participate in client portfolio development and search for new mandates.

Contribute to the development of goods for sale.

Organize visits and accompany clients in their real estate activities.

Your profile

Business employee SWC or training deemed equivalent.

Experience confirmed in real estate management, ideally complemented by knowledge of PPE administration.

Good knowledge of real estate accounting.

Aisance with computer tools (Microsoft Office, WINNIZE, CITI).

Excellent mastery of French

  • Good knowledge of English appreciated.

Organizational sense, rigour, autonomy and initiative.

Relationship, sense of service and interest in commercial activities.

Ability to manage multiple files simultaneously and define priorities.

Essential vehicle.

We offer you

A versatile and varied position within a man-made real estate agency.

A function offering autonomy and responsibilities.

A pleasant working environment and close collaboration.

Opportunities for continuing training and skills development.
