Caregivers between 60 and 90% for an investment waiting bed unit at St-Amé Clinic

The Pôle de gériatrie du Valais Romand has several institutions specializing in the care of the elderly. It offers continuity of care tailored to the specific needs of patients by ensuring quality medical, nursing, medical, therapeutic, social and human support.

Mission

  • In your field of expertise, provide personalized care and support tailored to the needs of elderly patients, as well as their loved ones, in geriatric multi-purpose bed and/or rehabilitation units, in accordance with the Hospital Model of the CHVR.

Profile

  • AFP care and support (ASA) or training deemed equivalent.
  • Experience of at least 2 years in medical-social establishments and in desired occupational activities.
  • Interest in direct care of the elderly.
  • Sense of observation, reliability and ability to transmit useful information.
  • Professional, respectful and caring attitude towards patients and their loved ones.
  • Team spirit, adaptability and easy collaboration with different professionals.
  • Availability to work according to service schedules, including weekends, nights and holidays.

Offer

  • A daily collaboration within an experienced medical-care and interprofessional team.
  • A concrete role in maintaining patient autonomy, safety and quality of life pending placement.
  • Support for integration and opportunities to develop your professional skills.
  • Conditions of employment and social benefits at the Valais Hospital
