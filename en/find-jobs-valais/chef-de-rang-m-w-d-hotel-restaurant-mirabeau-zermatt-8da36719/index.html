Full description

“Le Mirabeau Hotel and SPA s” is a hotel with a total of 70 residential units. The hotel rooms and suites are divided between the two main buildings and are completed by the new serviced apartments in the chalet Antoine, which is located opposite. The hotel is located in the quieter part of the village and offers a majestic view of the Matterhorn and the surrounding Mischabel mountain range. With our renowned restaurant Veranda (alpin&casual), our newly introduced restaurant concept “Marie’s Deli” and “Le Bar” and our event space in the “Josef’s Wine Lounge”, we place a strong focus on the restoration or in other words on “the art of living”. Our buildings and recently renovated rooms are furnished in modern alpine style. The “Alpine Hideaway Spa” has several saunas, steam baths and rest rooms. In addition, there is a newly built pool, which is one of the largest in Zermatt and also includes a hot tub. Several treatment rooms in which you can book a wine therapy or a chocolate massage round off our wellness offer.
