Full description

Who you are

As an IKEA Food employee, you will strive to make people smile while offering irresistible food offers, in a welcoming atmosphere that gives our customers a good reason to come back.

  • You have experience in a high volume environment and in direct contact with customers, ideally in service or sales.
  • You are accessible, you like to work directly with customers and you always strive to provide them with an excellent service.
  • You are physically resilient and able to stand for an extended period of time, even when the frequency of clients is lower.
  • You have a strong team spirit, but you also like to take responsibility for your own work.
  • You flourish in a very dynamic and stimulating environment and work well under pressure.
  • You have mastered computer science, especially MS Office.
  • You are flexible and understand that your working hours depend greatly on the opening hours of the store.
  • You speak fluently French and have a good level of English. Knowledge of any other national language is an advantage.
  • You're able to work Friday all day, night and Saturday morning.

Your responsibilities

  • Serve and meet customers with an empathic and caring attitude based on IKEA values.
  • Ensure that foods, beverages and products in your area of responsibility are of the highest quality and freshness and are presented in the most attractive way possible.
  • Ensure your area of responsibility is always clean, organized and attractive.
  • Accept payments and process cash transactions at the cashier.
  • See the food sector through the eyes of our customers to constantly improve our products and meet their needs.
  • Find more efficient working methods to reduce costs and be more aware of costs, including controlling waste, correcting portion sizes, verifying production levels and updating cooking methods.
  • Respect and monitor internal rules, policies and daily operational routines regarding quality, food security and stock numbers.

About this industry

Thanks to us, customers, employees and people can improve their daily lives at home with food that is good for them and for the planet. Together, we serve more than 660 million guests each year in IKEA restaurants around the world, highlighting our Scandinavian heritage and offering modern Swedish dishes. We also offer different food products in our bistros and in our Swedish grocery store. Our motto: We laugh harder where there's food!
