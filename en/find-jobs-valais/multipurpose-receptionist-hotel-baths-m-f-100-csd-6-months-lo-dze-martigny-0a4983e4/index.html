Full description

The Quartier LôDzé is looking for a duration of 6 months of multipurpose receptionist/e Hotel & Baths (M/F) 100% to strengthen its teams.

We are looking for a smiling, organized person with an excellent sense of hospitality, able to ensure the smooth running of the daily operations of hotel receptions and public baths, and to guarantee a quality customer experience, from booking to departure.

Your missions

  • •Accommodate guests with professionalism and friendliness, and ensure check-in and check-out of the hotel and public baths.
  • •Teach and accompany guests (in person, by telephone and by e-mail) about the times, rates, facilities and services of the hotel, baths and outlets F&B.
  • •Manage reservations, modifications, cancellations and special requests, as well as gift vouchers, subscriptions and proposed forms.
  • •Effect invoicing, cashing, cash closes and updating of accounts receivable in the PMS.
  • •Ensure the smooth running of the daily operations of public baths, respecting the standards of quality, hygiene and safety, as well as the rules of procedure.
  • •Control cleanliness and order of spaces, ensure storage of changing rooms and drying of floors, replace used linen and report resupply needs.
  • •Ensure fluid communication between the hotel reception, the baths and the various services (accommodation, baths, restaurant).
  • •To manage complaints and sensitive situations with diplomacy.
  • • Advise customers on Valais' tourist, cultural and sporting activities and contribute to the development of additional sales.
  • •Report any technical or security problems and ensure continuity of service in the absence of the Manager or Supervisor.

Profile sought

  • Prior experience in a reception post, ideally in hotels, wellness or tourism.
  • •Experience in a combination of accommodation and leisure activities is an asset.
  • •Excellent relational skills, a developed sense of hospitality and customer orientation.
  • •Presentation and positive attitude.
  • •Very good capacity to organise and manage priorities.
  • •Polyvalence, autonomy and adaptability.
  • •Easy with computer tools and hotel software (PMS, reservation and cashier systems).
  • •Good command of French and English; any other language is an asset.
  • •Team spirit and excellent communication.
  • • Commercial awareness and interest in the sale of supplementary services.
  • •Good knowledge of Valais or interest to discover it.
  • •Flexibility and availability according to the needs of the establishment (morning, day or evening hours), including weekends and holidays.

We offer you

  • •Reduction for employees at different points of sale
  • • 5 holiday weeks, 6 holidays
  • •Salary according to the applicable CTC and 13th month.
  • •A dynamic and varied working framework.

We will only examine the complete files (CV, cover letter, work certificates, etc.) of the candidates corresponding to the profile sought.
