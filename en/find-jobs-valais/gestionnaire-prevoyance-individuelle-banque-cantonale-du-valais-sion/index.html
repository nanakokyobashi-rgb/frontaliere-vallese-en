Individual Provident Manager

  • Zion
  • 80-100%

You benefit from commercial training with a specialisation in the field of occupational or individual pensions. In this context, you have extensive experience in dealing with foresight cases, with advanced knowledge of the legal standards governing this field. If you are looking for an opportunity to value your skills, then this position is your chance.

Specifically?

  • You will manage all kinds of foresight events for our clients (ownership, disability, divorce, departure abroad, buyback of contribution year etc.).
  • You will administer, under your responsibility, savings 3 and free pass accounts and bank insurance contracts.
  • You will exchange regularly with external stakeholders (foresight institutions, guarantee funds, supplementary institution, review body, supervisory authority).
  • You will collaborate in the operational management of the savings foundations 3 and BCVs free passage and in the establishment of annual accounts.
  • You will ensure the quality and speed of processing of files.
  • You will support our internal and external clients and provide them with useful information.

Do you want the challenge? This post is for you if...

  • You have commercial training, with specialisation and experience in the field of personal provision, certificate or patent, an asset.
  • You have good knowledge of German.
  • You are able to manage your priorities and work independently.
  • You value the quality and accuracy of your work.
  • You are a pragmatic and rigorous person; you provide constructive improvement solutions and create natural relational ease.

Would you like to join us? Let's meet! We look forward to discovering your application. To do this, send your complete file to Marisa Berclaz, our HR Business Partner, by 14 August 2026 to: recruitment@bcvs.ch .
