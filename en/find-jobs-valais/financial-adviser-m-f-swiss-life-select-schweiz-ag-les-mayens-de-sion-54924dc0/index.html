Become a financial adviser

Looking for a career that offers you freedom, evolution and meaning? At Swiss Life Select, we train and accompany those who want to reinvent themselves in the exciting world of finance — even without prior experience.

*What is waiting for you

  • You accompany your clients at all important stages of their financial life—from first job to retirement, life and investment projects.
  • You are developing tailor-made financial plans, based on carefully selected partners solutions.
  • You choose your own path: become an expert in financial advice or evolve towards management responsibilities.

What we offer

  • Complete training until the IAF degree, with accompaniment by experienced pros.
  • Total freedom of organization – work independently, at your own pace.
  • Transparent and motivating compensation based on your results.
  • Custom career – specialize in consulting or evolve into management.
  • A caring, ambitious and dynamic community of entrepreneurs.

You?

  • You have completed a vocational training (CFC, Maturity, Bachelor, Master).
  • You are at least 23 years of age and live in Switzerland.
  • You are curious, motivated and organized, with a real desire to learn and build something lasting.
  • You like human contact, personal advice and have the soul of an entrepreneur.
