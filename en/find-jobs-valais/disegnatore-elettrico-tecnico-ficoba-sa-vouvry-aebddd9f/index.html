FICOBA has been an active recruitment firm since 1982 as an expert for the temporary and fixed placement of qualified candidates. We act as an exclusive partner or not, with a demanding clientele in the choice of their future employees. Mandated by our client, we are looking for a:

Technician Drafter Projector Electric

As part of the development of our client's activities, the Designer / Projector to join and participate in the design and implementation of industrial projects in demanding environments.

Your responsibilities

You are involved in industrial automated plant projects and are responsible for:

  • Study and design of automated industrial installations, from the search for solutions to the choice of automation components and the design of electrical cabinets.
  • Develop project technical documentation:

+ plans for the manufacture of equipment sets; + detailed electrical diagrams; + nomenclatures; + I/O lists; + lists of cables and other technical documents.

  • Provide technical support during testing and validation in the workshop.
  • Participate, according to your skills and experience, in the commissioning of installations in French-speaking Switzerland.
  • Gradually move towards complementary responsibilities such as:

+ technical support to project managers; + implementation of figures and offers; + project management and monitoring; + development of technical specialization after training, e.g. in the ATEX field. Your profile

  • SWC, ES, HES training in electricity, automation or training deemed equivalent.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience confirmed in the EAI study office, with practical experience of the field in the industrial environment indispensable.
  • Very good control of an electrical CAD software, ideally EPLAN P8\.
  • Good control of IT tools, including MS Office, as well as project monitoring tools.
  • Experience in the pharmaceutical, chemical or biotechnology sectors is a real asset.
  • Mastery of French and good knowledge of English or German essential.
  • Analytical spirit, autonomy, rigour and taste for complex technical projects.
  • A sense of teamwork and ease in interacting with different project stakeholders.
