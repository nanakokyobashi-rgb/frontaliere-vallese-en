Put to the Point

You are an open personality with a strong organizational talent and enjoy the active work inside and outside. With this task, the Cyber command offers you the opportunity to ensure the perfect operation of our ICT infrastructure. A unique environment with exciting challenges awaits you as well as the opportunity to contribute and develop your expertise.

  • Date of entry: Immediately
  • Type of employment: temporary
  • Reference No: JRQ$540-20516
  • Place of work: Pont des Iles 2, 1950 Sion

You can make this contribution

  • Install, exchange and decommission HW/NW components in comprehensive, highly available and complex transmission and communication systems (including participation in the provision of basic ICT infrastructure)
  • Inventory and documentation systems as well as in the installation and Installing/extending planning of HW/NW components
  • Analyse and correct complex hardware-side faults and carry out restart procedures
  • Local ICT operational responsibility incl. Perceive site coordination and carry out functional tests
  • Working with and supporting operational projects

This makes you unique

  • Basic technical training (EFZ) in the field of electrical engineering, electronics or computer science with further training in telecommunications and/or systems/network technology
  • Professional experience in the operation of ICT infrastructures benefiting from networked expertise in ICT and TGA activities
  • Knowledge of cabling systems according to the current state of the art including the necessary standards and standards such as SIA, KBOB etc.
  • Advantageous system/manufacturer-related training for the operation and maintenance of radio and NW systems
  • Personal responsibility, resilience, good basic physical fitness and exact working method
  • Willingness to take responsibility coupled with forward thinking and action

Your commitment to security and freedom The Cyber Command (Kdo Cy) stands for competence in the areas of cyber and digitalization. We combine the enthusiasm for our task with know-how, innovation, state-of-the-art technologies and agile working methods. We provide secure, robust and resilient IT services for the Swiss Army and our partners in the Swiss Security Association. The Kdo Cy thus gives the Swiss army the necessary knowledge and decision-making advantage – for a safe Switzerland.

The Swiss Army employs more than 9,000 people in four military and around 200 civilian occupations – at 110 locations throughout Switzerland. The next generation is also taken care of: The Swiss Army trains learners in more than 30 professions.
