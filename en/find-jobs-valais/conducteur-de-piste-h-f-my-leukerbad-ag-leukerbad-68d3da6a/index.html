Full description

In winter, the torrent tracks offer access to a 50 km Alpine ski area up to 2,610 metres above sea level. From beginner slopes to black, challenging slopes, a fun park with various rails and boxes for freestylers and a panoramic trail with magnificent views of the Valais four-thousanders, the area contains a diverse offer.

Your tasks

  • Off-road use: expert preparation of slopes
  • Partially with a vehicle with winch and SnowSat in use
  • Transports: transport of materials of various types (snow guns, snow lances, slopes and rescue equipment...)
  • Maintenance: arrange prescribed maintenance, update the log every day, carry out minor repairs independently in agreement with the supervisor
  • Violating and distributing technical snow

Your profile

  • Category B driving licence
  • Experience in running large machines is an advantage
  • Joy in the Snow
  • Good knowledge of German
  • Working hours mostly at night and on weekends
  • Flexibility and a resilient personality
