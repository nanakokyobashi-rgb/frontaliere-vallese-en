Role overview

You ensure high quality therapeutic massage services on delegation of physiotherapists, in accordance with professional standards, therapeutic prescriptions and institutional values.

You actively contribute to the well-being, safety and dignity of patients throughout their rehabilitation journey.

  • You ensure high quality therapeutic massage services on delegation of physiotherapists, in accordance with professional standards, therapeutic prescriptions and institutional values.
  • You actively contribute to the well-being, safety and dignity of patients throughout their rehabilitation journey.

Application process

  • You perform treatments tailored to the needs and capacities of each patient, including using techniques such as classical massage, massage of fascia and connective tissue, plantar reflexology, manual lymphatic drainage (depending on specific training) and certain thermo - or electrotherapeutic applications.
  • You support patients in achieving their rehabilitation goals, monitor their clinical evolution and provide relevant information to the interdisciplinary team members to ensure coordinated and efficient management.
  • You participate in the continuity and quality of services by working closely with physiotherapists, physicians, caregivers and other professionals and professionals involved in care.
  • You also contribute to the integration and supervision of new and new collaborators, students and trainees.
  • Rigourous or rigorous and organized, you know how to define therapeutic priorities, manage your activity independently and maintain accurate documentation in the electronic patient file.
  • You have excellent relational skills, empathy and a strong team spirit.
