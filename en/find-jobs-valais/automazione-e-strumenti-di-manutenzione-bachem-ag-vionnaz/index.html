Full description

In order to strengthen our Maintenance & Engineering team on our Vionnaz website, we are looking for a Maintenance Automatician & Instrumentation (a) passionate about technique, troubleshooting and continuous improvement. Your Impact As an expert in automation, instrumentation and electrical systems, you play a key role in the availability, safety and performance of our production equipment. You intervene directly on strategic facilities in a demanding and highly regulated environment. Your Responsibilities

  • Ensure assembly, commissioning, preventive and corrective maintenance of electrical equipment, instrumentation and automation.
  • Diagnosis and resolve breakdowns to ensure continuity of our operations.
  • Participate in improving the reliability of installations and propose innovative solutions to optimize their performance.
  • Identify the problems of obsolescence and contribute to the definition of the technical standards of tomorrow.
  • Ensure compliance with GMP requirements, CAPA and Change Control processes.
  • Perform periodic inspections and calibration activities of instrumentation.
  • Ensure compliance with ASE, ITTO and NIBT standards.
  • Participate in the technical picket service in order to guarantee the support necessary to operate the site. Your profile You are a field professional who likes to understand technical problems, find durable solutions and work in multidisciplinary teams. You ideally have the following qualifications:
  • SWC of automatic, assembler-automatician or installer-electrician.
  • Experience in industrial maintenance and automation systems.
  • Good knowledge of Siemens S7 controllers and industrial communication protocols.
  • Controlling the reading of electrical, pneumatic and mechanical diagrams.
  • Knowledge of GMP or regulated environments providing an advantage.
  • ITTO certification Art. 13 desired.
  • Good control of common computer tools and SAP.
  • Current French
  • English and/or German are assets. Beyond your technical skills, we look for an organized, self-sustaining person with a strong analytical spirit and enjoying teamwork. What we offer you
  • A modern and growing working environment.
  • Various projects with high added value.
  • A corporate culture based on collaboration, continuous improvement and innovation.
  • Opportunities for professional development and continuing training.
  • The possibility of directly contributing to the production of products that have a positive impact on patient health. Ready to meet the challenge? Join a growing international company where your technical expertise will contribute every day to the reliability and performance of our facilities. We look forward to receiving your application.
