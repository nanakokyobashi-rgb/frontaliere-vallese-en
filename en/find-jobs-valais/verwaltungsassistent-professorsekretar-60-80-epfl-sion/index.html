Mission The École Polytechnique Fédérale de Lausanne (EPFL) is looking for a dynamic and reliable administrative assistant to assist a professor and his or her laboratory. The candidate ensures the smooth running of administrative processes and acts as a central interface with EPFL's departments (HR, finance, purchasing). Main Duties and
