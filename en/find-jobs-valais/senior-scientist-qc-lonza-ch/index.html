Senior Scientist, QC — Lonza

This position is no longer active. Below you'll find the original details and other open positions.

Full description

At the Visp site, a scientist (m/f/d) is sought for microbiology within quality control.

Your responsibilities

  • Technical expertise in technical and analytical matters, as well as handling investigations, change requests and deviations under cGMP
  • Recording, analysis and evaluation of verification data
  • Identification and assessment of weaknesses, as well as planning, implementation and monitoring of corrective actions.
  • Preparation of verification plans, reports and technical documentation.
  • Participation in method development and verification
  • Support for other departments on quality-related matters.
  • Preparation, review and approval of documents to ensure compliance with regulatory guidelines
  • Representative for project-specific inquiries in customer audits and regulatory inspections
  • Active support of continuous. improvement processes within QC Microbiology.
  • On-call duty with assignments agreed in advance as part of the on-call service

What we are looking for

  • Completed degree in a natural sciences field (MSc / PhD) with a focus on microbiology, biology or a comparable qualification.
  • Professional experience in the pharmaceutical industry, ideally in microbiology
  • Experience with QC software systems such as MODA, LIMS, TrackWise or comparable systems is an advantage.
  • GMP knowledge is an advantage.
  • Independent, reliable and quality-conscious way of working.
  • Enjoyment of working in a dynamic, regulated environment
  • Very good command of English. German language skills are an advantage.
