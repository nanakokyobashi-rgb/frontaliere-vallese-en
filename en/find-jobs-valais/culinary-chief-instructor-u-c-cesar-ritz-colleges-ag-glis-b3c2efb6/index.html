Full description

Swiss Education Group (SEG) is the larget private educator in Switzerland. We currently operate 4 of the world! top hospitality schools and have more than 6000 students enrolled in our races. With near 40 years experience in hospitality, business, and ultimate arts education, we are firmly rooted in the great tradition of Swiss hospitality.

We are committed to equipping our students with the leadership and entrepreneurial skills they need to drive in the hospital industry and beyond.

  • We offer a wide range of hospitality education courses, including Bachelors and Masters degrees. We also offer short professional races, so students can choose a program that is specific to their individual learning needs and professional interests.

For our campuses Cesar Ritz Colleges Switzerland and from January 6th 2026, we are looking for:

Culinary Chief Instructor

The successful candidate will join a dynamic team, committed to the delivery of high quality programs and will be required to teach to multi-cultural profiles.

Key

  • Responsibility:

Teach Kitchen theory and practical classes to students according to our standards Advise students on menu preparation and techniques Ensure and provide quality food production for students and staff Elaborate and update course material Ensure property maintenance and use of all equipment within the designed section Demonstrate health, cleanliness and security of the kitchen and storerooms to students

Requirements

Degree from a recognized academic school as a Cook (Professional Certificate or equivalent) At least 5 years Up-to-date with the latest trends and best practices in basic arts Culinary teaching experience within a hospitality school a strong asset Creative team-player, can to work in a very international environment Sens of Initiative, creativity, maturity, leadership skills Fluent in English written and spoken (mandatory), French is strong an asset.

Ideally based in the canton of Valais, between Le Bouveret and Brig.
