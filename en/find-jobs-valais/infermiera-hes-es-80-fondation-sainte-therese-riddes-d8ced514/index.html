Nurse HES / ES 80% (CDD 6 months)

The EMS Jean\-Paul Residence in Riddes welcomes 55 long-stay residents and 1 short-stay bed. An institution of human size, we place individualized accompaniment at the heart of our mission, respecting the rhythm, needs and dignity of each person.

Proximity and benevolence guide our daily commitment to provide quality support to our residents.

as part of a replacement temporary (6 months), we are looking for a

Infirmary\-ère HES / ES 80%

Your mission

  • Ensure the overall care of residents in accordance with their needs and their accompanying project.
  • Provide nursing care in accordance with current requirements and guidelines.
  • Ensure the comfort, safety and well-being of residents on a daily basis.
  • Collaborate with the interdisciplinary team to ensure quality support.
  • Actively participate in the proper functioning of the service and foster harmonious collaboration within the team.

Your profile

  • Nursing degree ES or HES.
  • Experience in geriatric institution, an asset.
  • Excellent relationship skills, team spirit and benevolence.
  • Responsibility, autonomy and organizational capacity.
  • Interest in the support and care of the elderly.
  • Mastery of the French language, both oral and written.
  • Use of IT tools and BESA software.

We offer you

A human-sized work environment with a warm working environment.

A meaningful activity at the service of our residents.

Attractive working conditions consistent with the SLD CTC.

Free private parking available to employees.

Onward: immediately or to be agreed
