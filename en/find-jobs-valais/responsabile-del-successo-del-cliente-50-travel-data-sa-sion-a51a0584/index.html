German version

Customer Success Manager – 50% Travel Data develops data-based solutions and control tools for the tourism industry, especially for tourist destinations. We collect, structure and analyze data from different sources so that our customers can better understand their business development, identify changes early on and assess future trends. Tourist destinations have more and more data. Our task is to make this data accessible, understandable and usable for informed decisions. To support our further development, we are looking for a curious, numerical and communicative personality who wants to develop further at the interface between tourism, data analysis and consulting. Their tasks They help to successfully use our solutions in tourist destinations and partners and show their added value. They accompany our customers in the use of our platforms, support them in interpreting their data and actively participate in opening up new business opportunities.

Their responsibilities include in particular

  • Support and support of our customers in the use of our platforms for tourism monitoring and destination management;
  • analysis of tourist indicators and identification of key findings and developments;
  • preparation of presentations, evaluations and reports in order to comprehend data and support decision-making processes;
  • preparation and implementation of customer appointments and workshops;
  • Presentation of our solutions and active support in their marketing;
  • identifying customer needs and identifying new business opportunities;
  • Participating in the further development of our products as an interface between customers and data and development teams;
  • Support in the expansion and long-term support of our customer portfolio.

Your profile Above all, we are looking for a personality who likes to understand connections, explain complex content in an understandable way and enjoy contact with people.

They shall bring with them

  • higher education, ideally in tourism, business, management or a comparable field;
  • Strong interest in tourism, hospitality and the challenges of tourist destinations;
  • pleasure in dealing with numbers and the ability to analyze and interpret metrics;
  • Strong communication skills and enjoyment of exchange with customers and partners;
  • ability to process complex information in a clear, structured and convincing manner;
  • self-employment, curiosity and initiative;
  • Very good knowledge of German in words and writing, which are absolutely necessary for the care of our German-speaking customers and partners;
  • Very good knowledge of French and the ability to communicate in English.

Initial professional experience in tourism, in the hotel industry, at a tourist destination, in consulting or in a technology company is an advantage. Knowledge of business intelligence tools such as Power BI is welcome, but not a requirement. Very good knowledge of German is a mandatory requirement for this job. Applications that do not meet this requirement can unfortunately not be considered.

What we offer

A versatile position at the interface between data analysis, tourism and the concrete needs of tourist destinations. They become part of a small team and make a direct contribution to the further development of solutions with which tourist players can better understand their business development and identify future changes at an early stage. The position offers varied tasks in the areas of data analysis, customer support, workshops, product development and sales. In addition, you have the opportunity to continuously develop your skills and gradually take on more responsibility in customer support and business development. We offer flexible working conditions with a high degree of personal responsibility and the opportunity to work mainly in the home office. Conditions of employment Employment rate: 50 % Place of work: Sitten (Sion) / Siders (Sierre), Wallis Work organization: One day of attendance per week in customs or siders is required.
