Full description

A Director of Works Since 2012, ASV Construction Générale SA has been active in the direction of works in Valais. Member of the MBA Groupe SA, a group of companies specialized in construction, we are committed to providing quality, personalized and human-sized service.

  • To strengthen our team and support the development of our projects, we seek:
  • A Director of Works
  • Key Position Information:
  • Type of contract: 100% indefinite contract.
  • Schedule: regular hours, Monday to Friday.
  • Date of entry: to be agreed.
  • Location: Rue du Châble\-Bet 41, 1920 Martigny. Buildings located mainly in Valais.
  • Your mission:

You pilot the execution of our construction sites, from preparation to handing over the keys. A reference speaker for all the speakers, you are the guarantor of respect for deadlines, budgets and quality.

Your main responsibilities

  • Plan and coordinate all trades on your construction sites
  • Prepare tenders, analyse bids and prepare bids
  • Financial monitoring of projects: budgets, situations, amendments and final accounts
  • Conducting and following up on worksite sessions
  • Ensure compliance with quality standards and on-site safety rules
  • Organize book receptions, site records and maintenance
  • Accompany the buyers of our promotions in their choices and their gains\-values
  • Work hand in hand with the companies of the group, which carry out a large part of the work.
  • Your profile:

We are looking for a person who is structured and self-sufficient, able to run several front-end projects without losing the wire and who knows how to keep calm when an unforeseen event occurs. You like the terrain as much as driving, you have a sense of contact with both the customer and the subcontractors. To succeed in this role, you have the following skills and qualities:

  • Solid experience of the construction site Construction training:
  • Building CFC, technician, construction operator, architect or equivalent
  • Ability to read and understand plans
  • Ability to maintain a budget
  • Organizational sense
  • Access to IT
  • Excellent mastery of French (written and oral)
  • Our team:

Join a versatile team that advances together in a family atmosphere, within a modern and pleasant working environment. Here, collaboration is natural, exchanges are direct and good mood is part of everyday life. Professionalism, commitment and friendliness form the balance that characterizes our team dynamics. What makes us different? Beyond skills, we believe that the work environment plays a key role in professional development. Here is what makes us different: Proximity: Thanks to our human-sized organization and proximity management, we cultivate strong and authentic relationships within our teams. Exchanges are regular, and the working environment remains both positive and motivating. Meals and team activities are regularly organized to strengthen cohesion and friendliness in everyday life. Comfort and efficiency: Our spacious and bright rooms, combined with quality equipment, offer our teams optimal working conditions to stay efficient and focused throughout the day. Our own real estate promotions: We are not just executing others' projects. We develop and carry out our own promotions, from bare ground to key delivery. So you follow the projects over their entire duration, with a real latitude of decision and the satisfaction of seeing what you have been leading from the beginning succeed. Our companies integrated into the group: masonry, windows and doors, tiled, much of the work is done by our own teams. In concrete terms, this means faster decisions, less dependence on external subcontractors, and people who pick up when you call. A difference that is measured every day on a site.
