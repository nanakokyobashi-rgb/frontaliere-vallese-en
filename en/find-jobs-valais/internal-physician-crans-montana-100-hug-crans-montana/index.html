Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. To learn more about our institution, take a few minutes to view our 2025 retrospective by clicking here . The Clinic of Crans-Montana, nestled in the heart of the Valais Alps is a hospital site of the HUG. Its hospital tasks consist of caring for patients for rehabilitations in general and psychosomatic internal medicine, multi-purpose post-operative or post-hospital rehabilitations in acute settings. Crans Montana Clinic holds FMH recognition for general internal medicine and trains internal physicians. She develops academic research projects related to the Faculty of Medicine at the University of Geneva.

Description of post

You assume responsibility under the supervision of the medical hierarchy, medical care of patients entrusted, practical activities, clinical and therapeutic interventions. Your sense of organization and ability to work effectively allow you to enforce internal rules and procedures while ensuring the quality of medical services provided. You follow post-graduate training in general internal medicine or you must complete an optional year in other post-graduate courses. Your taste for human contacts, teamwork and interdisciplinary collaboration allow you to contribute to the training of intern physicians. Building on your empathy and excellent communication capacity, you are involved in developing the overall and interdisciplinary care of the patient. You are contributing to the development of therapeutic teaching and various institutional projects within the Clinic demonstrating your sense of priorities and synthesis. You guarantee quality medical services, both clinically and administratively and organizationally. You are brought or required to perform picket services

Qualifications

You have a federal medical degree or equivalent recognized by the Federal Medical Professions Commission (MEBEKO) and you are undergoing post-graduate training in general internal medicine. An excellent command of spoken and written French is required. For non-French-speaking people, a C1 certificate is required. An experience in internal medicine in Switzerland is an asset.
