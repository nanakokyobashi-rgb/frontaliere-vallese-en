Full description

Arxada is a global leader in innovative solutions for protecting our world. Our groundbreaking technologies, comprehensive regulatory expertise, and production and process development capabilities support our customers in protecting food, health, and infrastructure efficiently through chemistry and biotechnology that promote sustainability.

  • We offer a broad portfolio of ingredients and services for various end markets, including Health & Nutrition, Household & Personal Care, Professional Hygiene, Paints & Coatings, and Wood Protection. With customers in over 100 countries, the company achieved a revenue of 2 billion CHF in 2024. The headquarters of Arxada is located in Basel (Switzerland). Globally, the company employs 3,200 people at 24 production sites and 14 R&D centers, all dedicated to our customers' success. To strengthen our team in Visp, Switzerland, we are looking for a Yard Service Specialist. Do you love it when everything runs exactly as planned – literally? Do you have experience in yard service and want to contribute where precision, teamwork, and reliability matter? Then we are looking for you! To strengthen our experienced yard team in Visp, we are looking for an engaged personality who thinks with us, takes action, and keeps the internal goods flows on track. ## Key
  • Responsibilities:
  • Timely delivery and collection of railway wagons at the appropriate transshipment stations
  • Distribution and assembly of train compositions
  • Recording of incoming and outgoing weighings
  • Independent coordination with internal partners such as production, forwarding, and disposition to ensure timely shipments
  • Availability for on-call duty to ensure continuous supply of operational areas
  • Coordination and collaboration with various interfaces such as plants, forwarding, train service, and internal logistics ##
  • Qualifications and Experience:
  • Yard service license category Ai40 is mandatory
  • License category A40 is an advantage (or willingness to complete the training)
  • Good knowledge of common office applications (e.g., Excel, Outlook)
  • Organizational talent, structured work method, and good physical condition
  • High flexibility, team spirit, and a high degree of initiative ## What We Offer Arxada offers top-tier solutions in two distinct areas:
  • Microbial Control Solutions (MCS) focuses on microbial threats to humans and the environment. This sector comprises five independent business segments: Professional Hygiene, Household & Personal Care, Wood Protection, Paints & Coatings, and Material Protection.
  • Nutrition, Care & Environmental (NCE) provides materials for the production of compounds in electronics, aerospace, and other industries. Additionally, the portfolio includes vitamins and nutritional components, high-performance chemical intermediates, and raw materials for a wide range of advanced applications.
