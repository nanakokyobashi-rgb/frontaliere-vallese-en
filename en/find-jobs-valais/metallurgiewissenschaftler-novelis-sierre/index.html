Our research and technology centre in Sierre, canton of Valais (Switzerland), is looking for a metallurgical specialist with expertise in the development of automotive alloys (series 5xxx and 6xxx) as well as basic research. You will be involved in the development of future generations of automotive aluminum products and the continuous improvement of manufacturing processes deployed within the global network of Novelis plants.

  • Lead strategic projects to develop new laminated aluminium products for the automotive sector, focusing on mechanical resistance, crash performance, formability and durability of alloys.

  • Provide technical expertise on the entire aluminium production chain, including hot rolling, cold rolling and finishing, to support production activities.
