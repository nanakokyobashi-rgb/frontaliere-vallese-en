We bring together what belongs together!

The Anker Group started in April 2021 with two people -- now we have already grown to 5 locations. Do you also want to be part of a future-oriented company?

Your challenge

This is what you bring with you

  • Compulsory self-employed and clean work according to plan
  • Good spatial imagination, craftsmanship
  • Motivation, personal responsibility and reliability are required
  • You are team capable and flexible
  • You are a doer(!) and open, in case of need on the man also briefly help in the workshop

What we offer you

  • Great working environment
  • Long-term deployments
  • Attractive employment conditions with good pay
  • A team that is there for you and your needs
