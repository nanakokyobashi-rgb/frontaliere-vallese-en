# Teamleader Production (m/f/d) #

To strengthen our team in Raron, we are looking for a responsible personality as Teamleader Manufacturing (m/f/d).

In this permanent position, the job owner assumes a central role in the production operation from immediately or by agreement.

In shift operation, she and the team ensure the timely production of high-quality products and help to provide patients with the required products reliably.

Teamleader (m/f/d) coordinates daily production processes, further develops employees and ensures compliance with quality, safety and production standards. Through its practical and solution-oriented working method, it actively contributes to the optimization of processes and the achievement of production goals.

TASKS

Key responsibilities include

  • Ensuring an efficient material flow and optimum personnel deployment
  • Planning and coordination of employee assignments (day, week and holiday planning)
  • Compliance with quality, safety and regulatory requirements
  • Coordination of troubleshooting and maintenance work on production facilities
  • Contact person in case of production deviations and take appropriate measures
  • Introduction, support and further development of employees and promotion of polyvalence
  • Monitoring tool consumption and scrap processing
  • Participation in employee assessments and cross-divisional projects
  • Ensuring order, cleanliness and workplace organisation in the production sector
  • Communication of production indicators and regular team meetings
  • Support for production when needed
  • Promoting an open, ethical and compliance corporate culture

Requirements

To be considered for this position, the ideal candidate brings:

  • Pre-apprenticeship, apprenticeship or similar practical training completed
  • First experience in the production environment, ideally in a coordinating or professional management function
  • Confident knowledge of German, every other national language is an advantage
  • Strong communication and organisational skills
  • Responsible and solution-oriented working method
  • Readiness for shift work

ANNEX II

  • Progressive employment conditions with a long-term perspective
  • A responsible and varied activity in a global company with a strong local presence
  • A high degree of personal responsibility and independent work
  • Freedom for creative thinking as well as personal and professional development
  • Attractive bonus program, additional holidays and other attractive benefits
