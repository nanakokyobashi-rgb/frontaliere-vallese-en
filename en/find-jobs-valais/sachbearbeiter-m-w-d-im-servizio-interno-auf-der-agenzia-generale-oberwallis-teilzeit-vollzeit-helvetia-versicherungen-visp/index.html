Workload: 50-100% Workplace: Experience level: With professional experience FlexOffice: on-site

Setting new standards together and shaping the future – for us, this means much more than simply completing tasks. Here you work with people who take responsibility, have high standards, and have the courage to break new ground. With us, you will find an environment where trust and performance go hand in hand, and where your ideas, initiative, and contribution count. This gives you the opportunity to truly make a difference for our customers, your team, and your personal development.

What you achieve

  • You actively support the field sales force throughout the entire sales process and thus contribute significantly to our mutual success.
  • As a central contact person, you answer technical and specialist questions in the areas of non-life private clients, non-life corporate clients, individual life, accident, health, occupational pensions (BVG), as well as retirement and asset management solutions.
  • You look after our private and corporate clients by phone, in writing, and in person with high professional competence and dedication.
  • With a feel for individual needs, you develop holistic, tailor-made concepts that optimally combine risk, retirement provision, and wealth.
  • Together with the team, you actively shape market development and sales promotion.
  • You accompany our apprentices in their daily professional lives and specifically promote their professional and personal development.

What you bring

  • Commercial insurance training and/or several years of experience with an all-lines insurer.
  • Sound knowledge in the areas of retirement provision and ideally also wealth solutions.
  • Enjoyment of direct customer contact and a pronounced service orientation.
  • Sales-oriented thinking and enthusiasm for developing customized customer solutions.
  • Efficient, solution-oriented cooperation with the field service and relevant specialist departments.
