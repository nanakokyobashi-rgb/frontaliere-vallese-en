Full description

Pure Clinics is a group of medical and dental clinics in Suisse Romande offering, with a high level of ethical requirements, medical and dental consultations, scheduled and urgent. Pure Clinic Group offers quality care in a comprehensive approach to health. We integrate the principles of holistic medicine, aimed at taking care of the patient's health as a whole, in addition to dental care.

  • Type of contract: indefinite

By joining our group, you will benefit from a modern working environment and the constant support of our teams dedicated to medical assistance and administrative management, allowing you to focus on your practice and your patients.

YOUR MISSIONS

  • •Take care of patients presenting themselves
  • •Ensure monitoring, balance sheets and treatments
  • •Collaborate with other health professionals
  • •Ensure that medical records are updated in accordance with regulations.

YOUR QUALIFICATIONS

  • •To the benefit of a federal diploma in medicine (General Internal Medicine or Medical Practitioner) or a qualification recognised by the Medical Professions Commission (MEBEKO)
  • •Authorisation of practice on the canton of Valais
  • •French minimum C1
  • •Polyvalence, organizational capacity and administrative ease

OUR ATOUTS

  • •Access to state-of-the-art technologies (impedancemeter, multi-frequency, ECG, MAPA, ...)
  • •External and internal training to support your ongoing professional development.
  • •A strong commitment to continuous improvement of our processes and care.
  • •Our clinics are also training sites for apprentices, thus contributing to the transmission of our know-how.
  • •An excellent team atmosphere, conducive to personal and professional development.
