Senior QA Specialist

Are you looking to make a meaningful impact on cutting\-edge pharmaceutical manufacturing? Join our dynamic team in Visp to champion quality excellence, tech transfer, and operational compliance in our state\-of\-the\-art facility.

This is a fully site‑based role. Working together in person supports close, real‑time collaboration and the technical precision needed to manufacture medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What you will get:

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace.
  • Competitive hourly rate.

What you will do:

  • Drive project\-specific QA operational oversight during commissioning, qualification, and tech transfer activities.
  • Make timely, risk\-based decisions on shop\-floor events and deviations to ensure continuous cGMP compliance.
  • Review and approve critical GMP production and batch records in accordance with approved procedures.
  • Assess, manage, and mitigate quality risks impacting routine GMP manufacturing using structured tools like FMEA.
  • Partner closely with cross\-functional teams across Manufacturing, Engineering, and Quality Control to align and execute planned milestones.
  • Participate actively in facility walkthroughs, internal audits, and regulatory inspections.
  • Serve as a quality mentor and trusted operational advisor on the production floor.

What we are looking for:

  • Bachelor of Science in Life Sciences or a related field (or equivalent 3\- 4 years of relevant industry experience).
  • Proven Quality Assurance or operational compliance experience within a regulated pharmaceutical cGMP environment (pharmaceutical background is essential
  • Demonstrated experience supporting shop\-floor manufacturing, qualification, or technical transfer projects.
  • Practical knowledge of quality risk management methodologies (e.g., FMEA) and electronic quality management/ERP systems (e.g., TrackWise, SAP).
  • Excellent collaboration and communication skills, with full professional fluency in English.

About Lonza

At Lonza, their people are their greatest strength. With 30\+ sites across five continents, their globally connected teams work together every day to manufacture the medicines of tomorrow. Their core values of Collaboration, Accountability, Excellence, Passion and Integrity reflect who they are and how they work together.
