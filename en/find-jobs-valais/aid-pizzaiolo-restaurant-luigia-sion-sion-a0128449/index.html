AID PIZZAIOLO WHO ARE WE?

Luigia is a group of fast growing Italian restaurants: 11 restaurants in Switzerland, 2 in Italy and 1 in Dubai.

Our identity is inspired by the cuisine of Italian grandmothers: respect for regional recipes, quality and authenticity of products, generosity, hospitality and a simple principle: not to waste anything, to value everything.

Luigia is a B Corp certified company, recognized for its social and environmental commitment. With 3 Spicchi Gambero Rosso, a presence among the world's top restaurants and a recognition in the 50 Top Pizza ranking, Luigia confirms every day its quality and passion for Italian cuisine.

THE ROLE

As Pizzaiolo Help, you will assist the pizzeria brigade in daily preparation and during service, guaranteeing order, cleanliness and respect of Luigia standards. You will work closely with Chef Pizzaïolo to ensure a smooth rhythm and constant quality.

RESPONSIBILITIES

  • Know the ingredients and products used in pizzeria.
  • Work effectively with Chief Pizzaiolo and the brigade.
  • Prepare for the daily establishment of the pizzeria station.
  • Prepare and cut the trim according to Luigia methods.
  • Provide pizza garnishing, exit control and support during service.
  • Take part in the low and baking stages of pizza according to internal procedures.
  • Know and apply Luigia standards in cooking, presentation and quality.
  • Know and meet HACCP.
  • Keep work plan, containers, utensils and production area clean.
  • Guarantee the cleanliness of the pizzeria post at the end of service.

PROFILE

  • A first experience in pizzeria or a strong motivation to learn.
  • Good dexterity, speed and attention to detail.
  • Serious, reliable and team spirit.
  • Organizational sense, cleanliness and hygiene rigour.
  • Knowledge and compliance with HACCP standards.

  • 3 days paid immersive experience to discover Luigia before starting.

CULTURE AND VALUES

We value diversity and mutual respect. At Luigia, we believe in teamwork, trust and talent development, providing an inclusive environment where everyone can express their personality and contribute to collective success.
