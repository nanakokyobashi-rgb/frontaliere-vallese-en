23.11.2026 Saxon

Be part of our team!

As a Funding Administration Collaborator, you ensure the processing and administrative follow-up of funding files and contribute to the quality of service provided to our clients.

What are you waiting for?

  • Full administrative processing of credit applications and financing.
  • Control of files, preparation of contractual documents and implementation of funding.
  • Management and monitoring of credit positions, guarantees and mortgage data.
  • Administrative support to client advisors in case processing.
  • Making necessary clarifications with advisors and external partners.
  • Contribution to the quality, compliance and effectiveness of credit processes.

What are you bringing?

  • Commercial, banking or equivalent training.
  • Experience in banking, ideally in the processing of credit or financing in Switzerland.
  • Rigor, sense of organization and high reliability.
  • Assistance in the processing of administrative files.
  • Team spirit and sense of service.
  • Excellent mastery of French, both oral and written.

You appreciate the demanding administrative tasks, be rigorous and precise, and want to contribute to the smooth running of mortgage financing for our customers? Join our team! Joëlle Chervaz Head of Finance Administration Why Raiffeisen?
