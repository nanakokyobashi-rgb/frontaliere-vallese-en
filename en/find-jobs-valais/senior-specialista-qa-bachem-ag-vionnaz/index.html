Full description

To strongthen our Quality Assurance team at our Vionnaz production site, we are looking for a Senior Specialist QA – Operations (a) 100%. In this role, you will help ensure GMP compliance across manufacturing activities while acting as a key quality partner for operational teams and site projects.

Your Responsibilities

  • Coordinate and perform batch release activities for finished products and intermediates.
  • Review and appropriate quality events, root cause analyses, CAPAs, and effectiveness checks.
  • Support and appropriate laboratory investigations.
  • Assess, coordinate, and approve changes requests.
  • Review and approve master manufacturing documentation.
  • Approved qualification protocols and reports for facilities and equipment.
  • Conducted internal and external audits and support customer and regulatory inspections.
  • Representative Quality Assurance in site projects and contribution to SOP creation and revision.

Your Profile

  • Masters degree in Engineering, Chemistry, Pharmacy, or related field.
  • Minimum 5 years of experience in the pharmaceutical or chemical industry; QA Operations experience is highly desirable.
  • Strong knowledge of GMP requirements and their practical application in a manufacturing environment.
  • Ability to independently assess issues, make sound decisions, and appropriately escalate risks.
  • Solution-oriented mindset with a strong customer and service focus.
  • Excellent organizational and communication skills.
  • Fluent in English and French, both written and spoken.
  • German language skills are considered an asset.

Our Offerer

  • A modern and dynamic working environment within a fast-growing international company.
  • Challending projects of high scientific and technical value.
  • Opportunities for professional development and continuing professional development.
  • A corporate culture based on collaboration, innovation and excellence.
  • Attractive working conditions and competitive employee benefits.
