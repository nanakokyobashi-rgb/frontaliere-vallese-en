for the biomedical maintenance unit of the Biomedical and Purchasing Service. Mission:

Attached to the head of the biomedical maintenance unit, with a strong link with the CHVR operating department in the prioritization of tasks, you are part of a team of 6 people to manage the park including laboratory medical devices, as well as their computer connection and their compliance.
