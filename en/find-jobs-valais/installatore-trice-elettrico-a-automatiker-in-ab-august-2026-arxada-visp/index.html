Full description

Arxada is a global leader in innovative solutions to protect our world. Our groundbreaking technologies, extensive regulatory expertise and manufacturing and process development expertise help our customers efficiently protect nutrition, health and infrastructure through chemistry and biotechnology that promote sustainability.

We offer a broad portfolio of ingredients and services for various end markets, including health & nutrition, household and personal care, professional hygiene, paints & coatings, and wood protection. With customers in over 100 countries, the company achieved a turnover of CHF 2 billion in 2024. The headquarters of Arxada is located in Basel (Switzerland). Worldwide, the company employs 3,200 people at 24 production sites and 14 R&D centers, all committed to the success of our customers.

To strengthen our team at Arxada in Visp, Switzerland, we are looking for an electrical installer/automatic: from August 2027.

Arxada offers world-class solutions in two clearly defined areas:

Microbial Control Solutions (MCS) focuses on threats from microorganisms to humans and the environment. This area comprises five independent business areas: Professional hygiene, household and personal care, wood protection, paints & coatings and material protection.

Nutrition, Care & Environmental (NCE) supplies materials for manufacturing composites in electronics, aerospace and other industries. In addition, the portfolio includes vitamins and nutrient components, chemical intermediates with high performance and raw materials for a variety of demanding applications.
