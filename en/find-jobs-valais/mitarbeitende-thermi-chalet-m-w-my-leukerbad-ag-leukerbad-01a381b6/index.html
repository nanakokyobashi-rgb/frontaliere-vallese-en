Full description

My Leukerbad AG is the operating organisation of Leukerbad’s most important service providers. These include the Leukerbad Therme, the torrent railways, the sports arena, the Snowpark Sportarena, the LLB Verkehrsbetriebe Leuk-Leukerbad and Leukerbad Tourism.

The restauration facilities of the sports arena include a served restaurant for individual guests and sports groups with terrace and a Thermi-Chalet with self-service offer for the ski guests of the Snowpark, the adjacent family ski area. The offer is supplemented by food supplies for the daycare center, the lunch table and the old-age apartments as well as by the implementation of banquets.

For the coming winter season we are looking for you as a reinforcement in the Thermi-Chalet.

  • Compliance and execution of the HACCP concept

Your profile

  • clean and structured functioning
  • Flexibility and resilience
  • friendly attitude towards guests and within the team
  • very good knowledge of German and French
