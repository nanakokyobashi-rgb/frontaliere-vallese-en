Full description

Crans-Montana Food and Beverage SA (CMFB) is looking for a (M/F) Cashier for one of its restaurants and self-service in Crans-Montana.

Your missions: to welcome customers, record items and make receipts. Ensure opening and closing operations, verify the accuracy of transactions and ensure the cleanliness of your workspace. Contribute to the fluidity of cash flow during periods of high influx.

Your profile: first cashier experience appreciated. Rigor, reliability, ease with numbers and computer tools. Feeling welcome, speed and team spirit. Good command of French; a foreign language is an asset.
