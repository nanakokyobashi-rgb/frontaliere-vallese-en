Full description

Your tasks

As a production manager (f/m/div.), you are responsible for production control in the production department. You are the link between logistics, purchasing and production. Your tasks essentially include:

  • The processing of appointment requests and the weekly capacity planning fall within your area of responsibility
  • You guarantee material and product availability in production
  • You work closely with production, purchasing and logistics to ensure smooth processes
  • You analyze production key figures and derive optimization measures from them
  • The examination of the wrong material and the replacement planning round off your area of responsibility

Your profile

  • Training: Basic technical training (e.g. polymechanic, automatic technician (f/m/div.)) ideally with motivation for further training as process specialist (f/m/div.)
  • Personality and working method: Communicative, team-capable and resilient personality with a structured working method
  • Experience and know-how: Experience in production technology of advantage, secure application of the MS Office pallets as well as good SAP knowledge desirable
  • Languages: Good communication and expressiveness in German
