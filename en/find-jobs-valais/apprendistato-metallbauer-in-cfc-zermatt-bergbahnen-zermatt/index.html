About us

Jobs and careers

Metalworkers manufacture and assemble various metal parts for buildings. These include, for example, facades, metal scaffolding, windows, doors, railings, stairs or fences. They manufacture the parts either from prefabricated profiles or to measure in the workshop, transport them to the construction site and assemble them on site. They also repair and replace damaged metal structures.

And best of all: you can learn and implement all this at the Zermatt Bergbahnen!
