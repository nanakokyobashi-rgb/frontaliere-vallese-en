Full description

Do you want to engage in a meaningful role within a human-oriented professional environment? Join our social service in Saxon and use your daily skills in a dynamic and committed team.

Position to be filled

A 60% Social Assistant

Your daily role

You accompany and support your beneficiaries with different profiles according to their needs and problems. You set up social and professional integration projects with them and support them in their administrative processes. To do this, you conduct interviews and participate in networks. You will have to carry out various administrative tasks, including reports, policy contracts and procedures related to social insurance. You are working in a framework and organization oriented training and projects while being part of a caring multidisciplinary team. You use the ASP software to track social assistance files.

Your profile

  • Bachelor of social work or equivalent training;
  • Practical experience in the field of Valaisan social assistance;
  • Good knowledge of the Valais social health network;
  • Experience in socio-professional integration would be an asset;
  • Collaboration with institutional partners (CII);
  • Rigor and methodology in social and administrative work;
  • Initiative and synthesis, listening ability and availability.

What we offer you

  • A varied and meaningful activity, in a changing sector;
  • A dynamic and collaborative working environment;
  • Mobile and modern work tools;
  • A framework for the reconciliation of private and professional life;
  • opportunities for further training and skills development;
  • Attractive social benefits and benefits;
  • Working conditions consistent with the LTC SLD.

Practical information

  • Entry into service: January 1, 2027 or to be agreed
  • Deadline for application: 10 December 2026

We look forward to receiving your full application file (CV, cover letter, diplomas, etc.) via our online application platform.
