EI\&C Construction Lead (m/f/d)

As an EI\&C Construction Lead in Visp, you will lead on-site execution, lead specialized subcontractors and form the interface between engineering and operations for strategically important CAPEX\ projects for the production of life-saving drugs under a 12\-month contract.

This is a fully location-based role. On-site personal collaboration supports close real-time cooperation and the technical precision required to produce medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What to expect

  • An agile working environment and a dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace.
  • A competitive hourly rate.

The remuneration for this position shall be based on the qualifications, skills, experience and relevant knowledge of the candidate.

What you will do

  • Coordination of daily electrical, measuring, control and control technology (EMSR/EI\&I) construction activities on site for the timely handling of CAPEX\ projects.
  • Control and monitoring of electrical, instrumentation and automation companies as well as suppliers at the site.
  • Ensure strict compliance with all requirements regarding occupational safety (HSE), GMP, quality and technical standards.
  • Examination of construction directly on the basis of engineering\ drawings, flow diagrams (P\&IDs) and electrical circuit diagrams.
  • Interface management and close coordination between the teams from engineering, commissioning and operation.
  • Support for loop tests (loop checks), function checks, SAT (site acceptance tests), troubleshooting (punch\ list) and system transfers.
  • Identification and solution of technical and structural issues directly on site to ensure project progress.

What you bring

  • Technical education or studies in electrical engineering, automation, mechatronics, MSR\-technology or a comparable discipline.
  • Demonstrable experience in managing EI\&I\- / EMSR\ construction activities in industry\-, chemical\-, bio\- or pharmaceutical\-CAPEX\ projects.
  • In-depth understanding of installation practices in electrical and MSR technology as well as the ability to safely read P&IDs and schematics.
  • Experience in managing contractors, subcontractors and external partners on the construction site.
  • Professional experience in a GMP\-regulated environment is beneficial.
  • Fluent German language skills in word and writing are required
  • English skills are beneficial.
