Full description

Key responsibilities Looking to take your first steps in the exciting world of finance? Do you like to advise customers on the phone? Do you want to work in a dynamic team? Do you have a strong interest in future technologies?

This position not only offers an excellent opportunity to join our bank, but also provides a solid basis for developing your career at UBS. The Customer Solution Switzerland (CSS) team will be the launch of an excellent career and will allow you, after about 2 years, to develop within the organisation.

The position is to be filled in our centres in Renens, Carouge and Sion.

To ensure optimal integration and training, an activity rate of 100% is desired during the first four weeks. Nevertheless, we are aware that some candidates, including students, have a compulsory school day and usually work at 80%. These situations can be assessed individually. Subsequently, you will work, as agreed, between 80% and 100%. However, Monday must be worked out.

The team You work in the PB Client Front Desk for Private Clientele at the Contact Center Switzerland in Renens, Sion or Carouge. The Customer Solutions Switzerland Hub is our hub. From there, we advise our customers based in Switzerland on all kinds of questions. Together, we ensure that our clients receive competent support for their requests. We offer flexible working hours and teleworking options. We appreciate the open feedback culture, clear processes and casual dress code. The Customer Solutions Switzerland Hub is an ideal starting point for acquiring a wide range of specialized knowledge and offers a springboard to other domains or to UBS agencies. You will be prepared and supported in this role in a professional way by your colleagues during integration training.

Your skills and experience

  • completed banking apprenticeship or equivalent training, an experience in a call center is an asset
  • fun to work in a dynamic environment and interest for customer contact
  • passion for sales and talent to inspire and convince your interlocutors
  • ability to explain complex problems in a simple and understandable way
  • ease of efficient use of IT tools
  • speak fluently French and speak another national language (D / IT) and English (min. B2)
  • empathy, resilience and flexibility in response to different customer needs
