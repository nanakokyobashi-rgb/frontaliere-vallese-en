Factory employee: in the power and frequency converter plant Massaboden Bitsch 60-100% | SBB - - - - - - Move Switzerland with us. Works employee at the Massaboden Bitsch power and frequency converter plant Bitsch, October 1st, 2026 or by arrangement, 60-100% Apply now

You can look forward to this. In this role you will work in a collegial team of 6 employees. Together you will ensure the smooth operation of our systems and thus make an important contribution to a sustainable energy supply for SBB. The Massaboden plant is one of several that reliably supply rail operations in Switzerland with electricity. You can find out more about SBB Energie at this link. With us you benefit from attractive working conditions and numerous benefits. This includes free GA, discounted travel abroad – also for your family – and other modern employment conditions. You gain additional free time by compensating for on-call duty allowances. Alternatively, you can have part of these allowances paid out. This means you benefit from more flexibility and can organize your free time according to your needs. You can move that.

  • You carry out various maintenance work on our high and low voltage systems in the Massaboden power and converter plant in Bitsch as well as the associated water systems and substations.
  • In this role you will carry out repair and inspection work. This also includes cleaning and maintenance work on and around the buildings.
  • With the necessary calm and overview, you locate, analyze and resolve any problems that arise. In the event of major disruptions, you coordinate internal and external specialists.
  • In addition to routine tasks such as inspection tours or inspections, you like the variety. This includes help with renovations or new buildings as well as other special tasks.
  • You attach great importance to safety and quality and actively contribute to the continuous optimization of work processes.

This is what you bring with you.

  • As part of a small team, you enjoy working independently, are reliable and helpful. You like to take responsibility and are open to change.
  • You enjoy and are interested in developing yourself in a targeted manner and constantly expanding your investment-specific knowledge.
  • You live in the region and can therefore adhere to the specified intervention times during your on-call duty. In order to be able to carry out your on-call duty, you must have a category B driving license.
  • We require a completed vocational apprenticeship in the mechanical or electrical engineering field.
  • Very good knowledge of spoken and written German (C1) is required
  • Knowledge of a second national language is a plus.
