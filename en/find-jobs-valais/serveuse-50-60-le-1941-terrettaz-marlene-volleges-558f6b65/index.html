Waitress 50\-60% \- Join Team 1941

Are you passionate about customer service and want to work in a warm and friendly environment? The 1941 seeks a motivated, dynamic, reliable and conscientious waitress to join his team at a 50\-60% activity rate.

Responsibilities

  • Welcome customers with a smile.
  • Take orders and advise customers on dishes and drinks.
  • Provide timely and quality service while ensuring customer satisfaction.
  • Prepare and serve drinks.
  • Maintain cleanliness and organization of the room and work spaces.
  • Prepare the dishes on the menu: fondue, tartar, planks...

Qualifications

  • Prior experience as a waitress or in a similar role, an asset.
  • Excellent communication skills and customer service.
  • Ability to work effectively in a dynamic environment.
  • Good command of French (oral), other languages are a plus.
  • Flexibility for working in variable hours, including evenings and Saturdays, Sunday leave.
  • Positive attitude, team spirit and careful presentation.

Benefits

  • A warm and friendly working environment.
  • A dynamic and passionate team.
  • An opportunity to develop your skills in catering.
  • A unique and welcoming setting within our establishment.

Information to apply

If you are interested in this position and would like to join our team, we would be happy to receive your application. We are looking for a motivated, smiling person who is ready to offer quality service to our customers.
