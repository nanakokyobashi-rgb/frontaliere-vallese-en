Building Manager

You have a solid experience in real estate management and appreciate as much contact with tenants as technical and administrative monitoring of a portfolio of objects? Do you want to put your skills at the service of a recognised company and actively contribute to the valorisation of its real estate assets? Join us and become a key player on our team.

Specifically?

  • Thanks to your real estate expertise, you offer solutions for the maintenance, renovation or enhancement of the buildings you are responsible for and coordinate the various works. You're taking part in the condominium meetings.
  • You ensure the rental management of the objects, from the organization of visits to the establishment of the leases, through the state of the premises.
  • You manage real property sales processes or effectively oversee external stewardship mandates.
  • You ensure the quality of the administrative monitoring, establish the necessary reporting and participate in the administrative tasks related to the building department.
  • Your organizational sense and rigor allow you to ensure professional follow-up of the files while ensuring the satisfaction of the various interlocutors.

The challenge is tempting you? This post is for you if...

  • You have a commercial training in real estate or equivalent training.
  • The Federal Certificate of Building Manager is an important asset.
  • You benefit from a confirmed experience of a few years in a similar function.
  • You have an excellent sense of negotiation and professionally defend the interests of the company.
  • With a real sense of contact, you appreciate exchanges with a variety of people and easily create relationships of trust.
  • You have excellent knowledge of German in oral and written language, which is an important asset in your professional exchanges.
  • You have a driving licence and a private vehicle.
  • Finally, you stand out for your autonomy, initiative and ability to manage multiple files simultaneously.

Let's meet! Would you like to join us? We look forward to discovering your application. To do this, send your complete file to: recruitment@bcvs.ch .
