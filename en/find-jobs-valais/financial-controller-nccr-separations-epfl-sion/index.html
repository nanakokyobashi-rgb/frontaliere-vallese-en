Role overview

Mission

The National Research Centre "CNCR Separation" is a national Swiss project financed mainly by the Swiss National Fund.

It brings together many high-level researchers from across the country and aims to develop new techniques in separation science and to accelerate technology transfer in order to achieve Switzerland's sustainable development goals and accelerate progress towards global climate neutrality.

  • Main tasks and responsibilities Financial management
  • Provide financial management and accounting for the NCCR Separations project.
  • Participate in budget development, monitoring and piloting in close collaboration with NCCR management and Program Managers.
  • Ensure budgetary monitoring of the various partner institutions and prepare internal and external financial reports.
  • Manage the collection, audit, reimbursement and accounting of project expenditures in collaboration with all partners.
  • Collect, monitor and maintain the financial data required for interim and final reports to the Swiss National Fund (NSF) via the reporting portal.
