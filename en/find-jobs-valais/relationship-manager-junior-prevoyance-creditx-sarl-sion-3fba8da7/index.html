Relationship Manager Junior \- Planning (M/F)

RELATIONSHIP MANAGER JUNIOR (M/F) — SION Salary fixed CHF 100,000 /year \+ bonus · AFA training · Fintech in Sion

Develop your expertise. Build lasting relationships.

CreditX is a fintech specialized in foresight, which combines personalized advice and digital tools. We support our clients in understanding their situation and developing solutions tailored to their disability, death and retirement needs.

We are looking for a Junior Relationship Manager who wants to learn the business, develop a portfolio of private clients and participate in CreditX's growth.

POSITION IN BRIEF

  • Location: Sion, on site.
  • Activity rate: 100 %.
  • Salary: CHF 100,000 annual fixed gross, independent of your commercial production.
  • Bonus: based on results, according to objectives and a scale defined in advance.
  • Training: course preparation for AFA qualification and field support.
  • Profile: experience in sales or customer relationship
  • Vocational retraining welcome.

VOTRE ROLE

You develop your customers and build relationships of trust over time. As you train and qualify, you are gradually taking charge of needs analysis, advice and case follow-up.

Your main responsibilities

  • Develop your portfolio through active prospecting, your network and customer recommendations.
  • Understand the personal, professional and financial situation of each client.
  • Identify foresight needs and present suitable solutions.
  • Support clients in their efforts and ensure rigorous follow-up.
  • Reassessment of needs during changes in circumstances.
  • Contribute to improving the customer experience and CreditX tools.

The acquisition of new clients is an essential dimension of the position. Contacts generated by CreditX can also be entrusted to you to support your business development.

VOTRE TRAINING

Do you want to move towards foresight without still having experience in this field? We accompany you in learning the profession.

You follow a course preparing for the AFA qualification and benefit from a local supervision: acquisition of knowledge, preparation of interviews and field support.

You gradually develop the technical and relational skills necessary to exercise with autonomy, rigour and professionalism.

VOTRE PROFIL

  • A first experience in sales, business development or customer relationship.
  • A strong interest in foresight and financial services.
  • The taste of prospecting and the ability to develop a network.
  • Initiative, sense of responsibility and good organization.
  • An ease with digital tools.
  • The motivation needed to complete demanding training and acquire new expertise.

An insurance experience is an asset, but is not indispensable. The post is open to persons undergoing retraining.

REjoIN CREDITX

You join a fintech where human advice and technology complement each other. Our tools support you in the analysis and monitoring of your customers' foresight. Your experience in the field contributes to the evolution of our services and application.

You have a fixed salary, a training course and a gradual assumption of responsibility within a developing company.

POSTIL

Please send your application exclusively via our website

All applications will be treated in a strictly confidential manner.
