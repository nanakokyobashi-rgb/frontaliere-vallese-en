Entry into office: 1 December 2026 Activity rate: 40% - 60% The Romance Rehabilitation Clinic (CRR) in Sion is a leading player in the field of specialized rehabilitation in Switzerland. A true pillar of the integrated care chain, it works closely with university hospitals and specialized centres to provide coordinated, innovative and high-quality care, focusing on functional recovery and the sustainable reintegration of patients. In order to complete our existing team (5) of the Technical Foot Orthopedics Workshop , we are looking for our future : Bottier-orthopedist (activity rate from 40% to 60%) Your main missions Make plantar supports and various orthopaedic adaptations. Change orthopedic standard shoes. Completely make custom shoes, simple or complex. Care for outpatients and/or hospitalized patients. Participate in the various multidisciplinary symposia. Participate in the management of the stock of equipment, the maintenance of machines and the invoicing of the services performed (via eOpale). Our CFC Expectations of Orthopaedic Botteries (or title deemed equivalent at the federal level) Ideally, 3 to 5 year post-training work experience Ability to work independently and in teams Very good communication skills, manual skill and a sense of precision French language proficiency, both oral and written (minimum B2 level) Our offer

In addition to the many social benefits and opportunities for development, you are in a multidisciplinary sector that enriches and values.
