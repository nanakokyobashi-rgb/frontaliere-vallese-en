Full description

Where necessary, eligible candidates and their families will be assisted in moving.

Become part of our engineering team as an investment engineer 80-100% (m/f/d) in Visp. As a plant engineer Ibex®, you are responsible for ensuring the availability of the plant(s) or sub-plant(s) allocated to you, taking into account cost-optimal operational engineering. You are responsible for the professional and economical implementation of maintenance, engineering and conversions. You contribute significantly to the ongoing optimization of processes, plants and internal processes. In addition, you are jointly responsible for ensuring the necessary technical knowledge of the operators of the plants.

What we offer you

  • An Agile Career and Dynamic Work Culture
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • In addition to a competitive salary, you can expect numerous benefits in the areas of lifestyle, family and leisure. The full list of our global services can also be found at: Benefits - Visp

Your tasks

  • Ensure a professional and economical implementation of the maintenance with at the same time sufficient plant availability, including dealing with the authorities (e.g.: SVTI etc.)
  • Preparation and coordination of maintenance work with workshops
  • Search for opportunities for improvement in the processes, facilities and processes, as well as in the knowledge of the staff
  • Maintaining safety, hygiene, environment, quality (cGMP) regarding technical facilities
  • Use of the possibilities offered in SAP for placing orders and the provided technical-commercial evaluations as well as capacity planning to optimize plant and personnel use
  • Cooperation in monitoring and complying with the available budget (maintenance, conversions and investments)
  • Ongoing update of R&I flowcharts, zone plans and installation plans
  • Continuous recording and maintenance of the data of technical locations, equipment and spare parts, associated parts lists, work instructions, maintenance of the history and mutations in SAP
  • GMP fair change management using the systems provided

Your requirement profile

  • Completed training (ETH/FH/HF) in the field of mechanical engineering / process engineering or the like
  • Professional experience ideally in the biotech/pharmaceutical environment
  • Good knowledge of GMP, COMOS (Plant Engineering Software), SAP - an advantage
  • Independent and flexible working, strong teamwork
  • Structured, focused and very well organized work behavior
  • Solution-oriented and open to the new ideas
  • Flowing German and good English skills
