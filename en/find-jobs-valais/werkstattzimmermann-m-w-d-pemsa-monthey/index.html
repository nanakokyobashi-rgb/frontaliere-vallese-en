Your assignment

  • Read technical drawings and prepare the pieces necessary for production.
  • Perform cutting, processing and assembly of wood elements.
  • Use conventional machines and CNC equipment.
  • Adjust and use the circular saw, the toupie, the planer and the flatterer.
  • Make custom furniture and furnishings.
  • Paste, sanding and finishing.
  • Check the quality and precision of the elements made.

And now we're talking about you?

  • At least 2 years of experience in the carpentry sector in Switzerland.

  • Experience with workshop machinery and CNC equipment.
  • Minimum French B2 level.
  • Compulsory category B driving licence.
  • Methodical person, autonomous and attentive to details.
  • Residence located in a maximum radius of 30 km from the workplace.
