Financial Officer \- Valais

Our client, operating in a complex and demanding environment, is looking for a Financial Manager to strengthen his Financial Directorate.

Your responsibilities

  • \- Pilot all financial and analytical accounting.
  • \- Supervise general accounting activities, suppliers, capital assets and billing.
  • \- Develop multi-year budgets, forecasts and financial plans.
  • \- Prepare reports and analyses for management and governance bodies.
  • \- Work closely with Controlling and the various internal and external interlocutors.
  • \- Manager a team of unit managers.
  • \- To support the evolution of accounting processes and practices.

Your profile

  • Advanced Training in Finance/Accounting: Finance Expert, Accountant Expert or Federal Patent.
  • \- Solid experience in a similar function, with a managerial experience.
  • \- Current French and good knowledge of German essentials.
  • \- Excellent mastery of Swiss accounting standards.
  • \- Leadership affirmed, ability to federate and support change.
  • \- Excellent analytical, communication and writing skills.

We offer you

  • \- A strategic and highly accountable function.
  • \- A stimulating and transformative environment.
  • \- Real autonomy and proximity to the Directorate.
  • \- Taking up position ideally in January 2027\.

Want to join a major Valais organisation and put your expertise at the service of a high impact project? Apply in complete confidentiality.
