A unique and rewarding professional experience! Lemco Précision SA is a major player in contacts production in connectivity industry, since 1965. our parts are assembled on various connectors integrated in critical electronic systems used in various sectors such as Civil Aviation , Aerospace & Defence , Medical , Transport (non-automotive), Telecommunications and many others. We have a qualified team of over 220 employees and we offer a pleasant working environment as well as opportunities for further training. In order to remain a major player in the connector sector, we believe that we must continuously research, consolidate and improve our capabilities, acquire new skills, and innovate. For this reason, we are constantly on the lookout for new talent. If you are looking for a rewarding experience and are ready to discover new adventures, consult our offers now and don't hesitate to apply. We are now looking for a: Décolleteur CNC (h/f) (Description in French) Experienced 09/14/2026 We are a Swiss screw machining company that has specialised in the manufacture of precision contacts for the international connector industry since 1965. We have a qualified team of 220 employees and offer a pleasant working environment as well as opportunities for further training. To complete our team we are looking for a Décolleteur CNC

Required profile

  • Diplôme de décolleteur, mécapraticien option décolletage ou mécanicien de production option décolletage
  • Expérience sur des machines CNC
  • Esprit d’équipe et d’initiative, capable de travailler de manière autonome et précise au sein d’une équipe dynamique et motivée
  • Consciencieux, ayant le sens du détail et le souci de la qualité des pièces produites

Responsibilities

  • Fabrication d’outillage
  • Réglage et préparation des machines, mise en train

  • Contrôle des pièces

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
