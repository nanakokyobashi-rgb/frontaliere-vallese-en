Full description

The Domus Foundation, a Valaisan institution for psychosocial rehabilitation, accompanies adults facing major mental disorders.

Nearly 150 collaborators engage with conviction and professionalism.

Our vision: self-determination for everyone, a solution-centric approach and professional practices marked by goodwill and responsibility.

We suggest you

  • provide accompanied persons and employees with healthy, tasty and quality meals with products exclusively labeled BIO;
  • provide the necessary ingredients and recipes for meals prepared within the accommodation;
  • transmit weekly menu planning to the host;
  • provide meals or snacks during institutional events; comply with the distribution protocol and enforce HACCP standards.

We offer

  • join a foundation guided by participatory management, positive dynamics and a strong spirit of openness;
  • to integrate an institution certified by Promotion Santé Suisse, concerned with the well-being of everyone and the teams where listening, collaboration, mutual assistance and friendliness are cultivated;
  • to take your place in a structure that regularly carries out many projects to which everyone can contribute; to benefit from a pleasant working environment and good social and wage conditions;
  • Continuing training every year via our training catalogue "Acting Together".

Your profile

  • you benefit from a cook CFC and from a wide professional experience, training in dietetics is an asset;
  • with a sense of organisation, management of priorities, you like to work as a team and independently;
  • you master the usual computer tools (MS Office);
  • you have a private vehicle.
