Full description

Your tasks

As Project Manager Mechanical Engineering (f/m/div.), you clarify the goal, the budget and the project durations at the beginning of a project. completion dates. You plan the individual steps, distribute tasks, coordinate the project teams and motivate them. When implementing projects, you make sure to achieve partial steps on time and to meet quality requirements and budget. You explain the tasks and goals to your team members. You also inform the clients and supervisors about the status of the projects. You document all steps and create the final documentation after the project ends. Your tasks essentially include:

  • The technical and commercial project planning of your own building plants as well as the timely commissioning are among your responsibilities.
  • You hand over the plant to production and work on open points such as quality management, documentation or occupational safety
  • You are responsible for budget planning as well as tracking and compliance with project costs
  • Capacity planning and coordination with external companies or suppliers fall within your area of responsibility
  • You support mechanical engineering in the procurement of assemblies or standard parts as well as in commercial topics that follow
  • In the project management tool you maintain the project status together with the team and their superiors and develop the tool continuously.

Your profile

  • Training: Commercial or technical basic training with further training as an operating specialist / operational technician (m/f/div.) or a higher specialist training in mechanical engineering / business administration
  • Personality and working methods: Flexible, solution-oriented and communicative personality with a high level of independence and initiative and international willingness to travel (weekly up to six times a year)
  • Experience and know-how: Experience in project management as well as very good MS-Office skills are advantageous
  • Languages: Good communication and expressiveness in German and English
