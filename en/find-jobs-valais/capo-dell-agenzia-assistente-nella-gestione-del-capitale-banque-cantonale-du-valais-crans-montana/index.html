Full description

You benefit from a higher level of business training and have an experience in banking? Are you looking for an opportunity to enhance your skills, to support our clients in the realization of their projects and to contribute to the development of our Crans-Montana agency? This position may be your chance. # Mansioni

  • Your mission will be to represent the bank with the customers attached to your agency.
  • You identify and exploit the potential of existing customers.
  • You lead the sale within your agency, lead the prospecting and acquisition of new customers and find the right motivational resources to achieve the set sales goals.
  • The aim will be to identify and exploit the potential of existing Swiss and foreign customers and prospects.
  • You will develop and maintain relationships of trust and proximity with customers through regular personalized contacts.
  • You will support your clients in choosing an investment strategy for each portfolio and alert them when the risk level of the strategy diverges from its risk profile.
  • You will continuously train on changes to the guidelines and develop your knowledge of the services and products available to this type of customer. ## Required
  • You have superior business training and banking experience with excellent relational skills.
  • Your journey offers you a proven experience in customer monitoring and management.
  • Your communication and negotiation skills allow you to fulfill the objectives set.
  • You are involved in the regional economic fabric and have a wide network allowing the development of our customer portfolio.
  • Dynamic and pragmatic, you benefit from a strong sense of initiative and contribute to teamwork.
  • You have a great sense of organization and are able to act quickly in unforeseen situations.
  • You have good English and Italian language skills, any other language is an asset. ## Contatto
  • To do this, send your complete file to Marisa Berclaz, our HR Business Partner, at recruitment@bcvs.ch .
