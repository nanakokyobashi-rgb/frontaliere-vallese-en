Administration \& Shipment Support

Are you looking to leverage your lab background and logistics skills in a dynamic healthcare setting? Located in Visp, this is a 60% site\-based role

Site\-based \- 60%

This role is primarily based on site. Working together in person supports close, real\-time collaboration and the technical precision needed to manufacture medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What you will get:

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace.

What you will do:

  • Directly support the global distribution of critical testing samples, enabling timely release of healthcare products.
  • Collaborate across multidisciplinary teams including QC laboratories, project managers, supply chain, and external logistics partners.
  • Prepare, manage, and audit key export and shipping documentation required for national and international material movements.
  • Execute sample dispatch operations strictly adhering to international GMP guidelines and laboratory safety standards.
  • Proactively track material shipments, identify potential customs or transport delays, and drive real\-time resolution.
  • Contribute to continuous process improvements within sample management and administrative quality systems.
  • Build hands\-on operational leadership by streamlining laboratory workflow logistics and mentoring cross\-functional peers on shipping protocols.

What we are looking for:

  • Proven practical experience working within a laboratory or scientific production environment.
  • Prior operational experience with sample management, material tracking, or technical supply chain logistics.
  • Solid understanding or practical exposure to a GMP\-regulated work environment.
  • Completed vocational qualification as a Laboratory Technician (EFZ) or an equivalent technical/scientific educational background.
  • Professional fluency in both German and English (oral and written) is essential for daily stakeholder communication and documentation.
  • Strong proficiency in standard MS Office tools; experience with enterprise systems ( , LIMS, SAP) is advantageous.
  • Excellent organizational and priority\-setting skills with a structured, quality\-minded, and solution\-oriented approach.
