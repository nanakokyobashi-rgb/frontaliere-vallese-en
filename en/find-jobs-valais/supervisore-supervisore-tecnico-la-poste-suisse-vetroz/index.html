Full description

Are you interested in maintaining and operating complex technical facilities? Do you like to intervene quickly to resolve incidents and ensure continuity of operation? In this varied function, you actively contribute to the monitoring, maintenance and troubleshooting of sorting facilities. With your practical sense and mind oriented solutions, you bring real added value to the operation of the site.

Make a difference

  • You ensure the technical monitoring of the operation and the monitoring of the installations by working in an alternating team system comprising a week of day and a week of night.
  • In addition, you perform the first operations in the event of a breakdown and solve the first level malfunctions in order to ensure the smooth running of the operation.
  • You understand the troubles that require technical intervention in SAP PM and ensure rigorous follow-up of interventions.
  • In addition, you accompany and support internal and external technicians and technicians during assembly, maintenance and repair work, allowing you to actively contribute to the success of the interventions.
  • You perform the planned maintenance as well as simple mechanical repairs as part of your skills.
  • In addition, you regularly monitor the safety features and ensure the technical cleaning of the installations, allowing you to guarantee optimal operating conditions.

What you bring

  • You ideally have basic training in a technical or mechanical field.
  • In addition, you have strong technical skills in the mechanical and/or electrical fields, allowing you to effectively bring your expertise.
  • You are able to diagnose faults and solve first-level malfunctions independently and responsibly.
  • In addition, the tools to track interventions, including SAP PM, allow you to effectively contribute to operations.
  • You show initiative, a common sense of analysis and a solution-oriented approach.
  • Very good knowledge of French and a good mastery of MS-Office complete your profile.
