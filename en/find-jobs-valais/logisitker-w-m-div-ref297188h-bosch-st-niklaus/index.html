Full description

Your tasks

As a logistician (f/m/div.) you are responsible in the 2 shift in the area of finished goods warehouses and form the link between production and logistics. Your tasks essentially include:

  • Incoming and outgoing goods bookings of the saleable products are part of your task
  • You pack the finished goods into the intended outgoing packaging
  • You are responsible for loading the truck (internal and external customers)
  • Creating the transport lists rounds off your area of responsibility

Your profile

  • Training: Completed training as a logistician EFZ or corresponding experience
  • Personality and working method: Resilient, flexible and motivated team player with a high level of commitment and readiness for shift work (2-shift)
  • Experience and know-how: SAP knowledge and driving licence category C are advantageous
  • Languages: Good communication and expressiveness in German
