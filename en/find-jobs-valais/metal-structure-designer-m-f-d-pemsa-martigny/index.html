We provide the most qualified talents, thanks to our specialist expertise and our deep knowledge of crafts. In Pemsa, our goal is to guarantee a high level of quality and professionalism. If you are a motivated, enthusiastic and ready-to-play person, join our community for the position of M/F/D Metal Structure Designer and build your professional future together.

Your assignment

  • Draw technical designs and designs for different metal construction projects.
  • Prepare executive projects, technical details and documentation necessary for production and assembly.
  • Ensure the preparation and updating of production files.
  • Make reliefs and measurements on site.
  • Adapt projects to the difficulties encountered in the field.
  • Ensure project monitoring from the design stage to their implementation.
  • Collaboration with customers, workshop teams, assemblers and various partners.
  • Ensure compliance with deadlines, costs and technical requirements.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Training as a technical designer in the field of metal structures, an experienced metal worker in design or equivalent training.
  • Experience in the metal construction industry in Switzerland.
  • Excellent mastery of design software such as AutoCAD, Tekla or equivalent.
  • Good understanding of the manufacturing and assembly techniques of metal structures.
  • Ability to read and interpret technical drawings.
  • Strict, organized and autonomous person.
  • Good team spirit and ease in communication with the different subjects involved.
  • Driving licence B.
  • French level B2 minimum.
  • Ideally located residence within a maximum radius of 30 km from the workplace.
