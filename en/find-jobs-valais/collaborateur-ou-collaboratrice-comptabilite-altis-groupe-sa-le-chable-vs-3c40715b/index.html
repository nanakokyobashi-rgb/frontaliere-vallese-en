Collaborator/Accounting Collaborator (eight-100%)

At ALTIS, we make energy our engine. The energy of our environment, of course, but also that of our more than 140 collaborators who deploy their expertise in the fields of electricity, water, heat, mobility and housing. We meet the needs of individuals, businesses and public authorities, mainly in Entremont.

If, like us, you want to act here and now in favour of the energy transition and you think that the small actions of everyday life count as much as the great achievements, then you may have your place among us, like:

Overview of your future missions:

  • Maintenance of the general and analytical accounts of the ALTIS Group
  • Management of vendor accounts
  • Management of accounts receivable (including litigation)
  • Management of client receipts
  • Tax management (direct and indirect taxation)
  • Control of accounting operations
  • Active participation in the closing of the accounts of the various ALTIS Group companies
  • Cooperation in preparing the audit of accounts
  • Participation in Finance Division projects

Your ideal profile:

  • Federal degree in finance and accounting, Bachelor of Business Economics or equivalent
  • Experience confirmed in a similar function
  • Good control of computer tools, including Microsoft 365
  • Excellent mastery of French, both in writing and oral
  • Analysis and synthesis capacity
  • Methodology and rigour in the follow-up of files
  • Relationship and teamwork
  • Ability to work independently
