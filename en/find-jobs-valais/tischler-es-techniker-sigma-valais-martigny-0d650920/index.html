As part of our Sigma Temporaire department, we are pleased to collaborate with a renowned company in the construction sector based in Valais.

To meet its needs, we are actively searching for a confirmed M/F frame technician:

structural ES technician

Technical skills (heart of the trade)

  • Reading and interpreting plans, diagrams and calculation notes
  • Knowledge of wood structures (traditional, industrial, laminated)
  • Mastery of assembly techniques (teins\-mortases, clutches, metal connectors)
  • Tracing, cutting and processing wood
  • Use and adjustment of machines and tools (saw, planer, mortiser, power tools)
  • Implementation of roofs and wood frames
  • Knowledge of building standards and regulations (DTU, safety rules)
  • Laying, mounting and lifting of structural elements
  • Quality control, stability and conformity verification
  • Material strength and load calculations

Additional technical expertise

  • Use of CAD/DAO software (e.g. Cadwork, AutoCAD, SEMA)
  • Reading technical documents and digital plans
  • Film bases and specifications
  • Worksite monitoring and coordination with other trades
  • Maintenance and maintenance of wood works

Secure and organizational competencies

  • Strict application of on-site safety rules
  • Use of personal protective equipment (PPE)
  • Organization of the workplace
  • Timeliness and procedures
  • Height work safely

Relational skills (knowledge)

  • Team spirit and collaboration with other stakeholders
  • Clear communication with colleagues, construction manager or clients
  • Autonomy and responsibility
  • Stress management and adaptation to site constraints
  • Compliance with instructions and hierarchy

Your profile

  • Diploma of ES technician
  • Planning, Reading and Planning Skills
  • Use of technical software (Cadwork, AutoCAD, etc.)
  • Interface between design office and construction site

Personal qualities

  • Rigor, accuracy and sense of responsibility
  • Reliability and professional commitment
  • Good communication and teamwork
  • Adaptability and stress management

Conditions

  • Driver's licence cat. B required
  • Availability for site interventions
  • Good physical condition

We offer

A long-term mission within a company recognized for its craft know-how. A friendly and professional working environment, with a team passionate about quality and precision. Various and stimulating projects, ranging from traditional structures to modern ones. Efficient tools and equipment to work in the best conditions. Personal support and follow-up throughout the mission. Continuing training opportunities to develop your skills and develop your profession. Stable and organized hours, promoting a good work/life balance. Compliance with safety standards and optimal working conditions.
