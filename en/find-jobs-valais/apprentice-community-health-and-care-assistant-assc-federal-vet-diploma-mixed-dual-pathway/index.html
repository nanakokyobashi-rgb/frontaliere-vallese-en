Full description

Do you want to learn a useful, humane and varied profession? Join our specialised rehabilitation clinic in Sion and help patients regain their autonomy! Apprentice Community Health and Care Assistant (ASSC) – Federal VET Diploma, Mixed Dual Pathway

Why choose the CRR? At the Romande Rehabilitation Clinic (CRR), we help patients regain maximum autonomy after an accident or illness. You will discover how to support people with spinal cord injuries, amputations or neurological conditions, and you will be able to follow their progress during rehabilitation. You will learn alongside care teams and other professionals, including physiotherapists and occupational therapists, in an interprofessional approach. To learn more about our clinic, visit our website www.crr-suva.ch/ Your pathway in the mixed dual system: the 1st year takes place at EPASC ( theoretical and practical teaching ). The 2nd and 3rd years take place in a company according to the dual model, with professional and inter-company courses. What you learn with us

  • You support patients in daily life activities and encourage them to develop their autonomy.
  • You gradually perform technical care, according to the skills acquired during your training and under appropriate supervision.
  • You observe patients’ progress, collaborate with the interprofessional team and contribute to the smooth running of the unit.

What you bring to the team

  • Your interest in the health field and your desire to support patients.
  • Your curiosity, kindness, listening skills and team spirit.
  • Your motivation to learn and gradually take on more responsibility.

Your commitment to training We expect you to be involved both at school and on the job, to be reliable, have team spirit and a desire to progress. A two-day discovery internship is required before starting your apprenticeship. What we offer you

  • A varied training, in contact with care and rehabilitation situations in different units of the clinic.
  • Structured support (supervision days, reference persons) , adapted to your progress.

Our benefits

  • Remuneration above market standards;
  • Meals at advantageous prices;
  • Free access to sports facilities and the swimming pool;
  • Time dedicated to your studies to be agreed with your supervisor;
  • The possibility to continue your professional career at the CRR after your Federal VET Diploma, depending on available positions.

Want to apply? If the world of health resonates with you and you want to join a team that will support you throughout your apprenticeship, apply with us!

  • Send us your complete application (cover letter, CV, copies of recent report cards and/or internship reports) via the form below
  • Deadline : 06.12.2026
  • Start date : 1 August 2027 at school and 1 August 2028 in the company
