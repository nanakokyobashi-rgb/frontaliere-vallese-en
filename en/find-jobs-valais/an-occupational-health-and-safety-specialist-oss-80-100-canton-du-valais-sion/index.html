Full description

An Occupational Health and Safety Specialist (OSS) - (80-100%) at the Mobility Service in Sion

Your tasks

  • You are the OHS reference for a service of more than 300 employees with various professions. With the Directorate, managers, HR and field staff, you pilot and coordinate the approach to accident, health and occupational disease prevention You identify and assess hazards and risks associated with work activities and situations, including field visits and job analyses You carry out and update the risk analyses and propose preventive measures, which you accompany the implementation and ensure follow-up You advise the Directorate, HR, managers, and employees on all issues related to occupational health and safety You analyze accidents, incidents and near-accidents, identify their causes and propose the necessary measures You organize staff training and awareness and develop a culture of prevention and safety You ensure the monitoring of OHS actions, documentation and reporting to the Directorate You collaborate with the cantonal administration's internal services, OSH specialists and external partners

Your profile

  • Advanced training in occupational safety, occupational health or risk prevention (federal STPS certificate, safety officer, safety engineer or equivalent training) You know the standards, guidelines and law in the field of occupational safety and hygiene at the workplace (responsibility of the ASSUD 35 branch solution) You justify a solid professional experience of several years in similar activities You have the sense of listening, communicate with ease and your persuasion ability is no longer to prove Autonomy, rigor and initiative You have the sense of organization and human relations and the ability to manage sensitive situations with diplomacy and pragmatism You have a great experience in accompaniment of operational managers and teams You are native French or German, with very good knowledge of the second official language
