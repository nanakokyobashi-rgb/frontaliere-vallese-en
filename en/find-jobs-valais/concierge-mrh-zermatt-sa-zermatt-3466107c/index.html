Full description

  • Professional, friendly, and competent service and advice to guests regarding the hotel's offerings, other Seiler Hotels, and the destination.
  • Provides information to hotel guests and assists with any problems.
  • Handles incoming and outgoing luggage, luggage storage (skis, etc., in cooperation with the ski manager), and issues the corresponding receipts.
  • Completes all types of reservations (hotels, restaurants, ski instructors, mountain guides, hairdressers, rental cars, taxi and flight transfers, train and plane seats, etc.).
  • Monitors the circulation of internal and external mail, as well as parcel post (mail transfer for arriving or departing guests).
  • Responsible for all luggage transfers from the train station or taxi stand to the hotel room and back: coordination with chauffeur, coachman, receptionist, welcome parking, and housekeeper.
  • Responsible for effective information management. - Promotional material, LHW & Swiss Deluxe brochures, Zermatt Tourism leaflets
  • Is the first point of contact in case of any kind of emergency
  • Is responsible for coordination and evacuation in case of fire
  • Is primarily responsible for the organization in case of fire alarms and lift failures
  • Runs various errands for our guests upon request, tries to fulfill every wish
  • Ensures that instructions and messages are forwarded correctly and promptly (messages, mail, attendance or absence information)
  • Monitors the cleaning and work schedules of the ski lift operators
  • Manages the inventory of all kinds of items and hygiene products (games, cards, table tennis rackets, toothbrushes, shaving kits, as well as adapters and transformers)
  • Responsible for the fireplace in the hall
  • Manages the cloakroom and luggage storage
  • Ensures the safety of guests
  • Verifies key issuance for guests unknown to them
