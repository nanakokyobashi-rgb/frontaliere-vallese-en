Full description

For our production plant in Raron VS we are looking for a CNC machine operator immediately or by agreement

Tasks

As a CNC machine operator, you work shifts and are responsible for delivering reliable and high-quality products to our patients at a reasonable cost.

  • Provision of tools, control means and devices according to instructions or documents
  • Operation, monitoring and partial installation of machines
  • Cleaning machines and carrying out maintenance work according to m. Maintenance plan
  • Processing of work orders according to QS guidelines and working documents
  • Reporting of deviations in default time
  • Correct and complete completion of production documents according to GDP
  • Performing tool changes and corrections—performing troubleshooting operations
  • Ensuring proper leaving of workplaces
  • Self-, first-piece and cross-check
  • Processing of orders according to instructions of the supervisor
  • Know and follow the guidelines regarding code of conduct, ethics and compliance programs, and other relevant regulations

Profile

  • Shortened formal training course (pre-apprenticeship)
  • A few months of systematic training in the workplace
  • Learning ability
  • Efficiency/effectiveness
  • Flexibility
  • Teamwork
  • Load capacity
  • Readiness for shift work

Advantages

  • Responsible and versatile work in a global company
  • Progressive employment conditions with long-term prospects
  • High personal responsibility and independent work
