Moderator of creative workshops in Sion

As part of the upcoming opening of a new multi-disciplinary creative recreation centre in Sion, we are looking for people who are passionate about manual, artistic and creative activities to join our team.

Our center will offer a variety of creative workshops in different disciplines, intended for a varied audience, in a friendly and inspiring atmosphere.

Your Mission

  • Host and accompany groups from 15 to 20 non-expert participants
  • Create a warm and friendly atmosphere
