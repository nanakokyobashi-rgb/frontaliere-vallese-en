Full description

Are you interested in leadership and employee development? Are you motivated by customer orientation and the achievement of common goals? In this versatile function, you make an active contribution to the quality and efficiency of our services. With you, our contact center is successfully developing.

You can do that.

  • You will take over the technical and personnel leadership of a team of 10 to 15 employees.
  • In addition, you ensure a high quality of service and efficient control of activities in order to achieve the set goals and achieve a concrete impact.
  • Qualitative and quantitative measures are implemented consistently and result-oriented.
  • You will also participate in improvement and innovation projects that contribute to the further development of the organization, bringing your expertise to bear.
  • With a strengthening and development-oriented coaching, you accompany the members of your team individually.
  • In the event of high workloads or operational needs, you actively support your team and take over customer contacts yourself.

You bring that with you

  • You have proven experience in team management and enjoy the development of employees.
  • In addition, you are characterized by a pronounced customer and performance orientation and thus achieve sustainable results.
  • You have completed a basic training and are ready to complete a leadership training if you do not yet have an appropriate qualification.
  • In addition, you convince with very good organizational and social skills.
  • You master the MS Office programs and move safely in a digital work environment.
  • Very good knowledge of French and German allows you to secure communication in both languages.
