Full description

The Office AI du Valais is the cantonal centre of competence for the Swiss Invalidity Insurance with more than 190 employees in various fields. We are looking for an entry-in-office to suit one Psychologist specialist in initial training 60% - 80% Your activities You advise and accompany young people and young adults, with a health problem in the development of a rehabilitation strategy for the acquisition of adequate initial vocational training and their sustainable placement in the labour market, by piloting the implementation of the measures and their monitoring. Your personality You are a dynamic and proactive person, with a great sense of communication and listening. You are comfortable thinking and acting in a methodical and objective way. You have negotiating skills and the ability to work in a multidisciplinary way. Autonomous, you have excellent stress resistance. Your mother tongue is French and you have good knowledge of German.
