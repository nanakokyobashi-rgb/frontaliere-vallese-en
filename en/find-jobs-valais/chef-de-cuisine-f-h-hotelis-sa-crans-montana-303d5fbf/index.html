Kitchen Chef (M/F)

Description of post

You will be in charge of the proper running and running of the kitchen on the operational level. To achieve your goals, you will have to supervise a small brigade of 8 people that you will have to manage on a daily basis. Your priorities will be focused on the quality of services, compliance with standards and budgets, and relationship within your team in close collaboration with the restaurant manager.

For the benefit of a kitchen CFC or equivalent training, your career has allowed you to take on positions of responsibility as a chef.
