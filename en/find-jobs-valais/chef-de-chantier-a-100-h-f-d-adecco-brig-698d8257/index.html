Introduction

For an architectural office, we are looking for a dedicated personality who takes over the coordination and monitoring of construction sites and works closely with the project teams and the executing companies.

Conductor 100% CDI (M/F/D)

Tasks

  • Independent management and monitoring of construction sites from preparation to construction acceptance
  • Preparation of tenders and analysis and comparison of tenders
  • Planning and coordination of the assignments of companies and various trades
  • Ensuring compliance with costs, deadlines, quality standards and applicable regulations
  • Organisation and management of construction meetings and drafting of meeting minutes
  • Maintaining a trusting cooperation with clients, companies and project partners
  • Ensure efficient communication between all project stakeholders

Profile

  • Training as construction manager or equivalent qualification
  • At least 3 years of professional experience in tendering and construction management
  • German as a mother tongue and good knowledge of French (level B1-B2)
  • Structured, independent and responsible functioning
  • Team-oriented personality with strong communication and organizational skills

Advantages

  • Secure permanent employment in a dynamic architecture office in central Valais
  • Varied projects with construction sites mainly in Upper Valais
  • Collegial working environment with a lot of personal responsibility and development opportunities
  • Job opening: by agreement
