ASSC (6-100%)

The CMS Sion\-Hérens\-Conthey provides medico-social benefits, assistance and social and vocational integration that meet the needs of the population.

Its mission is to foster home care, autonomy and social integration by providing individualised support in each situation and by offering a full range of services.

With its 5 sites and close to 560 collaborators, the CMS Sion\-Hérens\-Conthey accompanies more than 7'000 beneficiaries on all 16 communes in the region.

We are looking for our site of the Coteau, an\-e

Your Mission

  • You provide care tailored to the patient's needs.
  • You provide delegated medical and technical care and basic care.
  • You are actively involved in the dynamics of your team.

Your profile

  • SWC in Community Care and Health (or training deemed equivalent).
  • Adaptability, listening and autonomy.
  • Mastery of common computer tools
  • Carefolio's knowledge is an asset.
  • Driver's licence and vehicle required.

We offer you

  • Pleasant and dynamic working environment
  • Possibility of continuing training
  • Collaborating advantages with many partners (species discount, fitness subscription, wellness offers, optician, etc...)
  • Attractive occupational pension benefits

Working conditions

  • Contract type: CDI
  • Activity rate: between 6 & 100%
  • Onward: immediately or to be agreed
  • Workplace: Grimisuat

We gladly await your complete file (CV, cover letter, diplomas, certificates, work certificates...). Please specify in advance the activity rate and the site you are interested in.
