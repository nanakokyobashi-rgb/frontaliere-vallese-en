Full description

Who you are

  • You show a lot of passion and curiosity for interior design and everyday life at home.
  • You distinguish yourself with a creative mind and have an eye for aesthetics.
  • You see the trends in furniture and are interested in the different materials while having a sense of colours and shapes.

•You take responsibility, you commit and you are not afraid of change.

What you'll do every day

  • You design IKEA atmospheres according to certain rules while skillfully applying your knowledge in the field of layout and products.
  • You like going beyond the expectations of our customers and discovering their personal tastes.
  • You ensure that the products are well developed and thus facilitate the decision to purchase.
  • You simplify the purchasing process by means of appropriate communication measures so that our customers systematically and intuitively travel through the store.

Teamwork

Where others see furnishings, we see clever solutions to improve daily life. With a lot of passion, imagination and brainstorming, we transform colours, textiles and furniture into inspiring atmospheres that enthralle, attract and convince visitors to IKEA stores that they can reproduce the same in their home.

Any questions or need help? Contact us!
