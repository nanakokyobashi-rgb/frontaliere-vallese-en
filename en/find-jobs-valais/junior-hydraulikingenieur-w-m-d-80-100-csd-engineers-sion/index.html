Full description

With over 1,000 employees across 30 sites in Switzerland and Europe, the CSD Group is a dynamic and constantly growing company. Thanks to the ingenuity, expertise, and multidisciplinary approach that characterize our teams, CSD delivers innovative and sustainable solutions for our clients, with an added focus on quality of life and the environment. We are looking for a Junior Hydraulic Engineer for our CSD Environnement Valais branch in Sion (f/m/d) 80-100%.

  • Your responsibilities:
  • Primarily work in river hydraulics while contributing to various urban hydraulics projects
  • Analyze and manage hydrological risks in an integrated manner: river flooding, erosion, surface runoff
  • Design, dimension, and oversee projects for river management, safety measures, and renaturation (SIA 31–53 standards), particularly on multiple watercourses and within projects related to the third Rhône correction
  • Support urban hydraulics projects:
  • Develop and update municipal water drainage plans (PGEE)
  • Plan and dimension pressurized networks and infrastructure (collectors, pumping stations, discharge structures) according to SIA 31–53 standards
  • Actively participate in several municipal PGEE projects and the planning of new networks
  • Your profile:
  • Engineering degree with a specialization in river and urban hydraulics (hydrology, sediment transport, river management, hydraulic structures)
  • Strong interest in hydrological risks, mountain watercourses, and urban runoff, with a genuine desire to learn
  • Knowledge of Swiss norms and regulations (EPFL, EPFZ, or equivalent; experience in Switzerland is an asset)
  • Autonomy, critical thinking, versatility, and strong writing skills
  • Proficiency in QGIS and hydraulic modeling software (Basement, HEC-RAS, and/or SWMM)
  • Fluency in French
  • German and English are assets - Valid driver’s license
