Full description

Chauffeur malaxeur / replacement Centraliste 100% (CDI)

Place of work: Sierre

In order to strengthen our Transport team in the Valais region, we are looking for a dynamic, communicative and team-oriented personality.

What awaits you

Batch driver

  • Transport & Delivery: Reliable transport and delivery of our concretes using our heavy vehicles.
  • Maintenance of vehicles: Care and regular maintenance of the vehicle that will be entrusted to you. Piping the top.
  • Image & Clientele: Representation of the company with our customers and on the roads of the region.
  • Standards & Requirements: Compliance with and application of Holcim's high safety, quality and environmental standards.

Centralist replacement

• Replacement of centralists after a period of internal training in tools and process. • Processing and monitoring of production orders. • Organization, management and control of the delivery of raw materials to the site. • Aid for quality control of concretes. • Current maintenance of the plant. • Compliance with and implementation of internal health, safety and environment guidelines on the site.

Your profile

• Holder of a valid Class C driver's licence (Updated CPAO). • Prior experience as a mixer driver and/or in concrete production (an asset). • Comfortable with computer tools for production management. • Rigoury, organized and able to manage priorities during plant replacements. • Attentive/ve and sensitive to safety at work and product quality. • Flexible in missions and sometimes schedules (sometimes on weekends or at night). • Polyvalent, available and open to learning new tasks. • Service oriented and customers. • At ease in French.
