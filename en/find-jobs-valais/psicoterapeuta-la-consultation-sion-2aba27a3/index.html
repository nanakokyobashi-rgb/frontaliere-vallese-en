Psychologist Psychotherapist

The Consultation SA is an organisation of psychologists\-psychotherapists based on several sites in French-speaking Switzerland (Aigle, Bulle, Chavornay, Martigny, Montreux, Moudon, Ezieux, Payerne, Peseux, , Saint\- , Sion) and Ticino (Bellinzona). It offers individual psychotherapeutic care (children, adolescents, adults), couple and family care and seeks psychotherapeutic psychologists or psychotherapy training for several of its sites.

Description of post

  • Individual psychotherapy with children, adolescents or adults (optional), in a recognized orientation (systemic, TCC, psychodynamic, ACP)
  • Cognitive, Emotional and/or ASD Assessments (depending on interest)
  • Couple and/or family therapies (depending on interest)
  • Group Therapies (depending on interest)
  • Active collaboration in networking and with families
  • Regular participation in symposia
  • Preparation of reports
  • Engagement in post-graduate psychotherapist training or continuing training required by the PSF

Profile required

  • Master's degree in psychology (or equivalent degree recognized by the Commission of Psychologists) and training in psychotherapy recognized by OFSP.
  • For non-French speakers: certificate of knowledge of French level C1
  • Court case blank
