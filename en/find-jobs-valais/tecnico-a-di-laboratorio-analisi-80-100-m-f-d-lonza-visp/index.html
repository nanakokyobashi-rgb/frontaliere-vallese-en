The actual location of this job is in Visp, Switzerland. Relocation assistance is available for eligible candidates and their families, if needed.

Today, Lonza is a global leader in life sciences operating across five continents. While we work in science, there’s no magic formula to how we do it. Our greatest scientific solution is talented people working together, devising ideas that help businesses to help people. In exchange, we let our people own their careers. As an NMR laboratory technician, you and your team will be responsible for providing analytical support to R&D and production across the entire Visp site. Your responsibilities include routine tasks such as product identification and quantification, as well as contributing to the development and validation of test procedures and release testing for production in ISO and GMP environments.

In this role, you will work closely with our colleagues in research, production, occupational safety, and quality assurance. You will also support the Optical Spectroscopy team (IR, UV/VIS, optical rotation) in the areas of equipment cleaning and method development.

What you’ll do

As a laboratory technician in the Spectroscopy/NMR department, you will be responsible for the following tasks:

  • You will be responsible for providing analytical support to R&D and production across the entire Visp site.
  • Routine tasks such as product identification and quantification, as well as contributing to the development and validation of test procedures and release testing for production in accordance with ISO and GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) standards.
  • You will support optical spectroscopy (IR, UV/VIS, optical rotation) in the areas of equipment cleaning and method development.
  • Close collaboration with colleagues from research, production, occupational safety, and quality assurance.

What we’re looking for

  • You have completed training as a biology or chemistry lab technician or hold a bachelor’s degree in chemistry, biology, or biotechnology.
  • You may have gained some initial experience in an ISO and/or GMP environment ecific.
  • You have a good working knowledge of MS Office
  • Experience with Bruker software and ACD Labs is a plus.
  • You are self-motivated and responsible, with strong communication and teamwork skills, and you enjoy interdisciplinary collaboration.
  • In addition to German, you can also communicate at a basic level in English or French.
