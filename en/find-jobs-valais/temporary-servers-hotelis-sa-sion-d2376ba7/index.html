Temporary servers M/F

Company description

As part of temporary missions during the winter season, we offer opportunities to our customers, including hotel establishments of all categories in the different stations in the region, as well as traditional and community restaurants. These missions are mainly in the mountains, in the canton of Valais. As some establishments are located directly on the slopes, skier skills are essential to access the different mission sites. In this context, we are actively looking for:

Servers / Grade Heads (M/F)

Description of post

As a server/lead you will join the existing team and, under the direction of the manager, will participate in cleaning, setting up, and serving at the "à la carte" restaurant or for the event caterer and banquet section.

Profile sought

For the benefit of an experience of at least 6 months, with hotel establishments or catering. It is important to provide quality work oriented customer satisfaction. Your sense of detail and your investment make you a collaborator who can really bring more to our different customers. Level B2 in French minimum.

You master the practice of skiing, a prerequisite for certain missions on the slopes. Having your own vehicle is also a real asset to facilitate your travel at different stations. Please note, no accommodation is provided for these missions: you must therefore have your own accommodation solution.

We offer you

We offer you the opportunity to carry out many temporary missions during which stable placement opportunities are quite possible. You will work regularly in cut times as well as on weekends. You will benefit from interesting working conditions, in connection with the collective agreement, as well as the possibility to improve your knowledge through continuous training offered within our training centre.

Interested in this offer?

We look forward to receiving your application, which will be treated in complete discretion.
