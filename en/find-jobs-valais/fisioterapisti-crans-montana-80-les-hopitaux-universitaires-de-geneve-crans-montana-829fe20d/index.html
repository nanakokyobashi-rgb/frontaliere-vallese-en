Company description\\ With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. The \[Crans clinic\\-Montana\\\ ), nestled in the heart of the Valais Alps, is a hospital site of the HUG. Its hospital tasks consist of caring for patients for rehabilitations in general and psychosomatic internal medicine, multi-purpose post-operative or post-hospital rehabilitations in acute settings. Crans Montana Clinic holds FMH recognition for general internal medicine and trains internal physicians. She develops academic research projects related to the Faculty of Medicine at the University of Geneva. Description of position You develop, propose, apply and evaluate physiotherapy treatments in accordance with the physiotherapeutic assessment, diagnosis and medical prescription to recover, improve or maintain the bio\\\-psycho\\\-social functions and activities of patients. You ensure individual or group management of patients and ensure their physical and psychological comfort throughout the therapeutic process. You also use educational approaches such as therapeutic education, promoting adherence to treatment, prevention and health education. You are actively involved in interdisciplinary exchanges, symposia and case discussions. You work closely with physicians, health professionals, and all care partners to ensure high quality, coordinated care. You ensure the traceability of interventions in the patient file, participate in the management and maintenance of therapeutic equipment and contribute to the continuous improvement of service delivery. You participate in the reception, integration and supervision of new collaborators, students and trainees. You also contribute to the development of professional knowledge through your participation in training activities and, where appropriate, clinical research projects. Rigourous or rigorous, autonomous and organized or organized, you demonstrate excellent relational skills and appreciate working as an interprofessional team in a dynamic environment. \\

An experience in hospital or rehabilitation is an asset. You have an excellent command of oral and written French (minimum level C1 or mother tongue). You are comfortable with computer tools and electronic care documentation. You have autonomy, rigor, organization and good relational abilities.
