Civil Engineering Engineer (M/F) – Office and Construction

Guy Monnet \& Fils SA – Riddes (VS)

Activity rate:80 to 100 % Entry into office: early 2027 or to be agreed

Who are we?

Guy Monnet \& Fils SA is a Valais family company active in the fields of civil engineering, earthworks, roadworks and masonry.

In order to strengthen our team and to support the development of our activities, we are looking for a versatile civil engineering engineer, who wants to invest in both the technical preparation of projects in the office and in their implementation in the field.

Your missions

  • Study and technical preparation of construction and civil engineering projects.
  • Preparation of bids, bids, dates and counts.
  • Planning and organisation of construction sites.
  • Technical, administrative and financial monitoring of the work.
  • Coordination of field teams, subcontractors and suppliers.
  • Collaboration with contractors, architects, engineers and municipalities.
  • Quality, timeliness and cost control.
  • Participation in construction sessions and the search for technical solutions.

Your profile

  • Diploma of engineer HES or EPF in civil engineering, or equivalent training.
  • Young graduate or experienced engineer.
  • Interest in office work and site monitoring.
  • Good organizational capacity, autonomy and responsibility.
  • Practical spirit, dynamism and ease to work as a team.
  • Good command of French.
  • Driver's licence category B.

We offer you

  • A diverse and responsible position in a family-sized business.
  • An activity shared between office and construction sites.
  • Various projects in the field of civil engineering and construction.
  • Direct collaboration with management.
  • A great autonomy and possibilities for evolution.
  • Attractive salary conditions based on experience and skills.
  • Stable and sustainable employment in Valais.

Interested?

Would you like to join a dynamic company and participate in a variety of projects?

We look forward to receiving your application.
