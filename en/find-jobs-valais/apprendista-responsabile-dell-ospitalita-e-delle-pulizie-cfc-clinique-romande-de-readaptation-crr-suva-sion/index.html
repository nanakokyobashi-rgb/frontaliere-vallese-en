Full description

To complete our teams dedicated to advanced rehabilitation and professional reintegration of accident victims in French-speaking Switzerland, the Romande Rehabilitation Clinic (Suva) in Sion is looking for an

Apprentice Hospitality and Housekeeping Manager CFC

Our expertise We are specialists in advanced rehabilitation and professional reintegration of accident victims in French-speaking Switzerland. We also offer specific services for the rehabilitation of spinal cord injury patients (paraplegic and tetraplegic) , amputees, and burn patients.

Your main tasks

  • Welcome guests, clients and patients, advise them and respond to their needs and wishes;
  • Prepare rooms for seminars and serve refreshments during events;
  • Know, prepare and serve drinks and simple dishes;
  • In the cleaning domain: know materials, products, use and maintain equipment and machines according to HACCP hygiene standards;
  • Organise and carry out laundry tasks;
  • Receive, check and store goods (foodstuffs, cleaning products, etc.) while keeping an inventory;
  • Perform administrative tasks.

Profile sought The position is particularly suitable for a dynamic person, responsible and wishing to train within an establishment recognised for the quality of its services.

Our offer Are you looking for a rewarding and well-supervised apprenticeship? Join our establishment! In a caring environment, a dedicated trainer will guide you throughout your journey. Every day, you will discover varied tasks to develop your skills. In addition to professional experience, enjoy our sports facilities, swimming pool and a subsidised restaurant. Ready to start an exciting career? Apply now!

Our benefits

  • Remuneration above market standards;
  • Meals at advantageous prices;
  • Free access to sports facilities and the swimming pool;
  • Study time to be agreed with your supervisor.
  • The possibility to continue your professional career at the CRR after your CFC, depending on available positions

Application procedures

  • Send us your complete application (cover letter, CV, copies of recent report cards) via the form below;
  • Start date : 1 August 2027
  • Deadline : 6 December 2026
