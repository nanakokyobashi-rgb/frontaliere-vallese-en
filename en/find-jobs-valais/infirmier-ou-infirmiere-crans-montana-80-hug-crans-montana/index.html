Company description

With more than 13,000 employees representing 160 professions, the University Hospitals in Geneva are a reference institution at the national and international level. Its hospital duties consist of caring for patients for rehabilitations in general and psychosomatic internal medicine, multi-purpose post-operative or post-hospital rehabilitations in acute settings.

Description of post

You provide, independently and/or on medical delegation, personalized and comprehensive nursing care within the framework of a rehabilitation unit. You ensure accurate and rigorous transmission of information between multidisciplinary teams and contribute to the development of quality of care. You collaborate in the supervision and clinical teaching of new caregivers and students. You support the teams in place with great adaptability, flexibility and availability. You contribute to continuous improvement projects by showing your openness to change.

You have a Bachelor of Nursing degree or an equivalent nursing degree recognized by the Red Cross, and an excellent command of the spoken and written French language.
