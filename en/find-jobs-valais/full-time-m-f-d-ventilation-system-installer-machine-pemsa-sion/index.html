Do you want the freedom of temporary work and the security of permanent employment? For 30 years, we have been supporting hundreds of companies in the technical fields of building, construction and industry throughout Switzerland, offering them the best talent. But we won't lie to you: we're demanding. So, if you are a motivated and highly enthusiastic person, join our team for the position of ventilation system installer. Your task

  • Lay and install ventilation ducts, both square and circular, with precision and efficiency.
  • Cut the frames required for on-site adaptation in accordance with the technical drawings.
  • Install the various ventilation ducts, ensuring a watertight connection and reliable assembly of the components.
  • Assemble the monoblocs in compliance with the safety and quality standards in force.
  • Install grilles, ducts, filters, motors and extractors, ensuring their correct operation and integration into existing systems.
  • Working closely with the technical teams to ensure the quality and compliance of the installations within the established deadlines.

Now, let's talk about you.

  • Driving licence (essential): You hold a valid driving licence.
  • Technical training: ideally, a Federal Certificate of Proficiency (CFC) as a ventilation system installer or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland.
  • Professional experience: At least 3 years of experience in Switzerland in the field of laying and installing ventilation systems, with proven experience of at least 2 years in this position.
  • Advanced technical skills: proficiency in the installation and adjustment techniques for square and circular ventilation ducts, precise cutting of frames, assembly of monobloc units, as well as the installation of grilles, ducts, filters, motors and extractors.
  • Language: French spoken and written at native-speaker level, essential for understanding projects and communicating with teams.
  • Mobility: residence within 30 kilometres of the place of work, in order to ensure optimal availability.
  • Personal qualities: You are dynamic, thorough and have an excellent team spirit, which enables you to integrate quickly and effectively into our teams in the field.
