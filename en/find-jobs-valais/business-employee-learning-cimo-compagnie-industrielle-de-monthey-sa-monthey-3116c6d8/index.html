Business employee learning CIMO Compagnie industrielle de Monthey SA is a Swiss company founded in 1997 with 450 employees in 80 specialized trades. As part of its strategy for sustainable development and innovation, CIMO is active, inter alia, in the fields of energy production and distribution, waste treatment, technical maintenance, engineering, automation, hygiene and safety, environment, laboratory and occupational medicine.

  • Training plan
  • The apprenticeship takes place over 3 years and leads to the Federal Certificate of SWC Capacity.
  • Theoretical training:
  • The courses are offered at the Vocational Commercial and Crafts School (EPCA) in Sion for one day to two days a week.
  • Practical training:
  • In-company practical training takes place at CIMO, under the responsibility of a trainer.
  • The inter-company courses (CIE) are organised by the CIFC (Community of Interest for Commercial Training) in Sion in order to receive the practical basis of the trade.
  • The trade

As part of your learning, you will have the opportunity to discover very diverse fields. The work is done mainly through a PC, telephone or paper documentation. You will have the opportunity to make a turn between three services in which you will familiarize yourself with, among other things:

  • Sales administration: phone socket, scheduling and session management, order processing...
  • Finance: control and accounting of invoices, accounting transactions, processing of procurement requests...
  • Human resources: personnel data management, monitoring of employees' schedules, publication of documents related to social insurance...
