As an independent company, Zermatt Support AG takes over the finance and accounting for companies such as Zermatt Bergbahnen AG, Zermatt Hospitality Group AG and other service companies in the Upper Valais.

To complement our team, we are looking for a motivated person as of October 1, 2026 or by appointment who can look past the dry job title of “payroll specialist” and who can handle everyday life with humor despite complex cases as a payroll accounting clerk

Tasks

The job Your career with us

  • Responsible for ensuring that all employees receive their earned wages on time; including mutating master and wage data
  • Processing of daily allowance cases (registration, processing and control of insurance benefits in the event of illness, accident, maternity and paternity)
