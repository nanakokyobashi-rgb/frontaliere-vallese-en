Automation Engineer

Every day, Lonza’s teams across the world work together to turn scientific breakthroughs into life\-changing medicines that reach up to 100 million patients a year. Lonza is a place where people can make a unique impact, grow their careers and belong.

Based at our Visp site (41 hours/week, contract until December 2027\), you will play a key role in shaping, testing, and optimizing automated manufacturing processes in chemical pharmaceutical production—helping turn complex science into reliable, life\-changing treatments.

This is a fully site‑based role. Working together in person supports close, real‑time collaboration and the technical precision needed to manufacture medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What you will get:

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace.

What you will do:

  • Create, test, and optimize automated production processes to drive efficiency and reliability in chemical pharmaceutical manufacturing.
  • Design, build, and adapt manufacturing recipes within DeltaV control systems and Syncade Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES).
  • Collaborate closely on\-site with chemists, plant and automation engineers, quality assurance managers, and plant supervisors throughout planning and active production.
  • Participate in process deviation investigations and technical risk assessments to maintain uninterrupted operations.
  • Ensure thorough, compliant documentation across departments in line with pharmaceutical quality standards.
  • Continuously improve procedures and workflows while upholding strict safety, hygiene, environmental, and quality requirements.
  • Proactively identify potential safety or compliance risks and partner with cross\-functional teams to implement effective solutions.

What we are looking for:

  • Solid hands\-on experience in process engineering, production environments, and/or developing recipes in DeltaV and Syncade (MES).
  • Strong willingness to participate in comprehensive internal training to develop into a dedicated MES/DeltaV specialist.
  • Proven ability to collaborate effectively across multidisciplinary teams and internal interfaces.
  • Strong planning and organizational skills to manage complex technical projects with a consistently solution\-oriented mindset.
  • Fluency in and a solid working knowledge of English.

Why Lonza: Lonza is a place of unique opportunity, where your ideas, expertise and ambition can make a real difference. At Lonza, you will have the freedom to grow, the support to succeed and the chance to contribute to work that matters.

As Lonza’s talent acquisition partner, please note, Randstad is responsible for the engagement and contracting of this contingent role. Lonza are the makers of the medicines of tomorrow. Join them. Be you. Apply now.
