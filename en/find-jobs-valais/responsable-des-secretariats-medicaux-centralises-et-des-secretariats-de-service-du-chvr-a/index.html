Full description

Join a committed team at the heart of the administrative and operational functioning of our institution. At the head of a team of 60 employees, including 16 employees in direct supervision. The Valais Hospital is looking for the Valais Romand Hospital Centre Head of central medical secretariats and service secretariats of the CHVR at 80% Mission: To supervise, animate and support teams on the various sites of the CHVR (60 employees, including 16 live) Work closely with medical, health and administrative teams. Pilot and accompany institutional projects. Promote cross-cutting work and support change. Modernize and harmonize administrative tools, processes and practices between services and sites. Ensure the quality and effectiveness of services under responsibility. To contribute to the continuous improvement and organizational restructuring of the medical secretariats. Your areas of activity

  • Centralized medical secretariats Present on Sion, Sierre and Martigny, they provide: Medical documentary management of all medical and surgical disciplines of CHVR (excluding psychiatry). The typing, correction, validation and monitoring of medical documents and their transmission to the recipients concerned. The archiving and proper maintenance of medical records. The full composition of the medical record for coding and billing.
  • Secretaries Are they present at all CHVR sites: Work in direct contact with the Medical Service Chief and Senior Medical Officers. Provide day-to-day administrative management: organization of symposia, symposia and training; mail and e-mail management; coordination of various tasks related to medical activity. Profile: Basic commercial training supplemented by higher or equivalent training. Specific training in the hospital field is an asset. Experience confirmed in hospital patient administration and management of medical secretariats. Demonstrated skills in team management and multidisciplinary work. Successful experience in process optimization and organizational harmonization projects. Very good computer knowledge. French mother tongue, good knowledge of German. Only full bids and applications that meet the profile will be processed.
