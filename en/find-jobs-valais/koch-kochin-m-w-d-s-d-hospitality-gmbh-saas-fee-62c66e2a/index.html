Our profile

For more than eight years, we have been able to lead the Schäferstube (14 Gault Millau points) and inspire our guests with great pleasure. On the forest edge of Saas-Fee, SKI IN SKI OUT from the Stafelwald piste, the Schäferstube with its unique sun terrace is definitely worth a visit. As young hosts of the region, we revive the gastronomy of Saas-Fee. Both of us were able to work as management positions in upscale restaurants and hotels and acquire in-depth expertise there.

Honest craftsmanship mixed with creativity, precision and a lot of love for domestic, high-quality products set us apart.
