Full description

More than 1,000 employees at 30 locations in Switzerland and Europe: the CSD Group is a dynamic and growing company. Thanks to the ingenuity, competence and multidisciplinarity that characterize our teams, CSD offers innovative and sustainable solutions for our customers with a "plus" for quality of life and the environment. We are looking for our Environment branch in Sion (VS), a Geologist Project Leader (f/h/d) 80-100%.

  • Your missions
  • Management of projects in geology/natural hazards related to municipal and cantonal infrastructure (constructive projects, road networks, gas distribution, etc.)
  • Integrated natural risk management: hazard mapping, inventory of exposed objects and risk assessment, mitigation of risk through constructive measures (IAS 32-53) or implementation of organizational measures
  • Engineering geology: recognition of geological formations, sampling, determination of geomechanical parameters, interpretation of tests.
  • Provide assistance in other areas of activity such as the environment, polluted sites, or mining sites
  • Ensuring high technical quality services efficiently and effectively
  • Actively participate in the commercial development of CSD
  • Manage customer/partner contacts and ensure quality of services
  • Your profile
  • Geologist or engineer training deemed equivalent
  • 5 years of experience in an engineering office (ideally 2 years as project manager)
  • Specific technical skills in the above tasks, knowledge of hydrological or nivological hazards are assets
  • Ease of writing, autonomy, versatility, critical spirit and willingness to evolve
  • Knowledge of legal basis and SIA standards
  • Infancy/capacity to work in the Alpine environment
  • Mastery of natural hazard mapping and modelling software
  • Mastery of French, German and English are assets
  • Driving licence
