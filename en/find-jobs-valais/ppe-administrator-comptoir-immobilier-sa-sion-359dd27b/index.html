PPE Administrator

The Groupe Comptoir Immobilier is a leader in real estate services dating back to 1825\. Driven by a culture of a family and human nature, we now have more than 400 employees divided between the headquarters in Geneva and the various agencies located in the cantons of Vaud and Valais.

Based on our experience and know-how, we offer a full range of services such as stewardship, PPE administration, the sale and estimation of all types of goods, the management of promotions and architectural projects, renovations, as well as commercial and investment real estate.

Our commitment to providing a unique quality of advice, our commitment to sustainable development and our ethics based on respect and loyalty are the core values of our success. Recognised as a formative company, Comptoir Immobilier is also committed to offering its employees a stimulating and collaborative working environment by valuing the skills of everyone.

If you identify with our group and are passionate about real estate, don't wait anymore: join us!

For our agency in Sion, we are looking for a PPE Administrator

Your main responsibilities:

  • Administrative, technical and financial management of a condominium portfolio;
  • Audit;
  • Organization and holding of general meetings;
  • Preparation of agendas and management reports;
  • Implementation of decisions taken at general meetings in accordance with the legislation in force and the rules governing the co-ownership in relation to co-owners and/or management committees;
  • Contacts with co-owners, management committees, concierges and companies.

Your profile:

  • Business employee CFC or equivalent and additional real estate training;
  • Experience confirmed in a similar position in Switzerland;
  • Good knowledge of English, a real asset;
  • Excellent editorial skills and impeccable spelling;
  • Mastery of common computer tools;
  • Priority management, stress resistance and autonomy;
  • Responsible, organized, rigorous and proactive personality;
  • Relationship, priority and customer service.

We offer

  • Opportunity to join a dynamic family group;
  • A pleasant working environment close to all amenities;
  • 5 weeks of vacation;
  • A dynamic environment affecting the various real estate professions;
  • Attractive remuneration corresponding to your skills and performance;
  • Flexible hours.

Entry: to be agreed.

Loyalty, performance, dynamism and elegance are the qualities of mind that animate all our employees. If you also share these values, we will be happy to discover your complete application file (CV, cover letter, work certificates, diplomas) via JobUp directly.
