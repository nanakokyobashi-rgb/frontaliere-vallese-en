Service worker 80% (m/f/d)

Monthey Part-time 80% Term From 01.12.2026 to 28.02.2027

Each visit is unique, and it is precisely on this singularity that our service rests. Thanks to our team spirit, our agility and our close customer proximity, Manor is the No.1 department store for everyday life, both online and in our outlets. Do you like dynamic environments and want to fully value your assets? Then join us to shape tomorrow's shopping experience!

To strengthen our team in the field of Restauration, we look from 01.12.2026, for a fixed duration, until 28.02.2027, on the site of Monthey, for a person motivated\and engaged\ as Cooperative service, 80%.

Your responsibilities

  • You serve our clients with kindness and skill.
  • You're responsible for the smooth operation of the caisse.
  • You check that the buffet is complete and clean.
  • You're responsible for diving and cleaning the kitchen.
  • You are responsible for order and cleanliness throughout the restaurant.
  • You always respect the hygiene rules of the group.
  • You support the kitchen team if necessary.

Your profile

  • You have preferably completed basic training in retail or catering.
  • You can already promote professional experience in an equivalent activity.
  • You like to work independently and as a team and you know how to set priorities independently.
  • You are a reliable and committed person, who works with great pleasure and enthusiasm.
  • You release positive energy and you look neat.
  • You speak French fluently and you preferably know another national language.
  • You can adjust your availability according to the schedule.

Our offer

  • Personal discounts ranging from 10 to 25% on all product groups, including Manora restaurants and supermarkets
  • Training and development programmes for all levels and functions
  • Between 5 and 7 weeks of vacation per year
  • 3 weeks paid paternity leave up to 4 months additional maternity leave without pay possible
  • Possibility of a sabbatical of 1 to 6 months from the 3rd year of service
  • Internal pension fund with very strong coverage rate and above average savings pay
