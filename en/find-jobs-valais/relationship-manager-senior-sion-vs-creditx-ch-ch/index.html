CreditX continues its development and is looking for an experienced Senior Relationship Manager who wants to exercise his activity with a high degree of autonomy. This position is aimed at a confirmed business profile, with demonstrable results in the field of foresight and capable of developing its own clientele.

Your Mission

You accompany a private clientele in the field of foresight.

  • Analyze the foresight situation of clients and complete balance sheets.
  • Propose solutions adapted to the situation and objectives of each customer.
  • Develop and retain your customers.
  • Present the CreditX app and support customers in its use.
  • Collaborate with the back office and CreditX product specialists.

What CreditX makes available

  • Social benefits and insurance coverage above standard.
  • A modern and quality working environment in the heart of the Cour de Gare district in Sion.
  • The CreditX application and its latest generation analysis and simulation tools, designed for the consultant's work.
  • A CRM and administrative infrastructure that automate repetitive tasks.

Your profile

  • AFA degree required.
  • Minimum five years of experience in foresight counselling.
  • High, regular and demonstrable business results.
  • Personal and professional network directly mobilizable.
  • Experience confirmed in acquiring and retaining customers.
  • Excellent control of the Swiss foresight system.
  • Ability to analyze complex situations and make clear recommendations.
  • High sense of responsibility and professional ethics.
  • Autonomy and rigor in the organization of your activity.
  • Current mastery of French; any other language is an asset.
  • Very good mastery of digital tools.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
