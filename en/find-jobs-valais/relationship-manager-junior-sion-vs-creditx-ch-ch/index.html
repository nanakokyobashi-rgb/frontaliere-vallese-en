All Management & Consulting positions Relationship Manager Junior Sion 100% On-site Apply This announcement is published in French: the position requires a current professional French language, both oral and written. CreditX is a fintech specialized in foresight, which combines personalized advice and digital tools. We support our clients in their situation analysis and the development of solutions tailored to their disability, death and retirement needs. To support our development, we are looking for a Junior Relationship Manager who wants to acquire foresight expertise and develop a portfolio of private clients. Your role You build and maintain trust relationships with your customers. As you train and qualify, you are gradually taking charge of needs analysis, advice and case follow-up.

  • Develop your portfolio through active prospecting, your network and customer recommendations.
  • Understand the personal, professional and financial situation of each client.
  • Identify foresight needs and present suitable solutions.
  • Support clients in their efforts and ensure rigorous follow-up.
  • Maintain a lasting relationship and re-evaluate needs during changing circumstances.
  • Contribute to improving the customer experience and tools CreditX.
  • The acquisition of new clients is an essential dimension of the position. Contacts generated by CreditX can also be entrusted to you to support your business development.

Your training We support you in learning the trade, with a course preparing for the AFA qualification.

  • Community supervision, combining knowledge acquisition, interview preparation and field support.
  • The objective is to allow you to develop the technical and relational skills necessary to exercise your activity with autonomy, rigour and professionalism.

Your profile

  • A first experience in sales, business development or customer relationship.
  • A strong interest in foresight and financial services.
  • A capacity to build trust and network.
  • A spirit of initiative, a sense of responsibility and a good organization.
  • An ease with digital tools.
  • The motivation needed to complete demanding training and acquire new expertise.
  • Prior experience in insurance is an asset, but is not indispensable. The post is open to persons undergoing vocational retraining.

Our offer

  • An annual gross fixed salary of CHF 100,000, independent of your commercial production.
  • A bonus* based on your results, based on goals and a scale set in advance.
  • A training course for the AFA qualification.
  • Community support and progressive responsibility.
  • Digital tools dedicated to the analysis and monitoring of foresight.
  • The opportunity to participate in the development of a fintech based in Sion.
  • In the first year of service, the bonus is capped at CHF 200,000.

Do I need a cross-border work permit for a role in Valais?

EU/EFTA citizens living in an EU/EFTA state can apply for a G permit and can work anywhere in Switzerland: border zones no longer apply to them, and they must return home at least once a week. The Swiss employer files the request with Canton Valais's migration office after the contract is signed. Processing times vary by canton, so confirm the specifics with Valais's cantonal migration office or with HR during the application.
