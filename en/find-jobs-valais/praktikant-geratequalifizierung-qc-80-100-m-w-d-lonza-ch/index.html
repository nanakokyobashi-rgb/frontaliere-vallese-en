Full description

The place of work of this office is in Visp, Switzerland.

The employees of the Equipment Qualification QC III group support the QC departments in Visp in cooperation with the respective QC team leaders and laboratory managers. They are responsible for the process of initial qualification of analytical equipment (HPLC, GC, titration, scales, etc.) within the QC organization.

This body requires a continuous presence on site. Personal collaboration enables close, timely cooperation and the technical precision required for the production of medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What is waiting for you

  • An Agile Career and Dynamic Work Culture
  • An inclusive and ethical working environment
  • Compensation programs that recognize high performance
  • A variety of advantages, depending on function and location

Your tasks

  • Planning and preparation of equipment qualifications.
  • Coordination and implementation of initial qualifications (URS, DQ, IQ, OQ and PQ).
  • Creation, maintenance, review and archiving of qualification documentation.
  • Ensure compliance with regulatory requirements and internal quality standards.
  • Processing of deviations as well as support in CAPA and change management.
  • Coordination of relevant interfaces and participation in the continuous improvement of processes and processes.

What we are looking for

  • Completed or ongoing university studies in chemistry, pharmacy, biotechnology, process engineering or a comparable scientific-technical discipline
  • Experience in GMP-regulated environment, preferably pharmaceutical industry of advantage
  • Experience with analytical laboratory equipment and its qualification is advantageous
  • Good technical problem-solving and coordination skills.
  • Independent, structured and solution-oriented working method.
  • Good communication skills and enjoy working with different functions.
  • Good knowledge of German and English.
