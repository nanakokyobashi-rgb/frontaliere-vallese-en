Full description

To complete our team, we are looking for a 100% Charpentier (M/F) Technician/Contremaître-sse or equivalent training.

  • Type of contract: Permanent position (CDI)
  • Commitment Date: Immediately or to be agreed
  • Place of work: Val de Bagnes (VS)

About us Born in 1974, the company Frossard Bois SA, Charpente et Menuiserie is composed of 45 employees, 19 of whom are dedicated to the carpentry sector. Passionate about wood, we manufacture buildings and create furniture in the image of our basic material: with nobility and beauty. In order to satisfy its clients, to maintain an irreproachable quality of work and to ensure the well-being of its employees, our company is constantly evolving.

Your main missions As a professional in design and construction, you intervene on sustainable wooden construction projects. Your responsibilities are as follows:

Design and technical management

  • Study the plans and specifications of clients, architects and engineers by integrating technical, budgetary and normative constraints.
  • Perform static calculations.
  • Draw accurate execution plans using Cadwork software while ensuring that safety, quality and durability standards are met.

Project monitoring and logistics

  • Estimate quantities, estimate materials costs, and place orders.
  • Collaborate with the different actors of the project and ensure compliance of the execution.
  • Lead and monitor work in workshop and on site, participate in meetings and monitor quality.
  • Ensure strict compliance with budgets and deadlines.
  • Prepare invoices (drafting of texts and metering).

Secondary missions (in-house insured training)

  • Calculate prices, write and format offers via Triviso software.
  • Program the CNC machining centre for solid wood elements via Lignocam software.
  • Follow-up and training of apprentices within the company (regular interviews, monitoring of the programme).

Your profile

  • You have a training course in Wood Technician / Charpente or an equivalent title.
  • You have a thorough knowledge of wood construction materials, techniques and tools.
  • You master CAD Cadwork software as well as basic IT tools (Word, Excel).
  • You are recognized for your rigour, accuracy and sense of detail.
  • You have excellent team spirit, initiative, and anticipation and adaptation capabilities.
  • You share our sensitivity to ecology and sustainability.

What we offer you

  • Interesting and varied challenges within a recognized and firmly rooted company in Val de Bagnes.
  • A dynamic, human-sized environment where decisions are taken quickly and where each employee counts.
  • Passionate colleagues and a friendly working atmosphere every day.
