Are you passionate about the field of care and have the ability to make the daily lives of those who need help shine? Looking for an opportunity that will allow you to make significant improvements in the lives of others? Look no further, this exceptional opportunity is within your reach.

For our sites in Saxon and Martigny, we are looking for dedicated students in the care field.

Join our teams and contribute to our mission to care for our customers on a daily basis.

Care students

About us: You will not simply be a care professional, you will become a craftsman of well-being, actively contributing to improving the quality of life of our valuable clients. Your mission will be to provide quality care and assistance directly at home. By working closely with a multidisciplinary team, you will play an essential role in maintaining the well-being of our clients.

What we are looking for:

  • An EHS/ES or basic care (CRS) SWC or equivalent;
  • Experience in the area of home care and assistance would be an asset;
  • The ability to work within a multidisciplinary and autonomous team;
  • Knowledge of the canton's health, social and voluntary network;
  • Reflexivity and adaptability;
  • The mastery of common computer tools;
  • Available evenings and/or weekends;
  • No one's being transported.

What we offer

  • A varied activity in a growing sector;
  • A dynamic working framework in collaboration with the network and families;
  • Mobile work tools to facilitate customer tracking;
  • The development of skills and opportunities for continuing training;
  • First-rate social benefits;
  • Conditions in accordance with the SLD CTC.

Don't hesitate! Join us as soon as possible to make your positive contribution and make a difference in the well-being of our customers.
