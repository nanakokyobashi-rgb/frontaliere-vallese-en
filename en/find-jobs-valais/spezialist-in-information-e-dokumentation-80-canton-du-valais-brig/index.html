Full description

Specialist in information and documentation (80%) at the Department of Culture, Mediathek Wallis, Brig The mission of Mediathek Wallis - Brig is diverse. It is simultaneously a study and educational media library, a generally public media library and an educational documentation center.

Their tasks

  • You accompany and supervise people in training and in the internship and work on their work plans with you ensure the advice and the public service in the Pedagogical documentation You work according to the applicable rules in the media selection and procurement with you take over the documentary development and organize the magazine stock

Your profile

  • Bachelor's or Master's degree as I+D specialist proven experience in dealing with learners (vocational trainer course) Proven social skills
  • Organizational sense and sense of responsibility Sense of public services Experience in cataloguing ability to work independently and in a team Reliability and flexibility
  • Mother tongue German with good knowledge of the second official language

Job opening

  1. February 2027 or by agreement
