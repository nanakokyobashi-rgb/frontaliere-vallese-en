Your assignment

  • Interpret technical drawings and prepare production works.
  • Measure, track and cut materials according to the needs of the project.
  • Perform holes, grindings, adjustments and assembly of components.
  • Run MIG/MAG and TIG welding on steel, stainless steel and aluminum.
