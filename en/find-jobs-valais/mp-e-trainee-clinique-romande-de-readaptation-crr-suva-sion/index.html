Entry into office: 1 July 2027 Take your first professional steps in a state-of-the-art clinic, serving people who learn to live after an accident. Join the Romance Rehabilitation Clinic (CRR) in Sion as: MP-E Intern Professional Maturity Economics and Services (MP-E) What are we doing? At the RRC, we accompany people who have experienced an accident or have neurological disorders (para- or tetraplegia, amputation, burns, stroke, head injury) to regain their autonomy and, where possible, their place in working life. For more information: www.crr-suva.ch. Your internship in brief

  • Start: July 1, 2027
