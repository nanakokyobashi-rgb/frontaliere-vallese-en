Full description

The Valais Hospital is looking for the Central Institute of Hospitals

Chief Medical Officer, Forensic Clinic (60-100%)

for the Forensic Medicine Service

Join a dynamic service offering an activity covering the entire field of forensic medicine. Within a human-sized structure, you will benefit from a varied practice, a high degree of autonomy and direct involvement in the development of services and projects.

Mission

You carry out forensic investigations at the request of the authorities (among other body liftings, external examinations, autopsyes, identifications, clinical examinations, case studies). You are also involved in the development of the service, the training of employees and interdisciplinary collaboration with internal and external partners.

Profile

You have a federal degree in medicine or European equivalent and you have a specialist degree in forensic medicine. You show initiative, dynamism, excellent relational and communication skills, good teamwork and a constructive approach to mistakes.

You are native French with a good level of understanding in German. You are in possession of a Category B driver's licence.

Offer

The opportunity to exercise within a developing service in an environment conducive to autonomy, collaboration and innovation. You will benefit from continuing training and further training opportunities, career development prospects and attractive conditions of employment in a privileged setting in the heart of Valais.
