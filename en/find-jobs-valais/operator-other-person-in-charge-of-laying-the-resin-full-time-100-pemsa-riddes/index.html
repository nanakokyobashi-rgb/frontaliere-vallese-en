Your task

  • Accurately apply products with spatula or roller to ensure a quality finish.
  • Prepare and mix different materials according to specific formulations for each yard.
  • Perform the sanding and sanding of screeds or other coatings, ensuring the safety and compliance of surfaces.
  • Put technical resins on terraces, industrial floors and parking spaces in compliance with quality standards and deadlines.
  • Working independently, effectively organizing their own interventions and anticipating the specific needs of the yard.
  • Manage moving to the yards with a personal vehicle to ensure punctuality and responsiveness.
  • To actively contribute to the care and maintenance of the tools and equipment used.

And now we're talking about you?

  • French mastery at a minimum level B1, indispensable for understanding the instructions and communication on site.
  • Comprehensive practical experience in applying with roller or spatula, including mixing and preparing specific products.
  • Solid expertise in sanding and sanding of various screeds or coatings, with attention to detail and quality.
  • Ability to lay resins on terraces, industrial floors and parking spaces, in compliance with Swiss technical standards.
  • Several years of professional experience in this sector, ideally developed in Switzerland.
  • Compulsory driving licence, to facilitate movement between different yards.
  • Person motivated, conscientious and endowed with a spirit of initiative, ready to fully engage in his duties.
  • Resident in the Valais, to ensure the indispensable proximity to the places of intervention (it prefers a maximum distance of 30 km).
  • A successful professional experience of at least 3 years on the Swiss market is a serious advantage.
