We at Pemsa offer the perfect balance between the flexibility of temporary work and the safety of indefinite employment. Forty of 30 years of experience, we strongly support hundreds of Swiss companies specialized in the technical sectors of construction, construction and industry, providing them with top-level professionals. Our standards are high: we value motivation and enthusiasm, because they are the basis of excellence. If you want to join a dynamic and motivated team, the position of Carpentiere – wooden building (M/F/D) is waiting for you to face exciting challenges.

Your assignment

  • Perform carpentry and wooden structures on newly built buildings or under renovation.
  • Read and interpret projects to prepare and assemble the various elements.
  • Track, cut, drill and adjust the wood pieces.
  • Mount the structures in the yard and ensure the positioning with precision.
  • Install cover elements, facades, ceilings, stairs and other wooden works.
  • Make thermal and acoustic insulation.
  • Participate in the renovation, maintenance and restoration of existing structures.

And now we're talking about you?

  • Federal Capacity Certificate (AFC) as a carpenter or equivalent training recognised in Switzerland.
  • At least 2 years of experience in the industry.
  • Good mastery of wood construction techniques.
  • Ability to work on the basis of projects and to comply with technical indications.
  • French level B2 minimum.
  • Driving license B and availability to move welcome.
  • Residence located 30 km from the site.
  • Autonomous, meticulous person who appreciates teamwork.
