Senior Procurement Specialist

Attached to Supply Chain, the holder will be responsible for the direct supply of raw materials (bulk and packaged streams) and indirect purchases.

Your profile · Commercial or technical training (CFC), ideally supplemented by advanced training in procurement, logistics or supply chain (federal patent, ES, HES) or experience deemed equivalent. · Confirmed experience of at least 5 years in raw material supplies, preferably in the industrial field, with good control over supplier relations and operational negotiation (time limits, deliveries, transport). · Knowledge of procurement and inventory management techniques and strategies. · Sensitivity to EHS requirements and handling of dangerous goods (safety data sheets, transport of dangerous goods). · Basic financial knowledge and LES experience, ideally SAP MM. · English and French, German is an asset. · Ability to coordinate and unite a team, with the potential to move towards a Team Leader function. · Rigourous, autonomous and solutions oriented, with good management of priorities and an excellent sense of communication and collaboration.

Your Responsibilities

Procurement of raw materials and supplier management · Analyze requirements, initiate purchase requests and monitor current orders on a daily basis.

  • Negotiate with suppliers the execution of orders: acceleration or postponement of deliveries, changes in transport.
  • Manage different supply flows (bulk, packaged).
  • Evaluate suppliers, monitor lead times and analyze delivery delays, and manage improvement plans.

Indirect procurement · Manage indirect purchases: enter purchase requests in SAP or other business systems, request bids if necessary and resolve billing deviations with buyer and provider. Quality, EHS, audits and claims

  • Integrate EHS requirements into supply: packaging, connection and transport.

· Collaborate with Quality for customer and supplier audits.

  • Process claims (complaints) suppliers, monitor and organize returns if applicable.
  • Document procedures in company systems and ensure they are up-to-date and applicable.

Indicators and systems · Analyse KPIs and prepare periodic activity reports.

  • Ensure that SAP always reflects the current state (orders, stocks, deadlines).
  • Key user for SAP MM module.

Interactions and projects · Interact with the central purchasing service, which negotiates prices and conditions and turns purchase requests into orders.

  • Collaborate with Production Planning, Logistics, Warehouse, Production, Purchasing, Finance, Quality, EHS and website partners.
  • Contribute to projects to continuously improve supply processes and flows between suppliers, Supply Chain and the production site.
