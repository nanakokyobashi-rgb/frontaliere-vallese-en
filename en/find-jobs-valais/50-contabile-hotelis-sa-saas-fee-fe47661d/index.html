50% Accountant M/F

Company description

On behalf of our client, a group established in the Saas\-Fee region and active in the hospitality, catering and real estate sectors, we seek to strengthen its administrative department by:

Description of post

Connected to management and in close cooperation with the financial management, you manage the financial accounts of several companies in the group, until the annual closure. You are responsible for current accounting operations, including the management of creditors and debtors, bank reconciliations and monthly transactions.

You are also responsible for drawing up VAT accounts and participate in discussions with the review body and management. Depending on your skills and the needs of the organisation, you can also intervene in the management of wages and social insurance.

A true trusted person, you guarantee the reliability of the financial data and contribute to the smooth administrative functioning of the group, while ensuring rigorous and structured monitoring of your files.

Profile sought

For the benefit of a federal financial and accounting specialist certificate or equivalent training, you have several years of experience in a similar function. Experience in the hotel, restaurant or real estate sectors is a definite advantage.

A good knowledge of PPE accounting or hotel accounting specificities is an additional asset. You have a perfect command of German and good knowledge of English or French. Autonomous, organized and with an excellent sense of responsibility, you work with precision, reliability and discretion. Your ability to manage multiple entities and collaborate effectively with different interlocutors allows you to quickly integrate into a versatile structure.

###

  • We offer you

We offer you a 50 per cent range of positions within a dynamic group that brings together several business areas and provides a cross-sectional view of its operations.

You enjoy a great autonomy in the organisation of your work, with at least one day per week to be carried out on site in Saas\-Fee, the rest of the time can be arranged flexibly or on telework, according to agreement.

In direct connection with management, you will hold a responsible position in a human-sized professional environment. You will also benefit from attractive working conditions as well as benefits from the group's hotels and restaurants.

Start: immediately or to be agreed.
