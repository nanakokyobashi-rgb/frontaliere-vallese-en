Full description

The Domus Foundation, a Valaisan institution for psychosocial rehabilitation, accompanies adults facing major mental disorders. Nearly 150 employees are committed to it with conviction and professionalism. Our vision: self-determination for everyone, a solution-centric approach and professional practices marked by benevolence and responsibility. We are looking for our home in Ardon un.e:

Nurse in Psychiatry (60% to 80%)

or to be agreed upon

We offer you

  • ensure daily nursing care for persons accompanied as a whole, at the preventive, curative, medico-educational and palliative level, where appropriate;
  • managing all medical consultations and continuity of care;
  • provide support to beneficiaries within the Foyer d'Ardon in their daily activities with a view to psychosocial rehabilitation based on self-determination;
  • accompanying residents in their individual projects and objectives;
  • developing a privileged relationship with the network of accompanied persons;
  • collaborate in interprofessionality with social workers, ESAs, ASSCs, day centres and workshops.

We offer

  • join a foundation guided by participatory management, positive dynamics and a strong spirit of openness;
  • to integrate an institution certified by Promotion Santé Suisse, concerned with the well-being of everyone and the teams where listening, collaboration, mutual assistance and friendliness are cultivated;
  • make your place in a structure that regularly carries out many projects to which everyone can contribute;
  • benefit from annual scheduled work schedules and collaboration within a multidisciplinary team;
  • enjoy a pleasant working environment and good social and wage conditions;
  • Continuing training every year via our training catalogue "Acting Together".

Your profile

  • you benefit from a Bachelor of Nursing or equivalent title, CAS as psychiatrist an asset;
  • you have at least 2 years of professional experience. Knowledge of the RAI (Resident Assessment Instrument) software is an asset;
  • With a sense of organisation, management of priorities, you like to work as a team but also independently, you appreciate versatility;
  • you master the usual computer tools (MS Office);
  • you have a private vehicle.

Interested? Additional information can be obtained at 027 205 75 00. Please send us your complete file with CV, photo, cover letter, diplomas and work certificates by August 31, 2026. The files will be treated confidentially.
