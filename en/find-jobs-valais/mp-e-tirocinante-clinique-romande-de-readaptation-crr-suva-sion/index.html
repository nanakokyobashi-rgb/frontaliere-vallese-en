Full description

Take your first professional steps in a cutting-edge clinic, serving people who are relearning to live after an accident. Join the Romande Rehabilitation Clinic (CRR) in Sion as a:

MP-E Intern Professional Maturity in Economics and Services (MP-E) What do we do? At the CRR, we support people who have experienced an accident or suffer from neurological conditions (para- or tetraplegia, amputation, burns, stroke, traumatic brain injury) to regain their autonomy and, when possible, their place in professional life. To learn more: www.crr-suva.ch.

Your internship in brief

  • Duration : 12 months
  • Work rate : 100%
  • Location : Sion
  • Start : 1 July 2027
  • Planned rotations notably within the HR department, administration, and patient flow management

Your tasks

Depending on the department, you may for example

  • welcome and guide patients and visitors;
  • prepare, enter, and file records and data;
  • handle mail, orders, and invoicing;
  • participate in planning and organising the daily work of teams;
  • support the HR department (personnel files, contracts, recruitment).

Your profile

  • You are training for MP-E or about to start it;
  • You are curious, comfortable in contact with others, and caring;
  • You are reliable, punctual, precise, and responsible;
  • You are as comfortable behind a screen as face to face;
  • You want to join the healthcare sector and be part of our mission.

What we offer you

  • A concrete and rewarding immersion in the field;
  • The opportunity to discover several areas thanks to rotations;
  • Varied tasks that develop your administrative and organisational skills;
  • Structured support from a trainer throughout your internship.

Our benefits

  • Remuneration above market standards;
  • Meals at advantageous prices;
  • Free access to sports facilities and the swimming pool;
  • Time dedicated to your studies to be agreed with your supervisor;
  • The possibility to continue your professional career at the CRR after your CFC, depending on available positions.
