Full description

Pure Clinics is a group of medical and dental clinics in Suisse Romande offering, with a high level of ethical requirements, medical and dental consultations, scheduled and urgent. Pure Clinic Group offers quality care in a comprehensive approach to health. We integrate the principles of holistic medicine, aimed at taking care of the patient's health as a whole, in addition to dental care.

Employment information •Activity rate: to be agreed

  • Type of contract: indefinite

•Workplace: Sion.

By joining our group, you will benefit from a modern working environment and the constant support of our teams dedicated to medical assistance and administrative management, allowing you to focus on your practice and your patients.

YOUR MISSIONS

  • Care for patients presenting themselves
  • Monitoring, balance sheets and treatment
  • Collaborate with other health professionals
  • Ensure that medical records are updated in accordance with regulations.

YOUR QUALIFICATIONS

•To the benefit of a federal diploma in medicine (General Internal Medicine or Medical Practitioner) or a qualification recognised by the Medical Professions Commission (MEBEKO) •Authorisation of practice in the canton of Valais, or in the process of obtaining •French minimum C1 •Polyvalence, organizational capacity and administrative ease

OUR ATOUTS

•Access to state-of-the-art technologies (impedancemeter, multi-frequency, ECG, MAPA, ...) •External and internal training to support your ongoing professional development. •A strong commitment to continuous improvement of our processes and care. •Our clinics are also training sites for apprentices, thus contributing to the transmission of our know-how. •An excellent team atmosphere, conducive to personal and professional development. •Specialists in areas such as micronutrition, aesthetic care and General Sport Medicine.
