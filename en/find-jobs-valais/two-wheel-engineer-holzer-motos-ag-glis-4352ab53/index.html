Full description

We, a two-wheeled company in the Brig/Oberwallis area, can offer a full-year job with good employment conditions to a technically oriented clerk. The work includes the acceptance of customer orders, the recording of orders, if necessary with prior assessment of the vehicles, the organization until the workshop date, as well as the preparation of offers / invoices.

  • It also includes the organization around vehicle sales, where in addition to creating vehicle purchase and exchange offers, the work before and after
  • the conclusion of contracts for motorised two-wheelers.
  • In addition, customer service, transport of two-wheelers, as well as sale of accessories, clothing that is offered around the two-wheeler.
  • The employment requires the possession of a valid ticket of the cat. A
  • (motorcycles of unlimited power) and category B (passenger cars) A missing driving licence can also be obtained immediately after starting work.

We would like to get to know interested people who hold a diploma as a technical merchant or can prove several years of job experience matching the above-described job profile.
