Full description

Chubb Sicli, a subsidiary of the APi Group, has been operating in Switzerland since 1923, selling, installing and maintaining high-quality products in the fields of fire protection, firefighting, evacuation and electronic security.

For our office in the East region, we are looking for a motivated and reliable person.

Service technician (100%) French-German

Contract type: Permanent employment contract Job: By appointment Area of operation: Region of Sion and Upper Valais

Are you looking for a new challenge? We offer you an attractive opportunity with an interesting and varied activity within a group of companies. Professional training by qualified colleagues is guaranteed. As a service technician, you install fire extinguishers, safety markings and other fire protection materials. In addition, you carry out the annual maintenance of the deletion points at our various customers and sell our products as part of your assignments. They work very independently and in particular take on the following tasks:

  • Control and maintenance of fire extinguishers and extinguishing posts.
  • exchange and sale of fire extinguishers.
  • Control and maintenance of safety lighting.
  • Sale of various small security materials.
  • Agreement of customer appointments.

Required qualifications and experience

  • EFZ in a technical profession or training recognised as equivalent.
  • Experience in a technical function is an advantage.
  • Very good knowledge of French in word and writing (at least level C1) and mandatory knowledge of German at least level B2.
  • Category B driving licence.

Your profile

  • Versed in craftsmanship, assembling and using tools.
  • Self-employed, dynamic and happy to work in the field.
  • Reliable, accurate and conscientious.
  • Well organized and set priorities right.
  • Team capable and good interpersonal skills.
