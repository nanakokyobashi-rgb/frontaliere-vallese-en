Your task

  • Assemble by welding and glazing, attach the installations to the wall or ceiling with precision and rigor.
  • Install heating systems, including boilers and heat pumps.
  • Connect heat pumps efficiently in compliance with standards.
  • Run TIG welding on steel and stainless steel in place, ensuring the quality and durability of the joints.
  • Intervention on new construction and renovation sites, adapting to the specific needs.
  • Perform the art laying of radiant floors according to Swiss standards.
  • Putting the plants into operation, make the necessary adjustments such as pressure, temperature and run to ensure optimum operation.

  • Useful: At least 3 years of professional experience in Switzerland in the heating sector.
