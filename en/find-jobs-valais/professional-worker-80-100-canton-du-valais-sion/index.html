Full description

Position A professional worker (80-100%) with the Civil Security and Military Service, Cantonal Military Affairs Office, Cantonal Logistics Center, in Sion

Duties

  • In accordance with the Army Logistics Base directives, you ensure the cleaning and maintenance of all premises in the barracks buildings as well as the disinfection of sanitary facilities and kitchen hygiene
  • You report any malfunctions and damage discovered during daily maintenance to your supervisor
  • You ensure the handover and recovery of premises to the troops according to the allocation plan
  • You report to your supervisor any inventory shortages and damage identified for billing purposes
  • You support and supervise the troops in infrastructure management activities
  • You assist the troops in training support in accordance with requests from troops on site

Desired Profile

  • CFC certificate as a cleaning agent, recognized training in the field of building cleaning or equivalent training/experience
  • Practical experience in the maintenance of large buildings
  • Ability to work independently and as part of a team
  • Knowledge of the military sector would be an advantage
  • Category B driving license
  • Serious and conscientious person
  • French or German as mother tongue with knowledge of the second official language
  • Start date – special conditions October 1, 2026 or to be agreed
  • Permanent contract, subject to Confederation funding
