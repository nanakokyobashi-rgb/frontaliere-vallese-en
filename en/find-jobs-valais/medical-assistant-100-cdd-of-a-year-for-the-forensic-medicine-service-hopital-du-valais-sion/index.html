Full description

The Valais Hospital is looking for the Central Institute of Hospitals

Assistant physician 100% (one-year CDD)

for the Forensic Medicine Service.

Join a dynamic service offering an activity covering the entire field of forensic medicine. Within a human-sized structure, you will benefit from a varied practice, a high degree of autonomy and direct involvement in the development of services and projects.

Mission

Under supervision, you carry out forensic investigations at the request of the authorities (among other things body lifting, external examinations, autopsyes, identifications, clinical examinations, case studies). You take part in scientific seminars, theoretical and practical courses as part of the post-graduate training in forensic medicine.

Profile

You have a federal medical degree or a degree recognized by the Federal Medical Professions Commission (MEBEKO). One or more years of post-graduate training in forensic medicine is an advantage. You are native French or German, good knowledge of the second language is an advantage. You have a driver's license.

Offer

A stimulating and diverse environment allowing you to capitalize on your knowledge and experience, while gradually developing your skills and autonomy. Depending on your evolution and profile, longer-term collaboration may be considered.
