Our promise

Together, let's combine our energies! This is our creed.

At EUROMASTER, a 100% subsidiary of the Michelin Group, we aim to become the #1 partner for the equipment and maintenance of all types of vehicles.

To join us is to integrate a network of European dimension in the service of the mobility of all in complete safety. Our Group respects each personality, promotes diversity and actively promotes the well-being of its employees and the good work together.

As part of the opening at the end of January of our future Centre for Industrial Vehicles (VI \&VL) \to Martigny (VS), we are looking for our future:

Centre Manager / Service Center Manager (M/F)

VOTE MISSION

Attached to the Regional Director, you ensure the operational launch, commercial development and overall financial profitability of the new centre in Martigny. A true leader in the field, you drive and mobilize your team on a daily basis, while being the guarantor/provider of safety and operational excellence.

Your main responsibilities:

  • Accommodation customer:Receive, advise and retain our clients (individuals and professionals) on a daily basis. Deploy promotional offers, implement sales opportunities and ensure the development of the point of sale and reception space according to Euromaster standards.
  • Infrastructure Safety Guarantee: Incarnate and enforce strict health, safety and environmental protection (HES) rules. Ensure that everyone works safely and ensure the perfect maintenance of equipment, tools and premises.
  • Team Management: Recruit, integrate, animate and build your team members into skills. Set goals, conduct individual interviews and maintain a motivating and secure working environment.
  • Operational, administrative management: Ensure rigorous administrative management of the centre in accordance with internal procedures and audits. Monitor performance indicators (business figures, margins, loads), adapt workshop schedules to fluctuations in activity and rigorously manage stocks and restocks.

VOTRE PROFIL

  • Training: Holder of an SWC in the automotive industry (mechatronician/ne, mechanic/ne) or training deemed equivalent. Continuing training in business or customer relations is an asset.
  • Experience: Recognized experience from 3 to 5 years in a similar function combining customer reception and workshop, with 2 to 3 years of proven experience in team driving and B2C sales.
  • Technical expertise: Very good knowledge in the areas of VL/VI tyres and vehicle maintenance.
  • Computer tools:Excel mastery for tracking and analyzing operational reporting, and great ease in using internal business software (managing calendar/planning, ordering, billing). SAP knowledge is a plus.
  • Language: Excellent mastery of French (written and oral).
  • Personality: Acute customer service, natural leadership, rigor on safety instructions, autonomy and stress resistance.
  • Permits: Driver's licence (category B) required.

WHAT WE OFFER

  • The unique opportunity to open, drive and grow a brand new service centre
  • An indefinite contract (CDI) within a large group
  • A fixed wage paid over 13 months, supplemented by a variable share Euromaster \& bonus Michelin Group
  • Access to Michelin Group's shareholding plan
  • Beneficial employee rates on all our products and services
  • 5 weeks of paid leave per year
  • Prospects for professional development within the Michelin Group
