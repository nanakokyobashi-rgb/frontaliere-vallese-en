Your tasks

  • Planning and implementation of technical projects for plant and process optimization as well as product changes
  • Coordination and monitoring of entrusted projects in terms of function, performance, costs and deadlines
  • Supporting ongoing operations and ensuring optimum safety and availability of facilities through professional and economic planning and organisation of maintenance
  • Responsibility for compliance with cGMP guidelines and ensuring the qualification status of installations
  • Advancing technological innovations with an innovative attitude and participation in interdisciplinary committees

Your profile

  • Completed training (FH/HF/ETH) in electrical/automation, process engineering or equivalent specialisation
  • Initial experience in operational or project engineering, maintenance and new plant construction, ideally in a GMP-regulated sector of the pharmaceutical or chemical industry
  • Ideally, knowledge of GMP, COMOS (Plant Engineering Software), DeltaV (Process Control System), MES (Manufacturing Execution System) and SAP
  • Flowing knowledge of German in words and writing as well as reliable negotiation English
  • Joy of interdisciplinary cooperation
  • Business-oriented thinking, high sense of responsibility and reliability
