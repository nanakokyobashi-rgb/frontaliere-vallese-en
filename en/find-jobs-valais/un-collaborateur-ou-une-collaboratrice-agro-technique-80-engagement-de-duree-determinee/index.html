An agrotechnical collaborator (80%, fixed-term appointment) with the Agricultural Service, Office d'arboriculture et cultures maraîches in Châteauneuf (Sion)

Your tasks

  • You are involved in the management of arboriculture quarantine organizations. In particular, you draw up the various maps necessary for monitoring, monitor the territory and draw up the various reports. You plan and coordinate the various tests of the phytosanitary sector of the Office in collaboration with the field and the producers. You prepare protocols, analyze results and write reports You monitor pests and diseases in orchards in order to support phytosanitary releases and advise producers. You respond to producer requests for phytosanitary issues

Your profile

  • Bachelor or Master in agronomy, earth sciences or equivalent training
  • Good knowledge of plant protection, including bacterial fire
  • Good knowledge of fruit production and Valais orchard Results analysis and synthesis skills ArcGIS knowledge French mother tongue with second official language knowledge
