Full description

Satom SA is an active company in French-speaking Switzerland in the production of energy and material recovery. Through its commitment to the circular economy, it transforms waste into resources and contributes to the production of more sustainable energy. Joining Satom SA is a concrete participation in the energy and environmental transition.

Your mission

At the heart of our industrial installations, you ensure the reliability, evolution and optimization of automation systems contributing to the performance of our energy recovery sites.

Your responsibilities

  • Ensure the operation, maintenance and evolution of control systems\-command;
  • Participate in the commissioning and optimization of automated installations;
  • Diagnosing malfunctions and implementing appropriate corrective actions;
  • Develop and deploy technical solutions to meet the needs of operators and maintenance;
  • Pilot or accompany automation projects, from study to operation;
  • Work closely with production, maintenance and engineering teams;
  • Contribute to continuous improvement, digitization of industrial processes and OT cybersecurity.

Your profile

  • Diploma of ES Technician in Automation or training deemed equivalent;
  • Significant interest in complex industrial environments;
  • Knowledge of automation, supervisory systems and industrial networks;
  • Experience with Siemens, ABB, Schneider Electric or similar technologies, an asset;
  • Analytical capacity, initiative and responsibility;
  • Taste for teamwork and project management.

What we offer you

  • A key role within a company committed to sustainability and the circular economy;
  • Modern industrial facilities and various technologies;
  • Stimulating technical projects with a high degree of autonomy;
  • Opportunities for continuing training and professional development;
  • A competent and collaborative team;
  • Attractive working conditions and excellent social benefits.
