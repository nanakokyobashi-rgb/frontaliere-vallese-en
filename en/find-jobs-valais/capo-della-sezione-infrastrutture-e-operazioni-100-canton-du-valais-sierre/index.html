Full description

A Head of Section "Infrastructure and Operations" (100%) at the Territorial Unit III Service (SUT III), Martigny, Sierre or Brig The Territorial Unit III Service is one of the 11 units in Switzerland that deal with the maintenance of national roads. SUT III employs 120 collaborators who guarantee 24 hours the safety and operation of national roads in Valais. This network extends over 186 kilometres from the exit of the A9 motorway from Bex-north to Gondo.

Your tasks

  • As a member of management, you lead the infrastructure and operations section in terms of finance, organization and human resources
  • Your responsibilities include developing and monitoring the section's budget and ensuring efficient use of resources You plan, coordinate and supervise the maintenance of the infrastructure of the national roads and maintenance centres, in order to ensure a safe, available and efficient road network
  • You are also responsible for the fleet of vehicles and operating equipment of the entire service You represent the section with OFROU, authorities and partners and participate in the strategic reflections of Territorial Unit III You make an essential contribution to road infrastructure renewal, modernisation and development projects
  • You ensure that the safety, quality, availability and sustainability requirements of road infrastructure are met at all times In addition, you contribute to the continuous improvement of processes, the development of steering and management tools and the digital transformation of the section You participate in the management picket service

Your profile

  • You have a diploma in civil engineering (EPF, HES) or training deemed equivalent You benefit from a proven experience in leading teams
  • You also have a solid experience in road infrastructure management Proven experience in project management and budget management complements your profile With unifying leadership, you can mobilize your teams and support the development of your employees With excellent communication skills, you know how to convince and unite interlocutors with diverse interests Your organizational sense, your analytical spirit and your ability to make decisions are among your main assets. You work autonomously, pragmatically and resolutely results-oriented You master the usual computer tools; knowledge of OFROU standards and guidelines is an asset French or German mother tongue with excellent knowledge of the second official language Entry into office – Special conditions Date of entry: immediately or to be agreed Indeterminate commitment, but conditional on funding from Confederation
