Full description

Würth AG supplies artisans from all industries with high-quality products and coordinated solutions for workshop, warehouse and work on the move. The wide assortment includes more than 150 000 items: from screws, ankles and fittings to vehicle equipment and stock management, including tools, machinery, chemical and technical products and safety at work. Proven since 1945 and established in Switzerland since 1962, Würth AG offers its partners individual solutions with which they can simplify their daily work. Würth AG has its headquarters in Arlesheim (BL) and is part of the Würth Group, which is active worldwide.

Activity

  • Thanks to our omnichannel strategy (which includes all the points of contact between our customers and Würth, whether digital, physical or interpersonal), we allow our customers maximum flexibility. Making the link between customers and Würth, you develop strategic relations in the long term, based on trust and service.
  • You are interested in the processes and procedures of your customers, you identify their needs and you actively integrate the different points of contact with customers (direct sales, Würth Shops, E\-Business, Telesale) in the research for solutions.
  • You expand the customer portfolio by acquiring new customers in the craft sector and you develop business relationships with existing customers using the appropriate contact point mix.
  • As a collaborator of the external service, you are enthusiastic about your clients and you sell our branded products and our system solutions
  • high quality in the wood field. Your goal: find the best solutions with the biggest benefit for your customers.
