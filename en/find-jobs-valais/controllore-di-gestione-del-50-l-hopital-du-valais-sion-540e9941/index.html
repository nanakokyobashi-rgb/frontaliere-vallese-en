Requirements

Your mission

As a management controller, you track the annual regular investments, from budget creation to closure. You are involved in the financial and analytical (quarterly and yearly) winding-up of capital assets and prepare the various reports to the governing bodies. At the head's request, you support the unit in tracking supplier accounts and inventory. You are involved in the development and budgetary monitoring of regular and strategic investments, contribute to the management of capital assets, including the activation and disposal of assets, and collaborate on quarterly and annual transactions. You develop dashboards and reporting tools tailored to the needs of the unit, participate in the preparation of reports to the State of Valais and assist the person responsible in carrying out his or her various missions. Finally, you are actively involved in the development of capital asset accounting technical guidelines.

Your profile:

  • Professional degree in finance and accounting or Bachelor's degree in business economics or equivalent training;
  • 5 years of experience in a similar position;
  • ease with numbers and computer tools;
  • knowledge and control of accounting rules;
  • editorial convenience;
  • French mother tongue with good knowledge of German.
