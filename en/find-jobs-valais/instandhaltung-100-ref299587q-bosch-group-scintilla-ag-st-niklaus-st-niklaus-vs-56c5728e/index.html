Business description

This is what : About you, our business, our environment.

We shape the future with high-quality technologies and services that inspire enthusiasm and improve people’s lives. Our promise to you is solid: We grow together, enjoy our work and inspire each other.

With its accessories business unit, Power Tools is the world's leading supplier of accessories for \- and craftsmen and industrial customers. At the subsidiary Scintilla AG in St., a workforce of around 800 employees worldwide develops, manufactures and distributes more than 8,000 accessories for power tools.

Scintilla AG has a worldwide unique machine park, which is further developed by qualified specialists. Maintaining these machines is challenging and varied at the same time. If you have a basic electrical or mechanical education and are happy to take on responsibility, you are in the right place with us!

Job description

Become the heartbeat of our production! As a maintainer (f/m/div.), it is your mission to keep our facilities running. Together as a team you try not only to repair the plants, but also to solve preventive problems.

Your tasks essentially include

  • You assume responsibility for the maintenance and maintenance of our machine park.
  • Whether a mechanical problem or an electrical challenge – you find the cause and solve it sustainably
  • With an analytical sense, you show potential for improvement and implement it
  • On predefined days (twice a year) you work with the whole team on investment audits and self-responsible pikettdienst (4\-5 weekends a year)

Qualifications

  • Training: Basic technical training such as automatics, electricians, polymechanics (m/f/div.) ideally with further training as a maintenance specialist
  • Personality and way of working: Flexible, self-responsible, independent personality with a systematic and precise way of working and the willingness for shift work (3\-shift) and pikettdienst
  • Experience and know-how: SAP and MS Office knowledge is an advantage
  • Languages: Good language skills in German

Additional information

Benefits

  • A young, dynamic and experienced team with a collegial approach in a family environment awaits you
  • In this position, you can free your creativity for solutions and benefit from personal scope for action.
  • The shift work (3\-shift) allows you to reconcile work and private life
  • You can look forward to advanced training and further education opportunities

With us you have the opportunity to use your ideas and skills optimally.

Progressive employment conditions and benefits, a high level of technology, innovative machinery from our own special machine construction and a wide range of career opportunities.
