Full description

Joining the Raiffeisen Bank in Monthey is evolving within a modern, human-sized structure where proximity, teamwork and co-operative values guide our daily work.

You want to take on a new challenge and actively contribute to the development of our financing activity? We look forward to meeting you. Aufgaben: • Manage, retain and develop a portfolio of private clients through proactive monitoring and acquisition of new clients. • Support clients in their funding projects and key life stages. • Provide comprehensive and personalized advice on credit, provision, investment and insurance. • Develop solutions tailored to the needs of clients and encourage the adoption of digital services. • Contribute to the Bank's development and outreach through prospecting and local representation. Anforderungen: • Full banking training (banking CFC or equivalent), with SAQ certification or willing to obtain it as part of the function. • Federal patent or higher education, an asset. • Experience confirmed in consulting with private clients. • Strong funding skills and capacity to adopt a comprehensive heritage approach. • Entrepreneurial, autonomous and solutions oriented personality. • Strong business spirit and knowledge of the local economic fabric. • Home in Switzerland, ideally in Chablais. Wir bieten: Co-operative values are our pillars. Together. For each other. They shape how we work together and behave toward each other. The culture of mentoring promotes personal proximity and exchanges on an equal footing at all levels of function.
