Full description

The Territorial Unit III (TU III) Service is one of 11 units in Switzerland responsible for the maintenance of national roads. TU III employs 120 employees who guarantee 24/7 safety and management of national roads in Valais. This network extends over 186 kilometers, from the A9 highway exit at Bex-nord to Gondo.

Missions

  • As a member of the management team, you will lead the Infrastructure and Operations section from financial, organizational, and human resources perspectives
  • Your responsibilities include preparing and monitoring the section's budget as well as ensuring efficient use of resources. You plan, coordinate, and supervise maintenance work on national road infrastructure and maintenance centers, to ensure a safe, available, and high-performing road network
  • You are also responsible for the vehicle fleet and operational equipment of the entire service. You represent the section to OFROU, authorities, and partners and participate in the strategic deliberations of Territorial Unit III. You provide essential contributions to projects for renewal, modernization, and development of road infrastructure
  • You ensure at all times compliance with safety, quality, availability, and sustainability requirements for road infrastructure. Additionally, you contribute to continuous improvement of processes, development of management and monitoring tools, and digital transformation of the section. You participate in the management on-call service

Required Profile

  • You hold a degree in civil engineering (EPF, HES) or equivalent training. You have proven experience in team leadership
  • You also have solid experience in managing road infrastructure. Proven experience in project management and budget control complete your profile. With cohesive leadership, you know how to mobilize your teams and support the development of your employees. With excellent communication skills, you can convince and unite stakeholders with diverse interests. Your organizational sense, analytical thinking, and ability to make decisions are among your main strengths. You work autonomously, pragmatically, and results-oriented. You master common IT tools; knowledge of OFROU standards and directives is an asset. Native French or German speaker with excellent knowledge of the second official language

Special Conditions

  • Open-ended employment contract, contingent on Confederation funding
