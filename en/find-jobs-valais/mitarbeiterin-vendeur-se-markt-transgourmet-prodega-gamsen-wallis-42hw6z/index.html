Full description

Our heart beats for Swiss gastronomy. We are there for our customers with a lot of commitment. In our 31 cash-and-carry stores, via field service and delivery, as well as in the digital world. With a diverse range, attractive own brands, professional advice and innovative services. Transgourmet Schweiz AG is part of the international Transgourmet group. The company is a leader in Swiss delivery and collection wholesale and has around 2,100 employees and 100,000 customers (restaurants, hotels, canteens, homes and hospitals as well as retailers). Tasks

  • In addition to ensuring the freshness and presentation of goods, you are responsible for correct labeling
  • You ensure the constant availability of our products and carry out regular inventory
  • You accept complaints professionally and forward these accordingly
  • At the check-out you scan the goods and/or drawbar implement training (SUVA standard) is completed by your contact person Karin Arnold HR/Administration
