Company description ##

Our customer is a renowned player in the metal construction industry and is looking for a qualified assembly manager to support our team. As an assembly manager, you are responsible for the coordination and monitoring of all assembly work in the field of metal construction. Your skills and experience will play a crucial role in the timely and high-quality completion of our projects.

Job description ##

  • Management and organisation of assembly personnel at construction sites in the field of metal construction
  • Monitoring and coordination of assembly work according to project requirements and technical drawings
  • Ensuring compliance with safety regulations and quality standards during assembly work
  • Planning and coordination of material and resource requirements for assembly projects
  • Close cooperation with project management and other departments to ensure the smooth running of assembly work
  • Monitoring and monitoring the progress of assembly work and meeting schedules and budgets
  • Carrying out quality controls and ensuring a high quality of assembly operations

We are expecting ##

  • Several years of experience in assembly management in metal construction or a similar industry
  • In-depth knowledge of assembly technology and common metal construction methods
  • Experience in HR management and team coordination
  • Good knowledge of relevant safety regulations and quality standards
  • Strong organizational skills and the ability to manage multiple projects simultaneously
  • Strong problem-solving skills and decision-making skills
  • Good communication skills and the ability to interact effectively with different stakeholders
