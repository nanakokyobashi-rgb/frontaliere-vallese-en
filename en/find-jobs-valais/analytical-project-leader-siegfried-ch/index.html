"Expect to grow", personally and professionally: At Siegfried, we offer exciting challenges and many opportunities to showcase your talents. Your Role

Mission: As the Analytic Project Leader, you will lead the analytical activities of your portfolio's new R&D projects, from their development to their transfer and validation. You are the analytical referent of projects, in close connection with internal teams and external clients, and contribute to ensuring quality, GMP compliance and good project implementation.

Tasks

Lead and coordinate the analytical activities of your portfolio's new R&D projects. Develop and optimize analytical methods according to customer, GMP and regulatory requirements. Supervise the validations, transfers and analytical studies required for projects. Be the analytical reference to internal teams and external clients. Analyze and decide on actions to be taken in case of unexpected results, investigations or non-compliance. Coordinate CAPA, Change Controls and project resource tracking. Bring your expertise and scientific leadership to labs, lab leaders and other APLs, and contribute to project meetings and customer visits.

Your Profile

  • First job profile: Doctorate in Chemistry / solid knowledge in analytical and technical knowledge
  • Experienced profile: Doctorate or Master in Chemistry, with solid experience in pharmaceutical environment and/or analytical development. Experience in development, validation and transfer of analytical methods and mastery of techniques.

Complementary competencies

  • Excellent organizational, analytical and project management capabilities
  • Autonomy, rigor, leadership and team spirit

Founded in 1873 in Zofingue, Switzerland, Siegfried has become a global network of 16 sites spread over three continents. With a team of more than 4,200 highly qualified professionals, we bring the innovations of our pharmaceutical customers to the industrial scale and manufacture safe medicines for patients around the world. As a fully integrated CDMO, Siegfried is one of the few organizations able today to ensure the development and production of active ingredients (APIs) and finished galenic forms under one roof. This unique combination of know-how and experience makes us the most reliable partner in the pharmaceutical industry.

Siegfried is a leading CDMO (Contract Development & Manufacturing Organization) headquartered in Zofingen, Switzerland. The company operators manufacturing sites in Evionnaz (Valais), Zofingen (Aargau), and Schlieren (Zurich).
