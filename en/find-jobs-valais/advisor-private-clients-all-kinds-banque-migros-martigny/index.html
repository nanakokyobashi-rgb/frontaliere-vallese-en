Full description

With us, you can get involved and help forge the future customer advice and digitization of the banking sector. We are looking for a personality that anticipates. A person with whom to imagine the future, as a team, with new ideas and a new impetus. Join the bank, which, with some 1800 employees, has placed people at the centre of its concerns since 1958.

Mansioni

  • Through your professional advice in the basic areas of investment, financing, foresight and banking commodities, you help your clients achieve their financial goals
  • Your proactive approach allows you to develop business with your clients, with the aim of Migros Bank becoming their main financial partner
  • You bring your ideas, while supporting Migros Bank's digital transformation, and you help us shape our future.

Required

  • 2 to 3 years of experience in the 360° global council: Private customers
  • Completed professional learning (CFC): Financial Council
  • Good knowledge in the fields of investments, mortgages and providents
  • From experience in the 360° global consultancy to individual customers
  • A personality that corresponds to the human values of Migros Bank, lives them and shares them
  • A positive attitude, the ability to identify the needs of clients and to energize them through an innate sense of communication
  • French (Very good mastery)

Cosa offeriamo

  • Offers for employees: Annual generous "well-being" contribution
  • Child care: Up to CHF 300 per month per child for external childcare
  • Individual continuing training: Attractive contributions to external training
  • Migros Bank: Accounts, mortgages and financial benefits on preferential terms
  • Points Cumulus: Up to 5 additional points per purchase value franc
