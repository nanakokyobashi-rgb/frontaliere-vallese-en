Company description

leteam sa is a temporary and fixed placement agency renowned in Switzerland, operating with pride for over 39 years and owning 28 agencies across the country. Specializing in the placement and rental of staff, she is distinguished in the fields of building, crafts, industry, technology, pharmaceuticals and health.

What if your next job allowed you to work the wood accurately, evolve and really feel in your place? Here, we're not looking for a profile. We're looking for someone who likes the work well done, who likes the workshop, modern machines and team spirit.

Description of post

  • Prepare, trace, process and assemble structural elements
  • Working on modern machines
  • Control quality and conformity of parts before shipment
  • Read and interpret manufacturing plans
  • Work closely with the production team
  • Ensuring security

Profile sought

  • You are trained in wood frame or carpentry (CFC, CAP/BEP, Bac Pro or equivalent)
  • You have at least 3 years experience (but motivation also counts)
  • You master manual tools and production machines
  • You are rigorous, precise and appreciate teamwork
  • You're being carried.
