Full description

You know how to create a garden. Why don't you take over your team?

At Germa Paysages, we create outdoor living areas: gardens, swimming pools, terraces, pergolas and tailor-made amenities. To strengthen our creative sector in Monthey, we are looking for women and tradesmen who love well-made work and beautiful projects.

CHEFFES AND CHECKS OF THE PASSENGER TEAM – CREATION SECTOR

A land to transform. A swimming pool to integrate into a garden. A terrace, walls, plantations... Then the pleasure to take a step back and say: "It was our team that realized this. *

If that's what makes you love your job, we have projects to build together.

At Germa Paysages, you will put your experience at the service of complete creations: gardens, swimming pools, terraces, pergolas and tailor-made amenities. Projects where your practical sense, your ideas and the quality of your finishes will make the difference.

Already head or team leader? Take your place at home. Do you like organizing, anticipating, transmitting and advancing a project? We are looking for people who can take responsibility, with the autonomy and support of our construction managers.

You have experience, but not yet the title? Let's talk about it. You master landscape creation, your colleagues can count on you and you want to lead a team? We are interested in your application. Your next project may also be a new step in your journey.

By joining us, you will find

  • The variety of the trade, with achievements that combine construction, vegetation and relaxation spaces.
  • A concrete role on the ground, to realize, organize and see the results of your work.
  • The ability to transmit, advance your team and evolve yourself.
  • A place in a novel business that is developing, with new projects to be carried out.

What matters to us: a solid creative experience, detail care, reliability and team spirit. A landscape CFC or equivalent training is appreciated.

Permit B required BE trailer permit: a real plus.

Want to meet us? Send us your CV or simply a message with your experience and your desires for the next one. Some photos of your achievements? We'll be happy to find out.

Let's create beautiful gardens. And the rest of your journey.
