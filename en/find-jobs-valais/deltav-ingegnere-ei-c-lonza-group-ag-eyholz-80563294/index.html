Full description

Shape the future of biotech manufacturing at our Visp site by designing and optimizing cutting\-edge Emerson DeltaV automation solutions for major capital expansion projects.

This is a fully site\-based role. Working together in person supports close, real\-time collaboration and the technical precision needed to manufacture medicines to the highest quality and safety standards.

What you will get:

  • An agile career and dynamic working culture.
  • An inclusive and ethical workplace.
  • Competitive hourly rate.

What you will do:

  • Design, configure, and implement Emerson DeltaV automation solutions for high\-impact pharmaceutical and biotech CAPEX projects at Visp.
  • Configure DeltaV control modules, equipment modules, phases, recipes, graphics, alarms, and system interfaces following established company standards.
  • Collaborate closely with cross\-functional teams including process engineering, IT/OT, validation, and operations to deliver fit\-for\-purpose automation systems.
  • Develop and maintain GxP\-compliant automation documentation, including functional design specifications, test protocols, and validation records.
  • Support system architecture design, hardware configuration, network integration, and third\-party system interface development.
  • Troubleshoot automation, hardware, software, and communication issues during project execution, startup, and operational support.
  • Participate in change control, deviation investigations, risk assessments, and continuous improvement initiatives to drive operational excellence.

What we are looking for:

  • Degree in Automation Engineering, Electrical Engineering, Computer Science, or a related technical discipline.
  • Hands\-on experience configuring and administering Emerson DeltaV automation systems within pharmaceutical, biotech, chemical, or industrial manufacturing environments.
  • Strong understanding of process control design, instrumentation, and industrial automation architectures.
  • Working knowledge of GxP, GAMP5, and computerized system validation (CSV) within regulated environments.
  • Practical experience or strong familiarity with CAPEX project execution, system integration, and commissioning processes.
  • Excellent troubleshooting skills and a root\-cause\-analysis mindset for complex automation systems.
  • Professional fluency in German and/or English.
