With 6,260 employees, Spital Wallis is one of the most important employers in the canton. Every year, around 41,800 patients are treated in hospital and more than 670,300 outpatient consultations are carried out. The hospital Wallis is looking for a hospital for the hospital center Oberwallis in the clinic emergency medicine:

Your duties

  • Together with a motivated team, they ensure professional, empathetic and patient-oriented emergency care.
  • They actively promote interdisciplinary cooperation and appreciate the short line to the doctors.
  • With your own initiative and your solution-oriented thinking, you bring fresh ideas to the station’s daily routine.
  • They pass on their valuable knowledge and accompany them motivatingly through their education.

Your profile

  • You have completed training as a nurse (FH, HF) – preferably supplemented by NDS Emergency Care.
  • Ideally, they already have 1 year of professional experience and keep an overview even in hectic moments.
  • You beat for the people: you meet patients and relatives with a lot of sensitivity and appreciation.
  • They are communicative, like to tackle things and convince through a structured, exact working method as well as good IT knowledge.

Our offer

  • Exciting everyday life: A varied, responsible and independent activity in which no day is like the other.
  • Strong team: A collegial, committed and humorous team that stands together and keeps each other’s backs free.
  • Perspectives: Attractive opportunities for internal and external training (e.g. support for NDS emergency care).
  • Fair conditions: contemporary employment conditions, good social benefits and flexible working hours within the framework of the workload.
