Full description

Lawyer (60%) at DGB, Grundburiefer des Oberwallis (Brig and Leuk) The Land Registry Department is responsible for the state of the rights regarding the land (e.g. property, easements or liens). It consists of 6 basic houses, which are distributed throughout Valais. In addition, there are the Legal Office, the Land Registry Inspectorate and the support units. Teams are responsible for the introduction and computerization of the Swiss Federal Land Register into a fully computerized land register.

Their tasks

  • They participate in the technical and legal administration of the Land Registry
  • They check and process land register applications independently and participate in the updating and cleaning of the land register They support the introduction and computerization of land registers, as well as department-wide modernization projects They collect the hand change taxes and land register fees You ensure the publicity of the land register You participate in the education and training of employees You support other land registers or the management if necessary

Your profile

  • You have a university education in Swiss law at master’s level (Master of Law / lic. iur.), the notarial diploma and/or a lawyer’s diploma is of advantage You are particularly interested in public administration as well as in property and land registry law. They are team-oriented, but can work well independently
  • You act in accordance with customer and process requirements You carry out your tasks precisely
  • They are flexible and goal-oriented You are a German mother tongue and have a good knowledge of the second official language
