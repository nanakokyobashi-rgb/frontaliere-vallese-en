Full description

For one of our customers active in the field of glazing, Opus Emploi SA is looking for an experienced glassmaker for various missions in Valais.

Your tasks

  • Measurement and preparation of glass elements
  • Cutting, shaping and installation of glazing
  • Replacement of broken or damaged windows
  • Installation of glazing on windows, doors, partitions and other structures
  • Repair and renovation work
  • Installation of joints, mastics and fasteners
  • Manufacture and transport of glazing
  • Compliance with safety instructions and surface protection
  • Self-employment or team work according to construction sites

Your profile

  • Glass CFC or equivalent experience in the trade
  • Confirmed experience in installing and replacing glazing
  • Good mastery of the tools and techniques of the trade
  • Self-employed, accurate, careful and reliable
  • Good physical condition
  • Ability to work height as required
  • Driver's licence category B, an asset
