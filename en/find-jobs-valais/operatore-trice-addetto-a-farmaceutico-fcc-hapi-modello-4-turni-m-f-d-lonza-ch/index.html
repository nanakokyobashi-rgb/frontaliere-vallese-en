Become part of our team in Visp as operator/in-patient worker (4 shift operation)

Looking for a challenging and varied activity that will enable you to fully share your experience and expertise from your previous activity? Then you are right for us!

Removal support: Available for eligible candidates and their families.

Our location in Visp in the canton of Valais is growing continuously and we are currently looking for dedicated operators who want to strengthen our team. To prepare you optimally for your future role, we offer a comprehensive training program. After successful completion of the training program, you will take on the execution of the activities in our automated production facilities.

In your role, you perform the workflows and tours independently according to the specifications and record them carefully – always taking into account the strict quality requirements according to GMP. You ensure the high quality of our products and ensure that all safety standards are met. You are responsible for the order and cleanliness of the workplace.

With your experience, you support the induction of new, inexperienced colleagues and help them quickly get into the company. Your versatility and flexibility allow you to master special orders and special assignments with confidence.

In the team, you are actively working to achieve the agreed goals and continuously expand your knowledge by participating in training and courses.
